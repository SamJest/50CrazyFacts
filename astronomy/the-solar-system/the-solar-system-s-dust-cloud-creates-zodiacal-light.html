The Solar System’s Dust Cloud Creates Zodiacal Light

A cosmic glow.

Interplanetary dust scatters sunlight, producing a faint glow called zodiacal light visible before sunrise or after sunset. The dust comes from comets, asteroids, and collisions. Observing it helps map material distribution in the inner solar system.

Why This Matters

It matters because it provides insights into small particle dynamics and solar system formation.

It also affects astronomers’ observations of faint celestial objects.

Did You Know?

Zodiacal light is sunlight scattered by a vast cloud of interplanetary dust.
