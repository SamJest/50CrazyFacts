Pluto Has a Thin Atmosphere That Freezes

Sublimating gas.

Pluto’s atmosphere is extremely thin, mostly nitrogen, and can freeze onto the surface when it moves farther from the Sun. It expands and contracts with Pluto’s orbit. The New Horizons mission revealed haze layers and complex seasonal changes.

Why This Matters

This matters because dwarf planets can have dynamic atmospheres.

It also informs models of atmospheric evolution on small, cold worlds.

Did You Know?

Pluto’s atmosphere can literally vanish and reappear during its orbit.
