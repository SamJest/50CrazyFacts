Black Holes Can Break Neutron Stars Apart

Tidal shredding.

In close encounters, a black hole’s gravity can rip a neutron star into streams of matter before merging. This process releases gravitational waves and electromagnetic radiation.

Why This Matters

It matters because it produces heavy elements like gold and platinum in the cosmos.

It also provides observable signals for multi-messenger astronomy.

Did You Know?

Black holes can literally tear neutron stars to pieces.
