Red Dwarfs Can Show Stellar Activity Cycles

Pulsating patterns.

Some red dwarfs exhibit cycles similar to the Sun’s 11-year sunspot cycle, though often longer and more irregular.

Why This Matters

This helps predict stellar flares and radiation hazards.

It also contributes to understanding magnetic field evolution in small stars.

Did You Know?

Even tiny stars have cosmic mood swings.
