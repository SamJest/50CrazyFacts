Red Dwarfs Were Critical in Discovering Exoplanets

Pioneers of discovery.

Many of the first Earth-sized exoplanets were discovered orbiting red dwarfs because the dimness of the star makes planets easier to detect via transit methods.

Why This Matters

This matters because it changed the focus of exoplanet searches toward smaller stars.

It also accelerates the hunt for potentially habitable worlds.

Did You Know?

Red dwarfs helped us find some of the first truly Earth-like exoplanets.
