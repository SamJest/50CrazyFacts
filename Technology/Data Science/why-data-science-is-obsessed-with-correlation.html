Why Data Science Is Obsessed With Correlation

Correlation doesn’t mean causation—but it still matters. Data science thrives on patterns.

Correlation identifies relationships between variables. While it doesn’t prove cause, it guides hypothesis generation. Many predictive systems rely solely on correlation. This is powerful for forecasting but dangerous for explanation. Skilled data scientists know when correlation is useful and when it misleads. Understanding the distinction is essential.

Why This Matters

This matters because misinterpreting correlation leads to false conclusions. Decisions can go wrong fast.

It reinforces the need for domain knowledge alongside data.

Did you know?

Data science often relies on correlation for prediction, not explanation.
