Why Data Science Models Can Be Hacked

Algorithms aren’t immune to attack. Adversaries exploit weaknesses.

Adversarial attacks manipulate inputs to fool models into misclassifying data. This is critical in image recognition, fraud detection, and autonomous systems. Security measures are necessary to protect integrity. Models must be tested against malicious scenarios. This exposes vulnerabilities hidden in complex systems.

Why This Matters

This matters because critical applications can be compromised. Robustness is essential.

It also drives research in AI safety and model hardening.

Data science models can be tricked using adversarial inputs.
