The Surprising Military Roots of Modern Data Science

Data science didn’t start in tech companies. It started in war rooms. Numbers once guided battlefield decisions.

Many foundational statistical techniques were developed during World War II. Militaries used data to optimize supply chains, radar detection, and troop movement. Operations research emerged as a discipline focused on data-driven decision-making. These methods later migrated into business, healthcare, and technology. What began as survival analysis evolved into modern data science. Today’s tools still rely on principles born from conflict-driven necessity.
