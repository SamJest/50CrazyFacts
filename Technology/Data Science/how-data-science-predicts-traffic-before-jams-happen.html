How Data Science Predicts Traffic Before Jams Happen

Traffic jams form slowly—data science notices early. Patterns reveal congestion ahead of time.

Navigation apps analyze GPS data from millions of devices. Machine learning models detect slowdowns forming in real time. Predictions update continuously as conditions change. Routes are adjusted before jams fully develop. This dynamic optimization reduces commute times globally. Data science turns collective movement into actionable insight.
