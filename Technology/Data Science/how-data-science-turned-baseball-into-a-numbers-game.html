How Data Science Turned Baseball Into a Numbers Game

Baseball changed forever when data entered the dugout. Gut feelings lost ground to spreadsheets.

Data science revolutionized baseball through sabermetrics. Teams began analyzing player performance using advanced statistical models instead of intuition. Metrics like on-base percentage replaced traditional stats. These insights helped underfunded teams compete with wealthier rivals. The approach proved so successful that it spread to nearly every professional sport. Today, analytics guide drafting, training, and in-game strategy.
