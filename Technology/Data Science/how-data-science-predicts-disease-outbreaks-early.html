How Data Science Predicts Disease Outbreaks Early

Data science can detect epidemics before hospitals do. Patterns speak before symptoms spread.

By analyzing search queries, mobility data, and social signals, models can identify abnormal health trends. These early warnings help authorities respond faster. Data science played a role in tracking flu outbreaks and COVID-19 spread. While imperfect, it offers valuable lead time. The approach complements traditional epidemiology.
