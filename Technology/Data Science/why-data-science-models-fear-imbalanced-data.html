Why Data Science Models Fear Imbalanced Data

When one outcome dominates, models get lazy. Imbalance breaks predictions.

Imbalanced datasets occur when one class vastly outnumbers others, such as fraud detection. Models may appear accurate while ignoring rare events. Specialized techniques correct this bias. Without correction, predictions fail silently. Data scientists must actively address imbalance to ensure reliability.

Why This Matters

This matters because rare events are often the most important. Missed signals carry high cost.

It highlights the importance of careful evaluation metrics.

Did You Know?

Imbalanced data can cause misleadingly high model accuracy.
