How Data Science Created the Job That Didn’t Exist Before 2008

Data scientist wasn’t always a career. The title is younger than smartphones.

The term “data scientist” gained popularity around 2008. Companies needed people who could combine statistics, programming, and business insight. Traditional roles couldn’t handle exploding data volumes. The hybrid skillset became essential. Today, it’s one of the most in-demand tech careers. The role continues to evolve rapidly.
