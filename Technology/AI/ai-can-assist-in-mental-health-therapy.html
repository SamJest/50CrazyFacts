AI Can Assist in Mental Health Therapy

Imagine AI as a 24/7 emotional support system. AI mental health tools are becoming mainstream.

AI chatbots and virtual therapists can guide users through cognitive behavioral therapy, mood tracking, and coping strategies. While not a replacement for human therapists, AI provides scalable, accessible mental health support.

AD 1

Why This Matters

AI expands access to mental health resources and reduces barriers.

It also opens discussions about ethics, privacy, and the limits of AI care.

Did You Know?

Some AI systems can detect emotional distress from text input with over 90% accuracy.
