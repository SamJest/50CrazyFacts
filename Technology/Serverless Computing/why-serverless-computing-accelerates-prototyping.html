Why Serverless Computing Accelerates Prototyping

Ideas move faster when infrastructure doesn’t slow them down. Serverless removes friction from experimentation.

Developers can deploy small pieces of functionality instantly without configuring servers. This makes prototyping fast and low-risk. Features can be tested in production-like environments quickly. Failed experiments cost almost nothing since unused code incurs no charges. Serverless encourages rapid iteration and learning, which is ideal for innovation-driven teams.
