Volcano-Proof Cloud Data Centers

Data surviving a volcanic eruption sounds like science fiction—but some cloud providers made it real. Servers can endure lava, earthquakes, and extreme heat.

Volcano-hardened data centers are built with reinforced subterranean structures, heat-resistant materials, and fireproof insulation. They ensure uninterrupted service for critical sectors like finance, healthcare, and government. Engineers simulate catastrophic events to maintain uptime. Fire-dispersion systems, heat-resistant walls, and emergency power keep operations running. This extreme infrastructure protects data from nature’s worst scenarios.
