Holographic Meetings Powered by the Cloud

Forget Zoom—cloud servers are bringing holograms into your office. Your coworkers can appear in 3D from thousands of miles away.

Future cloud infrastructure supports real-time rendering and streaming of holograms. Servers handle depth, light, motion, and AI compression to reduce latency. Participants can join from standard devices while the cloud performs heavy computations. Holographic spaces can scale to thousands of users simultaneously. The cloud turns science-fiction-style meetings into immersive, interactive experiences.
