The Shocking Scale of Ransomware Botnets

Hackers coordinate thousands of infected devices like an army.

Ransomware botnets are networks of compromised machines controlled remotely. They can execute coordinated attacks, encrypt massive amounts of data, or spread laterally across networks. Botnets increase impact and evade simple defenses. Some botnets include hundreds of thousands of devices worldwide. Control servers manage the operation efficiently.
