Why Ransomware Now Targets Entire Cities

Cities have become lucrative targets for hackers. Entire infrastructure is at risk.

Modern ransomware attacks have escalated from individuals and businesses to municipalities. Hackers encrypt critical systems like utilities, traffic control, and emergency services. Demands can reach millions of dollars. Disruptions affect millions of residents and critical services. The trend demonstrates how ransomware evolves to exploit high-value targets.

Why This Matters

It matters because public safety and daily life can be disrupted. The stakes are no longer just financial.

It also forces governments to rethink cybersecurity investments and disaster recovery.
