Why Ransomware Can Shut Down Hospitals in Seconds

Hospitals aren’t just targets—they’re victims of life-and-death consequences.

Ransomware has hit healthcare systems globally. Malware encrypts patient records, lab results, and imaging systems. Doctors can’t access patient histories or treatment plans. Operations are delayed, emergencies compromised, and lives threatened. Recovery can take weeks without backups.
