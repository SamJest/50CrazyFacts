Why Backups Alone Aren’t Enough to Stop Ransomware

Even offline backups can be compromised. Hackers are clever.

Ransomware increasingly targets backup systems and cloud repositories. Attackers may encrypt backups or delete shadow copies. Offline backups mitigate risk but require careful management. Recovery strategies must include multiple isolated copies and tested restore procedures. Reliance on a single method is insufficient.
