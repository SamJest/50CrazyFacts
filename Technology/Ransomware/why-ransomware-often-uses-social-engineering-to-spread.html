Why Ransomware Often Uses Social Engineering to Spread

Hackers rely on brains as much as bytes.

Ransomware campaigns combine malware with social engineering. Tactics include phishing, fake invoices, tech support scams, and malicious downloads. Victims unwittingly trigger encryption or credential theft. Social manipulation increases infection success far more than technical exploits alone. It demonstrates that cybersecurity is as much psychological as technological.
