The Unbelievable Cost of Ransomware in 2023

Millions lost, services halted, chaos unleashed.

Ransomware damages reached billions globally in 2023. Hospitals, municipalities, and private companies were among the hardest hit. Recovery, downtime, and ransom payments accumulate rapidly. Cyber insurance covers some costs, but losses remain substantial. The financial impact drives organizations to invest heavily in prevention.
