How Ransomware Can Hijack Industrial Robots

Hackers can control machines that build cars or electronics.

Industrial robots connected to networks can be infected with ransomware. Malware may stop production, alter operations, or demand ransom to restore functionality. Attackers exploit poor segmentation and outdated protocols. Impact is both financial and operational. Recovery requires technical expertise and downtime. It demonstrates the evolution of ransomware from data-only to operational disruption.
