Quantum Speedup: Why Qubits Outperform Bits

Qubits can perform calculations exponentially faster than classical bits for certain problems.

Through superposition and entanglement, quantum computers can evaluate many possibilities at once. Algorithms like Grover’s provide quadratic or exponential speedups for search and optimization problems. While not universal, these advantages solve specific tasks unattainable for classical machines.
