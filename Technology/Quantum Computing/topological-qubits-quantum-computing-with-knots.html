Topological Qubits: Quantum Computing with Knots

Topological qubits use exotic particle states to store information more robustly.

These qubits rely on braiding non-abelian anyons, which encode information in their topology rather than physical properties. This makes them less sensitive to local noise and decoherence, increasing stability for long computations. Microsoft and other labs are actively pursuing this approach for fault-tolerant quantum machines.
