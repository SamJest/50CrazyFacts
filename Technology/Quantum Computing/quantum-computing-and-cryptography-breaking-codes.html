Quantum Computing and Cryptography: Breaking Codes

Quantum computers could one day break encryption that classical computers consider secure.

Algorithms like Shor’s can factor large numbers exponentially faster than classical methods, threatening RSA and other widely used cryptosystems. While this poses security risks, it also motivates the development of quantum-resistant cryptography, preparing for a post-quantum world. Governments and industries are already testing encryption that can resist quantum attacks.
