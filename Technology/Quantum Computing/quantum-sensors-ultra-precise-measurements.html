Quantum Sensors: Ultra-Precise Measurements

Quantum technology enables sensors that detect signals classical devices cannot measure accurately.

Quantum sensors exploit superposition and entanglement to measure time, magnetic fields, gravitational gradients, and temperature with extreme precision. Applications include navigation without GPS, detecting underground structures, and monitoring biological processes at nanoscale resolution.
