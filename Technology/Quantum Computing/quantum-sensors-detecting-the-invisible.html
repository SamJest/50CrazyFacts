Quantum Sensors: Detecting the Invisible

Quantum sensors detect extremely weak signals undetectable by classical devices.

Entanglement and superposition amplify sensitivity to magnetic, electric, or gravitational variations. Applications range from mineral exploration to early earthquake detection and medical imaging.
