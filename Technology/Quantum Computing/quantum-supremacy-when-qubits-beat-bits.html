Quantum Supremacy: When Qubits Beat Bits

In 2019, Google claimed “quantum supremacy,” performing a task classical computers could not feasibly do.

Quantum supremacy is the point where a quantum computer outperforms the best classical supercomputers at a specific task. Google’s Sycamore processor completed a random circuit sampling problem in 200 seconds that would take classical computers thousands of years. While the task had no practical application, it demonstrated quantum computing’s raw potential.
