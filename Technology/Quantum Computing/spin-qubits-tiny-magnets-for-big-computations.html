Spin Qubits: Tiny Magnets for Big Computations

Spin qubits use the intrinsic spin of electrons to encode information.

These qubits can be created in semiconductors like silicon or gallium arsenide. Spin qubits are promising due to long coherence times and compatibility with existing chip fabrication techniques.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Spin qubits allow integration of quantum processors with classical electronics.

They may lead to scalable, high-density quantum computers using existing semiconductor infrastructure.

Did You Know?

Researchers have demonstrated two-qubit gates and error correction with spin qubits.
