Quantum Networks: Building the Quantum Internet

Quantum networks connect qubits across distances for secure communication.

Combining entanglement, teleportation, and repeaters, quantum networks enable distributed computation and cryptography. This is the foundation for a future quantum internet.

Why This Matters

Quantum networks promise unhackable communication and collaborative quantum computing.

They could transform global communication, finance, and national security infrastructure.
