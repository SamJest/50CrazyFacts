Quantum Cryptography: Unbreakable Communication

Quantum key distribution (QKD) enables secure communication guaranteed by physics.

QKD uses entangled photons or quantum states to generate encryption keys. Any eavesdropping attempt disturbs the quantum states, alerting the communicators. This provides theoretically unbreakable encryption, unlike classical cryptography dependent on computational difficulty.

Why This Matters

Unhackable communication enhances privacy and national security.

Quantum cryptography could secure banking, government, and military communications worldwide.

Did You Know?

Prototype QKD networks exist in China, Europe, and the U.S., spanning hundreds of kilometers.
