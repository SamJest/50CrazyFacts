Entanglement Swapping: Linking Distant Qubits

Entanglement swapping extends quantum connections between unconnected qubits.

By performing specific measurements on intermediate entangled particles, two distant qubits become entangled. This technique is crucial for quantum repeaters and long-distance quantum communication networks.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Entanglement swapping allows scalable quantum networks without direct connections.

It forms the foundation for a potential global quantum internet.

Did You Know?

Experiments have demonstrated entanglement over hundreds of kilometers in optical fiber.
