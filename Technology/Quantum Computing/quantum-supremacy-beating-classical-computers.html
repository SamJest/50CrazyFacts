Quantum Supremacy: Beating Classical Computers

Quantum supremacy occurs when a quantum computer solves a problem classical computers cannot.

Google announced a milestone where its Sycamore processor completed a specific calculation in seconds, while the fastest supercomputers would take millennia. Although problem-specific, this proves quantum hardware can surpass classical limits in certain tasks.
