6G and Brain-Computer Interfaces: Telepathy for Machines

6G may allow real-time brain-to-device communication.

High-speed, ultra-low-latency networks could transmit neural signals to external devices instantly. This allows thought-controlled machinery, prosthetics, or computers.

Why This Matters

The boundary between human cognition and machines could blur.

Assistive technology and immersive gaming could reach unprecedented levels.

Did You Know?

6G research labs have transmitted neural signal simulations with less than 1-millisecond delay.
