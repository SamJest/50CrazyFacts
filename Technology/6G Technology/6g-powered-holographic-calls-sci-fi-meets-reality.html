6G-Powered Holographic Calls: Sci-Fi Meets Reality

Imagine making a video call where the person appears in your room as a hologram.

6G networks will allow real-time holographic telepresence. Using terahertz bandwidth and AI-driven compression, 3D holograms can be transmitted instantly without lag. This could replace traditional video calls and revolutionize remote collaboration.

Why This Matters

The way humans communicate could become fully immersive.

Remote education and business meetings could feel like in-person interactions.

Did You Know?

Holographic call experiments over 6G bandwidth have been able to transmit live 3D models at 120 frames per second.
