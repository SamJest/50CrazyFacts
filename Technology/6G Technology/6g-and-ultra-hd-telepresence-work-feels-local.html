6G and Ultra-HD Telepresence: Work Feels Local

6G could make remote work indistinguishable from being in the office.

With terabit-per-second speeds and near-zero latency, 6G allows ultra-high-definition telepresence. Holographic colleagues or classrooms could appear lifelike, enabling fully interactive collaboration. Combined with AI-driven spatial audio and gesture recognition, remote work may feel physical.

Why This Matters

This could transform how businesses and schools operate.

Collaboration may become global, with no sense of distance.

Did You Know?

Tests have streamed 16K holographic video in real-time over experimental 6G networks.
