The Surprising Environmental Impact of NFTs

NFTs are not just digital—they have a hidden carbon footprint.

Minting NFTs on energy-intensive blockchains like Ethereum requires enormous computational power. Each transaction consumes electricity comparable to powering homes for days. The environmental impact sparked backlash and motivated the development of “green” blockchains. NFT artists and collectors face scrutiny over their carbon footprint.

Why This Matters

It matters because digital ownership comes with real-world ecological consequences.

It also pushes the tech industry to innovate in energy-efficient blockchain solutions.

Did You Know?

Creating a single NFT can emit as much carbon as several days of electricity use in a household.
