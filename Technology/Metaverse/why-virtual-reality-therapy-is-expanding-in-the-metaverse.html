Why Virtual Reality Therapy Is Expanding in the Metaverse

Therapists go digital with immersive treatments.

Metaverse platforms host therapy sessions for anxiety, PTSD, and phobias. VR exposure therapy allows controlled, repeatable experiences. Avatars reduce stigma and increase comfort. AI assists in tracking progress and outcomes.

Why This Matters

It matters because Metaverse therapy expands access and interactivity.

It also provides data-driven insights for better treatment plans.

Did You Know?

Some patients experience faster improvement through VR therapy in the Metaverse.
