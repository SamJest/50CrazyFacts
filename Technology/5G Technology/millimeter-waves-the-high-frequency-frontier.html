Millimeter Waves: The High-Frequency Frontier

5G uses frequencies above 24 GHz, called millimeter waves, to deliver extreme speeds.

These high-frequency bands can carry massive amounts of data but have shorter range and less penetration than lower frequencies. To maintain coverage, operators deploy many small cells and beamforming technologies. Millimeter waves allow ultra-high-speed connections in dense urban areas or stadiums where thousands of devices compete for bandwidth.

Why This Matters

High-frequency waves support data-heavy applications like VR, 8K streaming, and connected factories.

They require innovative infrastructure planning and investment to maintain coverage.

Did You Know?

Millimeter-wave 5G has been tested with download speeds exceeding 5 Gbps.
