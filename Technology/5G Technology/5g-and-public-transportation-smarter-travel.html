5G and Public Transportation: Smarter Travel

Transportation systems benefit from 5G with real-time connectivity and coordination.

Buses, trains, and metros communicate over 5G for optimized routing, predictive maintenance, and passenger safety. Real-time information reaches commuters instantly via apps. Traffic signals and station systems adapt to passenger flows dynamically.
