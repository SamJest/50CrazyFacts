5G and Smart Cities: Infrastructure That Thinks

5G enables real-time citywide monitoring and management.

Sensors for traffic, pollution, lighting, and waste management communicate via 5G. AI analyzes the data to optimize traffic lights, energy usage, and public services. Emergency alerts are instant, and urban planning can adapt dynamically.
