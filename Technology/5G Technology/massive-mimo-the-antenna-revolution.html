Massive MIMO: The Antenna Revolution

MIMO sounds complicated, but imagine hundreds of antennas working together to boost your signal. 5G uses Massive MIMO to revolutionize wireless communications.

It stands for Multiple Input Multiple Output. In 5G, Massive MIMO arrays consist of dozens to hundreds of antennas on a single base station, sending and receiving multiple signals simultaneously. This increases network capacity, coverage, and efficiency. By directing beams to specific users, it reduces interference and enhances reliability.

Why This Matters

Enhanced connectivity ensures fewer dropped calls and faster mobile internet for dense urban areas.

Massive MIMO allows 5G to support thousands of devices per square kilometer, essential for smart cities and IoT networks.

Did You Know?

Some 5G antennas have over 256 elements to beam signals precisely.
