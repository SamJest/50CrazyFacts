Edge Computing in Oil and Gas

Oil rigs and refineries use edge computing to monitor equipment, pressure, and chemical levels locally for safety and efficiency.

Edge nodes track pipeline integrity, pump performance, and environmental conditions. AI predicts equipment failures and triggers alarms instantly. Cloud-based analytics are too slow for real-time safety-critical decisions. Edge computing minimizes downtime, prevents accidents, and improves compliance. Workers receive immediate alerts for hazardous conditions. Energy extraction is optimized using predictive models.

Why This Matters
