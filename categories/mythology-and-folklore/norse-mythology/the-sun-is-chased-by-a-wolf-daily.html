The Sun Is Chased by a Wolf Daily

Cosmic daylight is a near-miss every morning.

💥 Impact

The anomaly lies in turning cosmic stability into daily suspense.

The myth frames ordinary daylight as temporary victory. The sky is a chase scene.

🤯 Did You Know?

According to Norse myth, the sun survives because it runs fast enough.

In Norse cosmology, the sun is driven across the sky by the goddess Sól while a wolf named Sköll chases her relentlessly. Every sunrise is escape. Every sunset is a near capture. During Ragnarök, the wolf finally devours the sun. Daylight survives daily pursuit until its appointed end. The system builds tension into astronomy. Light exists under threat. Even routine sunrise is dramatic.
