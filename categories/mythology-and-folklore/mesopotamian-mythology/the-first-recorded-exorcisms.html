The First Recorded Exorcisms

When did demon removal become official?

💥 Impact

It marks one of the earliest formalized spiritual healing systems.

It demonstrates myth’s integration with early medicine.

🤯 Did You Know?

Mesopotamians recorded some of history’s earliest exorcism rituals.

Mesopotamia provides some of the earliest written exorcism rituals. Clay tablets describe incantations, protective circles, and appeals to specific gods. Illness was often attributed to spiritual intrusion, requiring ritual removal. These weren’t fringe practices—they were institutionalized. Scholars see in them the beginnings of medical-religious hybridity. The exorcist (āšipu) functioned as healer, priest, and psychologist rolled into one. Mythological worldview shaped treatment plans. The blending of narrative and medicine feels startlingly contemporary. Sometimes curing the body meant negotiating with spirits.
