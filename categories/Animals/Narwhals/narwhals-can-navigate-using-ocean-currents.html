Narwhals Can Navigate Using Ocean Currents

Water highways.

Narwhals exploit Arctic currents to conserve energy during migration, drifting with flow when possible.

Why This Matters

This energy-efficient strategy extends travel range and reduces metabolic stress. Currents can also concentrate prey for opportunistic feeding.

It highlights behavioral adaptation to maximize survival in harsh conditions.

Did You Know?

Narwhals align travel with tidal and seasonal currents to save energy.
