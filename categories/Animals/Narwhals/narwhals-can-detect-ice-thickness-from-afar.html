Narwhals Can Detect Ice Thickness from Afar

Arctic radar.

Narwhals sense ice thickness through vibrations and water pressure, helping them avoid traps or choose surfacing points.

Why This Matters

Accurate detection prevents entrapment, supporting survival in constantly changing ice fields. It guides migration routes and foraging areas.

It emphasizes adaptation to extreme and unpredictable habitats.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can detect variations in ice several meters thick from tens of meters away.
