Narwhals Can Dive Over a Mile Deep

Deep-sea adventurers.

Narwhals are among the deepest-diving marine mammals, reaching depths of over 1,500 meters. They can stay submerged for more than 25 minutes using oxygen stored in muscles and blood.

Why This Matters

These extreme dives allow them to access squid, fish, and other prey that other predators cannot reach. Their adaptations include slowed heart rates and collapsible lungs to manage pressure.

Deep diving gives narwhals a competitive advantage in hunting and reduces predation risks. It highlights extraordinary physiological evolution in Arctic mammals.

Did You Know?

Narwhals sometimes dive multiple times a day to such depths in search of food.

Source
