Narwhals Can Influence Arctic Predator Behavior

Top predator effect.

Narwhals’ presence can alter polar bear and orca hunting patterns, indirectly shaping local predator-prey dynamics.

Why This Matters

By moving strategically or forming protective pods, narwhals reduce predation risk for themselves and modify ecosystem interactions. Their behavior affects broader Arctic food webs.

It underscores the role of apex marine mammals in ecosystem balance.

Did You Know?

Narwhal movements can force predators to shift hunting locations seasonally.
