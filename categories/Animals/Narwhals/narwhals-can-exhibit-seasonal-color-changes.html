Narwhals Can Exhibit Seasonal Color Changes

Arctic camouflage.

Narwhals’ skin darkens in summer and lightens in winter, helping them blend with ice and open water for predator avoidance.

Why This Matters

Coloration changes align with environmental conditions for better survival. Seasonal adaptation improves hunting success and reduces predation.

It shows how physical traits can dynamically respond to seasonal pressures.

Did You Know?

Skin color change can take several weeks to fully adjust.
