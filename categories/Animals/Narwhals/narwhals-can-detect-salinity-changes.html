Narwhals Can Detect Salinity Changes

Arctic water sensors.

Narwhals’ tusks are sensitive to subtle changes in salinity, helping them navigate and locate optimal feeding areas in Arctic waters.

Why This Matters

This ability allows them to find prey and avoid areas unsuitable for survival. Sensory adaptation enhances navigation and foraging efficiency.

It underscores the multifunctional nature of tusks beyond mating or display.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can detect salinity differences of less than 0.1%.
