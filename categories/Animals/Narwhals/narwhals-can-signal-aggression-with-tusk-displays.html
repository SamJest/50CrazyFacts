Narwhals Can Signal Aggression With Tusk Displays

Spiral threats.

Males sometimes raise and display their tusks as a visual warning before engaging in sparring, avoiding unnecessary conflict.

Why This Matters

This behavior reduces physical injury in social disputes. Tusk displays communicate dominance efficiently.

It emphasizes how physical traits can function as communication tools.

Did You Know?

Subtle changes in tusk angle can indicate different social intentions.
