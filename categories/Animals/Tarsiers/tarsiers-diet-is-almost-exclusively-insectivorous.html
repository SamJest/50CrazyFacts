Tarsiers’ Diet Is Almost Exclusively Insectivorous

Predator of the night.

Tarsiers primarily eat insects, spiders, and small vertebrates like lizards. Their diet is highly specialized for protein-rich prey. Hunting involves stealth, precision, and powerful leaps. A strict diet reduces competition with frugivorous or herbivorous species. Nutritional reliance on insects shapes nocturnal activity and arboreal specialization. Dietary specialization reflects co-evolution with forest prey availability. Small body size and metabolism are supported by high-protein meals. Tarsiers’ feeding strategy demonstrates extreme niche adaptation.

Why This Matters

Diet specialization reduces competition.

It requires adaptations for nocturnal hunting and agility.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers eat almost exclusively insects and small vertebrates at night.
