Tarsiers Are Native Only to Southeast Asian Islands

Endemic exclusivity.

Tarsiers are found only on islands like Bohol, Sulawesi, and Mindanao in the Philippines and parts of Indonesia. Island isolation led to species diversification. Restricted ranges make them vulnerable to habitat destruction. Endemism drives conservation priority in tropical forests. Island-specific adaptations evolved to exploit unique ecological niches. Populations are naturally fragmented, increasing genetic vulnerability. Tarsiers’ geographic limitation emphasizes the importance of local habitat protection. Knowledge of distribution guides conservation and research focus.

Why This Matters
