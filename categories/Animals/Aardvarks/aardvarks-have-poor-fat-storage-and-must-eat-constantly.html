Aardvarks Have Poor Fat Storage and Must Eat Constantly

Aardvarks don’t store much energy. Missing meals quickly becomes dangerous. Their metabolism demands consistency.

Aardvarks have limited ability to store body fat. They rely on nightly feeding to maintain energy levels. Extended food shortages can be fatal. This makes them especially vulnerable to droughts that reduce insect populations. Regular access to termites is essential.

Why This Matters

It matters because aardvarks are early indicators of ecosystem stress. When insects decline, aardvarks suffer first.

This makes them valuable ecological warning species. Their health reflects environmental balance.

Did You Know?

During severe droughts, aardvark populations can decline rapidly even without hunting pressure. Starvation is the main cause.

Source

International Union for Conservation of Nature (iucn.org)
