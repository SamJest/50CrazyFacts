Aardvarks Can Eat Up to 50,000 Insects in One Night

Aardvarks may look slow, but their appetite is intense. When they feed, they commit fully. Entire insect cities disappear overnight.

Aardvarks can consume tens of thousands of ants and termites in a single night. They use their long, sticky tongue to lap up insects rapidly. One feeding session can last several minutes at a single mound. Over the course of a night, they visit many sites. This massive intake fuels their energy needs.

Why This Matters

It matters because aardvarks help control insect populations. Without them, termites could cause ecological damage.

This feeding behavior also shapes soil structure. Constant digging aerates the ground and improves nutrient cycling.

Did You Know?

Aardvark tongues can extend over 30 centimeters and are coated in sticky saliva. Insects have little chance of escape once contacted.
