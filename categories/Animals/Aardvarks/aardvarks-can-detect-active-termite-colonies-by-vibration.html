Aardvarks Can Detect Active Termite Colonies by Vibration

Aardvarks don’t just sniff their food. They listen to it move. Tiny vibrations give big clues.

Aardvarks can sense subtle vibrations produced by active termites moving inside mounds. Their sensitive feet and ears help detect these signals. This allows them to target only living, productive colonies. Digging into inactive mounds would waste energy. Vibration detection increases feeding efficiency dramatically.

Why This Matters

It matters because energy efficiency is critical for aardvarks. They can’t afford wasted effort.

It also shows how multiple senses combine during foraging. Smell alone isn’t enough.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks often pause with their heads against mounds before digging. This behavior helps confirm termite activity.
