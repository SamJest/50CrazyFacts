Aardvarks Can Dig Through Concrete-Hard Termite Mounds

Termite mounds are tougher than they look. Many animals can’t break them at all. Aardvarks tear through them anyway.

Aardvarks have exceptionally strong forelimbs and claws designed for breaking hard soil. Some termite mounds are as hard as concrete when dry. Aardvarks can penetrate them in minutes. Their digging technique targets weak structural points. This allows access to insects other animals can’t reach.

Why This Matters

It matters because this ability gives aardvarks exclusive access to food. Competition stays low.

It also influences termite population structure. Aardvarks act as selective pressure on mound placement and design.

Did You Know?

Aardvark claws can be over 5 centimeters long and blunt rather than sharp. This shape prevents breakage during heavy digging.
