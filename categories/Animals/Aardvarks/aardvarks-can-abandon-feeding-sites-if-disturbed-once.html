Aardvarks Can Abandon Feeding Sites if Disturbed Once

Aardvarks don’t push their luck. One bad experience is enough. Memory shapes behavior fast.

Aardvarks may permanently avoid feeding sites where they were disturbed or threatened. This includes human encounters or predator presence. They remember danger locations and adjust routes. This cautious strategy reduces risk. It also limits feeding options.

Why This Matters

It matters because habitat fragmentation increases disturbance frequency. Safe feeding zones shrink quickly.

This behavior makes aardvarks sensitive to human activity even without direct harm. Presence alone matters.

Did You Know?

Researchers have documented abandoned feeding routes lasting multiple seasons. A single event can reshape nightly movement.
