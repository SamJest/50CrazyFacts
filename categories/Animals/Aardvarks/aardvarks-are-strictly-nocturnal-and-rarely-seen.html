Aardvarks Are Strictly Nocturnal and Rarely Seen

Aardvarks are famous but rarely spotted. Many people live near them and never know it. Darkness is their chosen world.

Aardvarks are almost exclusively nocturnal, emerging only after sunset to forage. During the day, they stay hidden in deep burrows to avoid heat and predators. Their eyesight is poor, but their hearing and smell are excellent at night. This lifestyle makes them extremely elusive. Even researchers struggle to observe them in the wild.

Why This Matters

It matters because nocturnality protects aardvarks from predators and extreme temperatures. It’s a survival strategy shaped by Africa’s climate.

It also means population data is limited. Aardvarks can decline without humans noticing quickly.

Did You Know?

In some areas, camera traps record aardvarks only a few times per year despite healthy populations. They are masters of remaining unseen.
