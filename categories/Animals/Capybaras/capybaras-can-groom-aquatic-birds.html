Capybaras Can Groom Aquatic Birds

Unlikely friendships in the wetlands.

Capybaras often allow ducks, herons, or other birds to perch on their backs while removing parasites or cleaning feathers. This interspecies grooming provides mutual benefit: birds get food, and capybaras receive minor cleaning. Such tolerance demonstrates social flexibility and calm temperament. It also strengthens coexistence in shared habitats. Observing this reveals intelligence and prosocial behavior beyond species boundaries.

Why This Matters

Shows interspecies cooperation and social intelligence.

It emphasizes ecological interconnectedness in wetland habitats.

Did You Know?

Ducks, herons, and other small birds often use capybaras as floating platforms.
