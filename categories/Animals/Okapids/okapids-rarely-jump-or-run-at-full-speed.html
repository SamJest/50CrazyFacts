Okapids Rarely Jump or Run at Full Speed

Caution over speed.

Okapids prefer careful walking to fast movement. Running creates noise and visibility. Their forest habitat favors stealth. Sudden movement risks injury. Calm movement conserves energy. Escape relies on concealment, not sprinting.

Why This Matters

This challenges the idea that speed is always key. Survival can favor caution.

It highlights how habitat determines movement style. Forests reward patience.

Did You Know?

Okapids can move quickly if forced, but avoid it.

Source

Smithsonian National Zoo (si.edu)
