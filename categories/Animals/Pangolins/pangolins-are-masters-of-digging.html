Pangolins Are Masters of Digging

Tunnel visionaries.

Pangolins have strong, curved claws for breaking into termite mounds and ant nests. They can dig through compact soil efficiently. Muscular forelimbs allow quick excavation. Digging isn’t just for feeding—it creates burrows for resting and hiding. They carefully reconstruct mounds to prevent collapse. Digging is an essential survival skill.

Why This Matters

Habitat modification improves feeding efficiency and protection. Burrows serve as both shelter and nursery.

It demonstrates how specialized tools can evolve for both food acquisition and safety.

Did You Know?

Adult pangolins can dismantle termite mounds weighing over 10 kg in under 15 minutes.
