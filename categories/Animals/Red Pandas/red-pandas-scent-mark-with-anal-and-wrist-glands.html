Red Pandas Scent-Mark With Anal and Wrist Glands

Olfactory territory.

Red pandas have specialized scent glands near their anus and on their wrists. They use these to mark territory, signal reproductive status, and communicate with conspecifics. Juveniles learn scent marking by observing maternal behavior. Marks are left on trees, rocks, and branches within the territory. Scent-marking reduces direct conflict with neighbors. Chemical signals can last for days, maintaining social boundaries. This behavior is crucial for a solitary species.

Why This Matters

Scent-marking helps avoid conflict and locate mates.

It provides insights into social structure and reproductive behavior.

Did You Know?
