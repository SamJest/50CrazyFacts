Crows Can Use Cars to Crack Nuts

Street smarts in action.

Crows have been observed placing hard nuts on roads and waiting for cars to run over them, then retrieving the cracked nuts when traffic clears. They learn timing, safety, and traffic patterns, demonstrating problem-solving and patience. This behavior is often taught to younger crows by adults. Urban environments provide opportunities for such inventive strategies. It shows crows’ adaptability to human-dominated landscapes.

Why This Matters

Crows’ use of vehicles illustrates creativity in problem-solving.

It highlights intelligence in exploiting environmental resources.

Did You Know?

They wait at crosswalks for traffic lights and retrieve nuts safely afterward.
