Crows Hold Funerals for Fallen Companions

Yes, they mourn.

Crows gather around dead or injured crows in what researchers call “funerals.” These gatherings allow crows to learn about dangers, reinforce social bonds, and even express a form of mourning. They may call loudly, circle, or remain nearby for hours. This ritual shows awareness of death and social learning. Young crows observe adult reactions and adjust their behavior accordingly.

Why This Matters

Crows’ funerals indicate complex social cognition and adaptive learning.

It highlights their awareness of mortality and the social importance of group knowledge.

Did You Know?

They often gather silently or vocally around a deceased crow, sometimes calling repeatedly.
