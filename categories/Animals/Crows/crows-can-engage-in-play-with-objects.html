Crows Can Engage in Play With Objects

Toying with intelligence.

Crows often play with sticks, stones, or human objects, tossing or sliding them. Play teaches physical skills, problem-solving, and creativity. Adults and juveniles both participate, enhancing learning and social bonds. Object play supports exploration of the environment and fine motor coordination.

Why This Matters

Crows’ playful object use illustrates cognitive flexibility.

It emphasizes the importance of play in learning and intelligence development.

Did You Know?

They have been seen sliding sticks along wires or playing catch with stones.
