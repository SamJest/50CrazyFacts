Crows Can Use Mirrors to Inspect Objects

Clever reflections.

Crows use mirrors to look at hard-to-see areas of their own bodies or hidden objects. Mirror use demonstrates problem-solving, spatial awareness, and possibly self-recognition. This behavior helps them manipulate tools or retrieve food. Juveniles learn by experimenting with reflections. It reflects high cognitive ability.

Why This Matters

Crows’ mirror use shows abstract thinking and adaptability.

It highlights their inventive problem-solving skills.

Did You Know?

They may use reflective surfaces to locate hidden treats or check for parasites.
