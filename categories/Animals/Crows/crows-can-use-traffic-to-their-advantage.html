Crows Can Use Traffic to Their Advantage

Urban geniuses in feathers.

Crows place nuts on roads and wait for cars to crack them open, demonstrating an understanding of human activity and timing. They often retrieve the food safely after vehicles pass. This behavior shows patience, foresight, and problem-solving. Younger crows learn by watching adults, demonstrating social learning. Urban environments provide new challenges that crows adapt to creatively.

Why This Matters

Crows’ use of traffic reflects intelligence in navigating complex environments.

It highlights their ability to exploit human-modified landscapes.

Did You Know?

They may place hard-shelled nuts on busy streets and collect the cracked pieces afterward.
