Crows Can Play Games With Other Animals

Play isn’t just for humans.

Crows engage in aerial acrobatics, sliding on snow, or interacting playfully with other species. Play builds coordination, strengthens social bonds, and teaches younger crows survival skills. They may even play tug-of-war with sticks or chase dogs in harmless games. Such behavior indicates a sense of fun, learning through play, and social awareness.

Why This Matters

Crows’ playful behavior highlights cognitive flexibility and social engagement.

It emphasizes the role of play in learning and intelligence.

Did You Know?

They have been observed sliding down snowy roofs repeatedly for amusement.
