Crows Can Use Leaves as Napkins

Yes, really.

Crows sometimes hold food with leaves or use leaves to clean beaks and claws after feeding. This demonstrates tool use, hygiene awareness, and problem-solving. Young crows learn these behaviors through observation. Using natural “tools” enhances efficiency and cleanliness while eating. It reflects behavioral flexibility and cognitive complexity.

Why This Matters

Crows’ leaf use shows intelligence in manipulating objects for practical purposes.

It highlights innovation and learning from environmental resources.

Did You Know?

They have been observed wrapping nuts or wiping beaks with leaves.
