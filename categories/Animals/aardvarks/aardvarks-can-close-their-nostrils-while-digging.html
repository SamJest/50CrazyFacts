Aardvarks Can Close Their Nostrils While Digging

Digging into insect nests is messy business. Dirt, dust, and ants fly everywhere. Aardvarks solved this problem elegantly.

Aardvarks can close their nostrils completely while digging. This prevents soil and insects from entering their nasal passages. Combined with thick skin and strong eyelids, this allows them to dig directly into active termite mounds. Few mammals have this ability. It enables aardvarks to feed efficiently without injury.

Why This Matters

It matters because this adaptation allows access to food other animals can’t exploit. It’s a perfect example of form matching function.

This also reduces infection and irritation. Aardvarks can dig for hours without respiratory harm.

Did You Know?

Their ears can also fold back while digging, adding another layer of protection. Nearly every part of their head is adapted for excavation.
