Sloths Eat a Leaf-Based Diet and Rarely Drink Water

Leaf lovers.

Sloths are mostly folivores, eating leaves, shoots, and some fruits. Their slow metabolism lets them extract nutrients from low-calorie leaves. They rarely drink water, obtaining hydration from food. Their diet requires specialized teeth and a multi-chambered stomach for fermentation. They are selective feeders, often choosing specific tree species. This diet supports low-energy lifestyle but limits growth and speed. Dietary specialization reduces competition with other rainforest mammals.

Why This Matters

Leaf-based diet sustains low-energy lifestyle.

It reduces competition and supports canopy survival.

Did You Know?

Sloths rarely drink water, relying on leaves for hydration.
