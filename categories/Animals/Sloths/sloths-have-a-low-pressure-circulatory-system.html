Sloths Have a Low-Pressure Circulatory System

Suspension biology.

Sloths’ hearts and blood vessels are adapted to hanging upside down for hours. Low blood pressure prevents excessive strain while inverted. Specialized veins ensure blood reaches the brain efficiently without causing dizziness. This system supports energy conservation by minimizing cardiovascular effort. Slow circulation complements their slow metabolism. Even while climbing, the low-pressure system reduces fatigue. Evolution fine-tuned their circulatory system for arboreal life.

Why This Matters

Low-pressure circulation prevents fainting upside down.

It is critical for long-term canopy dwelling.

Did You Know?

Sloths’ blood pressure is uniquely low to allow extended upside-down hanging.
