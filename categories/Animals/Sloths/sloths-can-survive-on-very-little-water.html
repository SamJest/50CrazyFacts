Sloths Can Survive on Very Little Water

Hydration hacks.

Sloths get most of their water from leaves and rarely drink directly. Their metabolism conserves water, allowing survival in areas with scarce freshwater. Leaves also provide minimal but sufficient hydration. Slow digestion reduces water loss via urine. This adaptation allows sloths to inhabit a range of rainforest microhabitats. It also contributes to low mobility, as energy need for finding water is minimal. Their water-conserving strategy is crucial for survival during dry spells.

Why This Matters

Low water need expands habitat range.

It supports a low-energy, slow-moving lifestyle.

Did You Know?

Sloths can survive with very little direct water intake, relying mostly on leafy hydration.
