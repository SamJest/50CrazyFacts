Sloths Sleep Up to 15–20 Hours a Day

The ultimate napper.

Sloths spend the majority of their lives resting to conserve energy. They sleep about 15–20 hours daily, mostly while hanging upside down. Sleep helps slow metabolism, supports digestion, and limits exposure to predators. Short bouts of activity between naps are enough for feeding and grooming. Their low-energy diet makes extensive rest essential. Sleeping in trees also provides safety from terrestrial predators. This lifestyle is a key adaptation to their ecological niche.

Why This Matters

Extended sleep supports energy conservation.

It’s critical for survival on a low-calorie diet.

Did You Know?

Sloths can sleep up to 20 hours per day while remaining safe in the canopy.
