Microgreens: Tiny Plants, Big Flavor

These miniature greens pack intense taste and nutrition into dishes.

Fine dining often uses microgreens as garnishes or flavor enhancers. They offer concentrated nutrients and vivid colors. Chefs harvest them at precise growth stages for optimal flavor. They’re often grown in-house to guarantee freshness.

Why This Matters

It matters because microgreens elevate both the visual and gustatory appeal of dishes.

It also promotes sustainable practices by reducing waste and encouraging local cultivation.

Did You Know?

Some microgreens can contain up to 40 times the nutrients of their mature counterparts.
