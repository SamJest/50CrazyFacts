Burger Boom: The American Icon

From backyard grills to billion-dollar chains, burgers became a symbol of speed and taste.

Hamburgers became the centerpiece of fast food in the 20th century. Chains like McDonald’s and Burger King standardized ingredients, cooking methods, and portion sizes, creating a consistent experience. Marketing reinforced the burger as an emblem of convenience and American culture. The burger’s success inspired international expansion and menu adaptation worldwide. Fast food burgers show how branding, standardization, and cultural identity intersect.

Why This Matters

It matters because it illustrates the globalization of taste and culture.

It also highlights the power of branding and product consistency.

Did You Know?

The Big Mac was first introduced by McDonald’s in 1967.
