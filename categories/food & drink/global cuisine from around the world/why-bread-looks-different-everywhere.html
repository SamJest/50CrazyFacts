Why Bread Looks Different Everywhere

Dough tells stories. Ovens vary. Culture bakes identity.

Global cuisine from around the world expresses itself through bread. Climate affects grains. Fuel affects ovens. Religion shapes form. Texture signals class. Bread reflects environment.

Why This Matters

This matters because staples define culture. Bread is anthropology. Cuisine encodes geography.

It also explains food diversity. Same need, many answers. Adaptation tastes unique.

Did You Know?

Sourdough predates written history.
