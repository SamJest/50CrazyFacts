How Colonial Sugar Changed Diets Forever

Sweetness rewired desire. Sugar spread addiction. Cuisine paid the price.

Global cuisine from around the world shifted with sugar availability. Desserts multiplied. Preservation changed. Energy intake rose. Labor systems expanded brutally. Cuisine masked exploitation.

Why This Matters

This matters because taste has consequences. Diets reflect economics. Cuisine hides history.

It also informs modern health debates. Sugar isn’t neutral. History lingers in cravings.
