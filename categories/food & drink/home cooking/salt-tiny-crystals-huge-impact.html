Salt: Tiny Crystals, Huge Impact

Sprinkling salt correctly can make flavors explode.

Salt enhances sweetness, balances bitterness, and intensifies aromas. It also affects texture in baking by strengthening gluten or preserving meats. Different salts—kosher, sea, fleur de sel—add unique tastes and crunch. How and when you salt can dramatically alter a dish.

Why This Matters

It matters because salt is an inexpensive tool to amplify flavors and reduce the need for added fat.

It also encourages mindfulness and precision in seasoning techniques.

Did You Know?

The average American uses roughly 3,400 mg of sodium daily, often hidden in processed foods.
