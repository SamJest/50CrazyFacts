Eggs: The Ultimate Versatile Ingredient

Eggs can be scrambled, poached, baked, or whipped into soufflés.

Eggs provide structure, moisture, and richness. Techniques like tempering, folding, and emulsifying impact final dishes. Eggs can also carry flavor in sauces and custards.

Why This Matters

Mastering eggs matters because they’re foundational to baking and cooking.

It also teaches precision, timing, and technique in home kitchens.

Did You Know?

Eggs have been a cornerstone of cuisine across all cultures for millennia.
