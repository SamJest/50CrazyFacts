The Secret of Umami

Umami is the “fifth taste” that makes dishes irresistible.

Umami comes from glutamates found in tomatoes, mushrooms, soy sauce, and aged cheeses. It enhances flavors naturally and can reduce the need for excessive salt. Combining umami-rich ingredients creates depth in soups, sauces, and main dishes.

Why This Matters

It matters because understanding umami elevates home cooking and improves flavor perception.

It also helps cooks make healthier choices while still producing rich-tasting meals.

Did You Know?

Japanese cuisine first codified umami in the early 20th century, recognizing it as a distinct taste.
