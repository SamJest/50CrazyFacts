Homemade Pasta: Fresh is Better

Fresh pasta has a texture and flavor store-bought can’t match.

Combining flour, eggs, and water creates dough that can be rolled, cut, and cooked immediately. Fresh pasta absorbs sauces differently, offering a softer bite and enhanced flavor.

Why This Matters

Homemade pasta matters because it improves taste, texture, and culinary creativity.

It also teaches manual skills, patience, and appreciation for traditional methods.

Did You Know?

Pasta-making has been practiced in Italy since at least the 13th century.
