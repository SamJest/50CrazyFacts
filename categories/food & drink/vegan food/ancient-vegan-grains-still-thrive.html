Ancient Vegan Grains Still Thrive

Ancient grains are vegan powerhouses!

Quinoa, amaranth, teff, and millet have been eaten for thousands of years for their high protein, fiber, and mineral content. They support traditional dishes while modern chefs integrate them into plant-based meals.

Why This Matters

This matters for preserving heritage crops and diversifying diets.

It also shows how historical eating patterns can inspire modern vegan nutrition.

Did You Know?

Teff is the primary grain in Ethiopian injera, a staple vegan flatbread.
