Vegan Chocolate with Superfoods

Cocoa meets chia and hemp!

Modern vegan chocolate bars include chia seeds, hemp hearts, and goji berries for added nutrients, protein, and antioxidants. This creates functional desserts for health-conscious consumers.

Why This Matters

Vegan desserts can be both indulgent and nutrient-dense.

It also expands the market for health-focused, cruelty-free treats.

Did You Know?

Some superfood vegan chocolate bars contain over 10 grams of protein per serving.
