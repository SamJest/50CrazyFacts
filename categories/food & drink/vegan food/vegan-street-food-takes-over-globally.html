Vegan Street Food Takes Over Globally

Street food is going plant-based!

From falafel wraps in Cairo to vegan tacos in Mexico City and jackfruit burgers in Bangkok, street vendors create fast, flavorful, and ethical meals.

Why This Matters

The trend shows vegan eating is accessible, affordable, and culturally integrated.

It also indicates how plant-based options are spreading into every culinary sector.

Did You Know?

Jackfruit mimics pulled pork texture and is a popular street food substitute.
