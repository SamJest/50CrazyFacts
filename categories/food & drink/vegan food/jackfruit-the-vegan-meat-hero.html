Jackfruit: The Vegan Meat Hero

Jackfruit isn’t just a fruit, it’s meat!

Its fibrous texture mimics pulled pork, chicken, or beef in tacos, sandwiches, and curries. Marinated and cooked properly, it absorbs flavors like a sponge.

Why This Matters

Jackfruit expands plant-based culinary creativity.

It also supports tropical agriculture and provides a sustainable meat substitute.

Did You Know?

Jackfruit trees can produce hundreds of fruits annually, feeding dozens of families.
