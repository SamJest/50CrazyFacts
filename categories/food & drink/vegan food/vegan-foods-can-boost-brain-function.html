Vegan Foods Can Boost Brain Function

Plants feed your brain!

Leafy greens, berries, nuts, seeds, and legumes provide antioxidants, omega-3s, and polyphenols that reduce oxidative stress in the brain. Vegan diets rich in these nutrients improve memory, focus, and cognitive longevity.

Why This Matters

It matters because diet directly affects neurological health.

It also suggests plant-based eating can support long-term mental wellness.

Did You Know?

Blueberries have been shown to improve memory in adults over 60 in multiple studies.
