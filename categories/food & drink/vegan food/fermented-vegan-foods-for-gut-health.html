Fermented Vegan Foods for Gut Health

Fermented foods boost digestion!

Kimchi, sauerkraut, miso, tempeh, and kombucha feed gut bacteria, supporting immunity and digestion. Fermented vegan foods enhance nutrient absorption and can improve mental health via the gut-brain axis.

Why This Matters

Demonstrates that vegan diets can maintain gut microbiome diversity.

It also connects plant-based diets to holistic wellness beyond physical health.

Did You Know?

Fermented soy tempeh can contain up to 1 billion probiotics per serving.
