Economic Benefits of Plant-Based Diets

Eating more plants can save money and reduce household food costs.

Beans, lentils, rice, seasonal vegetables, and grains are cheaper than meat. Plant-based meals reduce grocery bills while increasing nutrient density. Meal planning and batch cooking further enhance cost savings. Communities adopting plant-forward diets can reduce healthcare spending by lowering diet-related diseases.

Why This Matters

It matters because plant-based diets are not just healthy but economically viable.

It also encourages equitable access to nutritious foods across demographics.

Did You Know?

A study in the U.S. found plant-based diets can save families $750–$1,000 per year on groceries.
