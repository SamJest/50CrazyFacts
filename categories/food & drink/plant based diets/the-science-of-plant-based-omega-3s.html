The Science of Plant-Based Omega-3s

Omega-3 fatty acids aren’t exclusive to fish.

Flaxseeds, chia seeds, walnuts, and hemp seeds contain ALA, a plant-based omega-3. Our bodies convert ALA into EPA and DHA, the essential fatty acids for heart, brain, and joint health. Fortified foods and algae supplements provide direct DHA for plant-based diets. Cooking techniques preserve omega-3 content.

Why This Matters

It matters because adequate omega-3 intake supports cardiovascular and neurological health.

It also prevents nutrient misconceptions that could discourage plant-based adoption.

Did You Know?

Algae oil supplements are a reliable source of DHA for strict plant-based eaters.
