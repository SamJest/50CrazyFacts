Plant-Based Dairy Alternatives Are Nutritious

Non-dairy milks pack nutrients.

Almond, soy, oat, rice, and pea-based milks provide calcium, vitamin D, and protein when fortified. Many are lower in saturated fat than cow’s milk. Fortified plant yogurts and cheeses offer probiotics.

Why This Matters

It matters for lactose-intolerant individuals or those avoiding dairy.

It also reduces environmental impact compared with traditional dairy.

Did You Know?

Soy milk contains roughly the same protein as cow’s milk per cup.
