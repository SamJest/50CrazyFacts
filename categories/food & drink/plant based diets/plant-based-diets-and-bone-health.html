Plant-Based Diets and Bone Health

Plants strengthen bones without dairy.

Leafy greens, legumes, almonds, tofu, and fortified plant milks provide calcium, magnesium, and vitamin K. Weight-bearing exercise complements nutrient intake. Plant-based diets reduce acid load, which may improve calcium retention.

Why This Matters

It matters because osteoporosis risk can be mitigated with plant foods.

It also challenges the misconception that dairy is essential for strong bones.

Did You Know?

1 cup of cooked collard greens contains ~270 mg of calcium.
