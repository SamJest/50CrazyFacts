Plant-Based Diets Can Save Rainforests

Choosing plants reduces deforestation.

Meat production requires vast land for grazing and feed crops, driving rainforest loss. Plant-based diets reduce land demand for livestock and feed, protecting habitats. Beans, grains, and vegetables need far less land per calorie than beef or pork.

Why This Matters

Favors ecological balance and biodiversity preservation.

It also mitigates climate change through reduced deforestation.

Did You Know?

If the world switched to plant-based diets, deforestation could drop by 80%.

Source

[World Wildlife Fund, worldwildlife.org]
