Plant-Based Diets and Mental Health

What you eat can influence your mood and cognition.

Plant-based diets rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and fiber support neurotransmitter function. Omega-3s from flax or chia help brain health. Studies indicate vegetarians report lower anxiety and depression levels. High-fiber, low-processed-food diets improve gut-brain signaling. Mindful plant-based eating may promote mental clarity.

Why This Matters

It matters because diet is a modifiable lifestyle factor in mental well-being.

It can complement therapy and reduce dependence on medication for mild mood disorders.

Did You Know?

Fiber intake affects the production of short-chain fatty acids, impacting mood regulation.

Source

[National Institutes of Health, nih.gov]
