Plant-Based Diets and Diabetes Prevention

Plant-based eating can dramatically lower type 2 diabetes risk.

High-fiber plant foods slow sugar absorption, improving blood sugar control. Whole grains, legumes, nuts, and vegetables stabilize insulin levels. Research shows vegetarians have up to 50% lower risk of developing type 2 diabetes. Even partial plant-based eating, like the “flexitarian” approach, helps regulate blood sugar.

Why This Matters

Reducing diabetes risk improves long-term health outcomes and quality of life.

It also reduces reliance on medications and insulin management.

Did You Know?

Diets rich in legumes and whole grains are linked to a 20–30% decrease in diabetes risk.

Source

[American Diabetes Association, diabetes.org]
