Fermented Foods Boost Nutrition

Yogurt isn’t the only game-changer!

Kimchi, sauerkraut, miso, and tempeh enhance vegetarian diets with probiotics, vitamins, and minerals. Fermentation improves digestibility and adds tangy flavors.

Why This Matters

They make vegetarian diets healthier and more interesting.

Fermented foods preserve traditional techniques while introducing modern culinary creativity.

Did You Know?

Kimchi has been a Korean staple for over 2,000 years.
