Lentil Burgers: Ancient Legumes Modernized

Who knew lentils could rock a patty?

Lentils are mashed and combined with spices, grains, or vegetables to create vegetarian burgers. They provide protein, fiber, and a hearty texture, making plant-based burgers satisfying.

Why This Matters

Lentil burgers showcase the versatility of legumes in modern vegetarian diets.

They also encourage healthier, sustainable alternatives to meat-based fast food.

Did You Know?

Lentil-based burgers are a recent trend inspired by ancient legumes.
