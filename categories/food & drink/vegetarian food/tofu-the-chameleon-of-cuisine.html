Tofu: The Chameleon of Cuisine

Blends, grills, or fries—it does it all!

Tofu is made from soy milk, pressed into soft blocks. Its bland taste allows it to absorb flavors from marinades, sauces, or spices, appearing in Asian, European, and fusion vegetarian dishes.

Why This Matters

Tofu is a primary protein source for vegetarians and vegans.

It demonstrates the global adaptability of plant-based ingredients.

Did You Know?

Tofu originated in China over 2,000 years ago.
