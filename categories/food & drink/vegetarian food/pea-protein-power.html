Pea Protein Power

Peas aren’t just for sides—they’re protein superstars!

Pea protein isolates are used in burgers, shakes, and snack bars. They are hypoallergenic and high in amino acids. Blending with grains improves taste and texture.

Why This Matters

Pea protein supports vegetarian diets with complete protein sources.

It also drives sustainable agriculture since peas fix nitrogen in soil naturally.

Did You Know?

Pea protein has surged in popularity due to plant-based protein trends.
