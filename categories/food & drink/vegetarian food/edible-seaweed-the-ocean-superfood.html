Edible Seaweed: The Ocean Superfood

Seaweed isn’t just sushi garnish—it’s a nutritional powerhouse!

From nori to kelp, seaweed provides iodine, protein, and antioxidants. It has been a staple in Asian diets for centuries and is now trending globally as a superfood. Culinary chefs incorporate it into soups, snacks, and salads.

Why This Matters

Integrating seaweed enhances vegetarian diets with essential minerals.

It also reduces reliance on land-based crops, offering a sustainable food source.

Did You Know?

Some seaweed species can grow up to 2 feet per day under optimal conditions.
