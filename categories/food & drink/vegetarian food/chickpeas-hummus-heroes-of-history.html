Chickpeas: Hummus Heroes of History

From Middle East to your kitchen, these legumes shine!

Chickpeas are protein-rich legumes used in hummus, stews, curries, and salads. They provide fiber, iron, and essential nutrients, making them a vegetarian staple across cultures.

Why This Matters

Chickpeas are vital for plant-based diets, offering affordable protein.

They also support traditional cuisines and sustainable agriculture worldwide.

Did You Know?

Chickpeas were cultivated in the Middle East over 7,500 years ago.
