Mushrooms: The Umami Miracle

Fungi add depth and meaty texture to vegetarian meals!

Mushrooms, especially shiitake and portobello, offer umami flavor that mimics meat. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, they appear in stews, burgers, and stir-fries. Cooking techniques like roasting enhance their savory profile.

Why This Matters

They help make vegetarian meals satisfying and flavorful.

Mushrooms also contribute to sustainable agriculture due to low resource needs.

Did You Know?

Portobello mushrooms can reach the size of a dinner plate.
