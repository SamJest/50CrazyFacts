Avocado: Butter of the Plant World

Not just toast—avocados transform dishes!

Avocados are rich in healthy fats, fiber, and vitamins. Used in guacamole, smoothies, and even desserts, their creamy texture enhances vegetarian meals.

Why This Matters

They improve the taste and nutritional quality of plant-based diets.

Global avocado demand promotes sustainable farming innovations.

Did You Know?

Avocados were first domesticated in Mesoamerica over 5,000 years ago.
