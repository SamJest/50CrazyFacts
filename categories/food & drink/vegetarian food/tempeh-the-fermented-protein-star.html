Tempeh: The Fermented Protein Star

Fermentation meets flavor in this soy powerhouse!

Originating in Indonesia, tempeh is made from fermented soybeans pressed into a cake. It’s rich in protein, probiotics, and vitamins. The fermentation process enhances digestibility and adds a nutty flavor.

Why This Matters

Tempeh shows that plant-based proteins can be both healthy and delicious.

It also connects modern diets with traditional culinary techniques, preserving cultural heritage.

Did You Know?

Tempeh was first documented in Java in the 1600s.
