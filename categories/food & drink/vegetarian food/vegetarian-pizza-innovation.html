Vegetarian Pizza Innovation

Pizza isn’t just cheese and tomato anymore!

Vegetarian pizzas now feature toppings like eggplant, roasted peppers, arugula, and vegan cheese. Chefs experiment with cauliflower crusts and lentil-based sauces for nutritional upgrades.

Why This Matters

Vegetarian diets can include indulgent comfort foods without animal products.

It also inspires creativity in traditional dishes, making them healthier and more sustainable.

Did You Know?

Cauliflower crusts have become popular low-carb vegetarian alternatives.
