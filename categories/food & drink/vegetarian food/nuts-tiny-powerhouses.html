Nuts: Tiny Powerhouses

Almonds, cashews, and walnuts energize meals!

Nuts add crunch, flavor, protein, and healthy fats to salads, stir-fries, desserts, and snacks. Roasting enhances taste, and blending into butters adds versatility.

Why This Matters

They contribute essential nutrients to vegetarian diets.

Nuts also support sustainable agroforestry practices worldwide.

Did You Know?

Almonds have been cultivated in the Middle East for over 6,000 years.
