Artificial Sweeteners: Sugar Without the Calories

Low-calorie sweetness isn’t natural, it’s chemical magic!

Artificial sweeteners like aspartame, saccharin, and sucralose replace sugar in processed foods. They allow calorie reduction but may affect taste perception and gut health. Used in diet sodas, sugar-free snacks, and desserts, they have sparked debates over safety and health.

Why This Matters

Artificial sweeteners demonstrate how processed foods manipulate flavor for dieting trends.

They influence public health discussions and food regulation worldwide.

Did You Know?

Saccharin was discovered in 1879 by Constantin Fahlberg while working on coal tar derivatives.
