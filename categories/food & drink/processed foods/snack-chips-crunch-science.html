Snack Chips: Crunch Science

Thin slices, bold flavor, processed perfection!

Potato and corn chips rely on slicing, frying, and seasoning, often with preservatives and artificial flavors. Their consistent crunch and taste are engineered for addictive snacking.

Why This Matters

Processed snack foods like chips shape global eating patterns and snacking culture.

Addictive textures in processed foods affect dietary choices and public health.

Did You Know?

Lays introduced mass-produced potato chips in 1932 in the U.S.
