The Hidden History of Processed Foods

Processed foods aren’t modern inventions—they’ve been around for centuries!

Canning, pickling, and smoking are early forms of processed foods that allowed civilizations to survive harsh winters and long voyages. Napoleon’s army relied on canned goods in the early 1800s, making processed food a tool of military strategy. Understanding these origins shows how necessity drove food innovation long before fast food existed.

Why This Matters

Processed foods revolutionized human survival, letting populations expand beyond local harvests.

They also paved the way for modern convenience foods, influencing global trade and economy.

Did You Know?

In 1809, Nicolas Appert developed the first method of airtight food preservation in France.
