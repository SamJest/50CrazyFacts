Margarine: Butter’s Industrial Cousin

Yellow, spreadable, and scientifically crafted!

Margarine is made from hydrogenated vegetable oils with emulsifiers, colorants, and sometimes vitamins. It was developed as a cheaper, longer-lasting butter alternative.

Why This Matters

Processed margarine reflects how industrial processing meets economic and dietary needs.

They shaped household cooking habits and debates about nutrition for over a century.

Did You Know?

Margarine was first patented in France in 1869 by Hippolyte Mège-Mouriès.
