The Freeze-Dried Craze: Snacks From the Sky

Food that lasts decades isn’t sci-fi—it’s freeze-dried!

Freeze-drying removes moisture from foods under low temperatures, preserving nutrients, taste, and texture. It’s used for astronaut meals, emergency rations, and snacks. Many processed foods, from coffee to fruit, use this technique to increase shelf life dramatically.

Why This Matters

Freeze-dried foods allow global food distribution and emergency preparedness.

They show the intersection of food technology and extreme environment survival.

Did You Know?

NASA popularized freeze-dried meals for space missions in the 1960s.
