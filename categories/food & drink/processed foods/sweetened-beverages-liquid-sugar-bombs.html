Sweetened Beverages: Liquid Sugar Bombs

Soda isn’t just fun—it’s chemistry in a bottle!

Processed soft drinks combine carbonated water, sugars, acids, flavors, and preservatives. They are engineered for taste, fizz, and marketing appeal.

Why This Matters

Processed beverages show how flavor engineering drives consumer habits.

They are a major factor in global sugar consumption and public health debates.

Did You Know?

The first commercially sold soft drink was Coca-Cola in 1886.
