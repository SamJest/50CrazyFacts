Instant Noodles: Dehydrated Genius

Your ramen is a dehydrated globe-trotter of taste!

Instant noodles are pre-cooked, fried or air-dried, and packaged with flavoring powders and preservatives for long shelf life and easy preparation.

Why This Matters

Processed instant noodles revolutionized fast, low-cost meals worldwide.

They influenced student diets, emergency food, and global comfort food culture.

Did You Know?

Instant noodles were invented in 1958 by Momofuku Ando in Japan.
