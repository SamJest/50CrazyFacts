Why Breakfast Is a Cultural Invention

Morning meals aren’t universal. Breakfast was marketed. Cuisine followed industry.

Food culture and cuisine created breakfast traditions recently. Industrial work schedules demanded morning calories. Marketing defined foods. Health myths spread. Breakfast became routine. Culture followed commerce.

Why This Matters

This matters because meals aren’t biologically fixed. Cuisine responds to economy. Eating habits are designed.

It also empowers personal choice. Skipping meals isn’t unnatural. Food culture evolves.
