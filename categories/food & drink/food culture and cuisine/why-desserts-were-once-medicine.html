Why Desserts Were Once Medicine

Sweet healed bodies. Sugar masked bitterness. Cuisine prescribed pleasure.

Food culture and cuisine treated desserts medicinally. Honey cured wounds. Spices aided digestion. Sugar symbolized health. Doctors prescribed sweets. Cuisine blurred medicine lines.

Why This Matters

This matters because dessert wasn’t indulgence. Health once tasted sweet. Cuisine redefined wellness.

It also explains lingering beliefs. Comfort equals cure. Sugar fooled science.

Did You Know?

Marmalade was a remedy.
