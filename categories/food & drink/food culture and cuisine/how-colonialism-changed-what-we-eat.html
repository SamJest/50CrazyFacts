How Colonialism Changed What We Eat

Empires moved food. Diets were rewritten. Cuisine was forced global.

Food culture and cuisine transformed through colonialism. Crops relocated continents. Indigenous diets were disrupted. New staples emerged. Power dictated taste. Cuisine blended unevenly. Trauma lingered.

Why This Matters

This matters because modern diets aren’t organic. History imposed ingredients. Cuisine reflects oppression.

It also explains global staples. Potatoes and tomatoes traveled violently. Food remembers history.

Did You Know?

Potatoes saved Europe from famine.
