How Religion Shaped Daily Diets

Faith fed rules. Belief shaped meals. Cuisine followed doctrine.

Food culture and cuisine are deeply religious. Dietary laws defined identity. Fasting structured calendars. Feasts reinforced belief. Food separated communities. Cuisine became moral. Eating was worship.

Why This Matters

This matters because diets express belief systems. Food reinforces faith. Cuisine builds community.

It also explains persistent food rules. Religion outlasts science. Cuisine preserves tradition.
