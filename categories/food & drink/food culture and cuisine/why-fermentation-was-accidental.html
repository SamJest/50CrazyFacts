Why Fermentation Was Accidental

Rots saved lives. Mistakes became delicacies. Cuisine embraced microbes.

Food culture and cuisine discovered fermentation by chance. Storage failures produced flavor. Alcohol preserved calories. Bacteria improved safety. Taste evolved culturally. Cuisine mastered microbes unknowingly.

Why This Matters

This matters because microbes feed humanity. Preservation enabled settlement. Cuisine owes bacteria everything.

It also reframes “spoiled” food. Rot became resource. Science followed tradition.

Did You Know?

Beer predates bread.
