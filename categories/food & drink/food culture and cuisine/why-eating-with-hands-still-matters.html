Why Eating With Hands Still Matters

Fingers predate forks. Touch connects taste. Cuisine stays tactile.

Food culture and cuisine value hand-eating globally. Texture enhances flavor. Ritual reinforces respect. Community sharing deepens bonds. Utensils aren’t universal. Cuisine honors tradition.

Why This Matters

This matters because tools aren’t superior. Experience shapes taste. Cuisine is sensory.

It also challenges Western norms. Hands aren’t primitive. They’re intentional.
