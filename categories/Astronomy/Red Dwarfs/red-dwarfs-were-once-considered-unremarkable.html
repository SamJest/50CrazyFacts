Red Dwarfs Were Once Considered Unremarkable

Humble beginnings.

Early astronomers overlooked red dwarfs due to their faintness, considering them unimportant for cosmic studies.

Why This Matters

It matters because we now realize red dwarfs hold clues to galactic evolution and planet formation.

It also underscores how observational limits can bias scientific understanding.

Did You Know?

For decades, astronomers ignored red dwarfs because they were “too dim” to matter.
