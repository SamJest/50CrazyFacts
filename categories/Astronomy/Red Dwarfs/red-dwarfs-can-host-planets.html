Red Dwarfs Can Host Planets

Promising systems.

Many red dwarfs have planets, some in the so-called habitable zone, where liquid water could exist.

Why This Matters

These systems are prime targets for exoplanet research and potential life detection.

It also shifts the focus of habitability studies from Sun-like stars to smaller, long-lived stars.

Did You Know?

Some of the most Earth-like exoplanets orbit faint red dwarfs.
