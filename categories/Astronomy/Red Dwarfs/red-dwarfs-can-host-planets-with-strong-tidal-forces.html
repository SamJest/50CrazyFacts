Red Dwarfs Can Host Planets with Strong Tidal Forces

Ocean shapers.

Planets close to red dwarfs often experience extreme tidal forces, causing internal heating and possibly volcanism.

Why This Matters

This matters for understanding geological activity and habitability potential.

It also highlights unique planetary evolution scenarios in tight orbits.

Did You Know?

Red dwarf planets can have molten interiors due to relentless tidal squeezing.
