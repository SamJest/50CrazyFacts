Red Dwarfs Can Be Part of Exotic Triple Systems

Complex neighbors.

Some red dwarfs exist in triple-star systems, interacting gravitationally in ways that can dramatically affect planetary orbits.

Why This Matters

This impacts studies of system stability and planet survival.

It also creates rare observational opportunities to study dynamics in small star systems.

Did You Know?

A red dwarf can orbit a star duo in a cosmic three-body dance.
