Red Dwarfs Can Be Detected by Infrared Light

Hidden signatures.

Their cool temperatures make red dwarfs shine primarily in infrared, requiring special instruments for observation.

Why This Matters

This matters for mapping the true stellar population of our galaxy.

It also informs the design of telescopes for exoplanet searches around faint stars.

Did You Know?

Many red dwarfs are invisible in visible light but glow in infrared.
