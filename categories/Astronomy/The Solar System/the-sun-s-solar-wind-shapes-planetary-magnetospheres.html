The Sun’s Solar Wind Shapes Planetary Magnetospheres

Blowing space weather.

Solar wind, streams of charged particles from the Sun, interacts with planetary magnetic fields, creating auroras and affecting atmospheres. The solar wind also shapes comet tails and influences space weather throughout the solar system.

Why This Matters

It matters because it affects satellites, spacecraft, and planetary atmospheres.

It also demonstrates the Sun’s far-reaching influence beyond just gravity.

Did You Know?

Solar wind sculpts auroras and magnetic interactions across the solar system.
