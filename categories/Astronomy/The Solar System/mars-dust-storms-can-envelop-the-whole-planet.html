Mars’ Dust Storms Can Envelop the Whole Planet

Global dust storms.

Mars occasionally experiences planet-wide dust storms that last for weeks. These storms can block sunlight, reduce solar power for rovers, and alter surface features. Observing them helps understand Martian climate and weather patterns.

Why This Matters

It matters because global dust storms influence exploration missions and surface conditions.

It also provides a glimpse into extreme atmospheric events on other planets.

Did You Know?

Mars’ dust storms can cover the entire planet for weeks.
