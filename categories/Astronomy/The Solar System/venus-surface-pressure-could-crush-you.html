Venus’ Surface Pressure Could Crush You

Death by atmosphere.

Venus’ atmosphere exerts pressure 92 times that of Earth at sea level. That’s equivalent to being 900 meters underwater. Its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere makes landing spacecraft a challenge; most probes last only hours.

Why This Matters

This matters because it illustrates the extremes planets can present.

It also informs engineering for future missions to hostile worlds.

Did You Know?

Standing on Venus’ surface would feel like diving nearly 1 km underwater.
