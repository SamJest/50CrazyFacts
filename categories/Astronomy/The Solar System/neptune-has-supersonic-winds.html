Neptune Has Supersonic Winds

The solar system’s weather extremes.

Neptune, though the farthest planet from the Sun, experiences winds exceeding 2,100 km/h (1,300 mph). These supersonic storms create dynamic clouds and dark spots similar to Jupiter’s Great Red Spot. The source of these winds is likely internal heat, as Neptune receives little solar energy.

Why This Matters

It matters because Neptune demonstrates the diversity of planetary weather systems.

It also informs meteorology and climate modeling on Earth and beyond.

Did You Know?

Neptune’s winds are faster than the speed of sound on Earth.
