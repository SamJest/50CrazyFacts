The Kuiper Belt Is a Second Solar System Frontier

Beyond Neptune’s orbit.

The Kuiper Belt contains icy bodies and dwarf planets like Pluto, Haumea, and Makemake. It’s a remnant of early solar system material. Studying it helps scientists understand planetary formation and migration.

Why This Matters

This matters because the Kuiper Belt holds clues about the solar system’s origins.

It also challenges ideas of where planets and small bodies can exist.

Did You Know?

Pluto is just one of over a thousand known Kuiper Belt objects.
