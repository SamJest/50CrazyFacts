Depression Can Disrupt Eye Contact

Eye contact decreases naturally. Avoidance is unintentional. Social cues shift.

Depression reduces spontaneous eye contact. This is linked to social withdrawal circuits. Reduced engagement is often misread. The effect is unconscious. Eye tracking studies confirm this. Improvement follows symptom reduction. It affects relationship dynamics.

Why This Matters

It matters because eye contact influences trust. Reduced contact increases misunderstanding.

Education improves communication during recovery. It strengthens social support.

Did You Know?

Did you know eye-tracking technology is used to study depression severity. It detects subtle changes.

Source

[Journal of Affective Disorders, elsevier.com]
