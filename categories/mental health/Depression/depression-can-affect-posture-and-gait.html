Depression Can Affect Posture and Gait

Shoulders slump. Steps shorten. Movement tightens.

Depression changes body posture unconsciously. Gait becomes slower and closed. This reflects emotional state. Motion-capture studies confirm patterns. Improving posture can influence mood. The relationship is reciprocal.

Why This Matters

It matters because body language feeds emotion. Physical cues reinforce mood states.

Postural awareness supports recovery. It’s a low-cost intervention.

Did You Know?

Did you know posture-based therapies are being studied as adjunct depression treatments.
