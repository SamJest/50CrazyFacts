Depression Can Change Voice Tone

Speech sounds flatter. Energy drops. Others notice before the person does.

Depression alters vocal pitch and rhythm. Speech becomes slower and monotone. AI models can detect this reliably. Voice changes correlate with severity. This occurs unconsciously. Monitoring speech aids diagnosis. It may predict relapse.

Why This Matters

It matters because early detection saves lives. Voice analysis is noninvasive.

It supports remote mental health screening. Technology expands access to care.

Did You Know?

Did you know call centers unknowingly collect data used in depression voice studies. Ethical safeguards are now emphasized.
