Depression Can Mask Itself as Laziness

Energy collapses. Effort feels impossible. Judgment follows.

Depression reduces available mental energy. Tasks feel physically heavy. This is neurological, not moral failure. Mislabeling worsens shame. Understanding restores compassion.

Why This Matters

It matters because stigma delays treatment. People blame themselves.

Education reframes symptoms accurately. Help-seeking increases.

Did You Know?

Did you know brain energy metabolism is altered during depressive episodes.
