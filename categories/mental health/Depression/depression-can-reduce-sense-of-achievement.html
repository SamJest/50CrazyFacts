Depression Can Reduce Sense of Achievement

Accomplishments feel empty. Success doesn’t register. Pride vanishes.

Depression blocks reward processing. Achievements fail to activate pleasure circuits. This discourages effort. Dopamine signaling is impaired. Recognition alone doesn’t help. Internal reward must recover.

Why This Matters

It matters because motivation collapses. Progress goes unnoticed.

Tracking effort over outcome supports healing. It reframes success.

Did You Know?

Did you know reward-blunting is a core diagnostic feature of major depressive disorder.
