Depression Can Alter Moral Risk Assessment

Ethical choices feel heavier. Fear of harm increases. Judgment shifts inward.

Depression increases harm-avoidance behavior. Moral risk feels amplified. People overestimate consequences of mistakes. This leads to paralysis. Brain regions tied to ethics show heightened activity. Therapy reduces moral overweighting. This symptom is rarely discussed.

Why This Matters

It matters because it affects work and relationships. Ethical overthinking increases stress.

Recognizing this prevents mislabeling as indecisiveness. It improves workplace accommodations.

Did You Know?

Did you know moral decision tasks are used in depression research labs. They reveal cognitive load differences.

Source

[Journal of Affective Disorders, elsevier.com]
