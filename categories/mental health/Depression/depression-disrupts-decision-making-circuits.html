Depression Disrupts Decision-Making Circuits

Small choices feel overwhelming. The brain hesitates. Confidence drops.

Depression impairs the prefrontal cortex, affecting decision-making. Risk assessment becomes skewed. People avoid choices to reduce perceived failure. This leads to indecision and procrastination. Brain scans show reduced connectivity during tasks. Medication and therapy improve executive function. This explains functional decline.

Why This Matters

It matters because indecision is often misjudged. It is neurological, not moral.

Understanding this reduces shame. It supports structured decision aids in treatment.

Did You Know?

Did you know decision-making improves earlier than mood in some treatments. This predicts long-term recovery.
