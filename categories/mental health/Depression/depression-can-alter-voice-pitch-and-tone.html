Depression Can Alter Voice Pitch and Tone

Speech flattens. Pitch lowers. Energy fades.

Depression affects vocal production. Reduced muscle activation changes tone. Speech becomes slower and monotone. Voice analysis can detect depression. Changes reverse with improvement.

Why This Matters

It matters because voice signals emotional state. Misinterpretation affects relationships.

Voice-based screening tools are emerging. They support early detection.

Did You Know?

Did you know AI models can detect depression risk from short voice samples.
