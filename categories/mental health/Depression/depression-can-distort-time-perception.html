Depression Can Distort Time Perception

Time drags or disappears. Minutes feel elastic. Days blur.

Depression alters internal timekeeping. The brain misjudges duration. Negative emotion slows perceived time. Boredom intensifies distortion. Neurochemical imbalance affects temporal awareness. This impacts patience and planning. Recovery normalizes perception.

Why This Matters

It matters because distorted time worsens suffering. Waiting feels unbearable.

Understanding this reduces self-blame. It helps explain exhaustion and frustration.

Did You Know?

Did you know time distortion is also studied in pain research. Depression shows similar patterns.

Source

[Journal of Psychiatric Research, elsevier.com]
