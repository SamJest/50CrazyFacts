Panic Disorder Can Strike During Sleep

Nighttime attacks are real.

Panic attacks can occur while asleep, waking sufferers with intense fear, racing heart, and sweating. These nocturnal attacks are often mistaken for nightmares or sleep disorders. The brain triggers the same adrenaline surges at night as during the day. Sleep disruption compounds anxiety and fatigue.

Why This Matters

Awareness of nocturnal panic guides proper treatment.

It explains why some panic disorder sufferers develop insomnia.

Did You Know?

Did you know nighttime panic can occur without dreaming.
