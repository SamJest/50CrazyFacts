Panic Disorder Can Cause Micro-Freezes

Body stops in place.

Some sufferers experience sudden “freezing” moments during panic attacks, feeling unable to move or speak. This is linked to the fight-flight-freeze response. While rare, the freeze state is terrifying and often misinterpreted as paralysis or fainting. It usually lasts a few seconds to a minute but feels much longer.

Why This Matters

Therapists use grounding techniques to prevent micro-freezes.

It highlights how panic disorder affects both mind and body simultaneously.

Did You Know?

Did you know the freeze response is evolutionarily protective but can be triggered falsely.
