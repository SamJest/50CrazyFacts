Panic Disorder Can Cause Shortness of Breath

Suffocating feelings occur.

Panic attacks often trigger hyperventilation, leaving sufferers gasping for air. The brain misinterprets normal breathing as insufficient, prompting rapid shallow breaths. This can intensify dizziness, chest tightness, and fear. Learning controlled breathing reduces symptom severity.

Why This Matters

Recognizing this prevents ER panic and promotes self-regulation.

It underscores the importance of breathing exercises in treatment.

Did You Know?

Did you know controlled breathing can stop many panic attack symptoms.
