Schizophrenia Can Include Paranoia

Suspicion dominates. Some feel constantly watched or plotted against.

Paranoia in schizophrenia involves exaggerated mistrust or belief in conspiracies against oneself. It may accompany delusions or hallucinations and can severely impact social interactions. Cognitive behavioral therapy and medication help reduce paranoid thinking.

Why This Matters

Understanding paranoia helps caregivers approach patients safely and empathetically.

Reducing paranoia improves adherence to treatment and daily functioning.

Did You Know?

Did you know paranoia in schizophrenia can lead individuals to avoid everyday places or people due to perceived threats.
