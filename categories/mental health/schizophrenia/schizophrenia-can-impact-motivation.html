Schizophrenia Can Impact Motivation

Apathy can be overwhelming.

Negative symptoms like avolition reduce motivation to perform everyday tasks, pursue goals, or maintain hygiene. This is distinct from laziness and is neurologically based. Behavioral interventions, therapy, and sometimes medication enhance engagement.

Why This Matters

Recognizing motivational deficits prevents misjudgment and promotes empathy.

Encouraging small, achievable tasks supports independence and well-being.

Did You Know?

Did you know people with schizophrenia may desire activities but struggle to initiate them due to avolition.
