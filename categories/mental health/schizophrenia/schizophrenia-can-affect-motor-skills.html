Schizophrenia Can Affect Motor Skills

Movement may appear awkward or delayed.

Motor skill difficulties include tremors, poor coordination, or slowed movements. Symptoms can stem from neurological changes or medication side effects. Physical therapy improves mobility and safety.

Why This Matters

Understanding motor challenges prevents misjudgment of capability.

Targeted interventions help maintain independence and prevent accidents.

Did You Know?

Did you know some patients may have trouble with fine motor tasks like writing or buttoning clothes.
