Schizophrenia Can Lead to Social Withdrawal

Isolation may seem deliberate.

Negative symptoms can cause individuals to withdraw from family, friends, or society. Withdrawal is often due to anxiety, sensory overload, or difficulty understanding social cues. Social skills training and structured support encourage safe engagement.

Why This Matters

Recognizing withdrawal prevents misjudging behavior as intentional.

Supporting social integration improves quality of life and reduces relapse risk.

Did You Know?

Did you know social withdrawal can start years before other symptoms appear.
