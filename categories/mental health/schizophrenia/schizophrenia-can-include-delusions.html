Schizophrenia Can Include Delusions

Strong beliefs disconnected from reality.

Delusions in schizophrenia are false beliefs resistant to evidence. Common themes include persecution, grandiosity, or reference. Delusions can influence behavior, decision-making, and social interactions. Therapy, medication, and psychoeducation help manage delusional thinking.

Why This Matters

Understanding delusions prevents misjudgment and improves treatment adherence.

Recognizing delusions encourages compassionate support and safety measures.

Did You Know?

Did you know some delusions involve believing media or celebrities are sending secret messages.
