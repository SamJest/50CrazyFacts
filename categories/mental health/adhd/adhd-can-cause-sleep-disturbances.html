ADHD Can Cause Sleep Disturbances

Minds race. Rest eludes. ADHD often affects circadian rhythms and sleep onset, causing insomnia or restless sleep.

Poor sleep exacerbates attention difficulties, irritability, and mood instability.

Why This Matters

Behavioral strategies, sleep hygiene, and sometimes medication improve sleep quality.

Recognizing sleep challenges helps prevent compounded ADHD symptoms.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD adults often report delayed sleep phase, feeling alert late at night and exhausted in the morning.
