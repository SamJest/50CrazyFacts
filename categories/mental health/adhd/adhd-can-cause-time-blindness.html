ADHD Can Cause Time-Blindness

Deadlines vanish. Minutes disappear. ADHD affects perception of time, making intervals feel shorter or longer than they are.

Time-blindness leads to lateness, missed deadlines, and stress.

Why This Matters

Tools like timers, alarms, and planners compensate for distorted time perception.

Recognizing time-blindness allows structured supports to improve punctuality and task completion.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD individuals often underestimate how long tasks take, causing chronic lateness.
