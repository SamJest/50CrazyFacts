ADHD Can Impact Fine Motor Skills

Hands fumble. Writing struggles. ADHD may interfere with coordination and fine motor control, affecting handwriting, drawing, or tool use.

This can influence academic and occupational performance.

Why This Matters

Occupational therapy, practice, and adaptive tools enhance motor skills.

Recognizing motor difficulties prevents unfair judgment of competence.

Did You Know?

Did you know children with ADHD may exhibit messy handwriting despite normal intelligence.
