ADHD Can Cause Difficulty Regulating Emotions

Emotions swing. Anger flares. ADHD affects emotional self-regulation, leading to intense reactions and mood fluctuations.

This can strain relationships and increase stress.

Why This Matters

Emotional regulation strategies, therapy, and support systems improve coping and stability.

Recognizing emotional dysregulation reduces stigma and promotes adaptive management.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD emotional outbursts can happen even when the individual understands the situation logically.
