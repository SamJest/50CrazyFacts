ADHD Can Affect Reading Comprehension

Words blur. Focus drifts. ADHD impacts working memory and attention, leading to challenges understanding text or following long passages.

Reading difficulties affect academics, professional development, and self-confidence.

Why This Matters

Strategies like active reading, highlighting, and note-taking improve comprehension.

Recognizing reading struggles prevents mislabeling intelligence or motivation.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD students may reread paragraphs multiple times without absorbing information fully.
