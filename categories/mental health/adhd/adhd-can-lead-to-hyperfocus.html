ADHD Can Lead to Hyperfocus

On one task. Hours disappear. ADHD hyperfocus allows intense concentration on interesting tasks, often ignoring other responsibilities.

This can increase productivity in preferred tasks but cause neglect elsewhere.

Why This Matters

Structured breaks and planning help balance hyperfocus benefits with responsibilities.

Recognizing hyperfocus as a symptom can leverage strengths without harm.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD hyperfocus can make hours feel like minutes when engaged in stimulating activities.
