ADHD Can Affect Driving Performance

Attention drifts. Reactions vary. ADHD drivers are at higher risk for speeding, accidents, and impulsive maneuvers due to distractibility and impulsivity.

Driving safety is a critical concern for independence and legal compliance.

Why This Matters

Training, medication, and strategies like minimizing distractions improve road safety.

Understanding ADHD impacts on driving helps family and clinicians provide appropriate guidance.

Did You Know?

Did you know ADHD drivers have a higher likelihood of traffic violations if untreated.
