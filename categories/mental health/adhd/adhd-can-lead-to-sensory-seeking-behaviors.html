ADHD Can Lead to Sensory-Seeking Behaviors

Textures, sounds, lights fascinate. ADHD may produce cravings for tactile, auditory, or visual stimulation.

Sensory-seeking can impact daily functioning or social interactions.

Why This Matters

Safe sensory outlets improve engagement and reduce disruptive behavior.

Recognizing sensory needs prevents misinterpretation of hyperactivity as defiance.

Did You Know?

Did you know fidget toys and textured clothing are popular self-regulation tools for ADHD individuals.
