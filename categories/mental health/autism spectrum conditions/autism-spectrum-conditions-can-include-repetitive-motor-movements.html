Autism Spectrum Conditions Can Include Repetitive Motor Movements

Hand flapping, rocking, or spinning can self-soothe or express excitement.

Repetitive motor movements, or “stimming,” help regulate sensory input and emotions. They may appear unusual but are calming and functional.

Why This Matters

Recognizing stimming prevents unnecessary restriction and supports mental well-being.

Supporting safe and socially appropriate stimming enhances comfort and reduces anxiety.

Did You Know?

Did you know hand flapping can release tension and is often seen in happy or excited states.
