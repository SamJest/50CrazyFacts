Autism Spectrum Conditions Can Include Hyperlexia

Early reading superpowers. Some autistic children decode words far earlier than peers, sometimes without comprehension.

Hyperlexia is the ability to read words at an early age, often accompanied by strong memorization but limited understanding of context or social meaning. It can be a sign of advanced visual processing.

Why This Matters

Recognizing hyperlexia allows parents and educators to nurture reading while supporting comprehension and social skills.

Supporting comprehension alongside decoding skills maximizes academic and cognitive development.

Did You Know?

Did you know some autistic children can read full books before age 5 but may struggle with understanding the story's emotions.
