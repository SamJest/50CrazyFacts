Eating Disorders Can Alter Taste Perception

Food may literally taste different.

People with eating disorders often report altered taste perception. Sweet foods may taste overwhelming or unpleasant. Starvation affects taste receptors and brain processing. This reinforces avoidance of certain foods. Taste sensitivity often returns during recovery. The change is biological, not imagined.

Why This Matters

This explains why fear foods feel unbearable.

It supports gradual exposure approaches in treatment.

Did You Know?

Did you know zinc deficiency can reduce taste sensitivity.
