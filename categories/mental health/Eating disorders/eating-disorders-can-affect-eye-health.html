Eating Disorders Can Affect Eye Health

Vision can suffer silently.

Vitamin A deficiency impacts night vision. Dry eyes become common. Eye strain increases with dehydration. Severe cases cause corneal damage. Visual changes are often overlooked. Recovery restores function gradually.

Why This Matters

This highlights non-obvious medical risks.

It emphasizes full physical exams.

Did You Know?

Did you know vitamin A was discovered through eye research.
