Eating Disorders Can Affect Bone Density

Skeletons pay the price.

Low nutrition interferes with bone formation. Osteopenia and osteoporosis can develop early. Fracture risk increases significantly. Adolescents are especially vulnerable. Bone loss may not fully reverse. Prevention through early treatment is essential.

Why This Matters

This matters for lifelong mobility and strength.

It emphasizes eating disorders as whole-body illnesses.

Did You Know?

Did you know bone loss can begin within months of restriction.
