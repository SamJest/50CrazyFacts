Eating Disorders Can Disrupt Voice Quality

The voice may weaken.

Muscle wasting affects vocal cords. Acid reflux irritates the throat. Hoarseness develops. Singers notice early changes. Voice strength returns with recovery. Speech therapy may help.

Why This Matters

This matters for communication and identity.

It highlights unexpected functional impacts.

Did You Know?

Did you know vocal cords are muscles.
