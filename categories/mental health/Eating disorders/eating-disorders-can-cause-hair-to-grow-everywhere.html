Eating Disorders Can Cause Hair to Grow Everywhere

The body adapts in strange ways.

Severe restriction can trigger lanugo, a fine body hair growth. This is the body’s attempt to conserve heat. It commonly appears on the arms, face, and back. Lanugo is most associated with anorexia nervosa. It fades with nutritional recovery. The symptom surprises many patients.

Why This Matters

This highlights how the body prioritizes survival.

It underscores the seriousness of prolonged starvation.

Did You Know?

Did you know lanugo is normally seen only in newborns.
