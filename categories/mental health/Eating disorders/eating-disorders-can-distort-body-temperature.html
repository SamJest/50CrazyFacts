Eating Disorders Can Distort Body Temperature

The body loses thermal control.

People with eating disorders often feel constantly cold. Reduced body fat limits insulation. Metabolism slows to conserve energy. Circulation becomes less efficient. This symptom can persist into recovery. It reflects deep metabolic adaptation.

Why This Matters

This helps explain constant cold complaints.

It reinforces that symptoms are physiological, not dramatic.

Did You Know?

Did you know chronic cold sensitivity can signal energy deficiency.
