Eating Disorders Have the Highest Mortality Rate

They are among the deadliest mental illnesses.

Eating disorders have one of the highest mortality rates of any psychiatric condition. Death can occur due to medical complications or suicide. Anorexia nervosa, in particular, carries extreme physical risks. Electrolyte imbalances, heart failure, and organ damage are common causes. Early intervention dramatically improves survival rates. Despite this, eating disorders are often minimized or misunderstood.

Why This Matters

Understanding mortality risk emphasizes urgency in treatment.

It supports funding, research, and early screening initiatives.

Did You Know?

Did you know anorexia nervosa has a higher death rate than many anxiety and mood disorders.
