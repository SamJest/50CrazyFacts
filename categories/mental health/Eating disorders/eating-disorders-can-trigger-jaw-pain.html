Eating Disorders Can Trigger Jaw Pain

Jaw tension builds quietly.

Chronic restriction increases muscle tension. Teeth grinding becomes common. Purging irritates jaw joints. TMJ pain can develop. Headaches often follow. Dental care is important.

Why This Matters

This highlights hidden physical strain.

It encourages dental screening.

Did You Know?

Did you know jaw muscles are among the strongest in the body.
