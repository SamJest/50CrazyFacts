Social Anxiety Can Make Phone Calls Stressful

Voices become judgmental.

Even simple phone calls can cause panic or overthinking, as the lack of visual cues increases fear of misunderstanding or negative evaluation.

Why This Matters

This can affect work, school, and personal relationships.

It demonstrates the subtle ways social anxiety adapts to modern communication.

Did You Know?

Did you know social anxiety can make speaking on the phone more stressful than talking in person.
