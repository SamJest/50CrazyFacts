Social Anxiety Can Make Public Bathrooms Intimidating

Fear extends to privacy spaces.

Even using a public restroom can provoke stress about being observed, judged, or making noise. Anxiety heightens hyper-awareness.

Why This Matters

This leads to avoidance, discomfort, and sometimes health consequences.

It shows social anxiety’s subtle but pervasive influence on everyday life.

Did You Know?

Did you know social anxiety can make using public bathrooms a source of intense fear.
