Social Anxiety Can Cause Stomach Issues

Nerves upset the digestive system.

High stress from social anxiety can trigger nausea, stomach cramps, or diarrhea. Gut-brain communication means anxiety directly affects the digestive system.

Why This Matters

Recognizing physical discomfort as anxiety-related reduces unnecessary medical visits.

It highlights how social anxiety manifests in unexpected physical ways.

Did You Know?

Did you know social anxiety can cause stomach cramps or nausea before a presentation.
