Social Anxiety Can Trigger Overthinking of Words

Every sentence is analyzed.

After speaking, people may obsessively replay conversations, worrying about tone, choice of words, or impressions. This mental replay can last hours or days.

Why This Matters

It can prevent moving on from social events and reinforce self-doubt.

It shows how social anxiety extends well beyond the social encounter itself.

Did You Know?

Did you know social anxiety can make someone replay their own words repeatedly in their mind.

[National Health Service, nhs.uk]
