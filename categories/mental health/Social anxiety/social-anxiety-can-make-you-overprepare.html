Social Anxiety Can Make You Overprepare

Preparation becomes obsessive.

Those with social anxiety often rehearse conversations, speeches, or meetings excessively to reduce fear of embarrassment. This can take hours or days.

Why This Matters

Overpreparation can be mentally exhausting and ironically increase anxiety.

It reveals how social anxiety alters normal planning behaviors into extreme rituals.

Did You Know?

Did you know social anxiety can make someone spend hours rehearsing simple conversations.
