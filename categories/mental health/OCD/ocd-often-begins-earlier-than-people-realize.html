OCD Often Begins Earlier Than People Realize

Childhood onset is common.

Many people develop OCD before age 14. Symptoms are often mistaken for habits. Children hide obsessions out of fear. Delayed diagnosis is common. Early treatment improves outcomes.

Why This Matters

This highlights the need for awareness.

It supports early screening programs.

Did You Know?

Did you know pediatric OCD affects boys and girls equally.
