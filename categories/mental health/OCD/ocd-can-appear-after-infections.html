OCD Can Appear After Infections

Symptoms emerge suddenly.

In rare cases OCD follows infections like strep. This is linked to PANDAS in children. The immune system affects brain function. Sudden onset is dramatic. Early treatment improves outcomes. Research continues.

Why This Matters

This shows a biological trigger pathway.

It expands understanding beyond psychology.

Did You Know?

Did you know inflammation can alter brain signaling.
