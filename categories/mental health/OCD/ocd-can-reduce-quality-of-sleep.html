OCD Can Reduce Quality of Sleep

Nighttime becomes a battleground.

Obsessions intensify at night. Mental rituals delay sleep onset. Anxiety fragments rest. Fatigue worsens symptoms. Treating OCD improves sleep quality.

Why This Matters

This explains chronic insomnia complaints.

It shows sleep and mental health are linked.

Did You Know?

Did you know sleep loss amplifies anxiety loops.
