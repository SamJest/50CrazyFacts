OCD Can Alter Sense of Personal Responsibility

Responsibility feels extreme.

OCD exaggerates perceived control over outcomes. Small actions feel world-altering. This fuels compulsive prevention behaviors. Reality testing weakens under stress. Treatment restores balanced responsibility.

Why This Matters

This reduces guilt-based suffering.

It reframes moral fears clinically.

Did You Know?

Did you know responsibility distortion predicts OCD relapse.
