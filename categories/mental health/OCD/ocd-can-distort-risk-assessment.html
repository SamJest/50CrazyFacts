OCD Can Distort Risk Assessment

Danger feels magnified.

OCD causes overestimation of risk. Rare events feel imminent. The brain miscalculates probability. Safety behaviors reinforce fear. Therapy recalibrates risk perception.

Why This Matters

This explains excessive safety behaviors.

It supports exposure-based treatment.

Did You Know?

Did you know humans naturally misjudge rare risks.
