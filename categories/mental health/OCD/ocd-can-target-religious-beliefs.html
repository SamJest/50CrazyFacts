OCD Can Target Religious Beliefs

Faith becomes stressful.

Religious OCD involves intrusive moral fears. Prayers become compulsive. Beliefs feel hijacked. Sufferers fear offending deities. Treatment respects belief systems.

Why This Matters

This explains distress in religious communities.

It prevents mislabeling as loss of faith.

Did You Know?

Did you know this is called scrupulosity.
