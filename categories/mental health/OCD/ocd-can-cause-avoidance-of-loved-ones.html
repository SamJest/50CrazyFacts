OCD Can Cause Avoidance of Loved Ones

Distance feels safer.

Fear of harming others drives avoidance. Parents may avoid children. Partners feel confusingly unsafe. The fear is ego-dystonic. Therapy reduces avoidance behaviors.

Why This Matters

This explains painful relationship withdrawal.

It prevents misinterpretation as lack of love.
