Some researchers suggest the Phaistos Disc may have contained astrological or predictive information related to celestial events and ritual planning.

Some scholars theorize that the Phaistos Disc may contain astrological predictions, linking celestial events to human or natural phenomena. The repeated symbols and spiral arrangement might correspond to planets, stars, or zodiacal markers, guiding ritual or practical decision-making. Astrology played a role in early Mediterranean societies, informing agriculture, health, and religious observance. A compact disc could allow priests or astrologers to reference and interpret celestial cycles efficiently. Even without decoding, the artifact suggests sophisticated symbolic abstraction for organizing temporal and cosmic knowledge. Its design indicates an interplay between observation, prediction, and ritual performance. This interpretation situates the disc at the intersection of science, spirituality, and societal planning. The artifact exemplifies the Minoans’ integration of observation, abstraction, and practical application. It may represent one of the earliest attempts to encode predictive knowledge.

Even speculative, it encourages interdisciplinary approaches combining archaeology, astronomy, and anthropology.
