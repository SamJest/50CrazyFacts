Upon inspection, the Mary Celeste’s sails and rigging were in perfect condition, with no signs of recent storm damage or hasty tampering. Historians interpret this as evidence that the crew’s departure was not due to immediate environmental threats like a sudden storm. The orderly rigging contrasts with the sudden disappearance, creating a paradox that deepens the mystery. Maritime experts note that a vessel abandoned during an actual storm would show signs of disarray in its rigging and sails.
