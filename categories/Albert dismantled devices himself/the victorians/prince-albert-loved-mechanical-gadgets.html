Prince Albert Loved Mechanical Gadgets

Gears fascinated him.

So did precision.

Why This Matters

Prince Albert collected mechanical devices. Clocks and instruments filled his rooms. Engineering beauty impressed him. Gadgets symbolized progress. He tested inventions personally. Innovation thrilled him.

This encouraged inventors.

Did You Know?

It validated engineering culture.
