Why Anxiety Can Make Your Hands Feel Numb

Anxiety sometimes makes limbs feel strange.

Hyperventilation and stress increase oxygen and CO2 imbalances, leading to tingling or numbness in hands and feet. Panic attacks often reveal this symptom. Controlled breathing, grounding techniques, and therapy reduce sensations. Awareness prevents panic escalation.

Why This Matters

Numbness can intensify fear and panic.

Managing physical symptoms helps regain control during anxious episodes.

Did You Know?

Anxiety can cause numbness or tingling through hyperventilation and nervous system changes.
