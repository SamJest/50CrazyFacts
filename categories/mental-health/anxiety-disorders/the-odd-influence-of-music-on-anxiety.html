The Odd Influence of Music on Anxiety

Certain songs can spike or soothe your nerves.

Music affects the amygdala, the brain’s fear center, influencing stress levels. Fast, dissonant tunes can trigger anxious responses, while slow, harmonious music can reduce heart rate and cortisol. Personal preferences matter; familiar melodies often produce comfort. Music therapy is increasingly used in clinical anxiety treatments. Live music may provide social and emotional buffering effects. Even background music in workplaces can subtly influence anxiety.

Why This Matters

Selecting calming music can be a practical, immediate coping tool.

Music-based interventions can complement cognitive and behavioral therapies.

Did You Know?

Listening to your favorite relaxing music can decrease physiological stress markers.

Source

American Music Therapy Association
