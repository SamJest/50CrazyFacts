Depression Can Change Gut Bacteria

Depression alters the gut microbiome. The brain and gut talk constantly. Mood affects digestion.

Studies show different gut bacteria profiles in depressed individuals. Inflammation links gut health and mood. Stress hormones alter intestinal permeability. This can worsen digestive symptoms. Probiotics show modest mood benefits. Diet influences neurotransmitter production. The gut-brain axis is a growing research field.

Why This Matters

It matters because treatment may extend beyond the brain. Nutrition becomes part of mental healthcare.

This opens new interventions and personalized medicine options. It also reduces stigma around physical symptoms.

Did You Know?

Did you know some gut bacteria help produce serotonin precursors. The gut produces most of the body’s serotonin.

Source

[Harvard Medical School, health.harvard.edu]
