Digital Learning and Anxiety

Can Zoom school spike stress?

💥 Impact

Shows modern digital environments impact child mental health.

Supports structured, socially integrated digital learning approaches.

🤯 Did You Know?

Did you know excessive online learning can increase anxiety in children?

The shift to digital learning, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic, created novel stressors for children. Historical comparisons to home schooling show digital learning adds isolation, blurred routines, and screen fatigue. Anxiety, attention difficulties, and social withdrawal increased in children navigating online classrooms without peer or teacher presence. Studies report heightened emotional dysregulation, sleep disruption, and academic stress. Integrating structured breaks, offline activities, and peer interaction reduces negative mental health outcomes. Teachers observed that children adapt better when digital platforms include social engagement and collaborative learning. Understanding the psychological costs of digital learning informs hybrid education models. Mental health support in tech-mediated learning environments becomes crucial. Digital adaptation without emotional scaffolding can unintentionally harm mental health.

Source

Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry
