Online Activism Burnout

Mental strain from digital advocacy is real.

💥 Impact

Digital activism can induce emotional burnout and fatigue.

Structured offline engagement reduces psychological strain.

🤯 Did You Know?

Constant online activism can take a real toll on mental health.

Activists experience “compassion fatigue” online, constantly exposed to suffering, crises, and urgent causes. Historically, social reform required physical presence and limited media, but digital activism scales exposure globally and instantly. Continuous engagement can trigger emotional exhaustion, depressive symptoms, and anxiety. Neurobiological stress systems are activated with repeated digital exposure. Interventions include structured offline time, peer support, and emotional processing strategies. Recognizing burnout reframes social media engagement as a double-edged sword for mental health: socially meaningful but emotionally costly. Platforms and campaigns must consider user mental health alongside impact metrics. Awareness empowers advocates to sustain engagement without compromising well-being.

Source

Journal of Social Media Studies
