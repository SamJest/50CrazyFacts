Prince Albert Secretly Rewrote Royal Protocol

Rules bored him.

So he changed them.

Why This Matters

Prince Albert quietly rewrote many royal protocols. He simplified ceremonies and reduced outdated rituals. Albert believed efficiency mattered more than pomp. Courtiers resisted at first. Over time, his changes stuck. The monarchy became leaner and more modern.

This made the court functional.

Did You Know?

It also reduced royal excess.
