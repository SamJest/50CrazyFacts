Baboons Can Run at Speeds Up to 35 km/h

Fast and furious, baboons are savannah sprinters.

Baboons have muscular limbs adapted for running across savannahs and rugged terrain. This speed allows them to evade predators such as lions, leopards, and hyenas. They can sprint short distances rapidly and coordinate escape with group members. Young baboons practice running and climbing under adult supervision. Their cardiovascular system supports sudden bursts of activity. Speed combined with agility makes them highly resilient against natural threats.

Why This Matters

Shows the importance of physical fitness in predator avoidance.

It highlights the evolutionary pressure on primates to develop powerful locomotion.

Did You Know?

They can reach top speeds in seconds when startled by predators.
