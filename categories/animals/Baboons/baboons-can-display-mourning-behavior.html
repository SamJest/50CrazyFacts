Baboons Can Display Mourning Behavior

They grieve and show empathy.

Baboons sometimes touch, groom, or stay close to deceased troop members. Mothers may carry deceased infants for days. These behaviors suggest awareness of death and social bonds. Mourning reinforces family and troop cohesion. Emotional complexity impacts social interactions and learning. Observing such behavior sheds light on the evolution of empathy in primates.

Why This Matters

Shows emotional depth and social attachment in animals.

It provides insights into evolutionary origins of grief and empathy.

Did You Know?

They can carry and groom deceased infants or companions for extended periods.

Source

Madagascar Primate Research Program (mprp.mg)
