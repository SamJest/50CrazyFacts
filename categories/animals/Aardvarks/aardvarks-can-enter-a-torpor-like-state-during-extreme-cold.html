Aardvarks Can Enter a Torpor-Like State During Extreme Cold

When energy runs low, slowing down helps. Aardvarks have a backup plan. Metabolism can dip.

In extreme cold or food scarcity, aardvarks can lower their body temperature. This torpor-like state reduces energy use. Activity levels drop significantly. It’s not true hibernation, but it’s a survival slowdown. This response buys time.

Why This Matters

It matters because flexibility saves lives. Metabolic control extends survival windows.

This ability may become more important with climate variability. Extremes demand adaptation.

Did You Know?

Body temperatures have been recorded dropping several degrees below normal during cold spells. This is rare among tropical mammals.

Source

Proceedings of the Royal Society B (royalsocietypublishing.org)
