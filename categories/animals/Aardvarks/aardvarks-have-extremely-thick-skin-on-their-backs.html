Aardvarks Have Extremely Thick Skin on Their Backs

An aardvark’s skin is tougher than it looks. Beneath the sparse hair is serious armor. It needs to be.

Aardvarks have especially thick skin on their backs and flanks. This protects them from insect bites, scratches, and predator attacks. Termites and ants bite aggressively when disturbed. Thick skin prevents injury and infection. It also provides some defense against predators like lions and hyenas.

Why This Matters

It matters because physical protection allows aardvarks to exploit dangerous food sources. Many animals avoid termite mounds for good reason.

This adaptation shows how diet can shape anatomy. Eating insects requires more than just a long tongue.

Did You Know?

Aardvark skin can be tough enough to resist many insect stings completely. Bites rarely penetrate deeply.
