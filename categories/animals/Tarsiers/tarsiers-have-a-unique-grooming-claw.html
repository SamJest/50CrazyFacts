Tarsiers Have a Unique Grooming Claw

Self-care specialists.

Tarsiers have a specialized grooming claw on their second toe used to clean fur and remove parasites. This adaptation ensures hygiene in dense, humid forests where skin parasites are common. Grooming is essential for thermoregulation, reducing infection risk, and social signaling. The grooming claw allows tarsiers to maintain cleanliness without relying on group grooming, unlike many primates. Precision and flexibility make the claw highly effective. Healthy fur supports stealth and hunting efficiency. Evolution favors grooming adaptations that enhance survival and comfort.

Why This Matters

Grooming claw reduces disease risk.

It supports stealthy behavior by keeping fur parasite-free.
