Tarsiers Are Endangered

Conservation alert.

Tarsiers face habitat destruction, hunting, and illegal pet trade. Deforestation fragments populations, reducing genetic diversity. They are sensitive to environmental disturbances due to small size and specialized habitat requirements. Conservation programs include habitat protection and public education. Preserving forests is essential for tarsier survival. Research and ecotourism support awareness and protection efforts. Their endangered status highlights the fragility of tropical ecosystems and human impact.

Why This Matters

Endangered status stresses urgent conservation.

Protecting habitats preserves biodiversity and species survival.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers are endangered due to habitat loss and human activity.

Source

International Union for Conservation of Nature (iucn.org)
