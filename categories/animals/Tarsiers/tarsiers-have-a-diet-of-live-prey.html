Tarsiers Have a Diet of Live Prey

Hunting specialists.

Tarsiers feed almost exclusively on live prey such as insects, small birds, and lizards. Unlike many primates, they do not eat fruit or vegetation. Their keen eyesight, ultrasound communication, and powerful jumps make them skilled predators. They hunt mostly at night, using stealth and patience to ambush prey. Slow metabolism and high hunting efficiency balance their small body size. Their diet influences social structure, as food sources are scattered and hunting is mostly solitary. Tarsiers’ predatory behavior challenges the perception that all primates are herbivorous.

Why This Matters

Hunting live prey ensures protein intake necessary for survival.

It shapes nocturnal behavior and solitary tendencies.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers almost exclusively hunt live prey and rarely consume plant matter.

Source

International Union for Conservation of Nature (iucn.org)
