Tarsiers Can Detect Ultrasonic Sounds

Hearing beyond humans.

Tarsiers can perceive ultrasonic frequencies above 70 kHz, far beyond human hearing. This ability helps locate insect prey and communicate with conspecifics. Ultrasonic hearing is crucial for nocturnal hunting when visual cues are limited. Ears are highly mobile, capturing subtle sounds from all directions. High-frequency detection enhances predation success and predator awareness. Tarsiers’ auditory system exemplifies extreme specialization for arboreal nocturnal life. Understanding ultrasonic perception informs research on primate sensory evolution. It also guides habitat management for noise-sensitive species.

Why This Matters

Ultrasonic hearing improves hunting and communication.

It enables perception of prey and social signals invisible to other animals.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers can hear ultrasonic frequencies above 70 kHz for hunting and communication.
