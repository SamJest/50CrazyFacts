Tarsiers Can Jump Over 3 Meters

Record leapers.

Tarsiers’ hind limbs enable jumps exceeding 3 meters, more than 40 times their body length. Powerful muscles store elastic energy for explosive leaps between trees. Leaping allows rapid escape from predators and efficient travel between feeding sites. Precision landing is aided by flexible ankles, long fingers, and tail balancing. Such extreme leaping is unmatched by most primates of similar size. Long-distance jumps conserve energy compared to climbing every branch. Tarsiers’ acrobatic leaps illustrate evolution for arboreal survival.

Why This Matters

Leaping ability improves predator evasion.

It enhances foraging range and energy efficiency.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers can jump over 3 meters using powerful hind limbs and flexible anatomy.
