Tarsiers Can Eat Small Vertebrates

Protein hunters.

Though primarily insectivorous, tarsiers occasionally consume small lizards, frogs, or birds. Hunting small vertebrates provides high-protein supplements. Specialized teeth, claws, and acute senses facilitate capturing quick prey. Vertebrate prey supplements diet when insects are scarce. Inclusion of small vertebrates shows dietary flexibility within a strict protein niche. Tarsiers’ predatory versatility enhances survival during seasonal fluctuations. It reflects evolutionary optimization for nutrient-rich prey in complex habitats. Occasional vertebrate predation demonstrates their skillful hunting capabilities.

Why This Matters

Vertebrate diet increases nutrition variety.

It aids survival when insect populations fluctuate.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers sometimes eat small lizards, frogs, or birds in addition to insects.
