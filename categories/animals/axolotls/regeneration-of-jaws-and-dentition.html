Axolotls can fully restore jawbones and teeth after injury.

Axolotls regenerate jawbones and teeth after injury. Osteoblasts and odontogenic cells dedifferentiate and reconstruct tissue. Humans care because jaw regeneration research informs craniofacial reconstruction. Observations show restored biting and feeding ability. Molecular pathways overlap with limb and heart regeneration. Tissue patterning ensures proper alignment. Regrowth occurs without scarring. Nutritional status affects regeneration rate. Studying jaw regeneration provides insight into vertebrate craniofacial repair.
