Skin Pigmentation Variation

Colors of survival.

›

Pigmentation variation offers insight into natural and artificial selection. Genetics influence both survival and human-driven breeding.

Phenotypic diversity informs ecological adaptation studies. Artificial selection alters natural evolutionary patterns.

Some pigment genes in axolotls are shared with humans.

Axolotls evolved a range of pigmentations from wild-type brown to albino and leucistic forms, influenced by genetics and environment. Melanin patterns assist predator avoidance. Mutations in pigmentation genes create color variants. Observations suggest selective pressures maintain wild-type predominance. Studying pigmentation informs evolutionary genetics. Color variants often dominate lab populations. Humans care because pigmentation affects camouflage, mate selection, and captive breeding.
