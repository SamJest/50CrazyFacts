Exotic Food or Sacred Creature? Early Human Use

Humans have long eaten and revered axolotls simultaneously.

💥 Impact

Understanding this paradox highlights the complex human-animal relationship in history.

It shows that even revered species can be subjected to intense human exploitation.

🤯 Did You Know?

Axolotls were considered a delicacy during certain Aztec religious festivals.

Aztec writings and colonial accounts describe axolotls being consumed as food, particularly during religious fasts when meat consumption was restricted. Simultaneously, they were revered as divine symbols, illustrating a rare duality of sacred and practical use. European colonists were puzzled by this coexistence, struggling to reconcile culinary habits with mystical interpretation. The edible aspect increased hunting pressure, while ceremonial significance afforded some protection. This paradox demonstrates how human culture can simultaneously exploit and conserve a species. It also provides insight into the complex role animals play in societal norms, economy, and spirituality.

Source

Mexican Historical Society
