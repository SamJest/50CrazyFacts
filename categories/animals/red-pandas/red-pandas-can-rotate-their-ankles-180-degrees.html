Red Pandas Can Rotate Their Ankles 180 Degrees

Climbing headfirst made easy.

Unique among mammals, red pandas can rotate their ankles up to 180 degrees. This enables them to climb down trees headfirst safely. Juveniles practice controlled rotations before independent climbing. The adaptation reduces the risk of falls during arboreal exploration. It allows rapid escape from predators or efficient foraging. Combined with flexible wrists, it makes them exceptionally agile in forest canopies.

Why This Matters

This adaptation expands accessible habitat and feeding options.

It is crucial for survival in arboreal ecosystems.

Did You Know?

Red pandas can rotate their ankles fully to climb down trees headfirst.

Zoological Society of London (zsl.org)
