Pangolins Can Suffer From Habitat Fragmentation

Human impact.

Deforestation and land conversion fragment pangolin habitats. Fragmentation reduces prey availability and mating opportunities. Isolated populations face genetic bottlenecks. Travel between fragmented areas exposes pangolins to predators and humans. It complicates conservation planning. Fragmented habitats increase reliance on protected areas.

Why This Matters

Habitat fragmentation directly threatens species survival.

Understanding fragmentation aids effective conservation management.

Did You Know?

Connectivity between habitats is critical for population stability.
