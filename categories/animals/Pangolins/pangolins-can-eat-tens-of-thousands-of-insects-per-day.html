Pangolins Can Eat Tens of Thousands of Insects Per Day

Insect feasting.

A single pangolin can consume 20,000–70,000 ants or termites daily. High intake supports their metabolism and energy needs. Feeding is efficient due to sticky tongue and specialized claws. Digestion relies on strong stomach acids. They may eat for several hours per night. Large individuals have greater consumption capacity.

Why This Matters

High insect consumption affects local insect populations.

It illustrates the pangolin’s ecological role as pest control.

Did You Know?

Pangolins’ diet helps maintain forest insect balance.

Source

World Wildlife Fund (worldwildlife.org)
