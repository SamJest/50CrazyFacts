Aardwolves Help Control Termite Populations

They’re tiny pest managers. Grasslands benefit. Ecosystems balance.

By consuming hundreds of thousands of termites each night, aardwolves regulate insect populations, protecting plant life. Their feeding reduces termite overpopulation and structural damage in savannas.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because it highlights their ecological role. Small insectivores have large ecosystem impact.

It also shows how keystone species influence habitat stability. Their absence could lead to termite surges.

Did You Know?

Did you know aardwolves can consume more termites than any other local insectivore.
