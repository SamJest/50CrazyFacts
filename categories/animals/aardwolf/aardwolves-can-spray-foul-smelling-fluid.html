Aardwolves Can Spray Foul-Smelling Fluid

Their defense is disgusting. Smell beats strength. Predators back off.

Aardwolves possess anal glands that release a strong-smelling secretion. When threatened, they spray this odor to deter predators. The scent can linger for hours. This chemical defense compensates for their lack of physical power.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows nonviolent survival strategies. Chemical defenses are common across animal groups.

It also explains why aardwolves are rarely attacked despite being small. Smell can be stronger than teeth.

Did You Know?

Did you know the odor is detectable by humans from several meters away.
