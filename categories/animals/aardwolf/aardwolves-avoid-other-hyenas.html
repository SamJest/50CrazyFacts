Aardwolves Avoid Other Hyenas

Peace over conflict. Food lines don’t cross. Safety first.

Aardwolves actively avoid larger hyenas like spotted and striped species. They feed at night and stay solitary, reducing competition for territory and termites. This behavior lowers risk of predation and injury.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because niche partitioning reduces interspecies conflict. Survival depends on strategy.

It also demonstrates how smaller carnivores coexist with dominant relatives. Avoidance is as effective as aggression.

Did You Know?

Did you know aardwolves may shift foraging times to avoid hyena encounters.
