Aardwolves Steal Burrows From Others

They borrow homes. Digging is optional. Laziness pays off.

Aardwolves frequently occupy burrows made by aardvarks or porcupines. Digging their own is rare and energy-consuming. Reusing shelters provides safety and temperature control. This behavior is a form of ecological recycling.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows interspecies dependence. One animal’s work benefits another.

It also highlights how habitat loss affects multiple species at once. Burrow-makers are keystone animals.

Did You Know?

Did you know aardwolves may use up to a dozen burrows in one territory.
