Aardwolves Can Travel Several Kilometers at Night

They roam wide. Termites move too. Hunger drives distance.

Aardwolves may travel up to 10 km per night while foraging for termites. Wide-ranging movement ensures sufficient food intake and reduces local depletion. They follow termite availability.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because movement patterns impact energy budgets and territory sizes. Survival depends on mobility.

It also influences conservation strategies. Large ranges require protected corridors and habitat connectivity.

Did You Know?

Did you know aardwolves can cross territories of other aardwolves without direct confrontation.
