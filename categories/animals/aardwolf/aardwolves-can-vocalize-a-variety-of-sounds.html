Aardwolves Can Vocalize a Variety of Sounds

Secretive talk. Warning whispers. Courtship calls.

Aardwolves communicate using growls, hisses, and high-pitched calls. These signals convey alarm, mating readiness, or territorial boundaries. Vocalization complements scent marking.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because communication is vital for solitary species to avoid conflict. Efficient signaling reduces risk.

It also affects mating and reproduction. Individuals can locate each other during mating season via calls.

Did You Know?

Did you know aardwolves sometimes growl at themselves in reflection or shadows.
