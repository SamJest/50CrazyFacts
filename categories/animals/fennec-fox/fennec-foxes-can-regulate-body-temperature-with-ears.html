Fennec Foxes Can Regulate Body Temperature With Ears

Natural air conditioning.

Fennec foxes use large ears to dissipate heat, cooling the blood flowing nearby. During extreme heat, blood vessels near the ear surface expand, releasing excess heat. Juveniles refine ear positioning to maximize cooling.

Why This Matters

Ear thermoregulation prevents overheating and conserves energy.

It highlights anatomical adaptation for extreme environmental survival.

Did You Know?

They can lower body temperature several degrees by adjusting ear orientation.

Source

National Wildlife Federation (nwf.org)
