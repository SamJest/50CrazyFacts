Fennec Foxes Can Use Their Tails as Camouflage

Desert disguise.

Fennec foxes curl their cream-colored tails over their bodies when resting, blending with sandy environments. This reduces predation risk during daylight hours. Juveniles gradually learn this defensive behavior.

Why This Matters

Tail camouflage improves survival by reducing predator detection.

It emphasizes the role of behavior in environmental adaptation.

Did You Know?

They can remain almost invisible to predators while resting on dunes.

Source

National Wildlife Federation (nwf.org)
