Narwhals Can Detect Polar Predator Presence

Early-warning systems.

Narwhals can sense or hear predators like polar bears and orcas, adjusting behavior to avoid predation.

Why This Matters

Sensory vigilance enhances survival, especially for calves and juveniles. Predator detection is vital for Arctic ecosystem navigation.

It shows how sensory specialization protects vulnerable populations.

Did You Know?

Narwhals sometimes dive silently to evade detected predators.

Source

Smithsonian’s National Zoo (nationalzoo.si.edu)
