Narwhals Can Engage in Synchronized Swimming

Pod choreography.

Narwhals often swim in coordinated formations, synchronizing dives and surfacing to optimize hunting and evade predators.

Why This Matters

Synchronized movement reduces energy expenditure and increases efficiency in cooperative hunting. It strengthens social bonds.

It demonstrates advanced group coordination and collective intelligence.

Did You Know?

Pods sometimes mirror movements precisely, almost like underwater ballet.
