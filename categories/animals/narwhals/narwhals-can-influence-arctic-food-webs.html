Narwhals Can Influence Arctic Food Webs

Trophic engineers.

Narwhals’ predation on fish and squid impacts prey populations and nutrient distribution, shaping the Arctic marine ecosystem.

Why This Matters

By regulating prey abundance, narwhals indirectly affect other species and overall biodiversity. Their hunting behavior supports ecosystem balance.

Ecological influence of apex predators underscores the interconnectedness of Arctic marine life. Narwhals are key players in maintaining food web dynamics.

Did You Know?

Narwhal predation redistributes nutrients across hundreds of miles.
