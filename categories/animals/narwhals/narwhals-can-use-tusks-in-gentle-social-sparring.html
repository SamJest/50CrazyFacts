Narwhals Can Use Tusks in Gentle Social Sparring

Friendly duels.

Males sometimes tap tusks lightly against each other as practice or to establish hierarchy without injury.

Why This Matters

This form of non-lethal competition teaches young males social rules and reduces aggressive encounters. Sparring also strengthens tusk control.

It highlights the role of play in social and physical development.

Did You Know?

Sparring sessions can last several minutes with no harm done.

Source

Smithsonian’s National Zoo (nationalzoo.si.edu)
