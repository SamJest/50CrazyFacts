Narwhals’ Tusks Can Sense Environmental Changes

Arctic sensors.

Research shows narwhal tusks detect temperature, salinity, and pressure changes, acting like a biological antenna. This helps them locate prey and navigate under ice.

Why This Matters

Sensory tusks provide real-time environmental information, giving narwhals an edge in hunting and avoiding hazards. The tusk is multifunctional, used for mating, dominance, and sensory detection.

This adaptation illustrates evolutionary innovation in Arctic mammals. Tusks’ sensory role may also influence social behavior within pods.

Did You Know?

Narwhal tusks contain up to 10 million nerve endings.
