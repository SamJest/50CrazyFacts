Narwhals Can Use Tusk as Sensory Antenna

Biological probe.

The narwhal’s tusk acts like a sensory antenna, detecting changes in water chemistry, temperature, and movement, assisting in hunting and navigation.

Why This Matters

This sensory capability gives them a competitive advantage in extreme Arctic habitats. Tusk sensitivity supports survival, reproduction, and social interaction.

It highlights the evolution of multifunctional appendages in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

The tusk contains up to 10 million nerve endings.
