Narwhals Can Detect Salinity Gradients While Diving

Water detectives.

Their tusks and sensory systems allow narwhals to sense subtle salinity changes, guiding them to areas rich in prey.

Why This Matters

Salinity detection ensures optimal foraging efficiency and energy conservation. It reduces wasted effort in barren waters.

It shows how fine-scale environmental perception drives survival in harsh habitats.

Did You Know?

Narwhals may follow salinity gradients for dozens of miles.

Source

Smithsonian’s National Zoo (nationalzoo.si.edu)
