Narwhals Exhibit Seasonal Migrations

Arctic travelers.

Narwhals migrate between wintering grounds in deep fjords and summer feeding areas near the ice edge, covering hundreds of miles.

Why This Matters

Migration aligns with prey abundance and ice cover, optimizing survival. Seasonal movement affects ecosystem nutrient distribution.

It demonstrates the importance of migratory corridors for conservation planning.

Did You Know?

Some narwhals travel over 1,000 miles in a single migration season.
