Narwhals Can Recognize Pod Members After Months Apart

Memory masters.

Narwhals demonstrate long-term social memory, reuniting with familiar individuals after seasonal migrations.

Why This Matters

Recognition maintains social bonds, enhances cooperative behaviors, and reduces conflict. Long-term memory improves pod cohesion and hunting efficiency.

It emphasizes cognitive sophistication in Arctic marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Narwhals sometimes greet each other with specific vocalizations after reuniting.

Source

Smithsonian’s National Zoo (nationalzoo.si.edu)
