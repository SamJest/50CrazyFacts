Cassowaries Are Excellent Swimmers

Aquatic adventurers. Jungle floaters. Stream navigators.

Cassowaries can swim across rivers and lakes using strong legs and buoyant bodies. Swimming enables access to isolated food sources and escape from predators. Juveniles practice swimming under supervision. Waterproof feathers reduce drag and aid movement. Water crossings can cover hundreds of meters. Swimming also helps thermoregulation. This behavior is rare among large flightless birds. Adaptation allows movement across fragmented rainforest patches.

Why This Matters

Swimming expands feeding and territory options in complex habitats.

It supports gene flow and ecological connectivity across fragmented forests.

Did You Know?

Did you know cassowaries can swim across rivers, making them capable of long-distance travel in wet rainforest areas.
