Cassowaries Are Key to Maintaining Biodiversity

Ecosystem engineers. Plant protectors. Forest caretakers.

By dispersing seeds of over 100 plant species, cassowaries maintain tropical rainforest biodiversity. Their foraging and movement patterns ensure genetic variation and colonization of new areas. Loss of cassowaries can cause cascading ecological effects, affecting both flora and fauna. Juvenile learning ensures continuity of these ecological services. Seed dispersal helps prevent plant overcrowding and competition. Cassowaries’ selective feeding also favors certain species. Human encroachment threatens these ecological roles.

Why This Matters

Cassowaries are indispensable for rainforest ecosystem health and resilience.

Their presence supports forest regeneration, benefiting countless species. This underscores the link between animal behavior and ecosystem functionality.

Did You Know?

Did you know the decline of cassowaries in some regions has led to reduced regeneration of certain large-fruited trees.
