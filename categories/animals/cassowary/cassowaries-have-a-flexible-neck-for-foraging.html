Cassowaries Have a Flexible Neck for Foraging

Neck acrobatics. Fruit reachers. Jungle contortionists.

Cassowaries have long, flexible necks allowing access to fruit, leaves, and small prey at different heights. Muscular, prehensile movements support selective feeding. Neck flexibility aids scanning for predators. Juveniles practice feeding with awkward maneuvers before mastering efficiency. Neck mobility also helps during displays or combat. Combined with powerful legs, neck flexibility enhances survival. It supports ecological roles by accessing a variety of plant resources. Flexibility allows for subtle interaction with complex rainforest vegetation.

Why This Matters

Flexible necks improve foraging efficiency and predator vigilance.

It contributes to diet diversity and supports ecological niche as keystone frugivores.

Did You Know?

Did you know cassowaries can bend their necks in surprising ways to reach fruits high and low.
