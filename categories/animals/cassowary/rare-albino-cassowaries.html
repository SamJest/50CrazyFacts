Rare Albino Cassowaries

The ghostly giants of the forest.

💥 Impact

This rare mutation highlights genetics, survival challenges, and folklore.

It also underscores the remarkable variability within a single species.

🤯 Did You Know?

Albino cassowaries are extremely rare, with unique coloration due to genetic mutation.

Albino cassowaries are extraordinarily rare, with white plumage, pink skin, and pale eyes due to a genetic mutation affecting melanin production. Reports exist in Papua New Guinea and northern Australia, though sightings are sporadic. Albino individuals face higher predation risk, social challenges, and sensitivity to UV light. Historical records suggest that albino birds were often regarded with awe and superstition, sometimes incorporated into local myths. Genetic studies indicate that albinism can appear in small isolated populations due to recessive gene expression. Despite rarity, albino cassowaries survive if they avoid conflict and predation. Their existence challenges assumptions about camouflage and survival in dense rainforests. Observing an albino cassowary is witnessing a living anomaly, highlighting genetic diversity’s role in evolution.

Source

Journal of Avian Genetics
