Tarsiers Have Tiny Fingers With Large Pads

Fine manipulators.

Tarsiers’ fingers are slender with expanded pads for gripping branches and prey. Finger pads increase surface area for tactile feedback, improving grasping precision. This allows manipulation of insects, leaves, and perches. Finger adaptations support climbing, leaping, and hunting. Pads also cushion impacts during rapid movements. Such fine motor skills are rare among small nocturnal primates. Dexterous fingers enhance feeding efficiency and mobility. Tarsiers’ fingers combine strength, sensitivity, and flexibility for survival.

Why This Matters

Finger adaptations improve dexterity and grasping.

They enable precise prey capture and safe arboreal navigation.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers have slender fingers with enlarged pads for gripping and manipulation.
