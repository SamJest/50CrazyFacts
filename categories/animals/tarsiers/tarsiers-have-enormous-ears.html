Tarsiers Have Enormous Ears

Acute hearing.

Tarsiers’ large, mobile ears allow precise detection of prey movements at night. Ears rotate independently, localizing insects in dense foliage. Sensitive hearing complements their visual acuity and ultrasound communication. Ear size and mobility increase hunting success while reducing exposure to predators. It’s an evolutionary adaptation for nocturnal life in complex environments. Tarsiers rely on auditory cues as much as visual ones. This combination of senses allows them to be stealthy and precise predators.

Why This Matters

Enhanced hearing improves nocturnal hunting.

It supports predator avoidance and communication.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers’ ears are extremely large and mobile, providing acute nocturnal hearing.
