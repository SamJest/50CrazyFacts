Tarsiers Can Detect Motion From Several Meters Away

Acute perception.

Tarsiers’ visual and auditory systems allow detection of tiny insect movements from meters away. Their combination of eye size, ultrasonic hearing, and head rotation enables precise targeting. Motion detection is essential for nocturnal hunting. It reduces wasted energy and increases capture success. This skill supports survival in dense forests with limited visibility. Detecting motion also helps avoid predators. Such acute senses make tarsiers formidable nocturnal hunters despite their small size.

Why This Matters

Detecting motion increases hunting success.

It improves predator awareness and survival.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers can detect minute movements of prey from several meters away.
