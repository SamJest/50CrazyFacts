Tarsiers Communicate With Ultrasonic Calls

Secret conversations.

Tarsiers produce ultrasonic vocalizations above the range of human hearing to communicate. These calls help maintain social bonds, establish territory, and warn of predators without alerting larger animals. Ultrasonic communication reduces detection risk and enables discreet interactions. It also allows parents to coordinate with offspring silently. Researchers study these calls to understand tarsier social structures. The ability highlights sophisticated adaptations for nocturnal life. Tarsiers’ ultrasonic calls show complex, hidden communication strategies.

Why This Matters

Ultrasonic calls enhance survival and social cohesion.

They reduce predation risk while facilitating communication.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers communicate using ultrasonic vocalizations above human hearing range.
