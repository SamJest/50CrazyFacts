Tarsiers Rarely Drink Water

Water from food.

Tarsiers obtain most of their hydration from the fluids in insects and small prey. They rarely need to drink directly from water sources. This minimizes exposure to predators while hunting at night. Efficient water metabolism is vital for small mammals living in tropical forests with fluctuating water availability. Hydration from prey ensures energy conservation and safety. They occasionally drink water when necessary but rely primarily on diet. This strategy reflects adaptation to nocturnal, insectivorous life.

Why This Matters

Water from food reduces predation risk.

It supports survival in variable forest environments.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers rarely drink water directly, getting most hydration from their prey.
