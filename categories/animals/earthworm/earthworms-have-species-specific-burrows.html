Earthworms Have Species-Specific Burrows

Not random digging. Blueprints differ. Purpose matters.

Each earthworm species builds distinct burrow patterns. Some dig vertical shafts. Others create horizontal networks. Depth varies by climate. Feeding style influences structure. Burrows reflect ecological roles. Scientists identify species by tunnels. Soil architecture changes accordingly.

Why This Matters

Burrow diversity boosts soil complexity.

It supports multiple organisms underground.

Did You Know?

Did you know burrows can identify worm species.
