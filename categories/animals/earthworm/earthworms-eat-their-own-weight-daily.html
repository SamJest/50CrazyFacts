Earthworms Eat Their Own Weight Daily

Soil is dinner. Constantly. Digestion never stops.

Earthworms consume soil mixed with organic matter. They can eat nearly their body weight each day. Microorganisms in the soil are the real nutrition. The rest is processed and expelled as castings. This continuous feeding improves soil structure. It also redistributes nutrients. Earthworms are living compost machines.

Why This Matters

Soil fertility depends on their appetite.

Agriculture benefits enormously from this behavior.

Did You Know?

Did you know earthworm castings are richer than surrounding soil.
