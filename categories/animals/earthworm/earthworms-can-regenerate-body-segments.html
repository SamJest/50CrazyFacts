Earthworms Can Regenerate Body Segments

Cut in half and still alive. Not forever, but long enough to amaze. It feels like science fiction.

Earthworms can regenerate lost segments depending on species and injury location. The head end is more likely to regrow than the tail. Regeneration requires energy and favorable conditions. This ability evolved as a defense against predators. Not all earthworms regenerate equally. Myths exaggerate full regeneration into two worms. Still, partial regrowth is impressive biology.

Why This Matters

Regeneration improves survival after attacks.

It inspires medical research into tissue regrowth.

Did You Know?

Did you know only certain earthworm species can regrow segments.
