Earthworms Have Five Hearts

They pump blood in a very strange way. The system looks simple but works incredibly well. It has shocked scientists for centuries.

Earthworms technically have five pairs of aortic arches that act like hearts. These structures pump blood throughout the worm’s long body. Instead of one central heart, circulation is distributed. This setup helps move oxygen efficiently without lungs. It also allows survival if one section is damaged. The design is ancient and extremely reliable. Scientists still study it to understand decentralized circulation.

Why This Matters

Having multiple hearts increases survival chances.

It shows evolution can favor redundancy over complexity.

Did You Know?

Did you know earthworms have ten heart-like structures instead of one.
