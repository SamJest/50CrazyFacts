Earthworms Can Alter Soil pH

Acid shifts happen. Worms interfere. Balance changes.

Earthworms influence soil pH through digestion. Castings often neutralize acidity. Calcium carbonate plays a role. Soil chemistry stabilizes. Plants benefit from balanced pH. Effects vary by species. Long-term changes accumulate. Worms reshape chemical landscapes.

Why This Matters

Soil pH affects nutrient uptake.

Worms indirectly control plant health.

Did You Know?

Did you know earthworm castings can raise soil pH.
