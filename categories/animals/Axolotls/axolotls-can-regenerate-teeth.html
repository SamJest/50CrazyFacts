Axolotls Can Regenerate Teeth

Even their teeth aren’t off-limits when it comes to regeneration.

If an axolotl loses a tooth, it can grow it back fully functional. The regeneration involves dental stem cells and precise tissue coordination. Teeth regrow multiple times throughout their life, which is unusual among vertebrates. This ability ensures that feeding efficiency is never compromised, critical for survival in the wild. In labs, it allows long-term studies without dental health impacting research outcomes. Their teeth are small and conical, perfect for gripping worms and tiny fish. Tooth regeneration complements their jaw and limb regenerative abilities, forming a remarkable systemic capability.

Why This Matters

It shows that regeneration in axolotls is comprehensive, covering multiple organ systems.

It offers potential insights into tooth regeneration therapies for humans with dental issues.

Did You Know?

Teeth can regrow dozens of times during an axolotl’s lifespan.
