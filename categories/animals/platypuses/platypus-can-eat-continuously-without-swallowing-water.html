Platypus Can Eat Continuously Without Swallowing Water

They forage efficiently in turbulent streams.

💥 Impact

Water-excluding feeding maximizes foraging efficiency.

It demonstrates natural problem-solving in aquatic mammalian evolution.

🤯 Did You Know?

Platypuses can store prey in cheek pouches and expel water while eating underwater.

Platypuses close eyes, ears, and nostrils underwater, yet feed continuously using specialized cheek pouches. Pouches collect prey while water is expelled, preventing ingestion of excess water. They manipulate food mechanically with their tongue and bill before swallowing. Juveniles learn the technique under maternal supervision. This allows uninterrupted foraging even in fast-flowing streams. The adaptation reduces energy loss from repeated surfacing. Evolution optimized feeding mechanics for efficiency and survival. Cheek pouch size correlates with body condition and prey type. Field observations show remarkable efficiency: hundreds of prey items per dive without water ingestion. This feeding strategy exemplifies behavioral and anatomical integration.

Source

Australian Journal of Zoology
