Dingoes Can Adapt to Urban Edges

City survivors.

Dingoes increasingly inhabit areas near cities and towns. They adjust activity to avoid humans. Food sources include rodents and discarded waste. Urban-edge dingoes show altered movement patterns. Adaptability helps them survive habitat loss. However, it increases conflict risks. Understanding this behavior is vital for coexistence. Urban ecology studies often include dingoes.

Why This Matters

Urban adaptation shows behavioral flexibility.

It highlights the need for responsible waste management.

Did You Know?

Did you know dingoes live near some Australian cities.
