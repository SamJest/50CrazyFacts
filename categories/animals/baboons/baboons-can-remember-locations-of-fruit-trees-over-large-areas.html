Baboons Can Remember Locations of Fruit Trees Over Large Areas

They are natural cartographers.

Baboons remember the location, seasonality, and productivity of fruit trees. Troops travel efficiently between these trees to maximize caloric intake. Knowledge is passed down through observation and experience. Memory reduces wasted energy and increases foraging success. Seasonal tracking requires spatial awareness and environmental knowledge. Such capabilities illustrate advanced cognition in primates.

Why This Matters

Shows advanced spatial memory and planning.

It emphasizes the role of memory in survival and resource management.

Did You Know?

They can remember the exact location of productive fruit trees for months or even years.

Source

Madagascar Biodiversity Center (mbc.mg)
