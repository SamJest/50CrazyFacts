Crows Solve Puzzles Like Apes

Not kidding—smarter than expected.

💥 Impact

Problem-solving demonstrates intelligence comparable to primates.

It also emphasizes innovation, planning, and causal reasoning in birds.

🤯 Did You Know?

Crows can solve complex multi-step puzzles similar to chimpanzees.

In controlled experiments, crows have solved multi-step puzzles involving levers, pulleys, and water displacement to access food. Some crows understood the Aesop’s Fable test, using water to raise a floating treat, indicating causal reasoning. Juveniles learned from watching adults, suggesting observational learning and problem-solving culture. Their performance rivals that of chimpanzees on similar tasks, despite vastly different brain structures. These feats involve memory, planning, and adaptability—key hallmarks of intelligence. Observing their methodical approach challenges assumptions that large brains are necessary for complex cognition. Tool selection, sequence planning, and insight learning are all documented behaviors. This demonstrates evolution can produce intelligence in divergent lineages. Crows turn experimental setups into testing grounds, highlighting ingenuity and innovation.
