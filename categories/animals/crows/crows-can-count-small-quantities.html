Crows Can Count Small Quantities

Numerical wits.

Crows can recognize small numbers and distinguish between quantities of objects. This skill helps in foraging, deciding which food source is richer, and assessing group size. Experiments show crows can perform simple arithmetic and understand “more” versus “less.” Young crows learn counting-like behavior through observation. Numerical ability demonstrates advanced cognitive processing.

Why This Matters

Crows’ counting skills highlight intelligence in decision-making.

It emphasizes mathematical awareness in natural problem-solving.

Did You Know?

They can choose a tree with more nuts over a tree with fewer.

Source

Natural History Museum (nhm.ac.uk)
