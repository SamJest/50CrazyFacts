Crows Can Recognize Other Animals’ Calls

Observant to a T.

Crows listen to calls of other species, such as hawks or humans, and respond appropriately. Recognizing predator calls allows them to evade danger. It also informs social coordination and foraging behavior. Juveniles learn these cues from adults. Understanding heterospecific communication demonstrates flexible intelligence and environmental awareness.

Why This Matters

Crows’ ability to recognize other species’ calls improves survival.

It emphasizes adaptive learning beyond their own species’ communication.

Did You Know?

They respond to hawk cries by taking cover or calling alerts to their flock.
