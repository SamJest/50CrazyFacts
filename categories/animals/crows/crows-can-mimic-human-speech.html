Crows Can Mimic Human Speech

Feathered imitators.

Crows in captivity can learn to mimic human words or sounds. This requires listening, memory, and vocal flexibility. Mimicry can occur naturally in wild populations as they imitate urban noises. It helps crows communicate and adapt to new environments. Juveniles learn through observation and practice. Vocal mimicry shows advanced intelligence and learning capability.

Why This Matters

Crows’ speech mimicry reflects learning capacity and creativity.

It highlights their ability to adapt communication strategies to surroundings.

Did You Know?

They have been recorded saying simple words like “hello” or “bye-bye.”

Source

Natural History Museum (nhm.ac.uk)
