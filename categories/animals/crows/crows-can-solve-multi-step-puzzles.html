Crows Can Solve Multi-Step Puzzles

Problem-solving is their forte.

Experiments show crows completing multi-step tasks like pulling strings, using multiple tools, or manipulating objects in sequence to get rewards. These puzzles test memory, reasoning, and innovation. Crows often approach tasks creatively, sometimes using trial-and-error or observation of peers. Problem-solving is crucial in dynamic environments with human interference and predation.

Why This Matters

Crows’ problem-solving illustrates advanced reasoning beyond instinctual behavior.

It highlights intelligence comparable to primates and dolphins.

Did You Know?

They can figure out how to retrieve food by completing a three-step tool sequence.

Scientific American (scientificamerican.com)
