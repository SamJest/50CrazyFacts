Crows Can Engage in Cooperative Hunting

Teamwork in action.

Some crows coordinate with other crows to flush out prey, such as insects or small vertebrates. By working together, they increase hunting efficiency and share knowledge of food sources. Cooperation may involve signaling, positioning, and waiting for the right moment. Young crows learn strategies from observing adults. This demonstrates social intelligence and planning.

Why This Matters

Crows’ cooperative hunting highlights strategic thinking and group coordination.

It emphasizes social learning and survival advantages.

Did You Know?

They sometimes trap insects between leaves or use diversion tactics together.
