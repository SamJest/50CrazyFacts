Fennec Foxes Can Live in Captivity for Over 10 Years

Long-lived desert foxes.

While wild lifespans are shorter due to predation and harsh conditions, fennec foxes can live 10–14 years in captivity with proper care. Extended lifespan allows observation of complex social behaviors and learning. Juveniles raised in captivity develop hunting and social skills through interaction.

Why This Matters

Longevity in captivity provides insight into behavior and development.

It emphasizes the impact of environment on lifespan and well-being.

Did You Know?

Captive fennec foxes may outlive many other small desert mammals.

Source

Wildlife Conservation Society (wcs.org)
