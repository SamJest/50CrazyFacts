Fennec Foxes Can Swim Occasionally

Unexpected desert swimmers.

Though primarily desert dwellers, fennec foxes can swim if necessary to cross small streams or escape danger. They paddle with all four limbs and maintain buoyancy. Swimming is rare but demonstrates adaptability. Juveniles learn instinctive swimming through play and observation.

Why This Matters

Swimming ability shows flexibility in unexpected situations.

It highlights survival versatility beyond typical habitat use.

Did You Know?

They can navigate shallow water when required, despite being desert animals.

Source

Wildlife Conservation Society (wcs.org)
