Narwhals Use Echolocation to Hunt in Total Darkness

Arctic sonar specialists.

Narwhals produce clicks, whistles, and pulsed sounds to navigate and locate prey in dark, ice-covered waters. Echolocation helps them detect obstacles, prey movement, and other pod members.

Why This Matters

This adaptation allows hunting in conditions where sunlight never penetrates, giving narwhals access to deep-water prey. Echolocation is essential for pod coordination and survival.

It demonstrates the evolution of sophisticated sensory systems for extreme environments. Echolocation precision is critical for avoiding predators and obstacles.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can identify prey location down to a few centimeters using echolocation.
