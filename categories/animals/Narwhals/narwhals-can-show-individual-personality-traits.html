Narwhals Can Show Individual Personality Traits

Unique underwater personalities.

Research suggests narwhals exhibit distinct behaviors, preferences, and interaction styles, indicating individual personality.

Why This Matters

Recognizing personalities helps pods maintain social balance. It affects mating, foraging, and hierarchical interactions.

Understanding personality aids in behavioral ecology and conservation strategies.

Did You Know?

Some individuals are bolder or more exploratory than others.

Source

Natural History Museum (nhm.ac.uk)
