Narwhals Can Survive in Low-Oxygen Conditions

Deep-sea endurance.

Narwhals’ blood and muscle tissues store high levels of oxygen, allowing long dives in oxygen-poor deep waters.

Why This Matters

This adaptation enables them to hunt prey at extreme depths and avoid surface predators. It demonstrates specialized physiology for extreme marine life.

Low-oxygen tolerance supports Arctic ecosystem diversity and resilience.

Did You Know?

Narwhals’ heart rate slows significantly during deep dives to conserve oxygen.
