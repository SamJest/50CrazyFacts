Ferrets Can Run 8 Miles an Hour

Ferrets dash furiously. Play becomes a sprinting sport. Energy explodes.

Ferrets are surprisingly fast, reaching speeds up to 8 mph. Their long, muscular bodies enable sudden bursts. Wild ancestors needed this to catch prey. Domesticated ferrets retain this energy. Owners often see short zoomies around homes. Speed can surprise new pet owners. It reflects natural hunting instincts.

Why This Matters

Ferret speed demonstrates the importance of exercise.

High energy makes enrichment essential for well-being.

Did You Know?

Did you know ferrets can outrun some small dog breeds over short distances.
