Ferrets Have a Third Eyelid

Protection unseen. Vision shielded. Blinking hidden.

Ferrets possess a nictitating membrane, or third eyelid. It protects eyes from dust and debris. Movement occurs silently across the eye. It helps when burrowing. Wild ferrets rely on it in tunnels. Domesticated ferrets retain it. Minimal visibility makes it easy to miss. Health checks ensure it functions.

Why This Matters

The third eyelid supports eye safety.

It highlights unique mustelid anatomy.

Did You Know?

Did you know this membrane can be seen when ferrets are very sleepy.
