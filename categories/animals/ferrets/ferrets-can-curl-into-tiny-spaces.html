Ferrets Can Curl Into Tiny Spaces

Flexible frames. Burrow experts. Contortionists emerge.

Ferrets are extremely flexible due to loose spine and lack of rigid collarbones. They can squeeze into tubes, tunnels, and tight spaces. Wild ancestors used burrows to escape predators. Domesticated ferrets enjoy tunnels in play. Safety is important to avoid getting stuck. Flexibility aids exploration and hunting behaviors. Owners create enrichment with tubes.

Why This Matters

It demonstrates skeletal and muscular adaptation.

Ferret flexibility informs cage design and enrichment strategies.

Did You Know?

Did you know ferrets can fit through gaps as small as 1.5 inches in diameter.
