Ferrets Sleep Like Professional Nappers

Ferrets vanish for hours. Then wake suddenly. Energy resets fast.

Ferrets sleep up to 18 hours a day. Their bodies switch rapidly between deep sleep and intense activity. This pattern mirrors wild polecats. High metabolism demands frequent rest. Short bursts of energy dominate their lifestyle. They enter REM sleep quickly. Twitching paws are common. Sleep supports brain development.

Why This Matters

Sleep explains ferret behavior patterns.

It helps owners understand sudden energy bursts.

Did You Know?

Did you know ferrets can fall asleep mid-play.
