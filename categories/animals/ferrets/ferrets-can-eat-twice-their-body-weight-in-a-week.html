Ferrets Can Eat Twice Their Body Weight in a Week

Food disappears. Metabolism roars. Ferrets refuel constantly.

Ferrets have fast metabolisms, needing high protein intake. A ferret may consume the equivalent of 20–25% of its body weight in food weekly. Wild diet consists of small mammals. Domesticated ferrets require balanced, carnivorous food. Overfeeding risks obesity; underfeeding risks malnutrition. Meal frequency impacts energy levels. Food variety maintains health.

Understanding metabolism prevents health issues.
