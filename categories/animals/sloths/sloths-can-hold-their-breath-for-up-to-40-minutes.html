Sloths Can Hold Their Breath for Up to 40 Minutes

Underwater champions.

Sloths can slow their heart rate and hold their breath for up to 40 minutes underwater. This ability helps them cross rivers and evade predators without surfacing. They achieve this by reducing oxygen consumption and blood flow to non-essential organs. Water also provides buoyancy, making swimming easier despite their body structure. Juveniles quickly learn to swim alongside their mothers. This unusual skill contrasts sharply with their slow terrestrial movement. It showcases an unexpected aquatic aptitude for a tree-dweller.

Why This Matters

Underwater endurance increases mobility and safety.

It allows sloths to reach new feeding grounds and escape threats.

Did You Know?

Sloths can hold their breath for almost as long as some trained human free divers.
