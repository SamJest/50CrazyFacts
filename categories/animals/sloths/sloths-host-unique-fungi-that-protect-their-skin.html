Sloths Host Unique Fungi That Protect Their Skin

Micro-defense system.

Certain fungi growing on sloth fur provide antibacterial and antifungal properties. These microorganisms protect the skin from infections caused by rainforest humidity. The fungi also contribute to the greenish hue of fur for camouflage. This micro-ecosystem benefits sloths and adds biodiversity to the rainforest. Fungal communities vary between individuals and locations. Protective fungi reduce energy spent on immune responses. They exemplify how sloths exploit symbiotic relationships for survival.

Why This Matters

Protective fungi enhance survival without energy cost.

It represents a unique biological defense strategy.

Did You Know?

Fungi on sloth fur help shield them from infections while improving camouflage.
