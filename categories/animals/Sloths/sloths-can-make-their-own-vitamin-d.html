Sloths Can Make Their Own Vitamin D

Unique metabolism.

Sloths produce vitamin D through skin exposure and minimal sunbathing. Low mobility reduces typical sunlight intake, so slow production is vital. This supplement supports bone health and immune function. Sunbathing is usually done hanging in the canopy. Vitamin D synthesis complements their low-energy diet, which provides few nutrients. This adaptation is crucial for long-term survival. Without it, skeletal and immune issues would arise. It’s a rare metabolic strategy among mammals.

Why This Matters

Self-produced vitamin D supports health.

It compensates for limited diet and sunlight.

Did You Know?

Sloths can synthesize vitamin D through limited sun exposure to support bone and immune health.
