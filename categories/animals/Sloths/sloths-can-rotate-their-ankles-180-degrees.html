Sloths Can Rotate Their Ankles 180 Degrees

Hanging flexibility.

Sloths’ specialized ankle joints allow 180-degree rotation. This enables them to hang securely upside down while reaching for branches. Flexibility is combined with curved claws for optimal grip. It allows sloths to adjust posture without expending energy. Joint structure reduces strain during long hangs. Evolution has tailored their skeleton for suspended living. This adaptation is rare among mammals, showcasing sloths’ unique arboreal abilities.

Why This Matters

It maximizes canopy navigation and reduces energy use.

It prevents injury while hanging for extended periods.

Did You Know?

Sloths’ ankle joints allow extreme rotation to maintain grip while hanging upside down.
