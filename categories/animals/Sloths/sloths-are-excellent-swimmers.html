Sloths Are Excellent Swimmers

Canopy to water specialists.

Sloths are surprisingly good swimmers, using a doggy-paddle motion with all four limbs. They can cross rivers or flooded areas quickly despite their slow land movement. Water buoyancy reduces energy expenditure, making swimming easier than climbing. They also use their tails for stabilization. This ability expands their habitat range and access to food. Swimming can help avoid land predators that cannot navigate water. Evolutionary adaptation favors versatility despite an arboreal lifestyle. Being able to swim ensures survival in rainforests prone to flooding.

Why This Matters

Swimming increases habitat flexibility.

It provides predator avoidance and access to distant resources.

Did You Know?

Sloths can swim up to three times faster than they move on land.
