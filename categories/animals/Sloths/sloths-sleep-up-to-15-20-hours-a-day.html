Sloths Sleep Up to 15–20 Hours a Day

Nap experts.

Sloths are among the sleepiest mammals, resting 15–20 hours per day. They conserve energy due to a low-calorie leaf diet. Sleep occurs mostly hanging upside down in trees, which offers safety from predators. Despite long sleep periods, sloths are alert to disturbances. They alternate between light and deep sleep cycles. Sleep behavior varies slightly between species, with some being slightly more active during the day. This extreme sleep pattern supports their slow metabolism and energy conservation.

Why This Matters

Extended sleep helps survive on low-nutrition food.

It reduces energy expenditure and improves overall health.

Did You Know?

Sloths can spend nearly an entire day sleeping, making them masters of rest.
