Sloths Are Hosts to Unique Bacteria in Their Stomachs

Leaf digestion partners.

Sloths’ multi-chambered stomachs host specialized bacteria to ferment cellulose. These microbes break down tough leaves that other animals cannot digest. Bacterial diversity is crucial for nutrient extraction and energy conservation. Each sloth has a slightly different microbial community based on diet and habitat. This symbiosis enhances survival on a low-nutrient diet. It also reduces the energy cost of digestion. The bacterial ecosystem is a key component of sloth physiology.

Why This Matters

Specialized bacteria maximize nutrient extraction.

It allows survival on low-energy foliage.

Did You Know?

Sloths rely on unique stomach bacteria to digest fibrous leaves efficiently.
