Pangolins Have Extraordinary Sense of Smell

Nose knows.

Pangolins rely heavily on olfaction. Their long snouts detect ant and termite nests underground. Smell guides nocturnal foraging. They can locate nests several meters away without visual cues. Sensitive noses also detect predators. Olfactory input dominates sensory navigation. It compensates for poor eyesight.

Why This Matters

Sense of smell is critical for survival. Efficient foraging reduces energy expenditure.

It shows sensory specialization can shape lifestyle and behavior. Smell can substitute for vision entirely.

Did You Know?

Pangolins can detect prey buried 20–30 cm below the soil.
