Pangolins Are Ancient Mammals

Living fossils.

Pangolins belong to the order Pholidota, with fossils dating back 60 million years. Their body plan has changed little over millennia. This long evolutionary history shows stability in design. Pangolins have survived mass extinctions and shifting ecosystems. Their survival indicates evolutionary success. They are one of the few mammals relying on keratin scales. Evolution seems to have perfected their niche.

Why This Matters

It emphasizes their ecological importance and resilience. Ancient traits have been preserved due to effectiveness.

It shows some evolutionary paths prioritize protection and specialization over novelty.

Did You Know?

Pangolins have changed so little that ancient fossils are recognizable as their modern counterparts.
