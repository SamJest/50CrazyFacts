Crows Can Remember Where They Hide Food

Forgetful? Not a crow.

Crows are expert hoarders, hiding nuts and other food in hundreds of locations. They remember each hiding spot for months, sometimes even relocating caches when competitors are near. Spatial memory is supported by hippocampus size and environmental awareness. Young crows learn from adults about optimal hiding and retrieval strategies. This memory ensures survival during scarce periods.

Why This Matters

Crows’ impressive memory is crucial for survival and resource management.

It shows advanced spatial cognition and learning.

Did You Know?

They can retrieve nuts hidden months earlier, even under snow or leaves.
