Crows Can Remember Multiple Locations Simultaneously

Master multi-taskers.

Crows keep track of dozens of food caches and hiding spots at the same time. They recall not only location but also type of food and potential competitors nearby. This spatial memory allows for efficient resource management. Juveniles learn location tracking from adult guidance. Multi-location memory demonstrates intelligence and planning.

Why This Matters

Crows’ ability to remember multiple sites shows advanced cognitive mapping.

It highlights sophisticated memory systems in problem-solving and survival.

Did You Know?

They may have over a hundred food caches in a single season and recall them accurately.

Natural History Museum (nhm.ac.uk)
