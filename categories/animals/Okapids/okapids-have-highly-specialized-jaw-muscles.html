Okapids Have Highly Specialized Jaw Muscles

Powerful precision.

Their jaw muscles allow strong but controlled chewing. This helps process tough forest vegetation. Leaves and bark require endurance chewing. Efficient jaws prevent fatigue. This allows longer feeding sessions. Muscle design matches diet.

Why This Matters

This efficiency supports energy balance. Eating well means surviving longer.

It shows how anatomy adapts to food type. Diet drives muscle evolution.

Did You Know?

Okapids can chew for hours without strain.
