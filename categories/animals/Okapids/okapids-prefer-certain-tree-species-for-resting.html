Okapids Prefer Certain Tree Species for Resting

Selective resting.

Okapids choose specific trees to rest near. These trees offer better cover. Leaf density matters. Some trees block scent dispersal. Resting sites are chosen carefully. Safety guides comfort.

Why This Matters

This reduces detection while resting. Sleep remains protected.

It shows even rest is strategic. Safety never switches off.
