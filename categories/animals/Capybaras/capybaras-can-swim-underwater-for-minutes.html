Capybaras Can Swim Underwater for Minutes

They’re stealthy escape artists.

Capybaras can dive and swim underwater for up to five minutes. This skill allows them to evade predators, forage for aquatic plants, and move between habitats safely. They close their nostrils and ears to prevent water entry. Young learn diving techniques from adults through observation. Extended underwater swimming is essential in floodplain habitats with high predator density. It also allows them to navigate waterways that other herbivores cannot.

Why This Matters

Shows adaptation to semi-aquatic life.

It highlights the survival benefits of underwater mobility.

Did You Know?

They can completely submerge and silently move beneath the water surface.
