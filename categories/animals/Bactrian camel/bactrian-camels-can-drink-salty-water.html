Bactrian Camels Can Drink Salty Water

Saline survival. Salt tolerance. Desert advantage.

Bactrian camels can drink water saltier than seawater without ill effects, thanks to specialized kidneys and tolerance mechanisms.

Why This Matters

This allows survival in salt flats and saline desert springs.

It also provides insights into renal adaptations for extreme osmotic stress.

Did You Know?

Did you know wild Bactrian camels in the Gobi Desert routinely drink water with high salinity that would harm most mammals.
