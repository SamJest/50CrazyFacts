Bactrian Camels Have Triple Eyelids

Sand defense. Vision protection. Ocular adaptation.

Bactrian camels have three eyelids and long eyelashes to protect eyes from sandstorms and harsh sunlight. The middle lid acts as a sifter for fine particles.

Why This Matters

This adaptation preserves eyesight in extreme desert conditions.

It also inspires protective eyewear designs for humans and robotics.

Did You Know?

Did you know the middle eyelid of camels is translucent, allowing them to see even when filtering sand.
