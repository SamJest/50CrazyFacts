Why Aardvarks Rarely Became Domesticated

It was tried. It failed. Repeatedly. People stopped.

Historical attempts to keep aardvarks near settlements consistently failed. Studies on failed aardvark domestication attempts cite diet and nocturnality. A secondary semantic variant involves limits of animal domestication. They refused altered schedules. Stress levels rose. Humans care because not all animals are moldable. Domestication has boundaries.

Why This Matters

This matters for understanding human history. Agriculture depended on cooperation. Aardvarks declined the role.

The insight clarifies domestication theory. Temperament matters as much as utility. Nature resists control.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks stop feeding under confinement stress.

Source

Journal of Anthropological Science
