Aardvarks lose weight faster inside small fenced reserves.

Fenced reserves restrict aardvark movement between termite patches. Studies on aardvark survival in fenced conservation areas show reduced feeding success. A secondary semantic variant involves unintended impacts of wildlife fencing. Unlike grazers, aardvarks need scattered food. Barriers increase starvation risk.
