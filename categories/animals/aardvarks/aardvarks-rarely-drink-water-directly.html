Aardvarks Rarely Drink Water Directly

Despite living in dry regions, aardvarks don’t rely on waterholes. Their hydration comes from an unexpected source.

Food provides most of an aardvark’s water needs. Ants and termites contain enough moisture to sustain them. As a result, aardvarks may go long periods without drinking. This reduces their need to visit dangerous waterholes. It’s a crucial survival adaptation in arid environments.

Why This Matters

It matters because waterholes are high-risk zones with predators. Avoiding them increases survival chances.

It also shows how diet influences physiology. Food choice shapes water dependence.

Did You Know?

In some regions, aardvarks have been recorded surviving months without drinking free-standing water. Their kidneys are adapted for water conservation.
