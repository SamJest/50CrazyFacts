Why Aardvarks Are Rarely Seen on Camera Traps

They exist everywhere. Cameras miss them often. Timing is cruel.

Camera trap studies show aardvarks are under-recorded relative to abundance. Analysis of why aardvarks evade camera traps at night highlights movement speed and angle. A secondary semantic variant involves detection bias in wildlife monitoring. Their low profile avoids sensors. Burrow exits bypass lenses. Humans care because data can lie. Absence is misleading.

Why This Matters

This matters for conservation statistics. Population estimates may be wrong. Protection plans depend on accuracy.
