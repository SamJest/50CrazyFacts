Aardvarks Can Survive Minor Predator Attacks and Escape

Aardvarks aren’t helpless against predators. Toughness matters. Survival doesn’t always mean avoiding contact.

Aardvarks have been documented surviving attacks from large predators. Thick skin, quick digging, and persistence allow escape. Some bear scars and healed wounds. Survival after attack is not uncommon. Resilience is part of their strategy.

Why This Matters

It matters because survival isn’t binary. Escaping injured still counts.

This resilience helps maintain populations despite predation pressure. Toughness complements avoidance.

Did You Know?

Healed claw and bite marks have been observed on many adult aardvarks. Scars tell survival stories.

Source

Kruger National Park (sanparks.org)
