Why Some Aardvarks Live Longer Than Expected

They avoid danger quietly. Longevity follows. Numbers surprised scientists.
💥 Impact
›

This matters for life-history theory. It challenges assumptions about aging speed. Small mammals can live long.

The insight informs captive care and conservation. Longevity changes population math. Protection has long-term payoff.

🤯 Did You Know?
›

Captive aardvarks have exceeded thirty years.

Aardvarks can live over twenty years in the wild, unusual for insectivores. Longevity studies of wild aardvarks and survival patterns link this to low predation. A secondary semantic variant involves lifespan extension through behavioral avoidance. Solitary habits reduce conflict. Deep burrows offer refuge. Slow life history pays off. Humans care because lifespan is not size-bound. Behavior matters.
