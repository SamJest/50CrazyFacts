An aardvark can dig a human-sized hole in under ten minutes.

Aardvarks dig with remarkable efficiency due to specialized limb mechanics. Research into aardvark digging biomechanics and energy efficiency reveals optimized muscle leverage and claw curvature. Each stroke minimizes backward force. The claws act like hooked chisels that loosen soil cleanly. A secondary semantic variant involves mammalian excavation mechanics in hard ground. This allows continuous digging without overheating. Burrows form faster than expected for the animal’s size. Humans care because efficient movement reduces energy cost.
