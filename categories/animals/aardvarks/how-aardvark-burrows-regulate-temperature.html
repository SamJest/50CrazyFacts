How Aardvark Burrows Regulate Temperature

Outside heat fluctuates wildly. Inside, it stays calm. Physics does the work.
💥 Impact ›

Aardvark burrows demonstrate natural climate control. They show how insulation alone can regulate temperature. This principle applies to sustainable housing.

Architects study similar underground designs. Energy-efficient buildings borrow from burrow physics. Nature already tested the model.

🤯 Did You Know? ›

Burrow temperatures fluctuate less than five degrees across seasons.

Aardvark burrows maintain stable internal temperatures despite harsh surface conditions. Studies on aardvark burrow temperature regulation mechanisms show soil depth buffers extremes. Airflow is minimal, preventing heat exchange. A secondary semantic variant appears in subterranean thermal stability in mammals. This protects aardvarks from heat stress and dehydration. Other animals reuse the burrows for the same reason. The system works passively. Humans care because passive cooling saves energy.
