Fermentation Allows Raw Food Consumption

Fermentation replaces heat. Raw foods become safe. Nutrients remain.

Fermented foods are often uncooked. Pathogens suppressed by acid. Nutrients stay intact. Heat-sensitive vitamins survive. Ancient diets relied on this. Raw preservation succeeded.

Why This Matters

This matters because cooking isn’t always required. Fermentation preserved nutrients. Food safety improved.

The implication is diversifying preparation methods. Raw fermentation offers options. Balance matters.

Did You Know?

Kombucha is never cooked. Live cultures remain. Temperature matters.

[Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]
