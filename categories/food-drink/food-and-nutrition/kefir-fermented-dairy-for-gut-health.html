Kefir: Fermented Dairy for Gut Health

Kefir isn’t just tangy milk—it’s a probiotic powerhouse for digestive wellness.

Kefir is a fermented milk drink containing probiotics, vitamins, minerals, and bioactive peptides. Originating in the Caucasus Mountains, it was traditionally made in goat skins. Modern science supports kefir’s benefits for gut microbiota, immunity, and lactose digestion. Its microbial diversity can also enhance nutrient absorption. Kefir can be consumed plain, in smoothies, or as a cooking ingredient. Functional nutrition values kefir as a natural source of probiotics and bioactive compounds.

Why This Matters

Kefir promotes healthy gut microbiota and digestion.

It supports immune function and enhances nutrient assimilation.

Did You Know?

A single cup contains up to 10 billion probiotics.
