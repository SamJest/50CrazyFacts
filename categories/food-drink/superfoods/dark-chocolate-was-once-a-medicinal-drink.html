Dark Chocolate Was Once a Medicinal Drink

Chocolate was bitter. It was medicinal. Sugar came later. Health came first.

Dark chocolate originated as a ceremonial drink in Mesoamerica. It was believed to boost strength and vitality. Cocoa contains flavanols that improve blood flow. Modern studies link dark chocolate to heart health. The Victorians even prescribed it to patients. Dessert status came much later. The science still holds.

Why This Matters

This matters because pleasure foods can also be functional. Chocolate challenges the idea of guilty indulgence. Moderation changes everything.

The implication is that processing defines health outcomes. Sugar removed the benefits. Purity restores them.

Did You Know?

Dark chocolate must be over 70 percent cocoa for benefits. Milk chocolate does not qualify. Labels matter.
