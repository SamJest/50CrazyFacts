Aspartame: The Low-Calorie Sweetness That Changed Dieting

Aspartame lets us enjoy sweet drinks without sugar calories.

Aspartame is an artificial sweetener discovered in 1965 that’s 200 times sweeter than sugar. Used in diet sodas, sugar-free gums, and low-calorie foods, it provides sweetness without the caloric load. It’s made from two amino acids, aspartic acid and phenylalanine. Approved by multiple health authorities, it allows diabetics and dieters to enjoy sweet flavors safely.

Why This Matters

Reduces sugar consumption and calories in processed foods.

It enables low-calorie and sugar-free product lines for health-conscious consumers.

Did You Know?

Aspartame was accidentally discovered by chemist James Schlatter in 1965.

Source

[U.S. Food and Drug Administration]
