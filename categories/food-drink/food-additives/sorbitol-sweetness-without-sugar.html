Sorbitol: Sweetness Without Sugar

Used in sugar-free gum and candy, sorbitol adds sweetness and moisture.

Sorbitol is a sugar alcohol naturally found in fruits and used as a low-calorie sweetener. It retains moisture, preventing candies and baked goods from drying out. Unlike sugar, it doesn’t spike blood glucose dramatically. Excessive consumption can cause laxative effects, but small amounts are generally safe. Sorbitol revolutionized sugar-free products and diabetic-friendly foods.

Why This Matters

Provides sweetness while reducing sugar content.

It allows food manufacturers to produce diabetic-friendly and low-calorie treats.

Did You Know?

Sorbitol occurs naturally in apples, pears, and prunes.

Source

[U.S. Food and Drug Administration]
