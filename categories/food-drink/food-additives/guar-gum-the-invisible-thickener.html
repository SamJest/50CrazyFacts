Guar Gum: The Invisible Thickener

Guar gum thickens sauces, ice cream, and gluten-free bread without changing taste.

Extracted from guar beans, this polysaccharide swells in water, creating viscosity. It stabilizes emulsions, prevents separation, and improves texture. Used in dairy products, sauces, and bakery items, it’s also popular in gluten-free recipes to mimic the elasticity of gluten. Excessive intake can cause digestive discomfort in some individuals. Guar gum is an example of plant-based, multifunctional food additive that subtly enhances food.

Why This Matters

Allows manufacturers to improve texture without added fat.

Supports gluten-free and low-fat product innovations.

Did You Know?

Guar gum originates from guar beans grown mainly in India.
