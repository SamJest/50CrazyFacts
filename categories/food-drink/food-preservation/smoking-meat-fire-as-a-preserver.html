Smoking Meat: Fire as a Preserver

Smoke isn’t just flavor—it’s defense.

Smoking exposes meat, fish, or cheese to smoke from burning wood, which contains antimicrobial compounds. Hot smoking cooks while preserving, cold smoking primarily preserves and flavors. It’s been used worldwide from Native Americans to Nordic cultures.

Why This Matters

It adds flavor while prolonging shelf life.

Smoking influenced modern charcuterie and barbecue techniques.

Did You Know?

Traditional smoked salmon can remain edible for weeks refrigerated.
