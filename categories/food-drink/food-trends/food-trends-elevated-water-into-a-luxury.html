Food Trends Elevated Water Into a Luxury

Item purity sold.

Premium bottled water became a food trend with claims about mineral content and source. Brands marketed water like fine wine. Consumers paid high prices for hydration.

Why This Matters

The trend highlights branding influence.

It raises sustainability concerns.

Did You Know?

Bottled water sales surpassed soda in the US.
