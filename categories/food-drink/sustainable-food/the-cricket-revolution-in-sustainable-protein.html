The Cricket Revolution in Sustainable Protein

Crickets are tiny, but they pack a protein punch that could save the planet.

Crickets produce up to 80% protein by dry weight and require 12 times less feed than cattle. Farming crickets emits significantly fewer greenhouse gases and uses far less water. Some restaurants are already incorporating cricket flour into snacks and pasta to reduce environmental impact. This trend highlights a quirky yet highly efficient source of sustainable food.

Why This Matters

Using insects like crickets can drastically cut food-related emissions and reduce water consumption.

Cricket protein could become a mainstream source of nutrition, potentially reshaping global food production systems.

Did You Know?

Crickets have been eaten in Mexico for centuries as chapulines, showing ancient sustainable practices.
