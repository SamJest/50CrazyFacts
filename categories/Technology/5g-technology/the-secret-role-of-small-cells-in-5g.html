The Secret Role of Small Cells in 5G

Invisible towers make 5G possible. Small cells are the unsung heroes behind high-speed networks.

Small cells are compact, low-power radio access points that fill coverage gaps for millimeter-wave frequencies. They allow 5G to maintain speed and reliability in dense urban areas or stadiums where signals struggle to penetrate buildings. Deployed on street lamps, poles, or rooftops, small cells complement macro cells and provide seamless connectivity.

Why This Matters

Without small cells, 5G’s high-frequency signals would struggle to reach users.

Small cells increase capacity and reliability, especially in urban and crowded areas.

Did You Know?

Some cities deploy thousands of small cells per square mile to maintain 5G coverage.
