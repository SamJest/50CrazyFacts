5G and Agriculture: Farming Gets Smarter

5G enables precision agriculture with real-time monitoring of crops and livestock.

Sensors and drones collect soil, weather, and crop data, transmitting instantly via 5G. AI predicts irrigation needs, pest threats, and optimal harvest times.

Why This Matters

Yield and resource management improve.

Farmers can make informed decisions instantly, boosting efficiency and sustainability.

Did You Know?

Some 5G agricultural systems have increased crop yield by 15% through optimized watering and pest control.

John Deere (johndeere.com)
