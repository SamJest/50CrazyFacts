How Cybersecurity Defends Against DDoS Attacks

When traffic turns weaponized.

Distributed Denial-of-Service attacks flood servers with traffic, disrupting websites and services. Cybersecurity solutions include traffic filtering, cloud-based mitigation, and rate limiting. Attackers may demand ransom or cause chaos for political purposes. Critical systems can be temporarily disabled. Proper defenses maintain uptime and trust.

Why This Matters

It matters because service continuity is essential in a digital economy.

It also highlights the evolving tactics of cybercriminals.

Did You Know?

Largest DDoS attacks have exceeded 2.3 Tbps.
