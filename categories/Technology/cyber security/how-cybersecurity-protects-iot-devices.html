How Cybersecurity Protects IoT Devices

Your toaster could be hacked.

Internet of Things devices are often minimally secured. Cameras, thermostats, and appliances can be entry points. Cybersecurity ensures encryption, firmware updates, and access controls. Botnets have exploited IoT for massive attacks. Smart homes and cities rely on proper protection. It’s not just convenience; it’s safety.

Why This Matters

It matters because unsecured devices can threaten personal and public security.

It also shows the importance of integrating security at the design stage.

Did You Know?

Over 70% of IoT devices have critical vulnerabilities.
