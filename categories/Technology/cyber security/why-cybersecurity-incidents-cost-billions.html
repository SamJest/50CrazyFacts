Why Cybersecurity Incidents Cost Billions

The price of a breach is staggering.

Data breaches result in lost revenue, fines, recovery costs, and reputation damage. Corporate and government entities face multi-million-dollar consequences. Ransomware, phishing, and insider threats all contribute. Cyber insurance and incident response plans mitigate some losses. Prevention is far cheaper than remediation.

Why This Matters

It matters because financial impacts drive organizations to invest in security.

It also underscores why proactive strategies are essential.

Did You Know?

Average global data breach cost is $4.45 million.
