How Cybersecurity Protects Elections

Democracy depends on digital defense.

Cybersecurity safeguards voting machines, registration databases, and election communication networks. Threats include hacking, misinformation, and voter data theft. Advanced monitoring and incident response prevent interference. Governments invest in secure infrastructure, audits, and encryption. Protecting elections ensures public trust.

Why This Matters

It matters because the integrity of democracy is at stake.

It also highlights the intersection of cybersecurity and political stability.

Did You Know?

Cyber attacks on elections have been attempted in over 20 countries.
