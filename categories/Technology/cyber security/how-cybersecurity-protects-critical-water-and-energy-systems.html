How Cybersecurity Protects Critical Water and Energy Systems

Water and electricity aren’t safe by default.

Cyber attacks on utilities can cause widespread outages, environmental hazards, and public health crises. Cybersecurity includes monitoring, segmentation, and secure industrial control systems. Attacks may be state-sponsored or criminal. Prevention ensures continuity of essential services.

Why This Matters

It matters because infrastructure resilience depends on digital security.

It also highlights risks of interconnected networks.

Did You Know?

Over 40% of water utilities reported cyber attacks in the last two years.
