Why Cybersecurity Protects Against Insider Sabotage

Threats can lurk in plain sight.

Employees or contractors with legitimate access can intentionally damage systems or steal data. Cybersecurity monitors behavior, access logs, and unusual activity. Preventive measures include least privilege access and internal audits. Insider threats are harder to detect than external attacks. Policies and culture influence risk.

Why This Matters

It matters because insider threats can bypass conventional defenses.

It also emphasizes continuous monitoring and proactive management.

Did You Know?

Insider threats contribute to nearly a third of all data breaches.
