Why Cybersecurity Protects Educational Systems

Schools are under digital attack.

Educational institutions store student records, research, and financial data. Cybersecurity prevents breaches, ransomware, and phishing attacks targeting staff and students. Secure platforms support remote learning and online collaboration. Awareness training reduces risk.

Why This Matters

It matters because education relies on safe access to digital resources.

It also underscores the importance of securing institutions with limited IT budgets.

Did You Know?

Over 1,000 schools were impacted by ransomware in 2021.
