Why Hackers Love Human Error

Humans remain the weakest link.

Phishing, social engineering, and password reuse exploit human mistakes. No firewall or antivirus can fully protect against careless clicks. Attackers craft emails that manipulate trust, fear, or curiosity. Even security-aware individuals can be tricked. Cybersecurity training is crucial but imperfect.

Why This Matters

It matters because human behavior often dictates security success.

It also emphasizes that technical defenses alone are insufficient.

Did You Know?

Over 90% of successful cyber attacks exploit human error rather than software flaws.
