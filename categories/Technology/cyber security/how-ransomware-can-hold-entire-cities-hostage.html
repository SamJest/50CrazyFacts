How Ransomware Can Hold Entire Cities Hostage

Digital attacks with real-world consequences.

Ransomware encrypts data and demands payment for decryption. Entire municipalities, hospitals, and schools have been paralyzed. Public safety, emergency services, and records are vulnerable. Attackers often demand cryptocurrency to avoid traceability. Recovery can take months.

Why This Matters

Cybersecurity prevents disruptions that affect millions.

It also illustrates why incident response planning is essential for public services.

Did You Know?

Cities like Baltimore and Atlanta lost millions due to ransomware attacks.
