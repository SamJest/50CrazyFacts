Why Cybersecurity Is Critical for Online Banking

Your money isn’t safe by default.

Financial institutions face constant threats from phishing, malware, and fraud. Cybersecurity measures include encryption, anomaly detection, and multi-factor authentication. Banks invest heavily in monitoring systems. Even minor breaches can result in massive losses. Customer trust depends on secure transactions.

Why This Matters

It matters because digital finance underpins modern economies.

It also highlights the importance of user awareness and vigilance.

Did You Know?

Online banking attacks increased by over 150% in 2020.
