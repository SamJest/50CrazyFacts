How Cybersecurity Protects Healthcare Systems

Patient data is at stake.

Hospitals and clinics rely on secure systems to protect records and medical devices. Cyber attacks can disrupt life-saving services or steal sensitive data. Measures include encryption, access controls, and monitoring. Ransomware attacks on healthcare are increasingly common. Proper cybersecurity ensures continuity of care.

Why This Matters

It matters because human lives depend on secure systems.

It also emphasizes compliance with regulations like HIPAA.

Did You Know?

Healthcare breaches exposed over 40 million patient records in 2021.
