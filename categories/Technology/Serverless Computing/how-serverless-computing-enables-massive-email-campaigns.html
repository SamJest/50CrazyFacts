How Serverless Computing Enables Massive Email Campaigns

Sending millions of emails at once is risky without serverless.

Serverless functions can trigger per email or per batch. This distributes load evenly and prevents bottlenecks. Campaigns scale automatically based on demand. Failures are isolated to individual messages. This ensures high deliverability and reliability for large-scale email operations.

Why This Matters

Email systems remain stable during large campaigns. Deliverability improves.

This supports marketing automation and transactional messaging at scale.

Did You Know?

Serverless can send and process millions of emails concurrently.
