How Phishing Emails Hide Malicious Links

Links aren’t always obvious.

Attackers use hyperlinked text that looks legitimate or embed URLs in images. Hovering over links reveals mismatched destinations. Tiny typos in URLs deceive the eye. Some use URL shorteners to obscure targets. Even careful users can miss subtle differences.

Why This Matters

It matters because link manipulation evades basic caution.

It also underscores the importance of technical awareness and tools.

Did You Know?

Over 40% of phishing emails use hidden or misleading URLs.
