Why Phishing Isn’t Just Email—It’s Everywhere

Phishing is omnipresent.

Phishing exists on social media, messaging apps, phone calls (vishing), and text messages (smishing). Attackers mimic trusted contacts or services across platforms. Mobile devices are especially vulnerable due to smaller screens and quick interactions. Phishing has adapted to the channels people use most. It’s no longer confined to inboxes.

Why This Matters

It matters because defense strategies must be multi-channel.

It also stresses that vigilance is needed across all digital interactions.

Did You Know?

Over 30% of phishing attacks now occur via SMS or social media.
