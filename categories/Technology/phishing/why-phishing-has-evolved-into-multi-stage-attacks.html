Why Phishing Has Evolved Into Multi-Stage Attacks

It’s no longer a simple trick.

Phishing often begins with an email, followed by malware installation, credential theft, and lateral network movement. Multi-stage campaigns can last weeks or months. Attackers combine phishing with ransomware, keyloggers, and social engineering. Complexity increases impact and evasion.

Why This Matters

It matters because mitigation requires end-to-end security planning.

It also shows why incident response teams are essential.

Did You Know?

Modern phishing attacks often involve three or more sequential steps.
