How NFTs Are Creating New Forms of Digital Art Auctions

Auction houses are going fully digital with blockchain.

NFTs are sold via online auctions verified on-chain. Platforms track provenance, bidding, and sales transparently. Major auction houses like Christie’s and Sotheby’s have held NFT-only auctions. This removes geographical barriers and adds global accessibility.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs transform how art is sold and valued.

It also expands audience reach and introduces blockchain to traditional collectors.

Did You Know?

NFT-only auctions have sold multi-million-dollar artworks.
