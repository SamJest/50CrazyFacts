How NFTs Are Being Used to Preserve Digital Heritage

History goes on-chain.

Museums and archives are minting NFTs of digital artifacts, photographs, and documents. NFTs provide permanent proof of authenticity, ownership, and provenance. They allow global audiences to access cultural heritage online. Smart contracts track donations or exhibition rights.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs preserve digital culture and history.

It also creates new models for cultural access and monetization.

Did You Know?

NFTs are being minted to preserve digital cultural and historical artifacts.
