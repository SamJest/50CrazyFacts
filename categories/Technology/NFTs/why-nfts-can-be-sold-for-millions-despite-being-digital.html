Why NFTs Can Be Sold for Millions Despite Being Digital

It sounds insane—why pay millions for a digital file anyone can screenshot?

NFTs derive value from blockchain-verified ownership, uniqueness, and social status. Even if the artwork is viewable online by anyone, owning the NFT proves authenticity and provenance. Celebrity endorsements, community membership, and hype drive prices up. The digital scarcity concept is entirely blockchain-based.

Why This Matters

This matters because it challenges traditional notions of ownership and value in the digital age.

It also reveals how human psychology and social perception shape digital markets.

Did You Know?

NFTs can sell for millions because ownership is verifiable on the blockchain.
