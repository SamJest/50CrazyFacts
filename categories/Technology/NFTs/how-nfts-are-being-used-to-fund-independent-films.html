How NFTs Are Being Used to Fund Independent Films

Indie filmmakers find creative investors on blockchain.

Films are minted as NFTs, representing a stake in profits or special perks. Buyers may receive early screenings, exclusive content, or producer credits. Smart contracts distribute revenue automatically. NFT funding reduces reliance on traditional investors or studios.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs empower independent creators with new financing options.

It also changes how audiences engage with film production.

Did You Know?

NFTs are being used to fund and support independent film projects.
