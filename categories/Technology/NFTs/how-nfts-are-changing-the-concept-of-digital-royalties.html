How NFTs Are Changing the Concept of Digital Royalties

NFTs give creators ongoing revenue—automatically.

Many NFTs include smart contracts that pay royalties to the original creator each time the NFT is resold. This means artists can earn long after the initial sale, something impossible in traditional art markets. Resale tracking is automatic on-chain, reducing disputes. It provides a sustainable model for digital artists.

Why This Matters

It matters because it creates continuous income streams for creators, reshaping creative industries.

It also encourages innovation in ownership and royalty structures across digital media.

Did You Know?

Some NFTs automatically pay the original creator every time they are resold.
