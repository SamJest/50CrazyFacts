Why Doom Scrolling Is Strongest Late at Night

The worst scrolling happens after dark.

Nighttime doom scrolling coincides with lowered self-control. Fatigue weakens the brain’s impulse regulation systems. Blue light suppresses melatonin, delaying sleep. Emotional content feels more intense when tired. This creates a perfect storm for extended scrolling sessions. Many people fall asleep mid-scroll.

Why This Matters

This matters because sleep disruption impacts long-term health.

It also reinforces the habit through exhaustion.

Did You Know?

Late-night users scroll significantly longer than daytime users.
