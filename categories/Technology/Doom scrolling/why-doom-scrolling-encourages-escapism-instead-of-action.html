Why Doom Scrolling Encourages Escapism Instead of Action

Scrolling replaces doing.

Users feel compelled to keep up with crises but take no action. Passive consumption becomes a coping mechanism. Awareness replaces agency. Frustration grows. Engagement is habitual, not effective. The cycle continues.

Why This Matters

It matters because feeling powerless increases anxiety.

It also reduces proactive engagement with real-world problems.

Did You Know?

Awareness without action increases stress levels.
