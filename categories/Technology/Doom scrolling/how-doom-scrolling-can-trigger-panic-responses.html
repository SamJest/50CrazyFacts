How Doom Scrolling Can Trigger Panic Responses

Your body thinks danger is near.

Negative news activates fight-or-flight responses. Heart rate spikes. Breathing quickens. Cortisol rises. Chronic exposure creates repeated panic triggers. Symptoms appear even without real-world threats.

Why This Matters

The effect matters because frequent panic responses strain health.

It also reinforces compulsive scanning behavior.

Did You Know?

Digital alerts can trigger physiological panic within seconds.
