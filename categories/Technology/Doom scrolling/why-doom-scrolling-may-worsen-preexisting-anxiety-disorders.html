Why Doom Scrolling May Worsen Preexisting Anxiety Disorders

Triggers amplify conditions.

Those with anxiety respond more intensely to negative news. Doom scrolling reinforces hypervigilance. Stress responses spike. Avoidance behaviors grow. Emotional regulation weakens. Symptoms worsen over time.

Why This Matters

It matters because digital habits interact with mental health conditions.

It also complicates treatment and recovery.

Did You Know?

Anxiety-prone users experience heightened symptoms after doom scrolling.
