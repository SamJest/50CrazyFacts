Why Doom Scrolling Distorts Your Sense of Time

Time melts while scrolling.

Doom scrolling disrupts time perception. The brain loses track without clear breaks. Minutes turn into hours unnoticed. This happens because novelty suppresses internal clocks. The lack of stopping cues worsens it. Afterwards, time feels “missing.” This creates guilt and confusion.

Why This Matters

It matters because time awareness supports self-control.

It also impacts productivity planning.

Did You Know?

Endless feeds remove temporal landmarks.
