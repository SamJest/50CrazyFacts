How Doom Scrolling Alters Your Attention Span

Long scrolling sessions reshape focus.

Rapid content switching trains the brain to expect constant novelty. Deep focus becomes harder to sustain. Attention fragments into short bursts. Reading long-form content feels exhausting. This mirrors patterns seen in multitasking overload. The brain adapts to speed over depth.

Why This Matters

It matters because attention underpins learning and creativity.

It also affects work performance.

Did You Know?

Heavy scrollers show reduced sustained attention.
