Why Doom Scrolling Makes Time Feel Like It’s Disappearing

Hours vanish without warning.

Doom scrolling distorts time perception by overloading attention systems. Rapid content changes prevent memory formation. Without clear “chapters,” the brain can’t track duration. This creates time compression, where long sessions feel short. The same effect appears in casinos and fast-paced games. Users often underestimate their screen time by hours.

Why This Matters

This matters because lost time affects sleep, productivity, and wellbeing.

It also complicates self-regulation efforts.

Did You Know?

People consistently underestimate scrolling time by up to 50%.
