Why Doom Scrolling Reduces Empathy Over Time

Too much tragedy dulls feeling.

Constant exposure to crises triggers emotional numbing. The brain protects itself by reducing emotional response. This can look like apathy or cynicism. Empathy fatigue sets in gradually. Users may care less despite consuming more information. It’s a psychological defense.

Why This Matters

This matters because empathy drives social connection.

It also affects civic engagement and compassion.

Did You Know?

Empathy drops after prolonged exposure to distressing media.
