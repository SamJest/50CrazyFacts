How Doom Scrolling Can Affect Workplace Productivity

Scrolling bleeds into work hours.

Short “just checking” breaks extend into long sessions. Attention switching reduces efficiency. Brain fatigue accumulates. Priorities blur. Deadlines suffer. Emotional stress compounds.

Why This Matters

It matters because workplace performance declines.

It also affects career satisfaction and mental health.

Did You Know?

Even brief doom scrolling sessions can reduce productivity by 20–30%.

[Harvard Business Review, hbr.org]
