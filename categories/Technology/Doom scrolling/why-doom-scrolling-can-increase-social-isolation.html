Why Doom Scrolling Can Increase Social Isolation

More scrolling, less connecting.

Time spent consuming feeds replaces social interactions. Emotional numbing reduces motivation to connect. Isolation feeds further scrolling. Users may feel simultaneously connected and alone. Loneliness intensifies stress. Mental health declines.

Why This Matters

It matters because social support is critical for wellbeing.

It also perpetuates compulsive behavior cycles.

Did You Know?

Excessive doom scrolling correlates with higher loneliness scores.
