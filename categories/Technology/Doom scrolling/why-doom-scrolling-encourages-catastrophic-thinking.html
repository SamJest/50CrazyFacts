Why Doom Scrolling Encourages Catastrophic Thinking

Worst-case thinking grows.

Repeated crisis exposure primes the brain for danger. Small issues feel catastrophic. Probabilities are misjudged. The mind expects collapse. This thinking pattern reinforces anxiety. Doom scrolling becomes self-validating.

Why This Matters

It matters because fear shapes behavior.

It also impacts long-term outlook.

Did You Know?

Catastrophic thinking increases with threat exposure.
