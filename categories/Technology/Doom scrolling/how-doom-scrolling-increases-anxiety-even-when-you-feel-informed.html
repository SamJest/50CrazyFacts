How Doom Scrolling Increases Anxiety Even When You Feel ‘Informed’

More news doesn’t mean more clarity.

Doom scrolling floods the brain with unresolved threats. The mind evolved to respond to danger with action, not passive consumption. When action isn’t possible, anxiety accumulates. The illusion of being informed masks emotional overload. Instead of closure, the feed keeps reopening stress loops. This trains hypervigilance.

Why This Matters

It matters because information overload harms decision-making.

It also increases chronic stress responses.

Did You Know?

Consuming more news does not correlate with feeling safer.
