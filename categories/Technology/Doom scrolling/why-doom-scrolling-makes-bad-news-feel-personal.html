Why Doom Scrolling Makes Bad News Feel Personal

The brain doesn’t distinguish distance well.

Repeated exposure to global crises activates personal threat systems. Even distant events feel immediate. Emotional proximity increases stress. The mind reacts as if danger is nearby. This leads to chronic vigilance. Reality becomes emotionally compressed.

Why This Matters

It matters because stress responses are misdirected.

It also distorts risk perception.

Did You Know?

The brain processes digital threats similarly to real ones.
