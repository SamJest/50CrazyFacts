Why Doom Scrolling Feels Impossible to Finish

The brain seeks closure.

Stories without resolution create tension. Doom scrolling delivers ongoing crises with no endings. The mind waits for resolution that never arrives. This keeps scrolling behavior active. Each update feels like progress. Closure becomes an illusion.

Why This Matters

It matters because unresolved loops fuel anxiety.

It also explains compulsive checking.

Did You Know?

Open-ended information sustains attention longer.
