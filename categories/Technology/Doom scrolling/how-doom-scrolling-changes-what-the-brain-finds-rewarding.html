How Doom Scrolling Changes What the Brain Finds Rewarding

Reward systems adapt.

The brain releases dopamine for novel content. Over time, mild stimuli stop satisfying. Only extreme content triggers engagement. This escalates consumption patterns. Emotional intensity becomes the reward. Calm content feels dull.

Why This Matters

It matters because reward systems guide habits.

It also explains content extremity online.

Did You Know?

Dopamine adapts to stimulus intensity.
