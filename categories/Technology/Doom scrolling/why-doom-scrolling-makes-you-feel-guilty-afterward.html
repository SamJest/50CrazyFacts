Why Doom Scrolling Makes You Feel Guilty Afterward

Scrolling shame is real.

Doom scrolling often conflicts with personal goals. Users intend to relax but end up stressed. This mismatch creates guilt and frustration. The brain interprets lost time as failure. Shame then fuels more avoidance scrolling. It becomes a self-punishing loop.

Why This Matters

This matters because guilt worsens mental health.

It also reinforces compulsive behavior cycles.

Did You Know?

Guilt increases the likelihood of repeated scrolling.
