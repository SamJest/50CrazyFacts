How Doom Scrolling Alters Perception of Personal Safety

The world feels more dangerous.

Constant exposure to crises exaggerates threat perception. Everyday events seem risky. The brain activates fight-or-flight unnecessarily. This affects behavior, vigilance, and mood. Minor dangers feel amplified. Anxiety becomes chronic.

Why This Matters

It matters because skewed threat perception affects real-life decisions.

It also increases stress responses in daily life.

Did You Know?

People overestimate risk after prolonged exposure to negative news.
