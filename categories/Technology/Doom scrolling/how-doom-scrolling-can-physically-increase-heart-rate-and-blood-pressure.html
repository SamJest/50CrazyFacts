How Doom Scrolling Can Physically Increase Heart Rate and Blood Pressure

It’s not just mental stress.

Negative digital content activates the body’s stress response. Heart rate and blood pressure rise during prolonged scrolling. Cortisol remains elevated without resolution. Unlike real threats, there’s no recovery phase. The body stays on alert. This strains cardiovascular systems over time.

Why This Matters

It matters because chronic stress impacts physical health.

It also links digital habits to medical outcomes.

Did You Know?

Stress responses can begin within minutes of scrolling.
