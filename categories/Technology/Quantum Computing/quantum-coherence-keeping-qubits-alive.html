Quantum Coherence: Keeping Qubits Alive

Coherence time is the duration a qubit maintains its quantum state, essential for computations.

Qubits interact with their environment and lose coherence quickly, causing errors. Scientists are developing materials, shielding, and cooling techniques to extend coherence, allowing longer and more complex quantum operations. Maintaining coherence is crucial for practical, fault-tolerant quantum computers.

Why This Matters

Longer coherence times enable more reliable and intricate computations.

Research into coherence also informs fundamental physics and quantum control methods.

Did You Know?

Some superconducting qubits now maintain coherence for over 100 microseconds, a significant milestone.
