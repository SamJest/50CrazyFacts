Quantum Metrology: Precision Beyond Classical Limits

Quantum sensors exploit superposition and entanglement for ultra-precise measurements.

Using quantum effects, devices can measure time, magnetic fields, and gravitational variations with unprecedented accuracy. This improves GPS, medical imaging, and geophysical studies.

Why This Matters

Quantum metrology enhances precision in science and industry beyond classical constraints.

It enables breakthroughs in navigation, climate research, and fundamental physics tests.

Did You Know?

Quantum clocks now outperform classical atomic clocks in stability and accuracy.
