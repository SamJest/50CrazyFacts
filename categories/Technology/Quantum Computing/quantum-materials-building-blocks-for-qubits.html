Quantum Materials: Building Blocks for Qubits

New materials enable stable qubits and innovative quantum devices.

Materials like superconductors, topological insulators, and 2D semiconductors exhibit quantum behaviors essential for qubits and sensors. Understanding their electronic, magnetic, and vibrational properties allows engineers to design high-performance, low-noise devices.

Why This Matters

Material advances directly influence quantum computing performance.

Discovering and fabricating quantum materials accelerates the development of scalable, reliable quantum hardware.

Did You Know?

Research labs synthesize new materials tailored for coherence and entanglement experiments.
