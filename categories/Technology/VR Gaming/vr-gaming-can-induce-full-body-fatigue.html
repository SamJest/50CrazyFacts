VR Gaming Can Induce Full-Body Fatigue

Playing is literally exhausting.

Intense VR games require constant movement, dodging, punching, or squatting. Players often underestimate exertion due to engagement. Full-body motion, combined with haptic suits and treadmills, can tire muscles faster than traditional workouts. Safety guidelines recommend hydration and rest.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR is a potential fitness medium.

It also emphasizes responsible play to avoid injury.

Did You Know?

Players can burn hundreds of calories and feel full-body exhaustion in VR sessions.

Source

[Journal of Medical Internet Research, jmir.org]
