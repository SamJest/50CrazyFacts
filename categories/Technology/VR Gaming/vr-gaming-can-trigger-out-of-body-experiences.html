VR Gaming Can Trigger Out-of-Body Experiences

The mind and virtual body disconnect.

Some VR setups induce the sensation that the avatar is separate from the player’s real body. This can occur through perspective shifts, delayed feedback, or first-person versus third-person views. Researchers explore implications for consciousness and self-perception.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can alter fundamental perception of self.

It also shows VR’s potential in neuroscience and experimental psychology.

Did You Know?

VR users have reported feeling detached from their real body during immersive gameplay.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
