VR Gaming Can Trigger Motion Sickness in 40% of Players

Immersion comes at a cost.

Many VR players experience motion sickness, nausea, or dizziness due to sensory mismatch—the eyes perceive movement, but the inner ear does not. Developers counter this with frame rate optimization, teleportation movement, and visual cues. Individual susceptibility varies, influenced by age, VR experience, and genetics. Motion sickness limits playtime and accessibility for some users.

Why This Matters

Understanding this helps developers improve design.

It also influences hardware standards and comfort features.

Did You Know?

Nearly 40% of VR users report motion sickness during gameplay.
