The Dangerous Trend of Ransomware Targeting Education

Schools aren’t just classrooms—they’re digital treasure chests.

Educational institutions are increasingly attacked. Ransomware encrypts student records, research data, and learning management systems. Budget constraints often leave IT under-resourced. Downtime disrupts education for thousands. Payment demands can divert funding from core operations. It highlights the vulnerability of non-profit and public institutions.

Why This Matters

It matters because students’ data and education are jeopardized. Schools need specialized cybersecurity strategies.

It also raises awareness of public-sector cybersecurity needs.

Did You Know?

Ransomware attacks on schools are growing globally.
