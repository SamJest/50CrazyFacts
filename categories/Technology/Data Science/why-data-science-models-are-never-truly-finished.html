Why Data Science Models Are Never Truly Finished

A finished data model starts decaying immediately. Reality keeps changing.

Data distributions shift as behavior, markets, or environments evolve. This phenomenon is known as data drift. Models trained on old data slowly lose accuracy. Continuous monitoring and retraining are required. Data science is an ongoing process, not a one-time build. Maintenance is as important as creation.

Why This Matters

This matters because outdated models can cause silent failures. Accuracy degrades without warning.

It reinforces the need for long-term model governance.

Did You Know?

Data science models degrade over time due to data drift.
