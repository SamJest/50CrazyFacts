How Data Science Turned Marketing Into a Science

Marketing used to rely on instinct. Data changed everything.

Data science analyzes campaign performance, attribution paths, and conversion funnels. Models identify which channels drive results. A/B testing validates strategies statistically. Marketing decisions become measurable and repeatable. Creativity remains—but it’s guided by evidence.

Why This Matters

This matters because ROI becomes transparent. Wasteful spending declines.

It professionalizes marketing strategy across industries.
