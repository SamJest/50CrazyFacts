Why Data Science Still Needs Human Judgment

Automation is powerful—but not wise. Humans close the loop.

Data science models operate within assumptions. Humans evaluate whether outputs make sense. Ethical judgment and context matter. Final decisions often require oversight. The strongest systems combine human and machine intelligence. Balance prevents catastrophic errors.

Why This Matters

This matters because unchecked automation can cause harm. Responsibility remains human.

It defines the future of human-AI collaboration.

Did You Know?

Data science systems require human oversight for critical decisions.
