The Algorithm That Knows When You’re About to Quit

Some companies know you’re leaving before you resign. Data science spots the warning signs.

Employee attrition models analyze patterns like reduced engagement, schedule changes, and communication frequency. These models flag individuals at high risk of leaving. Managers can intervene proactively using this insight. While controversial, it has proven effective. The same techniques are used in customer churn prediction across industries. Data science turns subtle behavioral shifts into actionable signals.

Why This Matters

This matters because retaining talent saves massive costs. Early intervention can prevent unnecessary turnover.

It also raises ethical questions about workplace monitoring and consent.

Did You Know?

Data science models can predict employee turnover months in advance.
