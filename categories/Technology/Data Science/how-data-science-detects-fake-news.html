How Data Science Detects Fake News

Misinformation spreads fast—but data science tracks it.

Models analyze language patterns, source credibility, and sharing behavior. Fake news often shows distinct signatures. Detection systems flag suspicious content early. While imperfect, they slow misinformation spread. Data science supports information integrity online.

Why This Matters

This matters because public trust depends on accurate information. Democracy is affected.

It shows data science intersecting with social responsibility.

Did You Know?

Data science models can identify fake news patterns.
