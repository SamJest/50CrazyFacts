Queen Victoria Loved Highland Scotland

Tartan wasn’t just fashion.

It was royal passion.

Why This Matters

Queen Victoria adored the Scottish Highlands. She purchased Balmoral Castle. Tartan became royal-approved. Highland culture gained prestige. Tourism surged in Scotland. Her love reshaped national identity aesthetics.

It also boosted Scottish economic growth.

Did You Know?

Balmoral remains a royal residence today.
