Queen Victoria Had a Private Language with Her Children

She developed unique codes and phrases to communicate secretly with her nine children.

Victoria often created special words, ciphers, and gestures that only her children could understand. This playful secret language strengthened familial bonds and offered amusement in long palace days. She used it to pass messages during formal events or keep minor instructions hidden from servants and courtiers. The codes included whimsical names, invented terms, and symbolic references. Some historians argue this fostered creativity and intelligence in her children. The practice reflects Victorian domestic culture’s emphasis on structured yet imaginative upbringing.

Why This Matters

These secret codes highlight the importance of private life even in the public eye.

It also demonstrates the monarch’s role in shaping family dynamics and child development.

Did You Know?

Victoria’s invented language included playful nicknames and secret instructions for palace games.
