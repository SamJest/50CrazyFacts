Queen Victoria’s Funeral Broke Royal Tradition

Even death was unconventional.

She rejected black.

Why This Matters

Queen Victoria requested a white funeral. She wanted military honors. The coffin included personal items. Her funeral defied expectations. It symbolized personal agency. Even in death, she set precedent.

It also reflected her lifelong independence.

Did You Know?

She was buried with Albert’s robe.
