Queen Victoria Loved Spicy Food From India

Her tastes defied bland Victorian stereotypes.

Curry became royal-approved cuisine.

Why This Matters

Queen Victoria developed a fondness for Indian dishes. She enjoyed curries prepared by Indian servants at court. Her cooks adapted recipes to British tastes. This helped introduce curry to elite British dining. Over time, the dishes spread across society. It marked early globalization of British food culture.

It also reflects cultural exchange within empire dynamics.

Did You Know?

Victoria kept handwritten curry recipes.
