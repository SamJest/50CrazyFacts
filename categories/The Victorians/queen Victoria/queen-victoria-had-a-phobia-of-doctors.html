Queen Victoria Had a Phobia of Doctors

Despite her role as monarch, she was terrified of physicians and hospitals.

Victoria’s fear of doctors was well-documented, especially during childbirth and Prince Albert’s illness. She often refused medical advice or delayed treatment due to anxiety. Her phobia extended to hospitals, which she considered frightening and unhygienic. Contemporary doctors described her as a challenging patient with meticulous attention to medical procedures. This fear may have contributed to heightened personal caution and reliance on Albert for medical decisions. Even during widespread illness, she avoided public hospitals, preferring private care.

Why This Matters

Her phobia influenced royal healthcare, prompting private medical innovations.

It also demonstrates that even figures of immense power are subject to human vulnerability.

Did You Know?

Victoria’s fear of physicians caused her to rely heavily on Prince Albert and her personal attendants for healthcare decisions.
