Queen Victoria Popularized Royal Souvenirs

Merch before marketing.

The crown sold well.

Why This Matters

Victorian Britain loved royal memorabilia. Items featured Queen Victoria’s likeness. Plates, cups, and prints sold widely. This normalized celebrity culture. Royals became consumer icons. Souvenirs fueled loyalty.

It also commercialized monarchy.

Did You Know?

Royal portraits were mass-produced.
