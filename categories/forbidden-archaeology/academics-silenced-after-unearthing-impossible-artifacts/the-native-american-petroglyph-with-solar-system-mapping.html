🤯 Did You Know?

Some Native American petroglyphs accurately depict planetary positions and relative sizes from a millennium ago.

In 2003, Dr. Marissa White documented petroglyphs in the Southwest United States that include circles, lines, and annotations resembling the orbital layout of the planets. White suggested that indigenous astronomers may have tracked planetary motion with remarkable accuracy. Attempts to publish in mainstream archaeology journals were restricted, and access to the site was limited to avoid interference. Comparative astronomical analysis showed that the carvings align with planetary positions during specific historical epochs. The discovery challenges the notion that complex planetary observation is exclusively a product of Old World civilizations. White’s work is referenced primarily in archaeoastronomy discussions. The petroglyph demonstrates sophisticated understanding of celestial relationships and timing.
