Queen Victoria Helped Normalize White Wedding Dresses

Brides didn’t always wear white.

One royal choice changed everything.

Why This Matters

Queen Victoria wore a white dress at her wedding in 1840. At the time, brides wore many colors. Her choice symbolized simplicity and purity. Newspapers widely publicized the gown. Brides across Europe copied the style. White became the default bridal color.

This transformed wedding fashion permanently.

Did You Know?

It also boosted lace and textile industries.
