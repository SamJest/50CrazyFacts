Queen Victoria Had a Personal Indian Secretary

A rare position for the era.

The relationship shocked Victorian society.

Why This Matters

Abdul Karim served as Queen Victoria’s Indian secretary. He taught her Urdu and Indian customs. Victoria trusted him deeply, causing tension at court. Many officials resented his influence. After her death, his correspondence was destroyed. The episode exposed racial and class anxieties of the time.

It reveals hidden diversity within royal circles.

Did You Know?

It also shows how power disrupted rigid hierarchies.
