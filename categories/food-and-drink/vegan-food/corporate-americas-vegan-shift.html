Corporate America’s Vegan Shift

When did billion-dollar companies embrace beans?

💥 Impact

Corporate adoption mainstreamed vegan food globally.

It demonstrates how market demand can reshape food systems rapidly.

🤯 Did You Know?

Major fast-food chains now sell plant-based burgers nationwide.

In the 2010s, major fast-food chains introduced plant-based burgers nationwide. What was once fringe entered drive-thru culture. Investors poured billions into plant-protein startups. CEOs cited sustainability metrics and shifting consumer demand. Supermarkets expanded vegan aisles at record pace. The economic signal was unmistakable: plant-based food was profitable. Traditional meat companies launched vegan subsidiaries. Market forces accelerated cultural normalization. Vegan food moved from co-ops to corporate boardrooms. Capitalism adopted kale.
