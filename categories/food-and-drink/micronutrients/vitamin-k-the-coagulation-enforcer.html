Vitamin K: The Coagulation Enforcer

Think vitamin K is only about clotting? Its role reaches further.

💥 Impact

Vitamin K supports clotting, bone health, and vascular integrity.

Dietary and microbial sources of K work together to maintain health.

🤯 Did You Know?

Vitamin K literally activates proteins critical for blood and bones.

Vitamin K is essential for activating proteins that regulate blood clotting, bone metabolism, and vascular health. Historical accounts show that deficiency caused uncontrolled bleeding in newborns and populations with poor diets. There are multiple forms: K1 from leafy greens and K2 from fermented foods or animal sources, each with distinct biological effects. Vitamin K interacts with calcium, affecting bone mineralization and cardiovascular calcification. Deficiency can contribute to osteoporosis, arterial calcification, and hemorrhage risk. Modern supplementation and dietary awareness have reduced severe deficiency, though subclinical insufficiency exists. Its dual role in coagulation and bone health underscores the multifaceted nature of micronutrients. Appreciating vitamin K illustrates how diet, microbiome, and metabolism intersect. A vitamin once thought niche turns out to be a systemic regulator.
