Iron: The Oxygen Carrier That Runs the Body

Think iron just prevents anemia? Its impact is systemic.

💥 Impact

Iron transports oxygen, fuels energy, and supports development.

Global deficiency impacts cognitive and physical performance.

🤯 Did You Know?

Iron literally fuels the body’s oxygen delivery system.

Iron is critical for hemoglobin formation, oxygen transport, and energy metabolism. Ancient populations with low meat intake frequently suffered fatigue, pallor, and reduced work capacity. Iron also supports myoglobin in muscles and cytochromes in mitochondria, linking it to endurance and energy production. Heme iron from animal sources is more readily absorbed than non-heme iron from plants, which can be inhibited by phytates or polyphenols. Iron deficiency anemia remains the most common micronutrient deficiency worldwide. Excess iron is toxic, illustrating the delicate balance required for health. Iron’s role in brain development, immunity, and cellular respiration underscores its systemic importance. Understanding iron illuminates why dietary patterns, bioavailability, and supplementation all matter. This micronutrient literally powers every breath and every heartbeat.
