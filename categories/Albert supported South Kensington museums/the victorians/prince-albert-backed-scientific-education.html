Prince Albert Backed Scientific Education

Science felt like the future.

He was right.

Why This Matters

Prince Albert championed science education. He supported technical schools. Innovation mattered more than classics. Britain needed engineers. His influence boosted STEM learning. Education shifted direction.

This fueled industrial progress.

Did You Know?

It reshaped curricula.
