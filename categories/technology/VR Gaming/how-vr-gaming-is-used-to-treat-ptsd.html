How VR Gaming Is Used to Treat PTSD

Therapy enters the virtual realm.

VR simulations recreate stressful or traumatic environments in controlled ways. Patients with PTSD interact with these scenarios under professional guidance to desensitize triggers and practice coping strategies. Gamified VR exercises increase engagement and compliance. Studies show significant reductions in symptoms over repeated sessions.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR offers a non-invasive treatment alternative.

It also highlights VR’s potential in healthcare and mental health therapy.

Did You Know?

Veterans and trauma survivors have shown measurable improvement using VR therapy.

Source

[Journal of Anxiety Disorders, sciencedirect.com]
