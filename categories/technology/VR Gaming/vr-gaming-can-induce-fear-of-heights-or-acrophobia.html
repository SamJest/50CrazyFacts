VR Gaming Can Induce Fear of Heights or Acrophobia

Your brain reacts like it’s real.

Height simulation VR games trigger natural fear responses. The brain interprets visual cues of depth and distance as real, even though the player is safe. Some therapists use these games for exposure therapy. Motion tracking, haptic feedback, and realistic sound amplify the effect.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can be used therapeutically and for research.

It also demonstrates VR’s psychological realism.

Did You Know?

Players often feel vertigo and panic despite standing on solid ground.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
