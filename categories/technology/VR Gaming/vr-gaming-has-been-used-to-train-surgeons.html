VR Gaming Has Been Used to Train Surgeons

Precision practice without patients.

VR surgery simulations allow medical students and surgeons to practice operations safely. Haptic feedback replicates tissue resistance, while 3D visuals provide depth perception. Studies show VR-trained surgeons perform faster and make fewer errors. VR training reduces the need for cadavers or live patients.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR improves medical training safety and efficiency.

It also highlights VR’s transformative impact on professional education.

Did You Know?

Surgeons practicing with VR simulations make 30% fewer errors in real procedures.

Source

[Journal of Surgical Education, jsurged.org]
