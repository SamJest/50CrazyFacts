VR Gaming Can Improve Multitasking Skills

Juggle in 3D.

Complex VR games require attention to multiple tasks, resources, or enemies simultaneously. Immersion and real-time feedback challenge players’ cognitive flexibility. Studies show improvement in task-switching and divided attention skills.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can develop cognitive skills relevant in professional and academic settings.

It also demonstrates VR’s potential for mental fitness and cognitive training.

Did You Know?

VR gamers often outperform non-gamers in multitasking assessments.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
