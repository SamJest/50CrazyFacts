VR Gaming Can Burn Hundreds of Calories per Hour

Playing VR isn’t just fun—it’s fitness.

Active VR games like Beat Saber, Supernatural, or BoxVR involve full-body movement. Players dodge, swing, and step continuously, turning gaming into cardiovascular exercise. Studies show that vigorous VR sessions can burn 300–600 calories per hour, comparable to jogging or cycling. Developers gamify fitness by adding scores, levels, and multiplayer competitions. Fitness-focused VR is growing into a serious niche.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can combine entertainment and health.

It also emphasizes the potential of exergaming as a mainstream activity.

Did You Know?

An hour of intense VR gameplay can burn as many calories as running 5 miles.

Source

[Journal of Medical Internet Research, jmir.org]
