VR Gaming Can Help Stroke Patients Relearn Movement

VR therapy accelerates recovery.

Stroke patients use VR to practice arm and leg movements in a controlled virtual environment. Motion tracking and interactive games encourage repetition and engagement. Patients receive immediate visual and auditory feedback. Studies show VR rehabilitation can improve motor function more quickly than traditional methods.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR offers a safe, motivating, and adaptable recovery method.

It also highlights VR’s potential in medical and therapeutic applications beyond entertainment.

Did You Know?

Stroke patients using VR-assisted rehab can regain motor skills faster than conventional therapy.

Source

[Frontiers in Neurology, frontiersin.org]
