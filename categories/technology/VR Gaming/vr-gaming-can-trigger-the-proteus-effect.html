VR Gaming Can Trigger the “Proteus Effect”

Players can adopt new personalities.

The Proteus Effect occurs when a player’s avatar influences their real-world behavior. For example, tall or attractive avatars can boost confidence, while heroic avatars can increase helpfulness. VR’s immersive nature makes this effect stronger than in traditional games. Researchers study this to understand identity, social behavior, and psychological impact.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can shape real-world actions and personality traits.

It also raises ethical considerations for avatar design and virtual experiences.

Did You Know?

Players often act differently in real life after adopting certain VR avatars.
