VR Gaming Can Increase Empathy

Walk in someone else’s shoes.

Certain VR experiences place players in the perspective of another person, simulating challenges like disability, poverty, or social exclusion. Immersion and interactivity create emotional understanding beyond traditional media. Empathy-based VR has applications in education, training, and social awareness campaigns.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can shape social consciousness.

It also demonstrates VR’s potential as a tool for personal and societal growth.

Did You Know?

Players report stronger emotional connection after VR empathy experiences.

Source

[Stanford Virtual Human Interaction Lab, vhil.stanford.edu]
