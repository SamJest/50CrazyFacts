VR Gaming Can Help Reduce Stress

Mindful immersion.

Relaxing VR environments, meditation apps, or gentle gameplay can lower cortisol and heart rate. Immersion distracts users from real-world stressors, promoting calm and relaxation. VR wellness programs integrate visuals, sounds, and guided exercises.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR can complement mental health strategies.

It also demonstrates VR’s potential as a therapeutic tool for wellness and mindfulness.

Did You Know?

VR meditation users report measurable reductions in stress after sessions.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
