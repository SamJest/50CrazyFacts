The First VR Game Was Invented in the 1960s

VR gaming didn’t start with Oculus or PlayStation.

The first known virtual reality game concept emerged in the 1960s with the "Sword of Damocles," a primitive head-mounted display system. It was clunky, heavy, and required suspension from the ceiling. Despite its awkward design, it demonstrated the potential of immersive digital experiences. It inspired decades of innovation and experimentation in VR technology. Early VR was more about proving the concept than fun gameplay. Researchers had to invent motion tracking, stereoscopic displays, and input devices almost from scratch.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows VR’s deep-rooted experimental history.

It also highlights the decades of technological evolution leading to today’s realistic VR experiences.

Did You Know?

The original head-mounted display weighed over 20 pounds and required overhead suspension.
