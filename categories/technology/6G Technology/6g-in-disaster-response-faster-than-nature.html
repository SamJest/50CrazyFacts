6G in Disaster Response: Faster Than Nature

6G could save lives in emergencies.

Instant communication between drones, robots, and rescue teams enables real-time mapping and action. AI predicts disaster evolution for immediate intervention.

Why This Matters

Disaster relief becomes faster and more coordinated.

Lives and infrastructure can be preserved more effectively.

Did You Know?

6G test networks have coordinated multi-drone search-and-rescue operations in simulated earthquakes.
