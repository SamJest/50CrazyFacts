6G in Healthcare: Remote Surgery Anywhere

6G could make remote surgery routine.

High-speed, near-zero latency 6G connections allow surgeons to operate robotic systems across the globe. Haptic feedback ensures precision as if the surgeon were in the room.

Why This Matters

Patients in remote areas gain access to world-class healthcare.

Rural and underserved populations could receive specialized treatment without travel.

Did You Know?

6G-based remote surgery tests have performed complex procedures with response times under 1 millisecond.

Source

Siemens Healthineers (siemens-healthineers.com)
