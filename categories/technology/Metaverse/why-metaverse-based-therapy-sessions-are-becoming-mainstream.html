Why Metaverse-Based Therapy Sessions Are Becoming Mainstream

Therapists meet clients in immersive VR rooms.

Mental health professionals offer counseling, exposure therapy, and group sessions via the Metaverse. Avatars reduce social anxiety and stigma. AI can track progress and personalize exercises. NFT badges may reward milestones.

Why This Matters

It matters because therapy becomes more accessible, engaging, and scalable.

It also allows data-driven improvements in treatment methods.

Did You Know?

Virtual therapy in the Metaverse has served thousands of clients worldwide.
