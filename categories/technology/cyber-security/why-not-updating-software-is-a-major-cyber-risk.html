Why Not Updating Software Is a Major Cyber Risk

Your updates are more than cosmetic.

Missing patches allow attackers to exploit known vulnerabilities. Zero-day exploits can devastate unpatched systems. Regular software updates fix bugs and security flaws. Many breaches occur due to outdated software. Even devices like printers or IoT gadgets need attention.

Why This Matters

It matters because neglecting updates can undo all other security efforts.

It also shows the importance of automated patch management in organizations.

Did You Know?

Unpatched vulnerabilities cause nearly 60% of data breaches.
