Why Cybersecurity Protects Online Gaming Communities

Your avatar isn’t the only thing at risk.

Over 30% of gamers report having their accounts hacked.

Online games store personal data, financial information, and virtual assets. Cybersecurity prevents account takeovers, cheating, and DDoS attacks. Developers implement anti-cheat systems, secure payment gateways, and monitoring. Gaming platforms are prime phishing targets. Safe gaming requires both user vigilance and robust backend security.
