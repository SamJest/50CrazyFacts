The First Recorded Cyber Attack Happened in 1986

The dawn of digital crime started decades ago.

The 1986 “Morris Worm” was one of the first worms to spread across the early internet, created by Robert Tappan Morris. It infected thousands of computers, causing massive disruption. Even early networks with limited connections proved vulnerable. Security practices had to evolve quickly. It was a wake-up call for cybersecurity professionals.

Why This Matters

The historical importance shows how far cyber threats go back.

It also underscores the continual cat-and-mouse game between attackers and defenders.

Did You Know?

The Morris Worm affected around 6,000 computers, nearly 10% of the internet at the time.
