How Cybersecurity Protects Autonomous Vehicles

Driverless cars need digital defense.

Autonomous vehicles rely on sensors, AI, and network communication. Cybersecurity ensures vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure communications are secure. Attackers could manipulate navigation, control, or data. Real-time monitoring and encryption are essential. Safety depends on robust defense.

Why This Matters

It matters because vulnerabilities can directly endanger human life.

It also highlights the importance of security in emerging mobility technologies.

Did You Know?

Over 90% of autonomous vehicle security researchers report finding exploitable vulnerabilities.
