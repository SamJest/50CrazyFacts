How Cybersecurity Secures Blockchain and Cryptocurrency

Your digital wallet relies on more than passwords.

Cryptocurrencies are prime targets for hackers. Cybersecurity protects private keys, wallets, and exchange platforms. Multi-signature wallets, cold storage, and monitoring reduce risk. Attacks exploit both software and human error. Losses are often irreversible due to blockchain’s nature.

Why This Matters

It matters because digital currency underpins financial innovation.

It also highlights unique security challenges in decentralized systems.

Did You Know?

Over $3 billion in cryptocurrency was stolen via hacks in 2021.
