Why Small Businesses Are Surprisingly Vulnerable

Size doesn’t equal safety.

Many small businesses neglect cybersecurity due to cost or perceived irrelevance. Attackers know these organizations are less protected. Phishing, ransomware, and credential theft hit small enterprises hard. Even a single compromised account can disrupt operations. Awareness and affordable solutions are critical.

Why This Matters

It matters because small businesses are high-risk targets.

It also highlights the need for accessible cybersecurity solutions and education.

Did You Know?

60% of small businesses close within six months of a cyber attack.
