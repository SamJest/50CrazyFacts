How NFTs Are Influencing Intellectual Property Law

NFTs complicate copyright in unexpected ways.

Because NFTs are ownership tokens, not the artwork itself, legal questions arise: does owning an NFT grant copyright? How does resale impact IP rights? Lawyers and courts are navigating this novel terrain. Contracts and licensing are evolving to clarify rights.

Why This Matters

This matters because legal frameworks must adapt to blockchain ownership.

It also has implications for creative industries and digital rights management.

Did You Know?

Ownership of an NFT doesn’t automatically include copyright of the artwork.
