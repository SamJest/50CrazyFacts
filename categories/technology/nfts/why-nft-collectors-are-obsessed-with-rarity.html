Why NFT Collectors Are Obsessed With Rarity

Rarity is worth more than the art itself sometimes.

Many NFT collections assign rarity scores or traits to each token. Rare traits drive secondary market prices and collector competition. Randomized minting creates scarcity naturally. Communities debate which traits are most desirable.

Why This Matters

The impact matters because rarity affects valuation and speculation in digital markets.

It also encourages gamified collecting behavior among buyers.

Did You Know?

Rarity determines the value of many NFT collectibles.
