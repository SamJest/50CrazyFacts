Why Some NFTs Are Entirely Algorithm-Generated

NFTs don’t need humans to make them—they can create themselves.

Generative NFTs are produced using algorithms, AI, or procedural code. Collections like CryptoPunks and Art Blocks feature thousands of unique algorithmically-created artworks. Collectors often buy randomly-generated traits, making rarity a key factor. This removes the need for traditional artistic effort while creating high-value, unique pieces.

Why This Matters

It matters because it challenges notions of authorship and creativity.

It also demonstrates the merging of technology, art, and market economics.

Did You Know?

Some NFTs are completely created by algorithms without human intervention.
