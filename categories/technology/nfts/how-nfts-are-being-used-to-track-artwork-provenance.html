How NFTs Are Being Used to Track Artwork Provenance

Ownership history goes on-chain.

NFTs store digital provenance for physical or digital art. Each transfer is recorded, preventing forgery and enabling transparency. Collectors can verify the full history of ownership. This increases trust and reduces fraud.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFT provenance protects authenticity and value.

It also changes how art is bought, sold, and insured.

Did You Know?

NFTs provide verifiable ownership and provenance for art pieces.
