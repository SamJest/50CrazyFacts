How NFTs Started as Digital Collectibles in 2014

NFTs weren’t always multi-million-dollar digital art—they began as quirky collectibles.

The first NFT project, “Quantum,” was created by Kevin McCoy in 2014. It was a simple digital artwork stored on a blockchain, designed to prove ownership of a digital item. At the time, people didn’t anticipate NFTs becoming a massive cultural and financial phenomenon. Quantum sold for a small amount initially, but the concept demonstrated that digital scarcity could exist.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows NFTs’ roots in proving digital ownership, laying the groundwork for the future market explosion.

Understanding early NFT history helps contextualize the current hype and speculation.

Did You Know?

The first NFT was created in 2014 and was a single digital artwork.
