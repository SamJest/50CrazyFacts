How NFTs Are Being Used to Tokenize Intellectual Property in Film

Movies go blockchain for investment and rights.

NFTs can represent film IP, giving buyers a stake in profits or exclusive perks. Smart contracts manage revenue sharing and rights tracking. This enables fractional investment in entertainment projects. Blockchain prevents disputes and ensures transparency.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs transform media financing and distribution.

It also allows fans to participate in creative industries.

Did You Know?

NFTs can represent ownership or rights in films or media projects.
