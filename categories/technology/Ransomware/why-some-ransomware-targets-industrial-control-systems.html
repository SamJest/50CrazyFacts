Why Some Ransomware Targets Industrial Control Systems

Hackers don’t just want data—they want machines.

Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and SCADA networks are increasingly targeted. Ransomware can stop production lines, chemical plants, or energy grids. Attacks like WannaCry and NotPetya demonstrated the risk of operational disruption. ICS environments often lack modern security defenses. Consequences can include physical damage and safety hazards.

Why This Matters

It matters because critical infrastructure is at risk. Operational continuity and human safety are threatened.

It also forces integration of IT and OT cybersecurity strategies.

Did You Know?

Ransomware can paralyze industrial control systems.
