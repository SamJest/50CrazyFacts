How Ransomware Can Target IoT Devices

Your smart fridge or camera could be next. Internet-of-Things devices aren’t safe.

IoT devices often have weak security defaults. Ransomware can lock smart cameras, home automation systems, or connected appliances. Botnets use IoT devices to spread malware. Limited update mechanisms make infection persistent. While less common than PCs, attacks on IoT are rising.

Why This Matters

It matters because personal safety and privacy are threatened. Security practices for IoT are lagging.

It also signals a future attack vector as homes and cities get smarter.

Did You Know?

Ransomware can infect IoT devices, locking or exploiting them.
