Why Ransomware Attack Recovery Can Take Weeks

Decrypting isn’t the same as recovering.

Ransomware recovery is complex. Even after payment or decryption, systems need validation, malware removal, and integrity checks. Dependencies, legacy applications, and network complexity slow restoration. Recovery teams often work under pressure to restore business operations. Downtime and lost productivity add to the financial impact.

Why This Matters

It matters because downtime affects revenue, reputation, and trust. Preparation reduces delays.

It also highlights the need for tested incident response and recovery plans.

Did You Know?

Ransomware recovery often takes weeks, even after decryption.
