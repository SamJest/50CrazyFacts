AI Can Diagnose Eye Diseases From Scans

Imagine AI detecting vision-threatening conditions before symptoms appear. AI ophthalmology is saving sight.

AI analyzes retinal scans and optical images to detect diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and macular degeneration. The technology identifies subtle anomalies invisible to the human eye. Hospitals deploy AI-assisted diagnosis to improve early detection and prevent blindness.

Why This Matters

AI enhances patient care and reduces strain on specialists.

It also demonstrates AI’s potential for precision medicine in routine screenings.

Did You Know?

Some AI systems diagnose eye diseases with over 94% accuracy in clinical trials.
