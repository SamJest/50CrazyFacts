How NFTs Can Include Interactive Experiences

NFTs aren’t static—they can be immersive.

Some NFTs embed interactive elements, such as animations, 3D models, or playable games. Ownership may unlock special features or experiences. This increases engagement and value beyond static images. Interactivity blurs lines between art, gaming, and technology.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs evolve into dynamic digital experiences.

It also demonstrates the convergence of media, entertainment, and blockchain technology.

Did You Know?

Some NFTs include interactive features like games or 3D animation.
