How NFTs Are Being Used for Music Rights Management

Blockchain tracks royalties automatically.

NFTs represent rights to songs, albums, or beats. Smart contracts distribute royalties to owners automatically when the NFT is sold or streamed. This reduces disputes and intermediaries. Artists retain control and transparency in revenue.

Why This Matters

It matters because NFTs improve efficiency and fairness in music royalty distribution.

It also empowers creators to monetize directly.

Did You Know?

NFTs manage music rights and royalty payments on blockchain.
