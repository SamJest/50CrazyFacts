How Serverless Computing Simplifies Compliance Audits

Compliance gets easier when infrastructure disappears.

Serverless platforms standardize infrastructure management across applications. Logging, access control, and encryption are handled centrally. This reduces variation and misconfiguration. Auditors review policies instead of individual servers. Compliance becomes more predictable and manageable.

Why This Matters

Audit preparation time decreases significantly. Risk exposure drops.

This benefits regulated industries like finance and healthcare.

Did You Know?

Serverless centralizes compliance controls at the platform level.
