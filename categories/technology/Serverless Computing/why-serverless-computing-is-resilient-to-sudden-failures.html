Why Serverless Computing Is Resilient to Sudden Failures

Failures are inevitable. Serverless is built expecting them.

Each serverless function execution is isolated from others. A failure doesn’t crash the entire system. Automatic retries and redundancy handle transient issues. There’s no single point of failure. This design increases overall system resilience.

Why This Matters

System reliability improves even under unstable conditions. Outages are contained.

This resilience is crucial for mission-critical applications.

Did You Know?

Serverless isolates failures at the function level.
