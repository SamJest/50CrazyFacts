The Hidden Orchestration Behind Serverless Workflows

Single functions are powerful—but chaining them is where magic happens.

Serverless workflows coordinate multiple functions into complex processes. Tools like state machines manage retries, branching logic, and error handling. This enables long-running business processes without long-running servers. Each step executes independently and recovers gracefully from failure. The result is reliable automation at scale.

Why This Matters

Complex processes become easier to manage and monitor. Failure handling is built in.

This unlocks sophisticated automation without traditional infrastructure overhead.
