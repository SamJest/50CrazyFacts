Quantum Networks: Building the Quantum Internet

Quantum entanglement allows computers to connect in ways classical networks cannot.

By linking quantum nodes through entangled photons, quantum networks transmit information with perfect security. Experiments demonstrate multi-node networks over kilometers, laying the groundwork for a global quantum internet.

Why This Matters

This could revolutionize communications, banking, and government operations.

It also enables distributed quantum computing, where tasks are shared across distant quantum devices.

Did You Know?

Quantum teleportation protocols allow secure, long-distance transfer of qubits across fiber-optic networks.
