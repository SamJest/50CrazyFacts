Quantum Optics: Light as a Computing Tool

Quantum optics manipulates photons for computation, communication, and sensing.

Photons act as qubits in linear optical quantum computing, taking advantage of superposition and interference. Optical circuits can perform complex operations at near-light speed.

Why This Matters

Photon-based qubits reduce decoherence and allow room-temperature operation.

Quantum optics could enable scalable quantum networks and integrated photonic processors.

Did You Know?

Experiments create entangled photons and logic gates on chip-scale optical devices.
