Why Phishing Remains Effective Despite Warnings

Training isn’t perfect.

Even with awareness campaigns, phishing remains a top cause of breaches. Attackers constantly evolve strategies, from email wording to domain spoofing. Humans naturally trust familiar cues. Complacency and fatigue reduce vigilance. Continuous adaptation is required.

Why This Matters

It matters because no single solution stops phishing.

It also shows why layered security is necessary.

Did You Know?

Over 90% of cyberattacks start with phishing, despite widespread warnings.
