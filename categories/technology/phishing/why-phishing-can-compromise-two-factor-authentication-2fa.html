Why Phishing Can Compromise Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Extra security isn’t always safe.

Attackers use phishing pages that capture passwords and 2FA codes simultaneously. Real-time harvesting allows immediate account takeover. SMS-based 2FA is especially vulnerable. Attackers exploit trust in security measures. Even multi-layered defenses can fail.

Why This Matters

It matters because phishing can circumvent supposedly secure systems.

It also highlights the need for app-based or hardware token 2FA.

Did You Know?

Over 10% of phishing attacks successfully bypass 2FA.
