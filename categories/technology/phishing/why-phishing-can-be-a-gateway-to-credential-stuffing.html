Why Phishing Can Be a Gateway to Credential Stuffing

One stolen password opens many doors.

Phishers often reuse credentials across platforms. Attackers test stolen passwords on multiple accounts automatically. If users reuse logins, multiple accounts are compromised instantly. Credential stuffing spreads the damage. It shows how one phishing success multiplies risk.

Why This Matters

It matters because password reuse dramatically increases exposure.

It also emphasizes password uniqueness and management.

Did You Know?

Over 80% of compromised accounts from phishing are reused on other sites.
