How Phishing Attacks Can Lead to Ransomware Infections

The click that locks everything.

Phishing emails are often the initial vector for ransomware. Links or attachments deliver malicious code. Once inside, ransomware encrypts files and demands payment. Organizations may face operational paralysis. Recovery is costly and complex. Attackers combine phishing and ransomware for maximum leverage.

Why This Matters

It matters because phishing is the first step in catastrophic cyberattacks.

It also highlights the need for proactive threat detection.

Did You Know?

Over 90% of ransomware incidents start with a phishing email.
