How Phishing Started and Evolved Into a Multi-Billion Dollar Threat

The origins of phishing are stranger than fiction.

Phishing began in the mid-1990s targeting AOL users with fake login prompts. Early attackers mimicked official emails to steal passwords. Over time, methods evolved into sophisticated campaigns using social engineering, fake websites, and urgent messaging. Today, phishing is the top vector for cybercrime globally. What started as amateur pranks has become an organized criminal enterprise.

Why This Matters

The history of phishing matters because understanding its evolution helps in developing countermeasures.

It also shows that even small vulnerabilities can scale into massive security threats.

Did You Know?

Phishing started as early “AOL password scams” in the 1990s.
