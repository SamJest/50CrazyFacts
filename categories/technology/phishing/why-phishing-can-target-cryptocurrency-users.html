Why Phishing Can Target Cryptocurrency Users

Crypto wallets are lucrative.

Attackers impersonate exchanges or wallets like Coinbase or Binance. Emails may request private keys, seed phrases, or login details. Cryptocurrency is irreversible once stolen. Phishers exploit lack of regulation and trust in unfamiliar interfaces.

Why This Matters

The tactic matters because digital assets are prime targets.

It also shows why security education is critical for crypto users.

Did You Know?

Over 20% of crypto-related breaches start with phishing.
