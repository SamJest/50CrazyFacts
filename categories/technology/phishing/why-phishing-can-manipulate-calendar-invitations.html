Why Phishing Can Manipulate Calendar Invitations

Calendar traps hide in plain sight.

Attackers send fake meeting invites via Outlook, Google Calendar, or Zoom. Accepting or clicking links can trigger malware or steal credentials. Automated calendar scripts increase reach. Users often trust invitations from familiar names. Phishing leverages routine workplace behavior.

Why This Matters

It matters because everyday tools can be weaponized.

It also highlights the need for caution with shared calendars.

Did You Know?

Calendar-based phishing attacks rose significantly with remote work.
