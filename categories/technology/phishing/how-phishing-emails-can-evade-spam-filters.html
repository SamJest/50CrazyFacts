How Phishing Emails Can Evade Spam Filters

Spam filters aren’t foolproof.

Phishers use techniques like randomizing content, using compromised domains, and embedding images instead of text. Filters rely on patterns, which attackers manipulate. Even sophisticated AI-powered filters can be bypassed. Users receive malicious emails in primary inboxes.

Why This Matters

It matters because technical defenses must be constantly updated.

It also shows why human verification is critical.

Did You Know?

More than 25% of phishing emails bypass traditional spam filters.
