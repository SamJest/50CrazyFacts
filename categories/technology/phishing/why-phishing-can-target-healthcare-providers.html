Why Phishing Can Target Healthcare Providers

Life-saving access is valuable.

Healthcare phishing targets patient records, billing systems, and internal communications. Attackers impersonate colleagues or vendors. Hospitals and clinics often lack robust cybersecurity. Compromised data affects patient safety, privacy, and billing. Healthcare is highly attractive for cybercriminals.

Why This Matters

It matters because the stakes are human lives and sensitive data.

It also drives regulations like HIPAA.

Did You Know?

Healthcare organizations report phishing as the most frequent cyberattack vector.
