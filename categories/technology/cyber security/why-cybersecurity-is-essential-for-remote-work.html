Why Cybersecurity Is Essential for Remote Work

Your home office is a target.

Remote work expanded the attack surface with personal devices, home networks, and unsecured endpoints. Cybersecurity ensures VPNs, multi-factor authentication, and endpoint monitoring. Phishing attacks exploit remote workers’ isolation. Secure collaboration tools reduce risk. Organizations must implement policies and training.

Why This Matters

It matters because remote work is likely to remain widespread.

It also highlights the integration of IT and security for productivity.

Did You Know?

Cyber attacks on remote workers surged over 400% during the pandemic.
