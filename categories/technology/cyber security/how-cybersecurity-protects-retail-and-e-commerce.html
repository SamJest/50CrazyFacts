How Cybersecurity Protects Retail and E-Commerce

Your shopping cart is a potential target.

E-commerce platforms handle payment information, personal data, and order history. Cybersecurity protects transactions, encrypts sensitive data, and detects fraud. Attackers exploit weaknesses in websites or third-party integrations. Safe shopping depends on secure coding, monitoring, and user awareness.

Why This Matters

It matters because retail breaches impact both finances and trust.

It also emphasizes the importance of PCI compliance and security audits.

Did You Know?

Over $100 billion in retail losses result from cybercrime annually.
