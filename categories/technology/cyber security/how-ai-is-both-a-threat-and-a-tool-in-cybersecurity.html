How AI Is Both a Threat and a Tool in Cybersecurity

Artificial intelligence fights fire with fire.

AI analyzes patterns, detects anomalies, and predicts threats faster than humans. But attackers also use AI to craft smarter phishing, bypass security, and exploit vulnerabilities. It’s a technological arms race. Defensive AI monitors networks, while offensive AI tests weaknesses. The dual-use nature complicates strategy.

Why This Matters

It matters because AI will define the next generation of cyber threats.

It also underscores the need for AI governance and ethical frameworks in cybersecurity.

Did You Know?

AI-powered cyber attacks are projected to grow alongside defensive AI systems.
