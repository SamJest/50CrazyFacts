How Cybersecurity Protects Smart Cities

Even urban infrastructure needs a firewall.

Smart cities integrate IoT devices, traffic management, energy grids, and public services. Cybersecurity ensures these systems remain operational and protected from attacks. Breaches could cause blackouts, traffic chaos, or public safety risks. Security involves monitoring, encryption, and emergency response planning. Coordination across agencies is critical.

Why This Matters

It matters because citywide systems affect millions of lives.

It also highlights the increasing dependency on secure digital infrastructure.

Did You Know?

Smart city systems have been targeted by hackers multiple times globally.
