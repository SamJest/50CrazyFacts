Why Cybersecurity Protects Transportation Systems

Planes, trains, and cars need digital defense.

Transportation networks rely on digital systems for safety, scheduling, and logistics. Cybersecurity prevents disruptions, hijacking, or data theft. From air traffic control to public transit, secure communication is essential. Failures can endanger passengers and commerce. It combines IT and operational technology protection.

Why This Matters

It matters because transportation underpins economies and safety.

It also emphasizes securing both public and private transit systems.

Did You Know?

Cyber attacks have disrupted airport operations and train networks worldwide.
