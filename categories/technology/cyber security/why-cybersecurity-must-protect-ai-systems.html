Why Cybersecurity Must Protect AI Systems

Even smart machines need security.

AI and machine learning systems are vulnerable to data poisoning, model theft, and adversarial attacks. Cybersecurity ensures integrity, confidentiality, and availability of AI systems. Malicious actors can manipulate outputs or leak sensitive data. Defense includes monitoring, validation, and encryption. AI security is critical for autonomous systems.

Why This Matters

It matters because AI underpins finance, healthcare, and critical infrastructure.

It also highlights emerging threats in the era of intelligent machines.

Did You Know?

Adversarial attacks can cause AI misclassifications with serious consequences.
