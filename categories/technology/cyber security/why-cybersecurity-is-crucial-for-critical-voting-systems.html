Why Cybersecurity Is Crucial for Critical Voting Systems

Your vote relies on digital protection.

Election systems store sensitive voter information and tabulate results electronically. Cybersecurity ensures encryption, access control, and monitoring against interference. Even minor vulnerabilities can undermine confidence in democratic processes. Governments deploy audits and redundant systems. Training and protocol adherence are essential.

Why This Matters

It matters because democracy can be compromised through digital attacks.

It also emphasizes continuous testing and resilience of election infrastructure.

Did You Know?

Over 100 million voter records have been exposed through cybersecurity incidents.
