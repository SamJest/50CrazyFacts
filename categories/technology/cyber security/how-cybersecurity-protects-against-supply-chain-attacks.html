How Cybersecurity Protects Against Supply Chain Attacks

The weakest link isn’t always yours.

Attackers compromise third-party vendors to infiltrate larger organizations. Malware, ransomware, and credential theft are common vectors. Cybersecurity requires vetting suppliers, monitoring integrations, and incident response. Notable attacks like SolarWinds demonstrate the scale. Supply chain security extends beyond corporate firewalls.

Why This Matters

It matters because interconnected networks can propagate vulnerabilities.

It also emphasizes third-party risk management.

Did You Know?

Supply chain attacks increased by 300% over the past five years.
