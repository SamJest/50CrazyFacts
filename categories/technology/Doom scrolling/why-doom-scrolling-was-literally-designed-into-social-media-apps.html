Why Doom Scrolling Was Literally Designed Into Social Media Apps

Doom scrolling didn’t happen by accident.

The endless scroll feature was intentionally engineered to remove stopping cues. Platforms learned early that if users never hit a “last page,” they stayed longer. Algorithms then learned to surface emotionally charged content because it keeps attention hooked. The result is a loop where bad news feels impossible to escape. Your brain never gets the signal to stop. It’s like a slot machine that refreshes itself.

Why This Matters

This matters because design choices directly shape mental health outcomes.

Understanding intent helps users regain control over their habits.

Did You Know?

Endless scroll was popularized by social platforms in the early 2010s to increase session time.
