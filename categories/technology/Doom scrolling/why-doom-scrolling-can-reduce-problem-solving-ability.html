Why Doom Scrolling Can Reduce Problem-Solving Ability

Stress narrows focus.

Overload from negative content impairs executive function. The brain focuses on threat rather than solutions. Creativity and flexible thinking decline. Users struggle to plan effectively. The more scrolling, the harder thinking becomes. Cognitive capacity diminishes.

Why This Matters

It matters because problem-solving is essential for daily life and work.

It also shows mental costs of compulsive media use.

Did You Know?

Heavy doom scrolling reduces cognitive flexibility.

Source

[National Institutes of Health, nih.gov]
