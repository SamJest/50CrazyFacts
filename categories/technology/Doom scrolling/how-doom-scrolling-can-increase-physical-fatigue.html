How Doom Scrolling Can Increase Physical Fatigue

Mental strain becomes physical.

Constant stress activation drains energy. Muscles tense subtly during scrolling. Sleep quality declines. Eye strain and posture compound fatigue. The body reacts as if under threat. Exhaustion follows without exertion.

Why This Matters

It matters because fatigue impacts health.

It also reduces resilience to stress.

Did You Know?

Digital stress can cause real fatigue.
