How Doom Scrolling Trains Your Brain to Expect Bad News

The feed rewires expectations.

Repeated exposure to negative headlines biases perception. The brain begins predicting danger everywhere. Neutral information feels less important. This negativity bias becomes self-reinforcing. Algorithms notice engagement and serve more of the same. Over time, optimism declines measurably.

Why This Matters

It matters because worldview shapes behavior and mental health.

It also affects trust and social cohesion.

Did You Know?

Negative content is shared faster than positive content online.
