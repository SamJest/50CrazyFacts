Why Doom Scrolling Is Linked to Burnout

Burnout isn’t just work-related.

Constant negative input drains emotional reserves. Recovery never fully occurs. The nervous system stays activated. Fatigue builds quietly. Symptoms mirror occupational burnout. Digital stress becomes cumulative.

Why This Matters

It matters because burnout impacts health.

It also broadens how burnout is understood.

Did You Know?

Non-work stress contributes to burnout risk.
