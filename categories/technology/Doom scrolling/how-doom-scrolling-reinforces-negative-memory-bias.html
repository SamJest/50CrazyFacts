How Doom Scrolling Reinforces Negative Memory Bias

The mind remembers the worst.

Repeated exposure to alarming content strengthens recall of negative events. Positive or neutral information fades faster. The brain encodes negativity more deeply. This bias influences judgments and mood. Over time, the world seems worse than it is. Emotional memory is skewed.

Why This Matters

It matters because biased memory shapes worldview and behavior.

It also fuels pessimism and anxiety cycles.

Did You Know?

Negative news is recalled more vividly than positive news.
