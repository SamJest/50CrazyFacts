Why Doom Scrolling Can Trigger Existential Anxiety

Big questions surface fast.

Exposure to global crises prompts existential thoughts. Mortality, meaning, and control come into focus. Doom scrolling accelerates these reflections. Without resolution, anxiety spikes. The mind spirals inward. Digital feeds amplify philosophical distress.

Why This Matters

It matters because existential anxiety is intense.

It also explains emotional overwhelm.

Did You Know?

Global threats increase existential thinking.
