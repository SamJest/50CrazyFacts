How Doom Scrolling Thrives During Times of Uncertainty

Chaos feeds the scroll.

Periods of uncertainty heighten information-seeking behavior. The brain craves predictability. Doom scrolling promises answers but rarely delivers closure. Each update resets the anxiety cycle. This keeps users locked into feeds. Uncertainty becomes monetized attention.

Why This Matters

It matters because crises amplify harmful habits.

It also explains usage spikes during global events.

Did You Know?

News consumption surges during uncertain periods.
