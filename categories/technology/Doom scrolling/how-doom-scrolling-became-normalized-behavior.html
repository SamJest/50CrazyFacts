How Doom Scrolling Became Normalized Behavior

It feels socially acceptable.

News consumption is culturally rewarded. Being “up to date” signals awareness. Doom scrolling hides behind responsibility. Social validation masks harm. Platforms reinforce the norm. What’s normalized feels harmless.

Why This Matters

It matters because norms shape habits.

It also delays recognition of harm.

Did You Know?

High news consumption is socially praised.
