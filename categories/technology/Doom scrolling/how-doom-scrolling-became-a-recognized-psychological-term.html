How Doom Scrolling Became a Recognized Psychological Term

So new it has a birthday.

The term “doom scrolling” entered mainstream language around 2020. It rose during global crises and lockdowns. Psychologists noticed a pattern of compulsive negative news consumption. Media adopted the phrase rapidly. It now appears in academic research and health guidance. Few digital behaviors gained a name so fast.

Why This Matters

This matters because naming behavior makes intervention possible.

It also validates shared experiences.

Did You Know?

Doom scrolling was added to dictionaries within years.
