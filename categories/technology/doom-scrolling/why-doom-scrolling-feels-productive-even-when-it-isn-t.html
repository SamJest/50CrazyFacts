Why Doom Scrolling Feels Productive Even When It Isn’t

Scrolling headlines feels like staying informed.

The brain mistakes information intake for action. Doom scrolling creates the illusion of preparedness. You feel busy, alert, and engaged. But no real problem-solving happens. This false productivity soothes anxiety temporarily. The relief fades quickly, restarting the loop.

Why This Matters

It matters because perceived productivity replaces meaningful action.

It also crowds out restorative downtime.

Did You Know?

Feeling informed does not equal being prepared.
