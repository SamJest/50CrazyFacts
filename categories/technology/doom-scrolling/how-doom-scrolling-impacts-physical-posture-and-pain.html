How Doom Scrolling Impacts Physical Posture and Pain

Sitting hurts more than you think.

Prolonged scrolling leads to slouching, neck strain, and eye fatigue. Micro-movements reduce circulation. Muscle tension accumulates. Poor posture triggers headaches and back pain. The body compensates over time. Pain and discomfort become routine.

Why This Matters

It matters because posture affects long-term musculoskeletal health.

It also influences overall comfort and activity.

Did You Know?

Chronic scrolling posture correlates with neck and back strain.
