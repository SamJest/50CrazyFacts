How Doom Scrolling Triggers the Same Brain Chemicals as Gambling

Scrolling bad news feels awful—yet addictive.

Doom scrolling activates dopamine pathways similar to gambling and video games. Every refresh offers the possibility of new information, triggering anticipation. Even negative content can reinforce the loop because the brain craves certainty. Stress hormones spike, but dopamine still fires. The brain confuses anxiety-driven vigilance with reward. This keeps users scrolling despite emotional exhaustion.

Why This Matters

It matters because addiction mechanisms are being activated unintentionally.

It also explains why stopping feels harder than expected.

Did You Know?

Brain scans show variable reward systems respond to unpredictable feeds.

Source

[Harvard Medical School, hms.harvard.edu]
