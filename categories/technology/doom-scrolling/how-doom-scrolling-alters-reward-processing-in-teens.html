How Doom Scrolling Alters Reward Processing in Teens

Teen brains are especially vulnerable.

Developing prefrontal cortices respond strongly to novelty. Doom scrolling floods dopamine pathways. Teens become more sensitive to highs and lows. Emotional reactivity increases. Self-regulation struggles. Habits may persist into adulthood.

Why This Matters

It matters because early exposure shapes long-term behavior.

It also influences emotional resilience and risk-taking.

Did You Know?

Teen engagement with endless feeds can reshape reward circuitry.
