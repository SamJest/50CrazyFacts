Why Doom Scrolling Is Harder to Stop Than Binge-Watching

Endless beats episodic.

Unlike shows, feeds lack natural endings. There’s no credits or episode wrap-up. The brain waits for resolution that never comes. Each scroll promises “one more update.” This removes stopping cues entirely. Self-regulation collapses over time.

Why This Matters

It matters because structure helps control behavior.

It also shows why design matters.

Did You Know?

Stopping cues reduce compulsive use dramatically.
