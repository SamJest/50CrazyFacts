VR Gaming Can Cause Virtual Addiction

Immersion has a dark side.

Extreme VR engagement can lead to addictive behaviors, with players spending excessive hours in virtual worlds. Symptoms include neglecting real-life responsibilities, social withdrawal, and disrupted sleep. Some governments and researchers are monitoring VR use and recommending healthy limits.

Why This Matters

It matters because VR designers must consider user wellbeing.

It also raises social and psychological concerns about virtual lifestyles.

Did You Know?

Clinical studies report compulsive VR use in a small percentage of players.

Source

[American Psychiatric Association, psychiatry.org]
