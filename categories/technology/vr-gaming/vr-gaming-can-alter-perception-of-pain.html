VR Gaming Can Alter Perception of Pain

Pain relief through immersion.

💥 Impact

It matters because VR offers a non-pharmacological pain management tool.

It also suggests broader therapeutic applications of VR technology.

🤯 Did You Know?

VR has been shown to reduce pain perception by up to 50% in clinical studies.

Studies show that immersive VR experiences can reduce perceived pain in medical procedures or chronic conditions. The distraction of VR, combined with engagement and visual stimuli, reduces activity in pain-related brain regions. Hospitals use VR for burn treatments, injections, and physiotherapy.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
