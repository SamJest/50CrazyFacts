VR Gaming Can Trigger Motion Sickness

The stomach reacts first.

Some players experience nausea, dizziness, or disorientation when VR visuals don’t perfectly match physical movement. High-speed motion, latency, or poor frame rates increase symptoms. Developers use teleportation mechanics, comfort modes, and calibration to reduce risk.

Why This Matters

It matters because user comfort is crucial for adoption.

It also highlights the importance of ergonomic and technical design in VR.

Did You Know?

VR-induced motion sickness affects up to 40% of new users.

Source

[Frontiers in Psychology, frontiersin.org]
