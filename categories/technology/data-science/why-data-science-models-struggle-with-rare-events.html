Why Data Science Models Struggle With Rare Events

Rare events don’t leave many clues. Data science has little to learn from.

Events like system failures or natural disasters occur infrequently. Limited data makes modeling difficult. Synthetic data and simulations help fill gaps. Still, uncertainty remains high. Data scientists must communicate limitations clearly.

Why This Matters

This matters because rare events often carry huge impact. Overconfidence is dangerous.

It reinforces humility in predictive modeling.

Did You Know?

Rare events are difficult to model due to limited data.
