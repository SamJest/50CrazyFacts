Prince Albert Encouraged International Cooperation

Nations could collaborate.

Even rivals.

Why This Matters

Prince Albert believed in international cooperation. He supported diplomacy through science and culture. Exhibitions united countries. Shared progress mattered. Peace felt possible. His ideas were ahead of time.

This softened nationalism.

Did You Know?

It influenced global exhibitions.
