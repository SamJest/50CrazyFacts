Asteroids and Mass Extinction Links

Some asteroid impacts have caused mass extinctions. Understanding them helps prevent repeats.

Cretaceous extinction events, like the one that wiped out the dinosaurs, are linked to massive asteroid collisions. Craters like Chicxulub provide evidence of catastrophic impacts. Studying these events teaches scientists about impact mechanics, global effects, and survival strategies. Planetary defense leverages this knowledge to identify potentially deadly asteroids early. By understanding past catastrophes, agencies design mitigation plans that could prevent similar future extinction-level events.

Why This Matters

Knowledge of historical impacts emphasizes the real stakes of planetary defense.

Research inspires public interest, educational outreach, and global commitment to monitoring near-Earth objects.

Did You Know?

Geologists estimate the Chicxulub impact released energy equivalent to billions of nuclear bombs.
