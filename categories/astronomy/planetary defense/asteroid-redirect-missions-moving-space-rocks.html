Asteroid Redirect Missions: Moving Space Rocks

Scientists are planning to capture small asteroids and move them. It’s like space gardening on a cosmic scale.

Redirect missions involve using spacecraft to attach to a small asteroid and alter its trajectory slightly. These missions test gravity tug techniques, ion propulsion, and anchoring technologies. The goal is to safely maneuver potentially hazardous objects while studying their composition. Lessons from these missions improve our understanding of asteroid structure, help develop mining technologies, and validate long-term planetary defense strategies. It’s a delicate operation, requiring precise coordination and years of planning.

Why This Matters

Redirect missions offer practical experience in asteroid manipulation, enhancing readiness for real threats.

These operations provide valuable engineering experience and could also enable resource extraction or scientific experimentation in space.

Did You Know?

Some proposed missions plan to move asteroids closer to study them in orbit around the Moon.
