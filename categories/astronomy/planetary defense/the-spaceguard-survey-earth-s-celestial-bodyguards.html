The Spaceguard Survey: Earth’s Celestial Bodyguards

There’s a secret army of astronomers scanning the skies every night. They track millions of asteroids before they hit Earth.

Spaceguard is a network of telescopes and astronomers dedicated to discovering near-Earth objects (NEOs). These scientists use wide-field surveys to detect even small asteroids, tracking their orbits meticulously. By cataloging and predicting potential collisions, Spaceguard forms the first line of planetary defense. Advanced software calculates gravitational influences, rotation, and potential fragmentation. Early detection allows humanity to plan deflection missions, evacuation procedures, or impact mitigation.

Why This Matters

Spaceguard reduces uncertainty about space threats, giving governments and scientists time to respond to potential impacts.

Continuous asteroid monitoring encourages international collaboration and strengthens global awareness of space hazards.

Did You Know?

During its first decade, Spaceguard discovered thousands of potentially hazardous asteroids, reshaping our understanding of near-Earth space.
