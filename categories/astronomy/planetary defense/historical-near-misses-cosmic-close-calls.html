Historical Near Misses: Cosmic Close Calls

Earth has dodged asteroids in the past. Some events would have been apocalyptic.

Historical records and geological studies show that Earth narrowly escaped large asteroid impacts multiple times. Tunguska in 1908 flattened 2,000 square kilometers of Siberian forest. Ancient craters reveal past extinction-level events. These near misses inspire planetary defense research and public fascination. Scientists analyze orbit patterns and impact probabilities to improve detection. Each close call informs future planning, emphasizing early detection.

Why This Matters

Understanding past near misses emphasizes the importance of planetary defense to prevent repeat catastrophes.

Historical awareness boosts public and political support for space monitoring programs and global cooperation on cosmic threats.

Did You Know?

Researchers estimate that Earth experiences Tunguska-sized events roughly every 200-300 years.
