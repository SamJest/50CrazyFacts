Gravity Tractor: Tugging on Space Rocks

Gently tugging an asteroid with a spacecraft could save Earth. Patience is the ultimate weapon.

Gravity tractor techniques rely on a spacecraft hovering near an asteroid. The mutual gravitational attraction between spacecraft and asteroid gradually shifts its trajectory. Unlike impact methods, it avoids fracturing or destabilizing the object. This method requires precise station-keeping, long-term commitment, and early detection to allow small but steady influence. Simulations help optimize spacecraft mass, distance, and timing. Gravity tractors exemplify controlled, non-invasive planetary defense.

Why This Matters

Gravity tractor missions minimize risks of fragmentation and unintended consequences.

The method also develops long-duration spacecraft operations and precision station-keeping capabilities.

Did You Know?

A gravity tractor could shift a 500-meter asteroid several kilometers over a decade.
