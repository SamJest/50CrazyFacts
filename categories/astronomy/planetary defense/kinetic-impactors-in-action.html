Kinetic Impactors in Action

Smashing asteroids might save Earth. DART proved it works.

DART (Double Asteroid Redirection Test) impacted Dimorphos to test orbit alteration. Kinetic impactors transfer momentum, slightly changing trajectory over time. Precise targeting is crucial to avoid unintended fragmentation. Missions gather data on asteroid composition, spin, and orbit for future deflection planning. Simulations estimate effect for different asteroid sizes and structures. Kinetic impactors are a primary non-nuclear planetary defense tool. Results inform both policy and technical strategies for threat mitigation.

Why This Matters

Successful tests validate non-nuclear asteroid defense methods.

They provide real-world data for future missions and international planetary defense planning.

Did You Know?

DART changed Dimorphos’ orbit by about 32 minutes.
