Asteroid Mining Meets Defense

Mining asteroids could help humanity and prevent impacts. Valuable minerals and safety in one.

Asteroid mining explores extracting metals and water, but also allows manipulation of asteroid trajectories. By attaching equipment, mass, or propulsion systems, miners could subtly alter paths. This dual-use concept blends commercial opportunity with planetary protection. Missions like OSIRIS-REx and Hayabusa2 improve navigation, sampling, and anchoring technologies. Partnerships between mining companies and space agencies may develop contingency plans for threatening objects. Mining efforts also teach us about asteroid composition, crucial for impact mitigation.

Why This Matters

Mining increases both economic and defensive capabilities in space.

Collaboration between private and public sectors can accelerate technology for safety and resource use.

Did You Know?

Extracting even small mass can shift asteroid velocity measurably over years.
