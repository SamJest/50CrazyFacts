International Planetary Defense Collaboration

Cosmic threats demand global cooperation. No country can face an asteroid alone.

International collaboration involves NASA, ESA, JAXA, and other space agencies sharing data, technology, and early-warning systems. The UN endorses coordination through COPUOS and the Space Mission Planning Advisory Group. Joint simulations, workshops, and deflection exercises build trust and readiness. Collaborative frameworks also standardize risk assessment, response protocols, and communication strategies. Planetary defense is inherently a global challenge requiring diplomatic, technical, and scientific partnerships.

Why This Matters

Global cooperation strengthens resilience, reduces duplication, and maximizes resources.

Collaboration fosters knowledge-sharing, joint missions, and unified public communication during potential crises.

Did You Know?

Planetary defense exercises have involved over 20 countries in simulated asteroid impact scenarios.
