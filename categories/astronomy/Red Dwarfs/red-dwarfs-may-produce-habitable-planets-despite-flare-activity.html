Red Dwarfs May Produce Habitable Planets Despite Flare Activity

Rough neighborhoods.

Although many red dwarfs flare frequently, some orbiting planets might still maintain atmospheres through magnetic shielding or thick gas layers.

Why This Matters

This challenges assumptions about habitability around small stars.

It also expands the potential targets for searching life beyond our solar system.

Did You Know?

Even flaring red dwarfs could host worlds with liquid water.
