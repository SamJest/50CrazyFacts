Red Dwarfs Can Have Variable Brightness

Shimmering lights.

Surface activity, flares, and star spots can cause red dwarfs to vary in brightness over days to years.

Why This Matters

This matters because it affects detectability of orbiting exoplanets and climate stability on nearby worlds.

It also provides clues to internal magnetic activity of low-mass stars.

Did You Know?

Red dwarfs can flicker unpredictably like cosmic lanterns.
