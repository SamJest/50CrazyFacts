Planetary Defence and Earth’s Geological Record

Earth’s surface holds impact scars. Studying craters helps refine planetary defence models. Each crater is a data point from cosmic history.

Geological evidence reveals impact frequency and energy levels. It helps estimate how often dangerous objects strike Earth. This historical record improves modern risk assessment. Ancient impacts influenced evolution and climate. Planetary defence learns from Earth’s scars.

Why This Matters

Understanding past impacts strengthens future preparedness. History becomes a defensive tool.

This research also connects astronomy with geology and biology. It unites multiple sciences under planetary defence.

Did You Know?

The Chicxulub crater helped confirm asteroid extinction theory.
