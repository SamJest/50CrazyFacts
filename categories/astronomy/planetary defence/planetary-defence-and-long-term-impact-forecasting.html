Planetary Defence and Long-Term Impact Forecasting

Some impact predictions extend centuries into the future. Planetary defence uses mathematical models to track tiny orbital changes over time. Even minuscule forces accumulate.

Long-term forecasting accounts for gravitational nudges, solar radiation, and planetary flybys. Predictions are constantly updated as new data arrives. This process reduces uncertainty gradually. It is a living model, not a fixed answer. Scientists refine probabilities continuously.

Why This Matters

This long view allows proactive mission planning decades ahead. It turns planetary defence into a preventative science.

Long-term models also help refine climate and orbital simulations. Knowledge gained spills into other research fields.

Did You Know?

A one-in-a-million impact probability can still trigger monitoring protocols.
