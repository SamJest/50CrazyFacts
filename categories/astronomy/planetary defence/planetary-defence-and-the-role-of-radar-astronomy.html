Planetary Defence and the Role of Radar Astronomy

Radar can see what telescopes cannot. Planetary defence uses powerful radar beams to map asteroid shapes and spin rates. These details are vital for accurate deflection planning.

Radar reveals surface features, rotation speed, and internal structure. This information helps predict how an asteroid will react to external forces. Radar also refines orbital calculations dramatically. Even small changes in trajectory become measurable. These insights guide mission design.

Why This Matters

Radar improves last-minute decision-making during potential impact threats. It reduces uncertainty when timing matters most.

This technology also supports future asteroid mining and exploration missions. Planetary defence tools often benefit multiple space fields.

Did You Know?

Radar imaging can resolve details as small as a few meters on distant asteroids.
