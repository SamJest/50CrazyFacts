The Largest Black Holes Could Be 40 Billion Suns

Cosmic behemoths.

Some ultra-massive black holes, like TON 618, have estimated masses of around 40 billion solar masses. They exist in galactic centers and challenge growth models.

Why This Matters

It matters because these extreme masses test theories of black hole formation.

It also shows the diversity of black hole scales across the universe.

Did You Know?

The largest known black holes can weigh tens of billions of times more than the Sun.
