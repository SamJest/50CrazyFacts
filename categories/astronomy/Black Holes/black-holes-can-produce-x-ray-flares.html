Black Holes Can Produce X-ray Flares

Explosive signals.

When matter falls onto a black hole, it heats up to millions of degrees, emitting powerful X-ray flares. These flares can outshine entire galaxies temporarily.

Why This Matters

It matters because X-ray flares reveal active accretion and black hole growth processes.

It also helps locate hidden black holes in distant galaxies.

Did You Know?

Some black holes emit X-ray flares that briefly outshine the stars around them.
