Black Holes Can Make Stars Spin Faster

Gravitational dance.

When a star closely orbits a black hole, tidal forces can transfer angular momentum, causing the star to spin rapidly. This can lead to enhanced stellar activity and mass loss.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows how black holes influence nearby stellar evolution.

It also provides observational signatures for detecting hidden black holes.

Did You Know?

A black hole can accelerate a star’s rotation just by gravitational interaction.
