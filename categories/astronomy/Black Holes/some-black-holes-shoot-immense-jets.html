Some Black Holes Shoot Immense Jets

Powerful particle accelerators.

Accreting black holes can launch twin jets of plasma traveling near light speed, extending thousands of light-years. Magnetic fields twist and accelerate the particles, creating spectacular radio and X-ray emissions.

Why This Matters

It matters because these jets influence galaxy evolution and interstellar environments.

It also allows astronomers to study particle physics in extreme conditions.

Did You Know?

Black holes can shoot jets of matter that stretch beyond entire galaxies.
