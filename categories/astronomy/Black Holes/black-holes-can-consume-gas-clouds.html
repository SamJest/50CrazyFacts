Black Holes Can Consume Gas Clouds

Interstellar snack.

Black holes in galactic centers sometimes capture massive gas clouds, rapidly increasing accretion and producing bright flares. Observations have shown gas clouds being shredded in real time.

Why This Matters

It matters because studying these events reveals black hole feeding behavior.

It also informs galaxy evolution and high-energy astrophysics.

Did You Know?

Black holes can rip apart entire gas clouds and devour them.
