Black Holes Can Trigger Quasar Activity

Feeding frenzy.

Accretion onto supermassive black holes powers quasars, making them some of the brightest objects in the universe. The energy output can exceed that of entire galaxies.

Why This Matters

It matters because quasars illuminate early cosmic history and galaxy formation.

It also provides insight into black hole growth rates.

Did You Know?

When black holes feast, they can outshine their entire host galaxies.
