Cosmic Rays Are Studied to Understand the Sun

Variations in cosmic ray intensity help scientists study solar activity and its cycles.

During solar maxima, increased solar wind reduces cosmic ray flux reaching Earth; during minima, flux increases. Instruments on the ground and in space monitor these changes. This helps predict space weather and understand the Sun’s influence on the heliosphere.

Why This Matters

Studying cosmic rays improves forecasting of solar events that can affect satellites, communications, and power grids.

It also provides insight into solar-terrestrial interactions and the long-term effects of cosmic activity on Earth.

Did You Know?

Did you know cosmic ray intensity on Earth inversely correlates with the 11-year solar cycle?
