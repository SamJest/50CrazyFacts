Cosmic Rays Can Cause Rare “Air Showers” Visible with Special Cameras

Extremely high-energy cosmic rays create extensive air showers that can be observed indirectly.

These showers involve millions of secondary particles spreading over kilometers. Specialized detectors and fluorescence telescopes capture the faint glow caused by these interactions. The first observations of extensive air showers were made in the 1930s, paving the way for modern cosmic ray observatories.

Why This Matters

Studying air showers provides insights into the highest-energy particles in the universe.

It also informs detector design and our understanding of particle physics beyond terrestrial accelerators.

Did You Know?

Did you know an air shower from a single cosmic ray can cover several square kilometers of Earth's surface?
