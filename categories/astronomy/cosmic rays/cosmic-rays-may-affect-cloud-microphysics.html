Cosmic Rays May Affect Cloud Microphysics

Cosmic rays might influence droplet formation in clouds by ionizing air particles, subtly affecting precipitation.

Ionization can create nuclei that water vapor condenses on, forming cloud droplets. Studies since the 1990s suggest a potential link between cosmic ray flux and cloud cover, though it remains debated. This research connects astrophysics to meteorology.

Why This Matters

Understanding this connection helps refine climate and weather models.

It also demonstrates how extraterrestrial phenomena can influence Earth's hydrological cycle.

Did You Know?

Did you know some scientists propose cosmic rays influence rainfall patterns on Earth?
