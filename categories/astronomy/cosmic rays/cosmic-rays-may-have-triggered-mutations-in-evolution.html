Cosmic Rays May Have Triggered Mutations in Evolution

Over millions of years, cosmic rays might have influenced genetic mutations on Earth, subtly shaping evolution.

As high-energy particles penetrate the atmosphere, they occasionally collide with DNA molecules, causing mutations. While most mutations are harmless, some can be beneficial and drive evolutionary changes. This idea is speculative but supported by the fact that cosmic ray flux varies over geological timescales due to solar and galactic events.

Why This Matters

Considering cosmic rays in evolutionary history adds a cosmic dimension to biology.

It may explain rare mutation spikes observed in fossil records and contributes to understanding environmental impacts on evolution.

Did You Know?

Did you know cosmic rays may have played a tiny role in creating genetic diversity over Earth’s history?
