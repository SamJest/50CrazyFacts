Cosmic Rays Can Reach Extremely High Energies

The highest-energy cosmic rays carry more energy than a well-served tennis ball moving at 100 km/h, compressed into a single particle.

This extreme energy challenges our understanding of particle acceleration in the universe. Detectors like the Pierre Auger Observatory and the Telescope Array track these rare particles to study their origin. Scientists theorize they come from extraordinary astrophysical sources like active galactic nuclei or gamma-ray bursts.

Why This Matters

Studying these high-energy cosmic rays pushes the limits of physics and helps test models of the universe.

It also inspires technologies for particle detection and ultra-sensitive instruments for space research.

Did You Know?

Did you know a single ultra-high-energy cosmic ray can carry 50 joules of energy, more than any man-made accelerator particle?
