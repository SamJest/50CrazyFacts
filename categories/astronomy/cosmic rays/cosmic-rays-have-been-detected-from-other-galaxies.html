Cosmic Rays Have Been Detected From Other Galaxies

Some cosmic rays originate outside the Milky Way, offering a glimpse into extragalactic phenomena.

The highest-energy particles detected are likely extragalactic, originating from supernovae, active galactic nuclei, or gamma-ray bursts. Detecting and studying them helps scientists understand the most extreme cosmic events. Techniques include ground observatories and space detectors like AMS-02.

Why This Matters

Studying these rays provides insights into the energy processes of distant galaxies.

It also allows astrophysicists to test theories of particle acceleration at the universe’s most extreme scales.

Did You Know?

Did you know some cosmic rays hitting Earth may have traveled millions of light-years from other galaxies?
