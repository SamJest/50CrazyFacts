Cosmic Rays Can Help Detect Volcano Activity

Muon tomography from cosmic rays can be used to image the interior of volcanoes, predicting eruptions.

Muons generated by cosmic rays pass through rock, but denser magma blocks some of them. By placing detectors around a volcano, scientists can create a 3D map of magma chambers. This technique, developed in the 1990s, provides a non-invasive method to monitor volcanic activity.

Why This Matters

Studying cosmic rays in this way improves natural hazard prediction and public safety.

It also opens new applications for cosmic ray imaging in geology and civil engineering.

Did You Know?

Did you know scientists used cosmic rays to successfully map magma inside Mount Vesuvius?
