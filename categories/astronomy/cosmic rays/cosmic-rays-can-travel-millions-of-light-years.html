Cosmic Rays Can Travel Millions of Light-Years

Some cosmic rays originate in galaxies far beyond the Milky Way, traveling enormous distances before reaching Earth.

The journey of extragalactic cosmic rays can span millions of years. Despite collisions and magnetic field deflections, some of these particles reach Earth intact, providing clues about distant cosmic phenomena. Their detection requires sensitive instruments and large observatories.

Why This Matters

Studying extragalactic cosmic rays helps map energetic events in the distant universe.

It also allows astrophysicists to test models of particle acceleration and cosmic propagation.

Did You Know?

Did you know some cosmic rays hitting Earth may have begun their journey before humans existed?
