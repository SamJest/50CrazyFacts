Cosmic Rays Help Study the Universe’s Magnetic Fields

By analyzing cosmic rays, scientists can map magnetic fields in space. These invisible fields guide particles across the galaxy.

Cosmic rays are deflected by magnetic fields, and by measuring the directions of many incoming cosmic rays, astronomers infer the structure and strength of these fields. This technique helps study regions that are otherwise invisible to telescopes. Magnetic mapping using cosmic rays began in the 20th century and continues with advanced detectors.

Why This Matters

Studying magnetic fields is essential for understanding galaxy formation and cosmic ray propagation.

It also informs astrophysics models and the planning of space missions exposed to radiation.

Did You Know?

Did you know cosmic rays act like tiny probes, revealing galactic magnetic field structures?
