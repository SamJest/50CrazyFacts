Cosmic Rays Contribute to Radiation in Space

Spacecraft and astronauts encounter higher radiation levels due to cosmic rays than on Earth’s surface.

Cosmic rays penetrate spacecraft shielding and human tissue, increasing the risk of radiation sickness, cancer, and equipment malfunctions. Understanding and mitigating these risks is crucial for missions beyond low Earth orbit. NASA and ESA constantly study cosmic ray exposure to improve astronaut safety.

Why This Matters

Monitoring cosmic rays is vital for long-duration human spaceflight.

It also guides the development of advanced shielding technologies for spacecraft.

Did You Know?

Did you know astronauts on a Mars mission could receive doses hundreds of times higher than on Earth due to cosmic rays?
