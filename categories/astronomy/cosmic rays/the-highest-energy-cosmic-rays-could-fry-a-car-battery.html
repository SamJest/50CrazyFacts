The Highest-Energy Cosmic Rays Could Fry a Car Battery

Some cosmic rays are so energetic that a single particle carries more energy than a conventional car battery. It’s hard to imagine such tiny particles packing that much punch.

The most extreme cosmic rays detected, sometimes called “Oh-My-God” particles, have energies exceeding 10^20 electronvolts. This is millions of times more energetic than particles produced in any human-made accelerator. Their exact source is still debated, but they could originate from supernovae, active galactic nuclei, or even gamma-ray bursts. Detecting them requires specialized observatories such as the Pierre Auger Observatory in Argentina.

Why This Matters

Studying these ultra-high-energy cosmic rays pushes the boundaries of our understanding of physics and energy in the universe. They challenge current models of particle acceleration.

They also inspire improvements in detection technologies and can inform theoretical research in astrophysics, potentially leading to discoveries of new particles or forces.

Did You Know?

Did you know that if one ultra-high-energy cosmic ray hit a watermelon, it could theoretically vaporize it instantly?
