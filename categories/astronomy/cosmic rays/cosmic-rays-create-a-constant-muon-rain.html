Cosmic Rays Create a Constant Muon Rain

Muons from cosmic ray showers constantly rain down on Earth, passing through everything—including your body—without notice.

Muons are secondary particles produced when cosmic rays strike the atmosphere. They are heavy and fast, capable of passing through kilometers of rock and concrete. Thousands pass through each square meter every minute, silently traversing the planet. Experiments detect them in deep underground labs and even in particle detectors inside mountains.

Why This Matters

Studying muons helps physicists understand cosmic rays and particle interactions.

It also enables applications like muon tomography, used to image volcanoes or archaeological sites.

Did You Know?

Did you know thousands of muons pass through your body every minute due to cosmic rays?
