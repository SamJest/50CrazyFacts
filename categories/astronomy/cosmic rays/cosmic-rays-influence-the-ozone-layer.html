Cosmic Rays Influence the Ozone Layer

Some cosmic rays can ionize molecules in the stratosphere, subtly influencing ozone chemistry.

High-energy particles create free radicals in the upper atmosphere, which can catalyze ozone formation or destruction. While minor compared to human-made factors, this process shows how space weather interacts with atmospheric chemistry. Research in the 1970s first suggested cosmic rays had measurable atmospheric effects.

Why This Matters

Understanding this interaction helps refine climate and atmospheric models.

It also highlights the interplay between cosmic phenomena and Earth’s protective layers, affecting UV radiation levels.

Did You Know?

Did you know cosmic ray ionization can contribute to the slow breakdown of ozone molecules in the upper atmosphere?
