Earth Is the Only Planet With Liquid Water on the Surface

Water makes all the difference.

Earth’s temperature range and atmospheric pressure allow liquid water to exist, supporting life. While Mars has ice and Venus has clouds of water vapor, Earth’s surface water coverage is unique. Oceans regulate climate, support ecosystems, and enable weather cycles. Understanding Earth’s water helps compare habitability in other planetary systems.

Why This Matters

It matters because water is a key ingredient for life.

It also highlights Earth’s uniqueness in our solar neighborhood.

Did You Know?

Earth is the only planet with stable liquid water on the surface.
