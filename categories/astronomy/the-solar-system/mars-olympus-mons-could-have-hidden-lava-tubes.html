Mars’ Olympus Mons Could Have Hidden Lava Tubes

Secret tunnels.

Olympus Mons may contain massive lava tubes beneath its surface, formed during volcanic activity. These voids could shield future explorers from radiation and extreme temperatures. Studying them helps understand Martian geology and volcanism.

Why This Matters

It matters because lava tubes could serve as habitats for humans or microbial life.

It also provides clues about the planet’s volcanic history.

Did You Know?

Olympus Mons may hide enormous tunnels beneath its volcanic surface.
