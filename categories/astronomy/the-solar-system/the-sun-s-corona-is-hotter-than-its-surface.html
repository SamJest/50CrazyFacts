The Sun’s Corona Is Hotter Than Its Surface

Temperature paradox.

While the Sun’s surface is around 5,500°C, the outer corona can reach millions of degrees Celsius. Scientists suspect magnetic reconnection and wave heating as causes. This extreme heat drives the solar wind that affects the entire solar system.

Why This Matters

It matters because it challenges conventional ideas about heat sources.

It also affects space weather and Earth’s magnetosphere.

Did You Know?

The Sun’s outer atmosphere is hotter than its surface.
