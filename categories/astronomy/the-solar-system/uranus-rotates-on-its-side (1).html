Uranus Rotates on Its Side

A planet in a tilt.

Uranus’ axis is tilted about 98°, making it essentially roll around the Sun on its side. This extreme tilt may have resulted from a massive collision with an Earth-sized object early in its history. It creates extreme seasonal variations lasting over 20 years.

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows how collisions shape planetary orientation.

It also challenges models of planetary formation and climate.

Did You Know?

Uranus rotates almost entirely on its side compared to other planets.
