Earth Shares Its Orbit With Asteroids

Co-orbital companions.

Some asteroids share Earth’s orbit in a 1:1 resonance, called trojans or quasi-satellites. They drift along gravitationally stable points and may be targets for future exploration. Studying them reveals the dynamics of near-Earth objects.

Why This Matters

It matters because these asteroids could pose impact risks or offer resources.

It also informs planetary defense strategies.

Did You Know?

Earth has asteroids co-orbiting with it in gravitationally stable zones.
