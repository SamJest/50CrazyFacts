Io’s Lava Is Hotter Than Most Volcanoes on Earth

Extreme volcanism.

Io’s lava reaches temperatures up to 1,600°C, far hotter than typical terrestrial lava. The heat comes from tidal flexing caused by Jupiter’s gravity. Observations of erupting lava help understand planetary volcanism under extreme conditions.

Why This Matters

It matters because it informs theories of planetary heating and volcanism.

It also provides a contrast to Earth’s volcanic activity.

Did You Know?

Io’s lava is hotter than any volcano on Earth.
