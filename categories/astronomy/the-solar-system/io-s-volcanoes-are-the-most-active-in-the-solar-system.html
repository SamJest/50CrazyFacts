Io’s Volcanoes Are the Most Active in the Solar System

Fire and fury on Jupiter’s moon.

Io, one of Jupiter’s moons, has over 400 active volcanoes. Tidal forces from Jupiter stretch Io’s crust, generating heat that fuels eruptions. Its lava flows are sulfur-rich and extremely hot. Observing Io helps understand tidal heating and planetary geology beyond Earth.

Why This Matters

It matters because Io shows how moons can be geologically active.

It also provides a natural laboratory for studying extreme volcanic processes.

Did You Know?

Some of Io’s volcanoes erupt lava hundreds of kilometers high.
