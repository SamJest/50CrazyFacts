Jupiter’s Moons Could Host Subsurface Oceans

Potential alien oceans.

Several of Jupiter’s moons, including Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto, may have oceans beneath icy crusts. Tidal heating from Jupiter flexes the moons, preventing the oceans from freezing. These hidden oceans may harbor conditions for life.

Why This Matters

It matters because they are prime targets in the search for extraterrestrial life.

It also reshapes ideas of habitability beyond Earth.

Did You Know?

Jupiter’s moons may have more liquid water than all of Earth’s oceans combined.
