Pluto’s Heart Shaped Glacier Is Young

Not as old as the planet.

New Horizons revealed a massive nitrogen ice glacier on Pluto shaped like a heart, likely only 100 million years old. The region shows signs of convection and ongoing geological activity.

Why This Matters

It matters because even distant dwarf planets can be geologically young and active.

It also reshapes ideas about the longevity of small, icy worlds.

Did You Know?

Pluto’s heart-shaped glacier is younger than many terrestrial landforms.
