The Solar System Has a Second Sun of Sorts

Binary surprises.

Some stars have distant companions, but our solar system may have a hypothetical "Planet Nine" influencing objects in the Kuiper Belt. While not a true star, some theories suggest a small stellar remnant could exist in the distant Oort Cloud. This highlights the complex dynamics at the solar system’s edge.

Why This Matters

It matters because the outer solar system may be more dynamic than assumed.

It also opens questions about hidden objects shaping planetary orbits.

Did You Know?

A mysterious massive body may lurk beyond Pluto’s orbit.
