Ganymede Is the Largest Moon in the Solar System

Bigger than Mercury.

Ganymede, Jupiter’s moon, has a diameter of 5,268 km, making it the largest moon in the solar system. It even surpasses the size of Mercury. Ganymede has a magnetic field, icy crust, and possibly a subsurface ocean. Studying it helps understand magnetic fields and the potential for life beyond Earth.

Why This Matters

It matters because Ganymede challenges the distinction between planets and moons.

It also suggests large moons could host oceans and potentially support life.

Did You Know?

Ganymede is the only moon with its own magnetic field.
