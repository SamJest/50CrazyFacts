The Big Bang Didn’t Happen at a Single Point

The universe didn’t explode from a dot in space.

💥 Impact

The idea removes cosmic privilege. Every observer can claim centrality. Perspective becomes relative. It reshapes cosmic imagination. The universe expanded its own stage.

It corrects pop culture imagery. Not all bangs explode. Some happen everywhere.

🤯 Did You Know?

There is no center of the universe where the Big Bang occurred. Every point in space participated at once.

The Big Bang wasn’t an explosion at a location. Space itself expanded everywhere simultaneously. There is no center. Every region was once compressed together. Asking where it happened is like asking where bread rises. The explosion metaphor misleads. Expansion is stranger.
