Precession of Mercury Revealed Relativity

Mercury’s orbit wasn’t perfect.

The precession of Mercury’s perihelion deviated slightly from Newtonian predictions, later explained by Einstein’s General Relativity.

Why This Matters

This confirmed that gravity affects spacetime and refined celestial mechanics.

It also highlights limits of classical physics in extreme environments.

Did You Know?

Mercury’s orbit shifts about 43 arcseconds per century beyond Newtonian calculations.
