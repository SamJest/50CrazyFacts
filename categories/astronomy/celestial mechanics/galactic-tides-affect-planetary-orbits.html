Galactic Tides Affect Planetary Orbits

Stars in the Milky Way tug subtly on solar systems.

Galactic tides from nearby stars and dark matter can perturb Oort Cloud objects, nudging comets into the inner solar system.

Why This Matters

This influences long-term comet activity and impact risk.

It shows celestial mechanics operates across stellar and galactic scales.

Did You Know?

Some long-period comets are sent sunward by galactic tides.
