The Sun Contains 99.8% of the Solar System’s Mass

Weight of the system.

Nearly all the mass in the solar system is concentrated in the Sun. Planets, moons, asteroids, and comets account for only 0.2%. This mass dominance drives orbital mechanics and gravitational interactions.

Why This Matters

It matters because the Sun’s mass controls the structure and evolution of the solar system.

It also emphasizes the Sun’s critical role in planetary dynamics.

Did You Know?

The Sun outweighs all planets, moons, and asteroids combined by far.
