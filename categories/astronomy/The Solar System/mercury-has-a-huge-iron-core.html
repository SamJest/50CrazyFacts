Mercury Has a Huge Iron Core

Small but mighty.

Mercury’s core occupies about 85% of its radius, much larger proportionally than any other planet. This metallic interior creates a weak magnetic field. Understanding Mercury’s core helps explain planetary formation and density variations.

Why This Matters

This matters because Mercury challenges models of planet composition.

It also provides clues about how solar proximity affects internal structures.

Did You Know?

Mercury’s iron core makes it dense despite being the smallest planet.
