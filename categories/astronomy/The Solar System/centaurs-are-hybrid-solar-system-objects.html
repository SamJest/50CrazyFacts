Centaurs Are Hybrid Solar System Objects

Both asteroid and comet.

Centaurs orbit between Jupiter and Neptune, showing characteristics of both asteroids and comets. They often have unstable orbits and may eventually become short-period comets. Studying them reveals the dynamical evolution of small solar system bodies.

Why This Matters

It matters because centaurs blur classifications and inform solar system migration.

It also helps predict future cometary activity.

Did You Know?

Centaurs can behave like comets but orbit like asteroids.
