Saturn’s Moon Mimas Looks Like the Death Star

Impact branding.

Mimas, a small moon of Saturn, has a huge crater called Herschel that dominates its surface, making it resemble the fictional Death Star. This crater spans 130 km, nearly one-third the moon’s diameter.

Why This Matters

It matters because large impacts can dramatically shape small moons.

It also offers insights into crater formation and planetary defense.

Did You Know?

Mimas’ giant crater gives it a Death Star-like appearance.
