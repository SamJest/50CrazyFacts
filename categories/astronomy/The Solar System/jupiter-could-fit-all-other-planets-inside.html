Jupiter Could Fit All Other Planets Inside

It’s the ultimate giant.

Jupiter’s volume is so immense that all the other planets could fit inside it and still have room to spare. Its gravity shapes asteroid belts and influences comet trajectories. The gas giant’s magnetic field is over 20,000 times stronger than Earth’s. Studying Jupiter helps us understand gas giants and planetary formation in other star systems.

Why This Matters

This matters because Jupiter acts as a cosmic shield, reducing asteroid impacts on Earth.

It also emphasizes the scale differences among planets in our solar system.

Did You Know?

Jupiter is so large that 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.
