Jupiter’s Red Spot Is a Storm Older Than Civilization

A storm that never quits.

The Great Red Spot is a massive anticyclonic storm on Jupiter, larger than Earth. It has persisted for at least 350 years, observed since the 17th century. The storm rotates counterclockwise, with wind speeds over 400 mph.

Why This Matters

It matters because long-lived storms inform atmospheric physics.

It also shows extreme stability in fluid dynamics on planetary scales.

Did You Know?

Jupiter’s Red Spot has been raging longer than modern human civilization.
