The Sun Is 99.86% of the Solar System's Mass

Our solar system is overwhelmingly dominated by the Sun.

If you put all the planets, moons, asteroids, and comets together, they still only make up 0.14% of the solar system’s mass. The Sun is so massive that its gravity keeps every planet and object in orbit. Its enormous energy output drives climate, weather, and life on Earth. Understanding the Sun’s mass helps astronomers predict planetary motion and cosmic events.

Why This Matters

It matters because the Sun’s gravitational dominance shapes every orbital path in the solar system.

It also underscores how small and delicate planets are compared to their central star.

Did You Know?

The Sun contains more than 99% of all mass in the solar system.
