Venus Rotates Backwards

Not all planets spin the same way.

Venus rotates from east to west, opposite to Earth and most other planets. A day on Venus lasts 243 Earth days, longer than its year of 225 Earth days. The slow, retrograde rotation is likely due to massive collisions early in its formation. Its thick atmosphere creates extreme greenhouse heating, making the surface hotter than Mercury.

Why This Matters

This matters because Venus challenges assumptions about planetary dynamics.

It also helps scientists understand how collisions can drastically alter a planet’s rotation.

Did You Know?

Venus rotates in the opposite direction of its orbit around the Sun.
