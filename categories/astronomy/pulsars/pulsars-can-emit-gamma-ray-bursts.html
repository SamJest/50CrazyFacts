Pulsars Can Emit Gamma-Ray Bursts

They release extreme energy.

Some pulsars, especially magnetars, produce sudden gamma-ray bursts when magnetic fields rearrange or starquakes occur.

Why This Matters

This matters because these bursts can briefly outshine entire galaxies and inform high-energy astrophysics.

It also impacts space weather and cosmic ray studies.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can unleash flashes of gamma-ray fury detectable across the universe.

Source

FERMI Gamma-ray Space Telescope [fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov]
