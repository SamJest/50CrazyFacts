Pulsars Can Cause “Spin Glitches”

Their rotation can suddenly speed up.

Pulsars occasionally experience sudden increases in spin rate, called glitches, likely caused by internal neutron superfluid dynamics.

Why This Matters

This matters because glitches provide insight into the interior physics of neutron stars.

It also helps refine models for pulsar timing and evolution.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can hiccup in space, suddenly spinning faster.
