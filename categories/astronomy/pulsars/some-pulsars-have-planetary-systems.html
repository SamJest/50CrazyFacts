Some Pulsars Have Planetary Systems

They can host exoplanets.

A few pulsars have been found with orbiting planets, detected via timing variations in their pulses.

Why This Matters

This matters because it challenges theories of planet formation around extreme stars.

It also opens possibilities for studying exotic planetary environments.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can have planets surviving deadly stellar explosions.

Astronomical Journal [ajournal.org]
