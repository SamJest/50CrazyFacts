Pulsars Were Discovered by Accident

In 1967, scientists spotted unusual signals.

Jocelyn Bell Burnell noticed rapidly repeating radio pulses while observing quasars, which turned out to be a new class of star: pulsars.

Why This Matters

This discovery changed astronomy forever, opening the study of neutron stars.

It also proved that unexpected observations can lead to paradigm-shifting discoveries.

Did You Know?

Pulsars were found because scientists were “listening to the wrong thing.”
