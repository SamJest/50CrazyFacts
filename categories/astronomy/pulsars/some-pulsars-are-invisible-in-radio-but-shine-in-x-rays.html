Some Pulsars Are Invisible in Radio but Shine in X-rays

Not all pulsars behave the same.

Certain pulsars, called X-ray pulsars, emit primarily in X-rays and are faint or invisible in radio wavelengths.

Why This Matters

This matters because multiwavelength observations are essential to detect the full pulsar population.

It also informs models of magnetosphere structure and emission mechanisms.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can hide in radio silence but blaze in X-ray brilliance.

Source

Chandra X-ray Observatory, chandra.harvard.edu]
