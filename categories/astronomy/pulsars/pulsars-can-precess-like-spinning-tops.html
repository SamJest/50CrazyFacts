Pulsars Can Precess Like Spinning Tops

They wobble in space over time.

Some pulsars exhibit precession, a slow wobble in their rotation axis, which changes the direction of their emission beams.

Why This Matters

It matters because observing precession informs about internal structure and angular momentum distribution.

It also allows testing theories of neutron star rigidity and deformation.

Did You Know?

A pulsar can wobble like a cosmic spinning top.
