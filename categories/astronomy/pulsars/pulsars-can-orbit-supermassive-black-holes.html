Pulsars Can Orbit Supermassive Black Holes

They survive extreme gravitational environments.

Some pulsars have been found orbiting close to the supermassive black holes at galactic centers, enduring immense tidal forces.

Why This Matters

This matters because they serve as precise probes of spacetime near black holes.

It also allows testing gravitational physics in previously inaccessible regimes.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can act as cosmic test particles near black holes.
