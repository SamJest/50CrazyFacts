Some Pulsars Emit “Nulling” Pulses

They occasionally go silent.

Certain pulsars randomly stop emitting pulses for seconds to hours, a phenomenon called nulling.

Why This Matters

This matters because nulling hints at changes in the pulsar’s magnetosphere and emission processes.

It also challenges the predictability of pulsar timing for navigation and experiments.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can take unexpected “power naps” mid-beam.
