Some Pulsars Have Extremely Strong Magnetic Fields

They’re cosmic magnets on steroids.

Magnetars, a type of pulsar, can have magnetic fields over a quadrillion times stronger than Earth’s.

Why This Matters

This matters because such fields twist space-time and accelerate particles to extreme energies.

It also influences how the pulsar emits radiation and interacts with its surroundings.

Did You Know?

A magnetar’s magnetic field could erase a credit card from 1,000 km away.
