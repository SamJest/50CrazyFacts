Pulsars Can Emit Polarized Radiation

Their beams have distinct orientations.

Pulsar emissions are often highly polarized, meaning the electric field of light is aligned in a specific direction.

Why This Matters

This matters because polarization helps map magnetic field structures and probe emission mechanisms.

It also aids in studying interstellar magnetic fields.

Did You Know?

Pulsars can “twist” light across the galaxy.

Source

Chandra X-ray Observatory [chandra.harvard.edu]
