Some Pulsars Are “Recycled” by Companion Stars

They get spun up again.

Old pulsars can accrete matter from a binary companion, increasing their spin rates to millisecond periods.

Why This Matters

It matters because it explains the existence of millisecond pulsars and their extreme rotational speeds.

It also shows how stellar interactions can rejuvenate dead or slow pulsars.

Did You Know?

Recycled pulsars are like cosmic fidget spinners with a refill.
