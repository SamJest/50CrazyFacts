Pulsars Could Help Navigate Space

They’re cosmic GPS beacons.

Because pulsars emit highly regular pulses, spacecraft could use them as natural navigation references, potentially replacing or supplementing Earth-based signals.

Why This Matters

It matters because it offers autonomous navigation for deep-space missions.

It also opens possibilities for interstellar travel using natural timing signals.

Did You Know?

Pulsars could be the GPS satellites of the galaxy.
