Cosmic Shadows Reveal Event Horizons

Black holes cast shadows.

The silhouette of a black hole against glowing gas reveals the event horizon as a dark circular shadow. The Event Horizon Telescope captured this for M87*, providing direct evidence of event horizons. The size and shape of the shadow depend on the black hole’s mass and spin. Studying these shadows allows testing general relativity in strong gravity. The observation bridges theoretical predictions with real cosmic images. Shadows also help calibrate models of light bending near extreme gravity.

Why This Matters

Event horizon shadows provide the first “photographs” of black holes.

They allow astronomers to measure black hole properties with unprecedented accuracy.

Did You Know?

Event horizons cast visible shadows by bending light around them.
