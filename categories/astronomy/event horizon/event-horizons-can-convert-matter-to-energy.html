Event Horizons Can Convert Matter to Energy

E=mc² on steroids.

Matter falling into an event horizon can be converted into tremendous amounts of energy, often released as jets. Accretion disks heat up, producing X-rays and gamma rays observable across galaxies. This energy release helps regulate black hole growth and surroundings.

Why This Matters

The event horizon is not just a trap but an engine of cosmic energy.

It impacts star formation and galactic dynamics in the vicinity.

Did You Know?

Event horizons can transform infalling matter into dazzling energy displays.
