Hawking Radiation Escapes Event Horizons

Invisible energy leaks out.

Stephen Hawking predicted that black holes are not entirely black; quantum effects allow particles to escape near the event horizon as Hawking radiation. This radiation is extremely faint but theoretically causes black holes to lose mass over time. It links quantum mechanics with general relativity. Detecting it is challenging due to its minuscule intensity, but it has huge theoretical implications. This concept reshaped our understanding of black holes as dynamic, not eternal.

Why This Matters

Hawking radiation shows that event horizons are not perfect traps.

It provides a pathway to explore quantum gravity theories experimentally.

Did You Know?

Event horizons can leak tiny amounts of energy in a quantum trick.
