Event Horizons Can Warp Magnetic Fields

Magnetism on steroids.

Event horizons of charged or rotating black holes distort magnetic fields in extreme ways. This warping affects accretion disks, particle acceleration, and jet formation. Understanding these interactions informs models of high-energy astrophysical phenomena. Observations of magnetized disks reveal horizon properties indirectly.

Why This Matters

Event horizons shape the behavior of nearby magnetic fields.

It also explains some energetic cosmic events like flares and gamma-ray bursts.

Did You Know?

Event horizons can twist and amplify magnetic fields dramatically.
