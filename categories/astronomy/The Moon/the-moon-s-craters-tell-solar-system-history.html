The Moon’s Craters Tell Solar System History

Impacts frozen in time. Craters are more than scars.

Lunar craters range from tiny pits to massive basins. They record billions of years of collisions with asteroids and comets. Crater density helps estimate surface age. Ejecta blankets and ray systems preserve impact history. Large basins shaped maria formation. Studying craters informs models of planetary bombardment. They serve as natural laboratories for impact physics. Lunar craters are less eroded than Earth’s due to lack of atmosphere.

Why This Matters

It matters because craters are time capsules revealing solar system evolution.

It also guides impact risk assessment for Earth and other planets. Lunar data calibrates impact frequency models.

Did You Know?

Some craters are over 2,500 km across.
