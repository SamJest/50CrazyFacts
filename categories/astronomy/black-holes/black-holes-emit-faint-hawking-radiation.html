Black Holes Emit Faint Hawking Radiation

Evaporation at the edge.

Stephen Hawking predicted black holes emit radiation due to quantum effects near the event horizon, gradually losing mass over trillions of years. Though extremely weak, this radiation reconciles quantum theory and gravity.

Why This Matters

It matters because Hawking radiation provides a theoretical path to unify general relativity and quantum mechanics.

It also suggests black holes aren’t completely eternal.

Did You Know?

Black holes slowly leak energy through a process called Hawking radiation.
