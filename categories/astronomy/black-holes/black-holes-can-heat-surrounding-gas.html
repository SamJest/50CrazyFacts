Black Holes Can Heat Surrounding Gas

Extreme ovens.

Accretion disks around black holes heat infalling matter to millions of degrees, emitting X-rays and gamma rays. This radiation can influence nearby star formation and interstellar gas.

Why This Matters

It matters because it explains energetic phenomena in galactic centers.

It also helps in detecting otherwise hidden black holes.

Did You Know?

Black holes can turn surrounding gas into superheated glowing plasma.
