Wormholes Might Connect Black Holes

Theoretical tunnels.

Some solutions of general relativity suggest black holes could connect to other points in spacetime via wormholes, though no evidence exists. These hypothetical bridges might allow shortcuts across vast cosmic distances.

Why This Matters

It matters because it explores the limits of physics and spacetime geometry.

It also inspires scientific and popular imagination about faster-than-light travel.

Did You Know?

Black holes might theoretically connect distant regions of the universe through wormholes.
