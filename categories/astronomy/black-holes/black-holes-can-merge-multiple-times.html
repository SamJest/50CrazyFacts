Black Holes Can Merge Multiple Times

Cosmic stacking.

Some black holes may undergo successive mergers, forming increasingly massive black holes in dense galactic centers. Each merger releases gravitational waves detectable across the universe.

Why This Matters

It matters because repeated mergers contribute to the growth of supermassive black holes.

It also tests models of hierarchical galaxy and black hole formation.

Did You Know?

Black holes can collide multiple times, creating ever-larger cosmic monsters.
