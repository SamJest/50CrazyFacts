Black Holes May Contain Exotic Matter

Singular mysteries.

The extreme density inside black holes could form exotic states of matter, like quark-gluon plasma or other unknown phases. Studying this helps test physics beyond current experiments.

Why This Matters

It matters because it pushes the boundaries of quantum gravity and particle physics.

It also inspires theories of matter under incomprehensible conditions.

Did You Know?

Inside black holes, matter may exist in forms never seen on Earth.
