A Black Hole Can Rip Stars Apart

Tidal destruction.

When a star ventures too close to a black hole, tidal forces can stretch it into a long stream of gas in a process called spaghettification. This material may orbit briefly before being swallowed or emitting bursts of X-rays.

Why This Matters

Tidal disruption events help astronomers locate otherwise hidden black holes.

They also provide insights into stellar structure and extreme gravity effects.

Did You Know?

Black holes can literally stretch stars into thin streams before swallowing them.
