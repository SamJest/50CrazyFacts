Black Holes Can Merge With Neutron Stars

Cosmic collisions.

Black holes and neutron stars can form binary systems that eventually collide, producing gravitational waves and electromagnetic radiation. These mergers are rare but highly energetic.

Why This Matters

It matters because such events produce heavy elements like gold and platinum.

It also provides insights into compact object formation and extreme physics.

Did You Know?

Black holes sometimes collide with neutron stars, creating cosmic fireworks.
