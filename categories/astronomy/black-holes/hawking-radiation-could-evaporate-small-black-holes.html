Hawking Radiation Could Evaporate Small Black Holes

Tiny time bomb.

Micro black holes lose mass through Hawking radiation, eventually evaporating completely over vast timescales. This process is incredibly slow for large black holes but significant for hypothetical small ones.

Why This Matters

It matters because it suggests black holes aren’t eternal and could disappear.

It also bridges quantum physics and gravity in theoretical physics.

Did You Know?

Some black holes may vanish entirely due to Hawking radiation over time.
