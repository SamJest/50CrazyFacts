Black Holes May Influence Galactic Collisions

Cosmic puppeteers.

During galaxy mergers, central black holes can interact, sending gravitational waves and reshaping stellar orbits. These interactions can trigger starbursts and alter galactic structures.

Why This Matters

It matters because black holes are key players in galactic evolution.

It also provides clues about the growth of supermassive black holes.

Did You Know?

Colliding galaxies often feature black holes that affect star formation and dynamics.
