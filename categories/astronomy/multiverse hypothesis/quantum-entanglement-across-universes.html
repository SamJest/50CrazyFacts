Quantum Entanglement Across Universes?

Could particles be linked across universes?

Some speculative theories propose that entangled particles could exist in separate universes, maintaining correlations without interaction. This radical idea stretches the meaning of entanglement and nonlocality beyond one universe. It’s mostly theoretical, but physicists explore mathematical frameworks for such possibilities.

Why This Matters

It matters because it redefines quantum mechanics on a multiversal scale.

It could offer new ways to think about quantum information and communication.

Did You Know?

Entangled particles might have counterparts in parallel universes.
