Are We in a Simulation Within a Multiverse?

The universe could be a computer program among infinite realities.

If many universes exist, some might be advanced simulations. This merges the simulation hypothesis with multiverse theory. Observing constraints in physical laws could hint at underlying computational rules. It’s a blend of philosophy, physics, and science fiction.

Why This Matters

It matters because it challenges assumptions about reality and observation.

It also impacts theories of consciousness and artificial life.

Did You Know?

Our universe might be a simulation existing in a multiverse of realities.
