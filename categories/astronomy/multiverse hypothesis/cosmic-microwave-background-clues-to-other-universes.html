Cosmic Microwave Background Clues to Other Universes

Our universe’s baby picture might contain multiverse secrets.

Collisions with other universes could leave imprints in the cosmic microwave background (CMB). Tiny temperature anomalies or circular patterns might hint at these interactions. Instruments like Planck and WMAP analyze CMB radiation for such clues. Although controversial, these signatures could provide the first empirical evidence of other universes.

Why This Matters

It matters because it makes multiverse theory partially testable.

It also pushes observational cosmology into realms once thought purely theoretical.

Did You Know?

Some subtle CMB patterns could be caused by other universes bumping into ours.
