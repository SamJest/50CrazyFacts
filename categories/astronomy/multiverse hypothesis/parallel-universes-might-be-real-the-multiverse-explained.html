Parallel Universes Might Be Real: The Multiverse Explained

Could there be other universes just like ours—or completely bizarre? The multiverse hypothesis suggests our universe is just one of many. Some may follow different physical laws, making life impossible, while others could mirror Earth exactly.

This idea arises from quantum mechanics, cosmic inflation, and string theory. Max Tegmark and other physicists classify multiverses into levels, from slightly different regions of space-time to entirely different physical constants. These universes might be completely inaccessible or have causal interactions. It challenges our notions of reality and uniqueness.

Why This Matters

The hypothesis matters because it expands the boundaries of cosmology and physics.

It also forces philosophers and scientists to rethink probability, fate, and cosmic inevitability.

Did You Know?

Some theories suggest an infinite number of universes exist alongside our own.
