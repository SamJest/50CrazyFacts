Quantum Many-Worlds: Every Choice Creates a Universe

Every time a quantum decision occurs, a new universe branches off. Your alternate self might exist in one of these parallel worlds.

Proposed by Hugh Everett in 1957, the many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics suggests that all possible outcomes happen, but in separate universes. Schrödinger’s cat is alive in one, dead in another. While we can’t travel to these worlds, experiments like quantum decoherence give indirect evidence.

Why This Matters

This matters because it reframes our understanding of reality and probability.

It also connects the smallest quantum events to the largest cosmological consequences.

Did You Know?

According to many-worlds theory, there could be countless versions of you making different choices.
