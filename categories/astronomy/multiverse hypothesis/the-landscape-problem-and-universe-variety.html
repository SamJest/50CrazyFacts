The Landscape Problem and Universe Variety

String theory predicts a cosmic landscape of possibilities.

Each vacuum state in string theory corresponds to a universe with its own physical laws. This leads to a mind-boggling number of potential universes, sometimes estimated at 10^500.

Why This Matters

It matters because it quantifies the multiverse’s scope.

It also shows why our universe’s properties may be just one set of countless possibilities.

Did You Know?

String theory allows potentially 10^500 distinct universes.
