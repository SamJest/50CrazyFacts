Time Loops Across Universes

Some universes might experience time differently.

In certain multiverse models, time could flow backward, stop, or loop in other universes. This challenges our fundamental perception of causality and chronology. It opens possibilities for universes where effects precede causes or where time repeats indefinitely.

Why This Matters

Understanding alternate temporal flows matters because it reshapes physics’ foundational laws.

It also affects cosmology, philosophy, and theoretical predictions about universe interactions.

Did You Know?

In some universes, yesterday could come after tomorrow.
