Cosmic Inflation Seeds Infinite Domains

Inflation doesn’t stop uniformly.

Cosmic inflation may continue eternally in different regions, producing infinite domains with independent universes. Each domain could have unique features, constants, and structures.

Why This Matters

It matters because it provides a natural mechanism for infinite universe generation.

It also implies that what we see is just a tiny patch of a vast multiversal landscape.

Did You Know?

Cosmic inflation may never end in some regions, creating endless universes.
