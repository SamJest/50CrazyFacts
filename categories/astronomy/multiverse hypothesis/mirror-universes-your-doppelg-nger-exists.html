Mirror Universes: Your Doppelgänger Exists

Alternate versions of ourselves may exist.

Mirror universes, a subset of the multiverse, could feature exact copies of our universe with subtle or major differences. This implies parallel “yous” living alternate lives, depending on minor divergences in history.

Why This Matters

It matters because it challenges our understanding of identity and probability.

It also inspires thought experiments in philosophy and physics.

Did You Know?

Some version of you might exist in a mirror universe.
