Detecting Other Universes: Is It Possible?

Some researchers hope we might see evidence of other universes.

Gravitational waves, unusual patterns in the CMB, or high-energy cosmic events could hint at collisions between universes. So far, no confirmed signals exist, but projects like WMAP and Planck provide datasets to analyze. Detecting other universes would revolutionize cosmology.

Why This Matters

It matters because potential observational evidence could transform multiverse ideas from theory to empirical science.

It also guides the design of future telescopes and experiments.

Did You Know?

If multiverse collisions occur, they could leave detectable imprints on our universe.
