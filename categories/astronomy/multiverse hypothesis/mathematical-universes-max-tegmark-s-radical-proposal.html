Mathematical Universes: Max Tegmark’s Radical Proposal

All mathematically consistent structures may exist as universes.

Max Tegmark proposes that reality itself is mathematics. Every coherent mathematical object corresponds to a physical universe. This radical idea removes arbitrary constraints on existence. It extends the multiverse beyond physics into pure abstraction. It’s controversial but intellectually thrilling.

Why This Matters

It matters because it offers a unifying framework for physics, math, and philosophy.

It also forces us to reconsider what “existence” really means.

Did You Know?

Every mathematical structure might physically exist as its own universe.
