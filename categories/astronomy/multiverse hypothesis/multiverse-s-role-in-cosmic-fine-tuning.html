Multiverse’s Role in Cosmic Fine-Tuning

Why is our universe “just right”?

The multiverse explains the fine-tuning problem: out of countless universes with varying constants, only some allow stars, planets, and life. Our universe is observed because it happens to be suitable.

Why This Matters

It matters because it reframes debates about design and randomness in physics.

It also influences research in cosmology, philosophy, and probability theory.

Did You Know?

We live in a universe that works because unfit universes exist elsewhere.
