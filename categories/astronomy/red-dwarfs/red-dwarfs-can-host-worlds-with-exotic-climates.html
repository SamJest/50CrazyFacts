Red Dwarfs Can Host Worlds with Exotic Climates

Alien weather.

Planets in close orbit around red dwarfs may experience extreme temperature gradients due to tidal locking or flare activity.

Why This Matters

This matters for understanding how life could adapt to such environments.

It also enriches planetary climate modeling for non-Earth-like conditions.

Did You Know?

Some red dwarf planets might have scorching days and freezing nights permanently.
