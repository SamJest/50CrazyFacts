Red Dwarfs Can Survive in Low-Metallicity Environments

Tough pioneers.

Even in regions of the galaxy with few heavy elements, red dwarfs can form and survive, unlike more massive stars.

Why This Matters

This matters for understanding early star formation in the universe.

It also offers clues about the longevity and abundance of small stars in primitive galaxies.

Did You Know?

Red dwarfs thrive where bigger stars would fail.
