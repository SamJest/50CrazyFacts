Dark Flow Could Be a Relic of Cosmic Inflation

Could the universe still remember its birth?

Dark Flow may preserve motions from the universe’s inflationary epoch. During inflation, rapid expansion could have left imprints on large-scale velocity fields. Some galaxies retain directional motion from that time, observable billions of years later. This relic motion offers a rare window into the earliest moments of cosmic history. Studying these patterns allows testing of inflation models and predictions. It could also explain why certain regions exhibit unexpected velocities. These observations merge cosmology, astrophysics, and high-energy physics.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because it connects present observations with the universe’s infancy.

It provides evidence supporting or refining inflationary theory.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may carry whispers from the universe’s birth.
