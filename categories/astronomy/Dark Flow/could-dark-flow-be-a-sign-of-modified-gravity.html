Could Dark Flow Be a Sign of Modified Gravity?

Are Newton and Einstein missing something?

Dark Flow may indicate that our understanding of gravity is incomplete. The motion of galaxy clusters might require modifications to general relativity on cosmic scales. Alternative theories, like MOND or emergent gravity, could explain the coherent flow. If Dark Flow results from unknown gravitational effects, it may change fundamental physics. Observations across various wavelengths test these hypotheses. Resolving this could unify cosmic dynamics with theory. It also encourages new simulations exploring non-standard gravity.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because it challenges conventional gravitational theory.

It could revolutionize physics and cosmology if confirmed.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow might hint at physics beyond Einstein.
