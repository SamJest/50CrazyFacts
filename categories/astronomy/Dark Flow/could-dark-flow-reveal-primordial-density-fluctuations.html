Could Dark Flow Reveal Primordial Density Fluctuations?

Could early universe ripples still move galaxies?

Dark Flow may be a remnant of primordial density fluctuations from the early universe. Tiny variations in density during the first seconds after the Big Bang can create lasting velocity fields. Over billions of years, these ripples could produce coherent cluster motions. Observing these flows helps constrain models of the initial density perturbations. It also offers insights into inflation and the distribution of dark matter. Connecting early fluctuations to present motion bridges cosmology and observational astronomy. Studying these effects refines predictions for structure formation and CMB patterns.

Why This Matters

It matters because it links ancient universe conditions to present observations.

It provides a method to test early universe physics indirectly.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may carry echoes from the universe’s first moments.
