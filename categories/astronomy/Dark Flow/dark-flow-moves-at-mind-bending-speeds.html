Dark Flow Moves at Mind-Bending Speeds

Galaxies aren’t just sitting still—they’re racing.

Measurements suggest galaxy clusters move at velocities up to 1,000 km/s relative to the cosmic microwave background. This speed is far greater than expected from local gravitational attraction. Such velocities hint at a coherent, large-scale force acting across billions of light-years. The motion was confirmed using X-ray data of galaxy clusters. It’s unprecedented in cosmic dynamics. These speeds could alter how we measure distances and expansion. Understanding them is crucial for cosmology.

Why This Matters

It affects how we model the expansion of the universe and its age.

High-velocity motions might influence dark energy calculations and universal topology.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow velocities challenge previous assumptions of cosmic calm.
