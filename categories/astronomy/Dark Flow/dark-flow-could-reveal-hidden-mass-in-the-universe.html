Dark Flow Could Reveal Hidden Mass in the Universe

What’s pulling galaxies? Dark Flow hints at invisible matter.

Dark Flow’s directional motion may be caused by unseen massive structures like superclusters or dark matter concentrations beyond the observable universe. Gravitational tugs from these hidden masses could explain the coherent motion. The universe contains vast amounts of dark matter, but some could exist outside what we can observe. Simulations show that such hidden structures could produce flows similar to Dark Flow. Understanding this could revise mass distribution maps. It also links cosmic motion to the mysterious dark sector.

Why This Matters

This fact matters for mapping mass in the universe, known and unknown.

It could redefine our understanding of large-scale structure formation.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may point to gravity acting across unimaginable distances.
