Dark Flow Remains One of Astronomy’s Biggest Mysteries

Galaxies moving in unison, unexplained.

Cosmic surveys reveal that despite decades of study, Dark Flow remains poorly understood. The direction, magnitude, and origin of the motion defy consensus. The effect spans hundreds of millions of light-years. It persists across multiple datasets, including Planck, WMAP, and X-ray observations. Some scientists argue it’s a statistical fluke; others insist it’s real. The mystery fuels debate over the limits of observable cosmology. Its resolution could transform our understanding of cosmic evolution.

Why This Matters

The mystery matters for fundamental physics and cosmology.

It drives innovation in observation and theoretical modeling.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may indicate forces or structures beyond the known universe.
