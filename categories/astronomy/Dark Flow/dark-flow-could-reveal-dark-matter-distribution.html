Dark Flow Could Reveal Dark Matter Distribution

Could cosmic currents map the invisible?

Dark Flow may trace concentrations of dark matter beyond observable regions. Its coherent motion hints at unseen gravitational sources. By modeling the flow, scientists can predict mass distributions that are otherwise invisible. This provides a complementary method to gravitational lensing for studying dark matter. Understanding these patterns refines galaxy formation models. It also informs simulations of cosmic structure growth. Dark Flow acts as a natural cosmic experiment for unseen mass effects.

Why This Matters

This matters because it gives insights into the universe’s most mysterious component.

It could improve dark matter mapping and theoretical models.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow might serve as a cosmic dark matter probe.
