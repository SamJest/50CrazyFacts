Dark Flow and Gravitational Lensing

Can invisible mass magnify motion?

Dark Flow may interact with gravitational lensing, where massive objects bend light and influence observed cluster motion. The alignment of lensed galaxies can reveal the underlying mass causing the flow. By combining lensing maps with velocity measurements, astronomers can estimate unseen gravitational sources. This method strengthens the link between Dark Flow and hidden mass, including dark matter. It provides a complementary approach to CMB studies for tracking coherent cosmic motions. Observing lensing patterns helps quantify the strength and direction of Dark Flow. These techniques improve models for large-scale structure formation and evolution.

Why This Matters

It matters because lensing confirms the gravitational impact behind Dark Flow.

It provides new ways to trace unseen matter influencing galaxy clusters.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow interacts with mass in ways visible and invisible.
