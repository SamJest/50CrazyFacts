Dark Flow Suggests Our Universe Is Part of a Larger System

Cosmic currents hint at something bigger.

Dark Flow could indicate that the observable universe is only a small part of a larger multiverse or super-structure. Gravitational influence from beyond our cosmic horizon could explain coherent motion. This concept challenges the notion of a self-contained universe. If true, it would have implications for physics, cosmology, and philosophy. Some models predict vast mass concentrations outside our sight. Such super-structures may subtly shape motion within our observable patch. Dark Flow may provide the first observational clue of these regions.

Why This Matters

It matters because it suggests our understanding of the universe’s scale is incomplete.

It impacts theories on cosmic inflation, gravity, and multiverse models.

Did You Know?

The universe may be connected to massive, unseen structures beyond our perception.
