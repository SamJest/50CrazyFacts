Dark Flow and Cosmic Anisotropy

Does the universe have a preferred direction?

Dark Flow introduces anisotropy in cosmic motion, implying directional differences in galaxy velocities. Standard cosmology expects isotropy at large scales, but this flow suggests otherwise. Detecting these variations requires precision measurements and statistical analysis. Cosmic anisotropy impacts interpretations of large-scale structure formation. It may also inform the nature of dark energy and its distribution. Understanding these anisotropies is key to refining cosmological models. Observing directional patterns helps map unseen cosmic influences.

Why This Matters

The fact matters because it tests the limits of the isotropic assumption.

It could redefine models for cosmic uniformity and evolution.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may reveal preferred directions in the universe.
