Dark Flow and Superclusters

Could the largest cosmic structures guide motion?

Dark Flow appears correlated with supercluster positions, indicating that these massive formations influence galaxy velocities. Superclusters act as gravitational anchors, subtly pulling clusters along coherent paths. Observations suggest the flow extends beyond the reach of these visible structures, hinting at even larger unseen forces. Mapping supercluster interactions with Dark Flow helps understand hierarchical cosmic structure. These studies refine our models of mass distribution in the universe. It also impacts predictions for galaxy evolution and mergers over billions of years. Researchers combine X-ray, optical, and CMB data to analyze these effects.

Why This Matters

It matters because superclusters may be partially responsible for cosmic flow patterns.

It can improve large-scale cosmological models and simulations.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow interacts with the universe’s most massive structures.
