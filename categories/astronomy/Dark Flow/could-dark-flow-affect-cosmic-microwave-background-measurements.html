Could Dark Flow Affect Cosmic Microwave Background Measurements?

Can galaxy motion distort our oldest light?

Dark Flow introduces subtle anisotropies in the CMB by moving galaxy clusters that scatter photons. These small distortions must be accounted for when measuring temperature fluctuations. Ignoring Dark Flow could bias cosmological parameter estimates. Researchers apply statistical corrections to separate true primordial signals from motion-induced shifts. Understanding these effects refines constraints on dark energy, dark matter, and the early universe. It also highlights the complex interplay between local motion and universal signals. This ensures precise cosmological models.

Why This Matters

Dark Flow matters because it influences precision cosmology.

It ensures accurate interpretations of the universe’s earliest signals.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow subtly modifies the cosmic microwave background patterns we observe.
