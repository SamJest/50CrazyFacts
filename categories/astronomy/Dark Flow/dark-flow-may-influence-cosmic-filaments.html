Dark Flow May Influence Cosmic Filaments

Do massive threads steer galaxies?

Dark Flow might be linked to the orientation and motion along cosmic filaments. Filaments are the dense threads of the cosmic web connecting clusters and superclusters. Flow along these structures may direct galaxy motions in coherent patterns. Observing Dark Flow helps determine how these filaments shape cluster dynamics. This interaction provides insight into matter distribution and evolution. Understanding filament influence informs simulations of large-scale structure. It also bridges observations of visible galaxies with unseen mass distributions.

Why This Matters

Dark Flow matters because it clarifies the role of cosmic filaments.

It helps model how large-scale structures guide motion.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow flows along the universe’s massive threads.
