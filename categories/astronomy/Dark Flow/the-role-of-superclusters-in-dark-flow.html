The Role of Superclusters in Dark Flow

Could huge cosmic structures be the culprits? Superclusters may be tugging galaxies along.

Dark Flow could result from the gravitational pull of massive superclusters outside our visible universe. These superclusters contain billions of stars, gas, and dark matter, generating significant gravitational influence. The motion of observed galaxy clusters aligns with the direction of these hypothetical structures. This suggests that what we see is only a fraction of a much larger gravitational landscape. Understanding this influence helps map mass beyond observational limits. Simulations show superclusters could explain some of the directional motion.

Why This Matters

The implication is that unseen superclusters may dominate cosmic dynamics.

It also guides astronomers in predicting future galactic motions and mapping mass distributions.

Did You Know?

Superclusters could act as hidden highways guiding galaxies across the cosmos.
