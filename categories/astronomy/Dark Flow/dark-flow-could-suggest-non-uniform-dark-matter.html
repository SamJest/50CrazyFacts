Dark Flow Could Suggest Non-Uniform Dark Matter

Does dark matter flow in currents?

Dark Flow may imply that dark matter is distributed non-uniformly on the largest scales. Coherent motions of galaxy clusters could be caused by unseen concentrations of dark matter pulling them. This challenges the assumption of a smooth, homogeneous dark matter distribution. Detecting these effects helps refine dark matter models and simulations. It may also provide clues about dark matter particle properties. Observing cluster velocities offers indirect evidence for unseen structures. These studies help reconcile discrepancies between theory and observations.

Why This Matters

Dark Flow matters because it impacts our understanding of dark matter’s behavior.

It could reshape cosmological simulations and dark matter theories.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may reveal hidden rivers of dark matter guiding galaxies.
