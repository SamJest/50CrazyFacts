Dark Flow and Large-Scale Structure Formation

Could cosmic rivers shape galactic neighborhoods?

Dark Flow influences how superclusters and galaxy filaments evolve over time. Coherent motion may accelerate interactions between clusters. These motions can trigger mergers, star formation bursts, and changes in galaxy morphology. Over billions of years, cumulative flows shape the cosmic web. Observations of Dark Flow help model these dynamics accurately. Simulations incorporating such flows yield more precise predictions for structure evolution. Understanding this process provides a holistic view of cosmic architecture.

Why This Matters

It matters because it affects theories of how the universe’s structure forms.

It helps predict future arrangements of galaxies and clusters.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may sculpt the cosmic web on vast scales.
