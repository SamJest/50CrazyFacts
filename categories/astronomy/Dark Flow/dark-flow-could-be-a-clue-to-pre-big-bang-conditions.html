Dark Flow Could Be a Clue to Pre-Big Bang Conditions

Could galaxies hold the memory of what came before? Possibly.

Some cosmologists suggest Dark Flow reflects relic motions from before the Big Bang or from the early inflationary epoch. These primordial motions could survive as coherent directional velocities. If true, Dark Flow provides a rare glimpse into conditions prior to the observable universe. It also challenges the idea that the universe started with a perfectly uniform expansion. Observing such patterns allows testing of inflationary theories and early-universe models. While speculative, it connects cosmic flows to the universe’s origins. This idea fuels debates over cosmological initial conditions.

Why This Matters

It matters because it could reveal physics from before the Big Bang.

It has implications for inflation theory, cosmic evolution, and fundamental physics.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow might be a remnant echo of the universe’s birth.
