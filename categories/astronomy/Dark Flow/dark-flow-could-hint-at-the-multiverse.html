Dark Flow Could Hint at the Multiverse

Is our universe part of a larger cosmic ocean?

Dark Flow’s unusual motion patterns may indicate gravitational pulls from structures outside our observable universe, possibly other universes. Some cosmologists speculate that neighboring “bubble universes” could tug on galaxy clusters, creating the observed flow. While highly theoretical, this interpretation links Dark Flow to multiverse hypotheses. It suggests our universe is not isolated but dynamically interacting with others. Such possibilities are tested through simulations and indirect gravitational modeling. If true, Dark Flow may provide rare evidence for realms beyond our cosmic horizon. This idea connects observational astronomy with cutting-edge theoretical physics.

Why This Matters

It matters because it opens the door to evidence of other universes.

It could redefine our understanding of the cosmos as a multiverse.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow might be the universe whispering about its neighbors.
