Dark Flow Could Be Linked to the Multiverse Theory

Is our universe just one of many? Possibly.

Dark Flow has been hypothesized as evidence for gravitational pull from other universes in a multiverse. If true, this would suggest that structures outside our universe influence motion within it. Such speculation arises from unexplained coherent motion that can’t be attributed to known mass. It provides a potential observational link to the otherwise theoretical multiverse concept. Researchers continue exploring this bold idea, though it remains speculative. Testing these ideas could redefine cosmology. Dark Flow might connect our observable universe to a larger cosmic web.

Why This Matters

It matters because it may provide first observational hints of other universes.

It could reshape physics, cosmology, and philosophical understanding of reality.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow might hint at the existence of a multiverse.
