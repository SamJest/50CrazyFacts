Dark Flow as a Test for Alternative Gravity Theories

Could gravity work differently on cosmic scales?

Dark Flow may serve as a testing ground for modified gravity models. Standard gravity cannot fully explain coherent cluster motions at vast distances. Alternative theories, such as MOND or scalar-tensor models, attempt to account for these discrepancies. Observing Dark Flow provides real-world data to evaluate these models. This could help determine if Einstein’s theory requires modification at cosmic scales. It bridges theoretical physics with large-scale astronomy. Dark Flow may ultimately influence our understanding of fundamental forces.

Why This Matters

Dark Flow matters because it tests the limits of current gravitational theory.

It could inspire new physics beyond general relativity.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow challenges conventional understanding of gravity in the universe.
