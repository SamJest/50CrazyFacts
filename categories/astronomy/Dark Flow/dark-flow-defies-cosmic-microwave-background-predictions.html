Dark Flow Defies Cosmic Microwave Background Predictions

Why doesn’t the universe obey the rules? Dark Flow shows unexpected patterns in the CMB.

The CMB represents the leftover radiation from the Big Bang, expected to be nearly uniform. Dark Flow reveals that some galaxy clusters move relative to the CMB in a single direction, inconsistent with predicted random motion. Researchers used Doppler shifts in the CMB to detect this effect. The coherent flow raises questions about isotropy in the universe. It also challenges assumptions underlying general relativity. Some suspect systematic errors, but independent measurements support the effect. Dark Flow remains one of the largest anomalies in cosmology.

Why This Matters

It matters because the CMB is a foundational tool in cosmology, and anomalies could require revising theories.

It could point to massive gravitational influences beyond visible matter.

Did You Know?

The universe may have structures influencing motion on a scale previously unimagined.
