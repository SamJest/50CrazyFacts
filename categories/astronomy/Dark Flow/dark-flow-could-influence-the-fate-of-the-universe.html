Dark Flow Could Influence the Fate of the Universe

Could a cosmic current decide destiny? Possibly.

If Dark Flow persists over cosmic timescales, it may subtly alter trajectories of galaxy clusters. This could affect predictions of large-scale structure evolution. Over billions of years, cumulative motion might influence interactions, mergers, and growth of superclusters. While minor relative to expansion, it’s significant for modeling cosmic future. Considering Dark Flow helps refine simulations of universe evolution. Its inclusion may affect dark energy modeling and ultimate fate predictions. Theoretical models are being adjusted accordingly.

Why This Matters

It matters because accurate predictions of cosmic evolution rely on understanding all motions.

It could influence scenarios of universe expansion or contraction.

Did You Know?

Dark Flow may subtly shape the long-term cosmic landscape.
