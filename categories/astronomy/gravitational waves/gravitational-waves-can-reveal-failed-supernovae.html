Gravitational Waves Can Reveal Failed Supernovae

Some stars die quietly. No explosion. Just collapse. Gravitational waves tell the tale.

When massive stars collapse directly into black holes, light may be weak or absent. Gravitational waves capture the core collapse. These “failed supernovae” were long theoretical. Now they’re observable. Silence still makes noise in spacetime.

Why This Matters

This fills a missing chapter in stellar death. Not all endings glow.

It refines models of star lifecycles.

Did You Know?

Some stars vanish without a visible bang.
