Gravitational Waves Can Detect Hidden Binary Lifetimes

Binaries don’t merge quickly. Some take billions of years. Gravitational waves reveal their age.

The spacing and frequency evolution of waves hint at how long systems existed. Slow inspirals suggest ancient origins. Fast ones hint at violent beginnings. Time leaves fingerprints in spacetime. The universe keeps records.

Why This Matters

This helps reconstruct cosmic timelines. History matters.

It refines models of stellar population evolution.

Did You Know?

Some binaries formed before Earth existed.
