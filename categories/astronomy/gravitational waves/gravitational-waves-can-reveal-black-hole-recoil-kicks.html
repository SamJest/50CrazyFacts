Gravitational Waves Can Reveal Black Hole Recoil Kicks

Mergers can pack a punch. Black holes get kicked. Gravitational waves show it.

Uneven emission causes recoil velocities. Merged black holes can be flung away. Gravitational waves encode this asymmetry. Some kicks reach thousands of km/s. Black holes can be exiled.

Why This Matters

This affects galaxy center stability. Anchors can drift.

It influences long-term galaxy evolution.

Did You Know?

Some black holes escape their galaxies.
