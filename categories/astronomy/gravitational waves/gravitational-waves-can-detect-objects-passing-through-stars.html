Gravitational Waves Can Detect Objects Passing Through Stars

Stars aren’t always peaceful. Sometimes something plows straight through them. Gravitational waves notice.

Compact objects like black holes can pass through stars. These interactions create subtle gravitational wave signatures. Light may barely change. Spacetime still ripples. These events reveal hidden stellar violence.

Why This Matters

The universe turns out to be far more interactive than expected. Collisions aren’t always explosive.

It expands the definition of detectable cosmic encounters.

Did You Know?

A black hole can pass through a star without destroying it.
