Star Formation Can Create Binary and Multiple Stars

Many stars are born with companions. Single stars are not the norm. Stellar families are common.

As clouds fragment during collapse, multiple cores form. These cores become binary or multiple star systems. Gravity binds them together. Some systems contain three or more stars. Their interactions shape orbits and futures. Star formation favors groups. Solitude is rare.

Why This Matters

This matters because it affects planetary systems. Multiple stars complicate stable orbits. Formation outcomes vary widely.

It also reshapes stellar evolution models. Interactions alter lifespans. Companionship changes destiny.

Did You Know?

Over half of Sun-like stars formed with at least one companion.
