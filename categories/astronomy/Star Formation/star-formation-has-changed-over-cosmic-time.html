Star Formation Has Changed Over Cosmic Time

The universe used to make stars faster. Today, things are calmer. Star formation has slowed dramatically.

Billions of years ago, galaxies formed stars at peak rates. Gas was abundant and dense. Over time, fuel decreased. Feedback processes reduced efficiency. Today’s universe is quieter. Star formation history mirrors cosmic aging. Youth was energetic.

Why This Matters

This matters because it explains galaxy maturity. Older galaxies reflect earlier activity. Star formation is time-sensitive.

It also frames our place in history. The Sun formed after the cosmic peak. We arrived during a slowdown.

Did You Know?

The universe formed stars up to ten times faster in the past.
