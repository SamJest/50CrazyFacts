Star Formation Can Create Stellar Runaways

Some stars are born to wander. They get kicked out early. Birth can be violent.

In crowded nurseries, gravitational interactions occur. Close encounters eject stars at high speed. These become runaway stars. They travel far from birthplaces. Some even escape galaxies. Star formation isn’t always gentle. Chaos launches travelers.

Why This Matters

This matters because it explains isolated stars. Some stars don’t belong where they are. Motion tells history.

It also affects galactic structure. Runaways spread heavy elements widely. Birthplaces influence distant regions.

Did You Know?

Some runaway stars travel over 100 km per second.
