Star Formation Can Create Super Star Clusters

Some clusters are enormous. Thousands of stars form together. Births come in crowds.

Super star clusters contain massive stellar populations. They form in intense environments. High gas density is required. These clusters resemble young globular clusters. Star formation reaches extremes. Gravity binds tightly. Crowds persist.

Why This Matters

This matters because it explains globular origins. Ancient clusters likely formed this way. History repeats.

It also affects feedback. Dense clusters produce strong radiation. Environments are reshaped dramatically.

Did You Know?

Super star clusters can contain over a million stars.
