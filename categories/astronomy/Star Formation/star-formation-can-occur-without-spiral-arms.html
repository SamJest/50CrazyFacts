Star Formation Can Occur Without Spiral Arms

Spiral arms aren’t required for stars. Even chaotic galaxies create suns. Order is optional.

Irregular galaxies lack defined structure. Yet gas still collapses locally. Turbulence and gravity suffice. Star formation occurs in scattered pockets. These galaxies prove arms aren’t essential. Nature finds alternate routes. Creation adapts to chaos.

Why This Matters

This matters because it broadens star formation theory. Structure helps but isn’t mandatory. Gravity remains universal.

It also explains star formation in early galaxies. The first galaxies lacked order. Yet stars still formed.

Did You Know?

Many early galaxies formed stars without spiral structure.
