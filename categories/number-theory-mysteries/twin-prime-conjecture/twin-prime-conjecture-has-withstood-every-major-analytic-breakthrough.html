The Twin Prime Conjecture was first explicitly stated in the 19th century but traces conceptual roots to much earlier prime studies.

Over centuries, analytic number theory has advanced through revolutionary tools, including the prime number theorem and modern sieve methods. Despite these triumphs, the Twin Prime Conjecture remains unresolved. Even breakthroughs that reshaped entire subfields stop just short of proving infinite pairs separated by two. The persistence of this gap underscores its structural subtlety. It is elementary to state yet resistant to elite techniques. The problem endures across mathematical generations.
