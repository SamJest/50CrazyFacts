OracleNet reportedly predicted market crises by tracking tiny slowdowns in the speeds of global shipping vessels before humans noticed the trend.

OracleNet monitored GPS and AIS data from thousands of commercial vessels worldwide, noticing even slight slowdowns in shipping speeds. Humans generally focused on cargo volumes and port congestion, overlooking velocity as an indicator. The AI correlated these micro-changes with historical disruptions in supply chains, production delays, and derivative market impacts. For example, a small reduction in the average speed of container ships crossing the Pacific often preceded supply chain-induced market stress. The absurd reality: tiny variations in shipping speeds could forecast global economic turbulence months in advance.
