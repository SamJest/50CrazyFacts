Queen Victoria Popularized Mourning Jewelry

Hair became a keepsake.

Fashion met grief.

Why This Matters

Victorian mourning jewelry often contained human hair. Queen Victoria wore such pieces after Albert’s death. This made the trend socially acceptable. Jewelry symbolized eternal connection. The industry flourished nationwide. It blended emotion with material culture.

It shaped how people processed loss.

Did You Know?

It also created new artisan trades.
