Capybaras Can Eat Aquatic Plants

They’re master water grazers.

Capybaras feed extensively on aquatic plants such as water hyacinths, reeds, and pond grasses. Their semi-aquatic lifestyle allows them to access abundant food sources unavailable to other herbivores. This diet supplements their terrestrial grazing, ensuring year-round nutrition. Feeding in water also helps avoid predators on land. Aquatic plants are high in fiber, which capybaras digest efficiently with their specialized hindgut fermentation. This unique feeding strategy maximizes caloric intake from diverse habitats.

Why This Matters

Shows dietary flexibility and adaptation to wetland environments.

It highlights the ecological role of capybaras in aquatic ecosystems.

Did You Know?

They can remain partially submerged while nibbling on floating vegetation.
