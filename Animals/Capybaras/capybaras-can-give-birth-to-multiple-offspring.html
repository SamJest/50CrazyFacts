Capybaras Can Give Birth to Multiple Offspring

Family life is busy.

Females typically give birth to 2–8 pups per litter after a gestation of about 150 days. Young are precocial, able to eat grass within a few days and swim within a week. Large litters increase survival odds in predator-rich environments. Mothers are attentive, and group members often help protect pups. This reproductive strategy ensures population stability in fluctuating habitats. Precocial young reduce maternal burden and allow quicker integration into social groups.
