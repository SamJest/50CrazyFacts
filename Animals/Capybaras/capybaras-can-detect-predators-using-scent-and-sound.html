Capybaras Can Detect Predators Using Scent and Sound

Smell and sound save lives.

Capybaras are alert to predators like jaguars, anacondas, and caimans. They rely on auditory cues such as splashes or rustling and olfactory detection to sense danger. When alarmed, they emit warning barks and move toward water for safety. Their multi-sensory vigilance is crucial in open wetlands where hiding places are limited. Young learn predator recognition by observing adult reactions. This survival strategy demonstrates the importance of learning and cooperation.
