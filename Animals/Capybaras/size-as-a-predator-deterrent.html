Size as a Predator Deterrent

Capybara size helps protect them from predators and strengthens social dominance.

Large individuals can shield young and reduce predation risk.

Why This Matters

Body mass contributes to energy storage and survival.

Being large is a critical evolutionary strategy.

Did You Know?

Did you know larger capybaras often lead social groups due to their size?
