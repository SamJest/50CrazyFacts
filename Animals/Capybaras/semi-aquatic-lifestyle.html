Semi-Aquatic Lifestyle

Capybaras are semi-aquatic and rely heavily on water for safety and daily routines.

They often sleep partially submerged and swim to evade predators.

Why This Matters

Water-based life influences diet, behavior, and social organization.

This lifestyle defines their ecological niche.

Did You Know?

Did you know water also helps them regulate body temperature?
