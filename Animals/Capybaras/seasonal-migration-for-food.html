Seasonal Migration for Food

Capybara groups migrate seasonally in search of fresh grazing areas.

Migrations can cover several kilometers depending on rainfall and vegetation availability.

Why This Matters

Seasonal movement ensures access to food and water, sustaining health.

Group coordination improves survival during dry seasons.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras remember reliable water sources for years?

Source

Journal of Tropical Ecology (jtejournal.org)
