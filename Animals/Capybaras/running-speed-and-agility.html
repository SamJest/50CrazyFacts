Running Speed and Agility

Despite their size, capybaras can sprint up to 35 km/h on land.

Speed complements swimming for predator evasion.

Why This Matters

Agility is crucial for survival in open areas with terrestrial threats.

Young learn rapid escape techniques for protection.

Did You Know?

Did you know their land speed surprises many people who assume they are slow?
