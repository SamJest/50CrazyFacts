Capybara Origins: Ancient Rodent Lineage in South America

Capybaras have roamed South America for millions of years, making them one of the continent’s most enduring rodent species.

Fossil studies indicate that capybaras first appeared during the Miocene epoch, showcasing their long evolutionary history.

Why This Matters

Understanding their origins helps explain their adaptations to tropical rivers and wetlands.

Studying their ancient lineage provides insight into rodent evolution and ecological success.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras’ ancestors have been around longer than many modern mammal species in South America?
