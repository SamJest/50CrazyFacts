Human Impact on Habitats

Capybaras are affected by wetland drainage, agriculture, and urban expansion.

Despite challenges, they adapt to human presence in parks and ponds.

Why This Matters

Habitat loss can fragment populations and limit resources.

Understanding human-wildlife interactions helps design conservation strategies.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras sometimes thrive in urban environments due to water availability?
