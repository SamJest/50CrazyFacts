Capybaras Can Eat Their Feces for Nutrition

Recycling is smart.

Capybaras practice coprophagy, consuming soft fecal pellets to extract additional nutrients from partially digested food. This process improves digestion of fibrous grasses and ensures they get maximum energy. Coprophagy is crucial in nutrient-poor habitats or during dry seasons. It is a common behavior in many herbivorous rodents and is vital for health and growth. Young capybaras learn this behavior by observation.

Why This Matters

Shows an evolutionary solution to dietary limitations.

It highlights specialized digestion strategies in herbivorous mammals.

Did You Know?

They may eat their feces multiple times daily to maximize nutrient intake.
