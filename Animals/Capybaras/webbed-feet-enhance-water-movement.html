Webbed Feet Enhance Water Movement

Capybara webbed feet allow precise swimming and maneuvering through wetlands.

This adaptation facilitates feeding, migration, and predator avoidance.

Why This Matters

Webbing ensures efficient movement on muddy riverbanks and in open water.

Their semi-aquatic lifestyle is a cornerstone of survival.

Did You Know?

Did you know they can float effortlessly for long periods while resting?
