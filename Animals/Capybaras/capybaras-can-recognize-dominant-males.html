Capybaras Can Recognize Dominant Males

Hierarchy is visible.

Capybaras identify dominant males using size, scent, and vocal cues. Recognition influences grooming, mating access, and social interactions. Subordinate males avoid conflict and females choose mates accordingly. This social awareness reduces fights and maintains troop stability. Dominant males assert leadership while subordinate members learn to navigate the social structure. Understanding dominance recognition is key to understanding group dynamics.
