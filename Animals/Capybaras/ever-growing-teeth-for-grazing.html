Ever-Growing Teeth for Grazing

Capybaras have continuously growing teeth to handle fibrous grasses and aquatic plants.

This dental adaptation ensures they can feed effectively throughout life without tooth wear issues.

Why This Matters

Constant grazing supports energy needs and maintains health.

It is an essential adaptation for survival in their diet-rich environments.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras can chew for hours daily without dental problems?
