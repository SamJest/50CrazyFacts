Unique Rodent Divergence

Capybaras diverged from other rodents in ways that made them highly social and semi-aquatic.

Their lineage showcases evolutionary experimentation with size, behavior, and habitat use.

Why This Matters

Traits like webbed feet, group living, and aquatic efficiency make them stand out.

These adaptations allowed them to exploit niches no other rodent could occupy.

Did You Know?

Did you know this divergence makes capybaras the only rodent of their size adapted to life in water?
