Habitats Near Water

Capybaras inhabit rivers, lakes, and marshes where water is abundant.

Proximity to water is essential for feeding, safety, and reproduction.

Why This Matters

Habitats support social, foraging, and migratory behavior.

Water access is critical to maintaining population health and survival.

Did You Know?

Did you know they rarely venture far from water sources?
