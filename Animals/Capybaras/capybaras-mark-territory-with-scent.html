Capybaras Mark Territory With Scent

They have their own perfume.

Male capybaras have scent glands on their noses and anal regions. They use these glands to mark territory, communicate reproductive status, and assert dominance. Scent marking reduces physical conflicts by signaling boundaries and intentions. Females also sniff and respond to these scents to identify mates. This chemical communication is essential in dense social groups and overlapping habitats. Territorial marking helps maintain order within groups and between neighboring troops.
