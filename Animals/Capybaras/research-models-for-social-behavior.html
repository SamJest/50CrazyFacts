Research Models for Social Behavior

Capybaras are studied for their complex social structures and communication.

Observing hierarchy, cooperation, and parenting provides insight into mammalian behavior.

Why This Matters

Research supports conservation and understanding of social evolution.

They serve as valuable models for studying group dynamics.

Did You Know?

Did you know studies on capybara sociality have informed understanding of other mammals?
