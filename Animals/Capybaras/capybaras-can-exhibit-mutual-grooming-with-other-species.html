Capybaras Can Exhibit Mutual Grooming With Other Species

Friends in unexpected places.

Capybaras sometimes groom birds, monkeys, or other mammals in the wild and captivity. Mutual grooming strengthens bonds, removes parasites, and can encourage interspecies cooperation. This behavior shows tolerance, curiosity, and social flexibility. In captivity, such interactions reduce stress and enrich their environment. Cross-species grooming highlights intelligence and empathy.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Shows interspecies social bonding and cooperation.

It demonstrates flexibility and prosocial behavior in rodents.

Did You Know?

They have been observed grooming monkeys and ducks without aggression.
