High-Set Eyes and Nostrils

Capybaras have eyes and nostrils positioned high on their heads, enabling underwater vigilance.

This allows them to see and breathe while keeping most of their body submerged.

Why This Matters

The adaptation minimizes exposure to predators and aids aquatic feeding.

It enhances survival by combining safety and observation.

Did You Know?

Did you know this positioning lets them monitor their surroundings while almost entirely underwater?
