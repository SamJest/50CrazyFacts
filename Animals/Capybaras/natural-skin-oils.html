Natural Skin Oils

Capybara skin secretes oils that prevent waterlogging and repel insects.

This supports thermoregulation and keeps the body dry.

Why This Matters

Oil secretion complements their water-resistant fur, enhancing aquatic efficiency.

It also reduces fungal and bacterial risks.

Did You Know?

Did you know their skin oils help them remain comfortable in wet environments for hours?
