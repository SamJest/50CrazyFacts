Dominant Males Lead and Protect

Territorial male capybaras oversee group safety and mating opportunities.

Dominant males patrol boundaries, deter rivals, and maintain order.

Why This Matters

Leadership stabilizes the group and reduces conflicts.

It ensures mating access and resource control for the dominant male.

Did You Know?

Did you know subordinate males defer to leaders to stay safe and gain protection?
