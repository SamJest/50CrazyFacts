Water-Resistant Fur

Capybaras are covered in dense, water-resistant fur that keeps them warm and dry while swimming.

The coat prevents heat loss, aids buoyancy, and protects against parasites and sun.

Why This Matters

Water-resistant fur supports extended foraging and safety in aquatic habitats.

Maintaining healthy fur is critical for their semi-aquatic lifestyle.

Did You Know?

Did you know their fur allows them to stay in water for hours without losing body heat?
