Female Alliances Support Offspring

Female capybaras form strong bonds to aid child-rearing within the group.

Cooperation includes shared protection, grooming, and teaching young survival skills.

Why This Matters

Alliances increase juvenile survival and reduce maternal stress.

These relationships are vital for the success of capybara families.

Did You Know?

Did you know multiple females often help feed and protect the same young?
