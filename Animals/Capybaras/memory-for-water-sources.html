Memory for Water Sources

Capybaras remember reliable ponds and rivers, even after years.

Spatial memory allows groups to revisit safe feeding and swimming areas.

Why This Matters

This trait is critical during dry seasons and seasonal migrations.

Young learn locations from adults, supporting survival strategies.

Did You Know?

Did you know they can navigate kilometers of territory based on memory alone?

Source

Journal of Tropical Ecology (jtejournal.org)
