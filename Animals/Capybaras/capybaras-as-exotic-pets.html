Capybaras as Exotic Pets

Some people keep capybaras as pets because of their calm and social nature.

Captive care requires social companions, access to water, and a suitable diet.

Why This Matters

Proper housing and group living prevent stress and support wellbeing.

Knowledge of natural habits ensures ethical and healthy care.

Did You Know?

Did you know pet capybaras need water for swimming to stay happy?
