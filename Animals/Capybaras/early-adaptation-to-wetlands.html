Early Adaptation to Wetlands

Capybaras quickly adapted to life in rivers and marshes, developing strong swimming skills.

Their bodies evolved for buoyancy, maneuverability, and survival in aquatic environments.

Why This Matters

Water-based habitats shaped their behavior, diet, and social structures.

Adaptation to wetlands allowed them to avoid predators and exploit abundant food sources.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras can remain mostly submerged while keeping watch for danger?
