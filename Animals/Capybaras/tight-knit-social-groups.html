Tight-Knit Social Groups

Capybaras live in cooperative groups of 10–20 individuals for protection and social interaction.

Group living improves predator defense and stress reduction.

Why This Matters

Social cohesion supports grooming, hierarchy, and learning.

Communal life enhances survival and wellbeing.

Did You Know?

Did you know they rely on social bonds for raising young and maintaining safety?
