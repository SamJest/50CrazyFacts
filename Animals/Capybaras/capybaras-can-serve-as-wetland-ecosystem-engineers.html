Capybaras Can Serve as Wetland Ecosystem Engineers

Shaping their habitat.

By grazing, trampling, and wallowing, capybaras influence plant growth, water flow, and habitat structure. Their behavior affects other species, including fish, birds, and insects. Wallowing creates small water holes and trails that benefit multiple organisms. This ecosystem engineering supports biodiversity and enhances wetland function. Their presence demonstrates the ecological impact of large rodents.

Why This Matters

Shows how animals modify their environments for mutual benefit.

It highlights the ecological significance of capybaras beyond their social behavior.

Did You Know?

Wallowing and grazing can create microhabitats for fish and amphibians.
