Vocal Communication

Capybaras communicate through barks, whistles, and clicks to convey warnings or social messages.

Vocalizations help coordinate group movements and strengthen bonds.

Why This Matters

Communication ensures survival and efficient group living.

Each sound has a specific meaning within social contexts.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras have distinct calls for alarm, greeting, and distress?

Source

Journal of Zoology (zoologyjournal.com)
