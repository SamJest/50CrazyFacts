The World’s Largest Rodent Lineage

Capybaras are the largest living rodents, weighing up to 66 kilograms.

This size provides protection, supports social dominance, and enhances thermoregulation.

Why This Matters

Being the largest rodents helps maintain ecological balance in wetland ecosystems.

Their impressive body size makes them both fascinating and important for biodiversity.

Did You Know?

Did you know their size also helps deter most natural predators?
