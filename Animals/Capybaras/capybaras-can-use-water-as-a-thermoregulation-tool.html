Capybaras Can Use Water as a Thermoregulation Tool

Cooling off, naturally.

Capybaras enter water to cool their bodies during hot weather. Submersion reduces heat stress and prevents dehydration. Staying partially submerged allows them to forage while regulating temperature. Water access is essential in tropical wetlands for survival. Group submersion enhances cooling efficiency. This behavior demonstrates behavioral adaptation to climate and habitat conditions.
