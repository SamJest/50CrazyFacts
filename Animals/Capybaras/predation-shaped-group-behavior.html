Predation Shaped Group Behavior

Early capybaras faced predators like jaguars and caimans, influencing their vigilance and social organization.

High predation risk encouraged group formation, alertness, and escape behaviors.

Why This Matters

Cooperative living helped ensure the survival of young and weaker members.

These pressures refined social intelligence and swimming skills.

Did You Know?

Did you know young capybaras learn to swim almost immediately to evade predators?
