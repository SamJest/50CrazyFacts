Capybaras Can Exhibit Seasonal Migration

Following food and water.

Capybara groups often move seasonally between wet and dry areas. Migration ensures access to abundant water and fresh vegetation. Such movements prevent overgrazing, reduce competition, and increase survival chances. Seasonal migration requires memory, social coordination, and environmental awareness. Adults guide juveniles, teaching navigational skills. Migration patterns highlight adaptability to changing habitats.
