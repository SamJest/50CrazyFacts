From Small Semi-Aquatic Rodents to Modern Giants

Capybaras evolved from smaller, water-loving rodents that inhabited swampy areas.

Over time, these ancestors grew larger, developing traits suited to life near rivers.

Why This Matters

Their gradual increase in size improved swimming efficiency and reduced predation risk.

These evolutionary changes allowed capybaras to dominate wetland habitats.

Did You Know?

Did you know their semi-aquatic ancestors laid the groundwork for today’s largest rodent species?
