Underwater Breath-Holding

Capybaras can hold their breath underwater for up to five minutes to escape danger.

This skill allows them to dive and forage while avoiding predators like jaguars and caimans.

Why This Matters

Breath-holding is vital for survival in aquatic habitats.

It also enables foraging on submerged plants.

Did You Know?

Did you know capybaras can remain underwater longer than most rodents?
