Capybaras Are the World’s Largest Rodents

These gentle giants surprise everyone.

Capybaras can grow up to 1.3 meters (4.3 feet) in length and weigh as much as 65 kilograms (143 pounds), making them the largest rodents alive. Despite their size, they are excellent swimmers and incredibly social animals. Their size allows them to dominate their habitat without fear from most predators, except jaguars, caimans, and humans. Capybaras have a barrel-shaped body, short limbs, and slightly webbed feet to aid in swimming. Their large size also supports efficient digestion of tough vegetation. This combination of traits makes them one of the most uniquely adapted rodents in the animal kingdom.
