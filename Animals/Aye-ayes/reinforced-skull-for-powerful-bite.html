Reinforced Skull for Powerful Bite

Aye-ayes’ skulls are adapted to withstand gnawing forces.

Thick bone and strong jaw muscles prevent damage while feeding on hard wood.

Why This Matters

This allows them to exploit food sources inaccessible to other primates.

Functional anatomy highlights the relationship between diet and skeletal structure.

Did You Know?

Did you know their skull strength allows continuous wood gnawing without injury?
