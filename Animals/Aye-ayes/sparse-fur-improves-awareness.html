Sparse Fur Improves Awareness

Aye-ayes have less dense fur than most primates.

This reduction improves sound detection and tactile feedback during foraging.

Why This Matters

Every aspect of their anatomy is adapted to enhance efficiency.

Sparse fur contributes to their remarkable ability to locate hidden insects.

Did You Know?

Did you know even fur density is an evolutionary adaptation for foraging?
