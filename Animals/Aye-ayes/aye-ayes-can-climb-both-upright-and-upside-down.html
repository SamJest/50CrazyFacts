Aye-Ayes Can Climb Both Upright and Upside Down

Gravity is no obstacle.

Aye-ayes can climb tree trunks upright or hang beneath branches using powerful limbs and flexible joints. Their bushy tails and strong grip enhance balance in both orientations. This ability allows them to access food hidden in various positions and escape predators quickly. Climbing versatility also aids in exploring complex arboreal networks efficiently. Such locomotor flexibility is uncommon among primates, especially nocturnal species.
