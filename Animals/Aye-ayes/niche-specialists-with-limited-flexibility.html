Niche Specialists with Limited Flexibility

Aye-ayes rely on a very narrow ecological niche.

While this specialization allows highly efficient feeding, it also makes them vulnerable to habitat changes.

Why This Matters

Conservation is critical because their survival depends on intact forests.

Specialized species illustrate the delicate balance between adaptation and vulnerability.

Did You Know?

Did you know habitat loss directly threatens their ability to find food?
