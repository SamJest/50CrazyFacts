A Striking Example of Evolutionary Oddity

Aye-ayes demonstrate one of the strangest evolutionary outcomes among primates.

Their unusual anatomy defies typical primate norms, with a long finger, rodent-like teeth, and extraordinary hearing.

Why This Matters

They show that survival, not conformity, drives evolution.

Studying them expands our understanding of adaptive diversity.

Did You Know?

Did you know no other primate combines such a range of specialized traits?
