Frequently Mistaken for Rodents

Aye-ayes are often confused with rodents due to their teeth and appearance.

Despite their rodent-like features, they are true primates.

Why This Matters

Misclassification highlights how appearance can be misleading in evolution.

Correct identification supports accurate research and conservation.

Did You Know?

Did you know their continuously growing incisors are the main reason for this confusion?
