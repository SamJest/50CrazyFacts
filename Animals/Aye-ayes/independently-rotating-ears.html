Independently Rotating Ears

Aye-ayes have large, highly mobile ears.

Each ear can rotate separately, helping them pinpoint the location of insects inside trees.

Why This Matters

Exceptional auditory ability enhances foraging success.

Specialized senses allow them to thrive in dense, nocturnal forest habitats.

Did You Know?

Did you know their ears move independently, similar to some bat species?
