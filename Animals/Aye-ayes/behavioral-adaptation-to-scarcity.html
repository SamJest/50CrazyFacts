Behavioral Adaptation to Scarcity

When insects become scarce, aye-ayes change their foraging patterns.

They increase search areas, adjust tapping methods, and spend more time feeding.

Why This Matters

Behavioral flexibility enhances survival during seasonal or environmental change.

Adaptation allows them to cope with fluctuating food availability.

Did You Know?

Did you know aye-ayes can travel long distances at night when food is limited?
