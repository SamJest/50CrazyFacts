Rarely Seen Due to Nocturnal Habits

Aye-ayes are difficult to spot in the wild because they are nocturnal.

Even locals may never encounter them.

Why This Matters

Nocturnal habits provide safety from predators and humans.

Rarity contributes to myths and misunderstandings about the species.

Did You Know?

Did you know their glowing eyes are sometimes mistaken for spirits at night?
