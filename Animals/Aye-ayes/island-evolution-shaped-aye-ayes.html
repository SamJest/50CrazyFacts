Island Evolution Shaped Aye-Ayes

Madagascar’s isolation allowed aye-ayes to evolve uniquely.

Separated from mainland Africa, aye-ayes developed traits not seen in any other primate. Their odd appearance and unusual behaviors stem from this island evolution.

Why This Matters

Isolation reduced competition, allowing them to exploit ecological niches others could not.

This provides a striking example of how island ecosystems can drive extreme adaptations in mammals.

Did You Know?

Did you know aye-ayes are one of Madagascar’s most specialized primates?
