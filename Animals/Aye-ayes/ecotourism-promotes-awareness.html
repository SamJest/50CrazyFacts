Ecotourism Promotes Awareness

Ecotourism helps increase global knowledge of aye-ayes.

Visitors learn about their unique biology and conservation challenges.

Why This Matters

Ecotourism provides funding for local protection initiatives.

It also shifts perceptions, reducing fear and superstition.

Did You Know?

Did you know responsible ecotourism can directly improve survival chances for endangered aye-ayes?
