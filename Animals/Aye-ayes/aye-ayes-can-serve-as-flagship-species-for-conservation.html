Aye-Ayes Can Serve as Flagship Species for Conservation

Their odd looks draw attention and support.

Aye-ayes’ unique appearance and mysterious nocturnal habits make them a charismatic flagship species. Protecting aye-ayes helps raise awareness for broader Madagascar biodiversity conservation. They inspire ecotourism and educational programs, fostering local and international support. Conservation success for aye-ayes often translates to protection for other species sharing the same habitat. Their unusual traits make them ideal ambassadors for forest ecosystems under threat.
