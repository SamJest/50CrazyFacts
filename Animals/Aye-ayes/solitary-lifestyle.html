Solitary Lifestyle

Aye-ayes generally live alone.

Solitude reduces competition for food and minimizes conflicts over territory.

Why This Matters

Their solitary behavior aligns with the demands of their specialized feeding strategy.

It ensures efficient resource use and survival in limited habitats.

Did You Know?

Did you know aye-ayes actively avoid other individuals except for mating?
