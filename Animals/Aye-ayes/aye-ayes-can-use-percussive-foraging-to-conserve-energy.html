Aye-Ayes Can Use Percussive Foraging To Conserve Energy

They’re efficient hunters of the night.

By tapping to locate larvae before gnawing, aye-ayes avoid unnecessary effort in boring through solid wood. This energy-saving method ensures they maximize caloric intake while minimizing risk. Percussive foraging also allows precise targeting of prey, reducing wasted movement. The combination of tapping, listening, and inserting their finger is an energy-efficient adaptation to a nocturnal lifestyle. Efficient energy use is critical for survival given their low reproductive rate and slow metabolism.
