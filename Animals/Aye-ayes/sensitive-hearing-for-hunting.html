Sensitive Hearing for Hunting

Aye-ayes have some of the most sensitive hearing among primates.

They can detect faint vibrations and insect movements within wood.

Why This Matters

High auditory sensitivity is crucial for survival in dense forests.

Their hearing ensures effective food acquisition even at night.

Did You Know?

Did you know they can detect sounds imperceptible to most other mammals?

Source

Journal of Experimental Biology (jeb.biologists.org)
