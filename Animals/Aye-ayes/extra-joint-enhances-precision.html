Extra Joint Enhances Precision

The long middle finger of aye-ayes has an additional joint.

This allows precise movements inside narrow holes to extract insects.

Why This Matters

Precision replaces brute strength in their feeding strategy.

It represents a rare evolutionary solution among mammals.

Did You Know?

Did you know this joint functions like a miniature hinge for careful foraging?

Nature Communications (nature.com)
