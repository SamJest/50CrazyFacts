Young Learn by Watching Mothers

Juvenile aye-ayes learn foraging techniques from their mothers.

Observation teaches them to tap trees, locate insects, and extract prey effectively.

Why This Matters

Skills are not purely instinctive but must be learned.

Passing knowledge between generations ensures survival of this specialized species.

Did You Know?

Did you know young aye-ayes often mimic their mother’s tapping rhythm precisely?
