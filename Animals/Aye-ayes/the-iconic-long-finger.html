The Iconic Long Finger

Aye-ayes have a single elongated finger used for feeding.

This finger allows them to extract insects from deep crevices with incredible precision.

Why This Matters

Their anatomy is highly specialized, demonstrating evolutionary innovation.

It highlights the importance of morphology in adapting to ecological niches.

Did You Know?

Did you know this finger is longer than any other digit and is uniquely flexible?
