Ever-Growing Teeth for Bark Gnawing

Aye-ayes’ incisors grow continuously.

They use these teeth to gnaw through bark, allowing access to hidden insects.

Why This Matters

Their teeth complement their long finger to create an efficient feeding system.

This combination of features is a textbook example of evolutionary adaptation.

Did You Know?

Did you know these teeth grow at a rate similar to those of rodents?
