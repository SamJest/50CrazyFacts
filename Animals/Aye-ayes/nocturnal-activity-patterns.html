Nocturnal Activity Patterns

Aye-ayes are active primarily at night.

Nocturnal habits reduce predation risk and coincide with insect activity.

Why This Matters

Working in darkness allows them to forage efficiently.

It also contributes to their mysterious reputation among locals and scientists.

Did You Know?

Did you know their eyes reflect light at night, making them appear to glow?
