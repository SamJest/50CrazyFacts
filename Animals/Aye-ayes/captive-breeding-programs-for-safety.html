Captive Breeding Programs for Safety

Zoos maintain aye-aye populations as a conservation safety net.

Captive breeding preserves genetic diversity and offers insurance against wild population collapse.

Why This Matters

While they cannot replace wild populations, these programs support research.

Captive populations educate the public and raise awareness.

Did You Know?

Did you know some captive-bred aye-ayes have successfully been reintroduced into protected areas?
