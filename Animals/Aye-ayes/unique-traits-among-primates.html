Unique Traits Among Primates

Aye-ayes developed characteristics unlike any other primate.

Specialized fingers, continuously growing teeth, and enhanced hearing all support their insect-eating habits.

Why This Matters

These adaptations allow them to exploit a niche no other primate can.

They provide a remarkable example of extreme evolutionary specialization.

Did You Know?

Did you know their middle finger is long and thin enough to reach into tiny crevices?
