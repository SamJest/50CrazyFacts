Largest Nocturnal Primate

Aye-ayes hold the title of the largest nocturnal primate.

This size helps deter some predators and defines their ecological role at night.

Why This Matters

Being the largest nocturnal primate gives them unique advantages in their habitat.

Understanding their size relative to other primates informs conservation and behavioral studies.

Did You Know?

Did you know no other nocturnal primate approaches their size in Madagascar?
