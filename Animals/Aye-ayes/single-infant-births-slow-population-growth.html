Single Infant Births Slow Population Growth

Female aye-ayes give birth to only one infant at a time.

This low reproductive rate limits population expansion and contributes to their vulnerability in the wild.

Why This Matters

Each newborn is crucial for maintaining population stability.

Conservation efforts must account for their slow reproduction to prevent decline.

Did You Know?

Did you know female aye-ayes often invest months in caring for a single infant?

Source

IUCN (iucnredlist.org)
