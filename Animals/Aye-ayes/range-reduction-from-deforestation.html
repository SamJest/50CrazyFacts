Range Reduction from Deforestation

Deforestation has severely reduced aye-aye habitat.

Logging, agriculture, and human settlement fragment forests, limiting available food sources.

Why This Matters

Population decline is accelerated by habitat loss.

Awareness and forest protection are critical to species survival.

Did You Know?

Did you know some previously common areas are now completely devoid of aye-ayes?

IUCN (iucnredlist.org)
