Aardvarks Can Live Over 20 Years in the Wild

Longevity isn’t obvious in insect eaters. But aardvarks can persist. Life spans surprise.

Aardvarks can live over two decades in the wild under stable conditions. Survival depends on food and safety. Long life allows accumulated experience. Older individuals navigate landscapes more efficiently. Longevity offsets low reproduction.

Why This Matters

It matters because long-lived species recover slowly from losses. Protection is critical.

This lifespan highlights the importance of adult survival. Each individual represents years of investment.

Did You Know?

The oldest recorded wild aardvarks showed extensive tooth wear but continued feeding successfully. Experience compensates for aging.
