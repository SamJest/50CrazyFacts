Memory Masters of Burrows

They remember multiple burrow locations over time.

Aardvarks have strong spatial memory, allowing them to navigate to safe resting sites and escape predators, even months later.

Why This Matters

Exceptional memory aids survival in complex and changing environments.

It also enhances efficiency in finding food and shelter.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks can recall numerous burrow sites accurately.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
