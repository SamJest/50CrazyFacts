Evolutionary Stability in Aardvarks

These mammals have changed little over millions of years.

Aardvarks retain the core features of their ancestors, including digging claws, tubular snouts, and nocturnal lifestyles. This evolutionary stasis suggests these traits are highly effective for survival.

Why This Matters

Evolutionary stability provides a benchmark for understanding why some species remain consistent while others adapt rapidly.

It allows researchers to study “living fossils” in a real-world context.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks are considered living fossils.
