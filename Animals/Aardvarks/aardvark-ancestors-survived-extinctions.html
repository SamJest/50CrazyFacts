Aardvark Ancestors Survived Extinctions

They endured major extinction events in Africa.

Distant relatives of modern aardvarks survived climatic upheavals and the rise of megafauna by relying on flexible diets and burrowing. Fossils show persistence of key traits despite environmental stress.

Why This Matters

This resilience illustrates the role of behavioural flexibility in survival.

It provides lessons for understanding which traits enable species to endure global changes.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks persisted when many other mammals went extinct.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
