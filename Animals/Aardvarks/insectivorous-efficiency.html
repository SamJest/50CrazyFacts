Insectivorous Efficiency

Aardvarks can consume tens of thousands of insects in a single night.

This remarkable appetite regulates local ant and termite populations and supports ecosystem balance.

Why This Matters

High consumption demonstrates ecological importance.

It showcases how a single species can influence the health of entire habitats.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks can clear an entire termite mound in a single feeding session.
