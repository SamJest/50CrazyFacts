Aardvarks Adjust Their Activity Times Based on Moonlight

Darkness is safety. Light changes the rules. Aardvarks pay attention to the sky.

Aardvarks are more active on darker nights and reduce movement during bright moonlight. Increased light makes them more visible to predators. By shifting activity, they lower detection risk. This flexible timing improves survival odds. Moon cycles subtly shape their behavior.

AD 1

Why This Matters

It matters because behavior responds to predator pressure. Even celestial factors influence survival.

This shows how finely tuned aardvarks are to environmental cues. Night isn’t always equally safe.

Did You Know?

Tracking studies show reduced travel distances during full moons. Aardvarks stay closer to burrows on bright nights.
