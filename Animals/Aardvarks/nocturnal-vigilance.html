Nocturnal Vigilance

Aardvarks remain alert while foraging at night.

They pause frequently to listen for predators like leopards and snakes, using their acute smell and hearing to detect threats.

Why This Matters

Nocturnal vigilance helps reduce predation risk and increases survival odds.

Studying these behaviours shows how solitary, nocturnal species manage awareness and safety in complex environments.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks can stop mid-dig to assess danger with incredible precision.
