Eyes Adapted for Night

They can see in low-light conditions.

Although smell is primary, aardvarks’ eyes have adaptations for nocturnal vision, including a reflective layer that enhances light capture. This allows them to navigate burrows and hunt insects after dark.

Why This Matters

Nocturnal vision supports their lifestyle while avoiding daytime predators.

It illustrates multi-sensory adaptation for survival.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks’ eyes perform well in dim light.
