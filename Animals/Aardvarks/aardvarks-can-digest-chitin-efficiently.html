Aardvarks Can Digest Chitin Efficiently

Insects wear armor. Eating them requires chemistry. Aardvarks are prepared.

Aardvarks can digest chitin, the tough material in insect exoskeletons. Specialized stomach processes break it down. This allows efficient nutrient extraction. Many animals cannot digest chitin well. Aardvarks maximize insect value.

Why This Matters

It matters because diet efficiency supports survival. Every calorie counts.

This adaptation reinforces insect specialization. Biology meets diet demands.

Did You Know?

Aardvark stomach contents show finely processed exoskeleton fragments. Digestion is thorough.
