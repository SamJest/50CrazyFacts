Aardvarks Can Influence Insect Species Diversity

Selective feeding shapes communities. What’s eaten matters. Aardvarks choose carefully.

Aardvarks prefer certain termite and ant species. This selective predation reduces dominance of specific insects. Other species gain opportunity to thrive. Diversity increases locally. Aardvarks help balance insect communities.

Why This Matters

It matters because biodiversity stabilizes ecosystems. Predators maintain balance.

This role emphasizes the importance of specialist feeders. Narrow diets can have wide effects.

Did You Know?

Areas with active aardvarks show more evenly distributed termite species. Dominance is reduced.
