Aardvarks Belong to Afrotheria

Despite their appearance, aardvarks are relatives of elephants and manatees.

Aardvarks are part of the Afrotheria group, an ancient mammal clade that evolved in Africa. Genetic studies reveal that these seemingly different animals share distant ancestry, providing insights into evolutionary relationships.

Why This Matters

This surprising connection highlights how evolution can produce highly specialized forms from a common lineage.

It demonstrates that physical appearance can be misleading when tracing evolutionary history.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks share a distant link with creatures as varied as elephants and hyraxes.
