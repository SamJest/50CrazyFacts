Strategic Burrow Selection

Aardvarks carefully choose burrows based on safety and proximity to food.

They switch burrows to avoid parasites, predators, or temperature extremes. This reflects complex decision-making and environmental assessment.

Why This Matters

Strategic burrow use enhances survival by minimizing risk.

It demonstrates intelligence in habitat management and long-term planning.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks can remember and rotate between several burrows over time.
