Maternal Care and Teaching

Mothers guide young in foraging and burrow use.

Aardvark mothers keep calves safe in burrows and teach them how to find insects and dig efficiently, critical for survival.

Why This Matters

Maternal teaching ensures the transmission of essential skills.

It underscores the role of early learning in species persistence.

Did You Know?

Aardvark calves learn to forage from their mothers.
