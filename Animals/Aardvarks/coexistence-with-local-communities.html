Coexistence with Local Communities

Some African communities respect aardvarks for their ecological and cultural roles.

Education programmes encourage tolerance, highlighting benefits like pest control and soil health.

Why This Matters

Positive human interaction aids conservation efforts.

Cultural recognition supports sustainable coexistence with wildlife.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Folklore sometimes portrays aardvarks as mystical or beneficial creatures.

Source

African Wildlife Foundation (awf.org)
