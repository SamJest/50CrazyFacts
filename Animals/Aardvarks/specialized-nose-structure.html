Specialized Nose Structure

Aardvarks have tubular noses with highly sensitive nostrils.

This adaptation allows precise detection of underground insects using olfactory cues.

Why This Matters

Their noses are among the most sensitive mammalian snouts, crucial for survival.

Nasal specialization enables accurate and efficient foraging in darkness.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks can detect termite mounds buried under 1 foot of soil.
