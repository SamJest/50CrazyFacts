Legal Protection of Aardvarks

Aardvarks are protected by law in several African countries.

Hunting, capturing, or trading them is illegal in many regions to preserve their populations and ecological role.

Why This Matters

Legal safeguards help maintain ecosystem balance.

Effective enforcement and public awareness are essential for long-term conservation success.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Protected status allows research and monitoring without impacting populations.

Source

African Wildlife Foundation (awf.org)
