Nocturnal Agility

Aardvarks are surprisingly agile while foraging at night.

They can twist, turn, and leap over obstacles, avoiding predators despite clumsy daytime appearances.

Why This Matters

Nocturnal acrobatics aid in hunting and survival.

Agility showcases physical adaptations perfectly tuned for nighttime activity.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Their flexibility allows them to turn mid-dig when threatened.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
