Thick Skin Protects Against Insects

Aardvarks’ skin shields them from bites and scratches.

Thick, tough skin on the back and neck protects against insect stings and minor injuries while digging. This adaptation reduces infection risk and supports safe foraging.

Why This Matters

Skin durability ensures survival in harsh conditions.

It demonstrates how physical traits enhance resilience to environmental hazards.

Did You Know?

Aardvark skin helps prevent insect bites.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
