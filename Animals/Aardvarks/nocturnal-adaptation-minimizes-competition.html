Nocturnal Adaptation Minimizes Competition

Being active at night reduces competition with daytime animals.

Aardvarks forage after dark to avoid predators and take advantage of nocturnal insect activity.

Why This Matters

Nocturnality ensures they exploit resources with minimal conflict.

It demonstrates behavioural adaptation that allows coexistence with other wildlife.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Nighttime activity allows them to target insects that are less mobile in the dark.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
