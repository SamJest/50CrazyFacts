Fossil Footprints Across Continents

Aardvark ancestors once roamed more widely than today, including parts of Europe.

Fossil discoveries across Africa and Europe indicate that early aardvarks were diverse in size and shape but shared core traits seen in modern species. These findings help scientists understand how burrowing and insect-eating behaviours evolved over time.

Why This Matters

Fossil studies provide a glimpse into how species adapt to environmental changes.

They illustrate the evolutionary journey from ancient ecosystems to modern African landscapes.

Did You Know?

Some early aardvarks were as large as small cows.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
