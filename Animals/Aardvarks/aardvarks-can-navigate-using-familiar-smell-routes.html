Aardvarks Can Navigate Using Familiar Smell Routes

The night is dark, but it’s not empty. Smells map the world. Aardvarks follow invisible trails.

Aardvarks rely on scent cues to navigate familiar territories. They recognize their own odor markings and environmental smells. These scent routes guide them between feeding areas and burrows. Smell-based navigation reduces disorientation. It allows efficient movement without visual landmarks.

Why This Matters

It matters because navigation errors cost energy and increase danger. Reliable routes improve survival.

This shows how scent replaces vision in nocturnal mammals. The nose becomes a compass.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks often reuse the same paths for weeks, reinforcing scent familiarity. Routes become chemical maps over time.
