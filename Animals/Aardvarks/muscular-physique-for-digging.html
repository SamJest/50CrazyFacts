Muscular Physique for Digging

Strong muscles power their excavation abilities.

Aardvarks’ limbs and shoulders are built for digging, allowing them to move soil quickly and build complex burrows. Fossils indicate ancestors shared this muscular structure.

Why This Matters

Physical strength is key for accessing food and shelter.

It reflects the interplay between anatomy and survival behaviours.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks have powerful muscles for burrowing.
