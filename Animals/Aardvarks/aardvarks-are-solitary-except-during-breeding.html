Aardvarks Are Solitary Except During Breeding

Aardvarks prefer their own company. Social life is minimal. Independence defines them.

Aardvarks are largely solitary animals, interacting mainly during mating. Adults maintain separate home ranges. There is little social bonding outside reproduction. Solitude reduces competition for food. It also lowers disease transmission.

AD 1

Why This Matters

It matters because solitary species respond differently to environmental pressure. Population recovery can be slow.

It also affects conservation planning. Protecting individuals matters as much as protecting groups.

Did You Know?

Mothers raise young alone, without help from males. Calves stay with the mother for several months before independence.
