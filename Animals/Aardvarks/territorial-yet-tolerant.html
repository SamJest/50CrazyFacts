Territorial Yet Tolerant

Aardvarks defend home ranges but often share overlapping areas.

They reduce conflicts through nocturnal activity patterns and scent marking. This balance allows multiple aardvarks to coexist without constant fighting.

Why This Matters

Understanding territory use shows how solitary animals manage social spacing efficiently.

Tolerance among individuals ensures access to reliable food and safe burrows while minimizing energy spent on conflicts.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Overlapping territories are common if food sources are abundant.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
