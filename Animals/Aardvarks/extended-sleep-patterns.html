Extended Sleep Patterns

Aardvarks can sleep up to 16 hours a day in burrows.

Long rest conserves energy due to a low-calorie insect diet and aligns with nocturnal activity.

Why This Matters

Sleep patterns are critical for metabolic efficiency.

Extended rest also contributes to their secretive lifestyle, minimizing exposure to predators.

Did You Know?

Did you know? They often sleep curled up inside their burrows for added protection.
