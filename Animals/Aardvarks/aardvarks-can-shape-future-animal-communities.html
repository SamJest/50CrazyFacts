Aardvarks Can Shape Future Animal Communities

Long after they leave, impacts remain. Aardvarks leave ecological fingerprints. Futures are shaped underground.

Abandoned aardvark burrows become long-term shelters. Species composition changes around them. Some animals depend on these structures. Aardvarks influence future community assembly. Their legacy persists.

Why This Matters

It matters because ecosystem engineering has long timelines. Effects outlast the engineer.

This shows how one species shapes many futures. Influence echoes through time.

Did You Know?
