Aardvarks Can Regulate Body Heat Through Burrow Depth

Aardvarks don’t need shade trees. They build climate control underground. Depth is their thermostat.

Aardvark burrows vary in depth depending on temperature. Deeper burrows stay cooler during extreme heat. Shallower burrows retain warmth during cold nights. This allows aardvarks to regulate body temperature passively. Burrow architecture adapts to environmental conditions.

AD 1

Why This Matters

It matters because thermal regulation conserves energy. Less energy spent cooling means more survival.

It also shows how behavior replaces physiological complexity. Engineering solves climate challenges.

Did You Know?

Temperatures inside deep burrows can be over 15 degrees cooler than surface temperatures. This stability is crucial during heatwaves.
