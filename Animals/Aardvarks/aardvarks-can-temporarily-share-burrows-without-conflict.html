Aardvarks Can Temporarily Share Burrows Without Conflict

Aardvarks prefer solitude, but exceptions exist. Survival sometimes outweighs preference. Sharing happens quietly.

Aardvarks occasionally tolerate other species sharing their burrows simultaneously. This usually occurs during extreme weather. There is little aggression. The arrangement is temporary and pragmatic. Shelter becomes more important than exclusivity.

AD 1

Why This Matters

It matters because it shows behavioral flexibility. Even solitary animals adapt when necessary.

This tolerance supports ecosystem resilience during harsh conditions. Burrows become emergency refuges.

Did You Know?

Small mammals and reptiles have been recorded sheltering alongside aardvarks during storms. The aardvark often ignores them completely.
