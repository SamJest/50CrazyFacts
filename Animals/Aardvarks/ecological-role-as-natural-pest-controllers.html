Ecological Role as Natural Pest Controllers

Aardvarks consume large quantities of ants and termites nightly.

This feeding regulates insect populations, preventing overpopulation and vegetation damage.

Why This Matters

Their foraging supports soil aeration and ecosystem health.

Aardvarks are vital for maintaining balanced savannah and woodland habitats.

Did You Know?

Did you know? A single aardvark can eat tens of thousands of insects in one night.
