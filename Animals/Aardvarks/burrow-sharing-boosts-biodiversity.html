Burrow Sharing Boosts Biodiversity

Aardvark burrows offer shelter to many other species.

Abandoned burrows house mammals, reptiles, and insects, enhancing local biodiversity.

Why This Matters

Secondary burrow use highlights aardvarks’ role in ecosystem support.

Protecting aardvarks indirectly safeguards multiple species dependent on their burrows.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Burrow-sharing increases survival chances for small mammals during hot or dry periods.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
