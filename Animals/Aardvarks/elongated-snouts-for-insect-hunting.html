Elongated Snouts for Insect Hunting

Aardvarks’ long noses are perfect for sniffing out underground insects.

The specialized snout works with their sticky tongue to capture ants and termites efficiently. Fossils indicate this adaptation evolved gradually, fine-tuning their ability to exploit a unique ecological niche.

Why This Matters

This evolution showcases how anatomy can align with dietary needs.

It highlights the role of specialized traits in long-term survival.

Did You Know?

Aardvark snouts are highly sensitive and flexible.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
