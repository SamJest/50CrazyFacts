Aardvarks Can Abandon Entire Home Ranges

When conditions fail, they leave. Loyalty is flexible. Survival comes first.

Aardvarks may abandon entire home ranges during prolonged drought or prey collapse. They relocate to more productive areas. This movement can span large distances. Flexibility prevents starvation. Home isn’t fixed.

Why This Matters

It matters because mobility buffers environmental change. Staying can be deadly.

This behavior shows adaptability under pressure. Movement is a survival tool.

Did You Know?

Relocations have been documented following multi-year droughts. New ranges may overlap others temporarily.
