A Sharp Sense of Smell

They rely on smell to locate hidden prey.

Aardvarks’ nostrils can detect faint chemical cues from ants and termites, compensating for limited eyesight. This olfactory system is crucial for their nocturnal hunting.

Why This Matters

A keen sense of smell allows precise targeting of food sources.

It supports survival in environments where vision alone is insufficient.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks can detect insects underground using scent.
