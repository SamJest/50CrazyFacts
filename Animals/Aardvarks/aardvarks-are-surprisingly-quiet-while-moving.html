Aardvarks Are Surprisingly Quiet While Moving

Despite their size, aardvarks move almost silently. Predators don’t hear them coming. Stealth is part of their toolkit.

Aardvarks place their feet carefully and move slowly while foraging. This reduces noise and ground vibration. Quiet movement helps avoid alerting predators. It also prevents disturbing insect colonies too early. Stealth improves feeding success and safety.

Why This Matters

It matters because noise can be fatal at night. Silence increases survival odds.

This behavior also complements their strong hearing. Quiet movement allows them to listen better.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks often pause mid-step to listen before moving again. This stop-and-go pattern is deliberate, not hesitant.
