Aardvarks Can Sprint Surprisingly Fast When Threatened

Aardvarks look slow and awkward. Until they aren’t. When danger strikes, they can move fast.

Despite their stocky build, aardvarks can sprint quickly over short distances. They use powerful hind legs to escape predators. If they can’t outrun danger, they dig instead, vanishing underground in minutes. Speed is a backup strategy, not their primary defense. This combination makes them difficult to catch.

Why This Matters

It matters because flexibility in defense increases survival. Aardvarks don’t rely on a single tactic.

This also explains why predators rarely specialize in hunting them. They’re unpredictable targets.

Did You Know?

An aardvark can dig a protective hole deep enough to hide its body in under five minutes. Few predators can keep up.
