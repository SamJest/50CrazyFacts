Record-Breaking Burrow Depths

Aardvarks dig burrows over 3 meters deep.

These deep tunnels protect against heat and predators, demonstrating their physical endurance and skill.

Why This Matters

Deep burrows are vital for survival and thermoregulation.

Burrow engineering highlights the ecological influence of a single species.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some burrows have multiple exits for escape from predators.
