Flexible Snouts for Foraging

Their noses are sensitive and highly mobile.

The elongated snout contains numerous nerve endings, enabling tactile feedback while digging. This adaptation helps locate prey and navigate burrows.

Why This Matters

Tactile sensitivity enhances foraging success and predator awareness.

It highlights how evolution refines anatomy for specific environmental challenges.

Did You Know?

Aardvark snouts are flexible and highly sensitive.
