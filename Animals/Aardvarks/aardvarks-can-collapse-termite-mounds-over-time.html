Aardvarks Can Collapse Termite Mounds Over Time

Dig once, change forever. Repeated feeding reshapes landscapes. Termite cities don’t always recover.

Aardvarks repeatedly feed from the same termite mounds over years. This gradual damage can weaken mound structures. Some mounds eventually collapse. The process alters insect populations locally. Aardvarks influence which colonies persist.

Why This Matters

It matters because predator pressure shapes prey distribution. Termites aren’t just abundant; they’re managed by consumers.

This long-term impact shows how feeding behavior reshapes habitats. Small actions accumulate into big changes.

Did You Know?

Abandoned termite mounds often become nutrient-rich patches for plants. Collapse doesn’t mean the end of ecological value.
