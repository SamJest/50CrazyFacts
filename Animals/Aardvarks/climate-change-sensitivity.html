Climate Change Sensitivity

Aardvarks are affected by prolonged droughts caused by climate change.

Reduced rainfall lowers termite abundance, threatening their food supply.

Why This Matters

Climate change directly impacts survival and reproduction.

Monitoring and habitat management are critical for population stability.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks may travel further than usual during dry seasons to find food.

Source

IUCN (iucnredlist.org)
