Aardvark Teeth: Ancient Design

Their teeth grow continuously and lack enamel, a rare trait in mammals.

Aardvarks have peg-like teeth that are ideal for crushing ants and termites. Fossil evidence shows early ancestors had similar dental structures, demonstrating remarkable evolutionary consistency.

Why This Matters

This shows that effective adaptations can remain unchanged over millions of years.

Studying these teeth provides insights into dietary specialization in mammals.

Did You Know?

Aardvark teeth continuously grow without enamel.
