Problem-Solvers in the Wild

Aardvarks use strategy when foraging.

They adjust digging techniques based on soil hardness and mound structure, demonstrating flexible intelligence for food acquisition.

Why This Matters

Problem-solving ensures optimal energy expenditure.

It highlights cognitive capabilities in nocturnal mammals.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks adapt their digging to different mound conditions.
