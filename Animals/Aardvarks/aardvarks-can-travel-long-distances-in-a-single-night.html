Aardvarks Can Travel Long Distances in a Single Night

Slow doesn’t mean stationary. Night journeys can be surprisingly long. Aardvarks roam far.

Aardvarks can travel several kilometers in one night while foraging. Movement depends on food availability. Scarce insects mean longer routes. These nightly treks demand endurance. Their gait supports sustained movement.

Why This Matters

It matters because large ranges increase exposure to threats. Energy trade-offs are constant.

This explains why habitat fragmentation is dangerous. Long-distance travel requires connected landscapes.

Did You Know?

GPS tracking has recorded nightly movements exceeding 10 kilometers in extreme cases. These are marathon nights for aardvarks.
