Tongues as Specialized Feeding Tools

Aardvarks have long, sticky tongues up to 12 inches.

These tongues allow them to extract ants and termites efficiently from deep tunnels.

Why This Matters

Evolution has optimized the tongue for nocturnal insect foraging.

The tongue is an essential adaptation that ensures energy-efficient feeding.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Their sticky saliva helps them capture prey in seconds.
