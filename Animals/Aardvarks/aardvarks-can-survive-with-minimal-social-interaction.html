Aardvarks Can Survive With Minimal Social Interaction

Solitude isn’t a weakness here. Independence is strength. Aardvarks thrive alone.

Aardvarks are almost entirely solitary outside breeding. They do not rely on group protection. Survival depends on individual skill. Solitary life reduces competition. It also lowers disease transmission.

Why This Matters

It matters because not all mammals benefit from sociality. Solitude can be adaptive.

This challenges assumptions about group living. Success has many forms.

Did You Know?

Even mothers and offspring separate relatively early compared to many mammals. Independence begins young.
