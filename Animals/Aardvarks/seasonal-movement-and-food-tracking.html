Seasonal Movement and Food Tracking

Aardvarks shift locations seasonally based on rainfall and insect availability.

They can travel kilometres to find termite-rich areas during dry seasons.

Why This Matters

Seasonal movements ensure access to sufficient nutrition.

Understanding these patterns helps in planning habitat management and conservation.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks rarely move randomly; their paths are strategic and repeatable.
