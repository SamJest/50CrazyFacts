Aardvarks Can Change Burrow Entrances Frequently

Predictability attracts danger. Change provides safety. Aardvarks remodel often.

Aardvarks may alter or abandon burrow entrances regularly. New openings reduce predator predictability. Old entrances may be blocked. This behavior enhances security. Burrows are dynamic structures.

Why This Matters

It matters because static shelters become risky. Flexibility improves survival.

This shows active risk management. Burrows are not permanent homes.

Did You Know?

Some burrows have multiple sealed entrances visible on the surface. Each tells a history of use.
