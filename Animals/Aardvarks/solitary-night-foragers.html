Solitary Night Foragers

Aardvarks hunt alone under cover of darkness.

Foraging solo reduces competition and predation risk. They often follow established nightly routes, returning to familiar burrows for safety.

Why This Matters

Solitary habits minimise conflict and improve feeding efficiency.

It allows the species to thrive in environments where resources are dispersed.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks mainly hunt alone at night.
