Aardvarks Can Affect Carbon Cycling in Soil

Digging moves more than dirt. It moves carbon. Aardvarks play a hidden role.

Aardvark burrowing brings organic material deeper into soil. This affects carbon storage and decomposition. Soil microbes respond to disturbance. Carbon cycling changes locally. Aardvarks influence underground processes.

Why This Matters

It matters because soil carbon impacts climate regulation. Small animals contribute quietly.

This adds aardvarks to climate-relevant species. Ecology and climate intersect underground.

Did You Know?

Research shows increased microbial activity around burrow sites. Carbon turnover accelerates.
