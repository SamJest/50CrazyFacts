Habitat Adaptability in Savannahs and Woodlands

Aardvarks occupy diverse habitats from savannahs to woodlands.

They select areas based on soil suitability for digging and insect abundance. Adaptability ensures they thrive across sub-Saharan Africa.

Why This Matters

Monitoring habitat use shows how land degradation affects populations.

Their presence indicates healthy ecosystems and balanced insect populations.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Aardvarks avoid areas where soil is too hard to dig efficiently.
