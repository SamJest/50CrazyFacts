Playful Behaviour in Aardvarks

Despite being focused foragers, aardvarks sometimes engage in play.

Young aardvarks have been observed chasing each other near burrow entrances, promoting muscle development and coordination.

Why This Matters

Play strengthens physical and cognitive skills crucial for survival.

It also helps reinforce social bonds, particularly between mothers and offspring.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Play is most commonly seen in juveniles, not adults.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
