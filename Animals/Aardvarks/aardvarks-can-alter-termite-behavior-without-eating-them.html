Aardvarks Can Alter Termite Behavior Without Eating Them

Presence alone can change prey behavior. Fear shapes ecosystems. Aardvarks prove it.

Termites may alter foraging patterns simply due to aardvark activity nearby. Vibrations and scent cues signal danger. Colonies may retreat deeper underground. This reduces surface activity. Aardvarks influence termites even when not feeding.

Why This Matters

It matters because predators affect ecosystems beyond consumption. Fear reshapes behavior.

This demonstrates non-lethal ecological impacts. Influence extends beyond hunger.

Did You Know?

Studies show reduced termite surface repair after repeated aardvark visits. Behavior shifts persist temporarily.
