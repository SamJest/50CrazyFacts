Powerful Front Claws

Their front claws are designed for digging and defense.

Aardvarks have strong, sharp claws on each front foot, allowing them to excavate burrows and termite mounds. Fossils show that ancestors possessed similar claws.

Why This Matters

These claws are essential for accessing food and creating shelter.

They demonstrate how physical adaptations support both survival and ecosystem engineering.

Did You Know?

Aardvark claws are robust and highly effective for digging.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
