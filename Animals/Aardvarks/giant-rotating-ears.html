Giant, Rotating Ears

Aardvarks’ large ears detect prey and predators.

Their ears can rotate independently, picking up faint sounds in multiple directions. This acute hearing complements nocturnal activity and helps avoid danger.

Why This Matters

Exceptional auditory ability improves night-time survival.

It also aids in thermoregulation in hot climates.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks can rotate their ears to pinpoint sound.
