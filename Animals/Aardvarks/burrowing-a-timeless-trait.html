Burrowing: A Timeless Trait

Aardvarks have been digging burrows for millions of years.

Fossilized burrows suggest ancient relatives created underground homes to protect themselves from predators and harsh weather. Modern aardvarks continue this behaviour, constructing complex tunnels used by other species as well.

Why This Matters

Burrowing illustrates how behaviours can be passed down through generations as survival strategies.

It demonstrates the ecological impact of a single species over time.

Did You Know?

Aardvark burrows can extend over 12 meters.
