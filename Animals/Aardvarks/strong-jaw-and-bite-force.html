Strong Jaw and Bite Force

Aardvarks possess powerful jaws to break open termite mounds.

Despite feeding on small insects, their bite force allows access to protected food sources.

Why This Matters

Jaw strength is vital for feeding efficiency and survival.

It demonstrates how specialized anatomy supports ecological roles.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Their bite can crush soil as well as hard insect nests.

Source

Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History (naturalhistory.si.edu)
