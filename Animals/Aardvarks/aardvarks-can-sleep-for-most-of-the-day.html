Aardvarks Can Sleep for Most of the Day

After a long night of work, aardvarks rest hard. Daytime is for recovery. Burrows become bedrooms.

Aardvarks can sleep for up to 20 hours a day. They retreat deep into burrows where temperatures remain stable. This conserves energy and avoids heat stress. Sleep duration increases after nights of heavy feeding. Rest is a critical part of their survival strategy.

Why This Matters

It matters because high sleep demand limits activity windows. Any disruption at night can have serious effects.

It also explains why disturbed aardvarks decline quickly. They lose both feeding and rest time.

Did You Know?

Aardvarks often change sleeping burrows daily. This reduces parasite buildup and predator tracking.
