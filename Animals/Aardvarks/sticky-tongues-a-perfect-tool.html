Sticky Tongues: A Perfect Tool

Aardvarks can extend their tongues up to 30 cm to gather insects.

The sticky surface allows them to efficiently extract ants and termites from deep within mounds. This adaptation reduces energy spent foraging and increases feeding success.

Why This Matters

Their tongue is an excellent example of how a single feature can shape an animal’s entire feeding strategy.

It shows evolution’s role in crafting highly specialized tools for survival.

Did You Know?

Aardvark tongues are coated in sticky saliva.
