Pangolins Can Live Over 20 Years in Captivity

Longevity under care.

With proper care, pangolins can survive 20 years or more in zoos or sanctuaries. Survival depends on diet, stress reduction, and environmental enrichment. Captive longevity exceeds average wild lifespan. Studies provide insight into physiology and reproductive biology. Captive populations aid conservation research. Monitoring health is critical. It demonstrates potential for long-term survival if threats are removed.

Why This Matters

Understanding captive care can improve conservation programs. Longevity shows resilience under safe conditions.

It illustrates how human intervention can prolong life and aid species survival.

Did You Know?

Pangolins require specialized insect-based diets in captivity.
