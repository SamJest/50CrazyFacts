Pangolins Are Excellent Climbers

Forest acrobats.

Some pangolin species can climb trees using strong claws and prehensile tails. This allows access to arboreal ant nests and safety from predators. Climbing ability varies by species. Muscles and scales support stability on vertical surfaces. They can hang upside-down briefly. Climbing expands feeding opportunities and habitat range.

Why This Matters

It reduces predation risk and increases food sources. Arboreal behavior diversifies survival strategies.

It’s a remarkable combination of strength, balance, and scale protection.

Did You Know?

Pangolins can climb smooth tree trunks despite heavy armor.
