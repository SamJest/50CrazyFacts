Pangolins Are Extremely Rare in the Wild

Rarity and mystery.

Pangolins are elusive, nocturnal, and sparsely distributed. Low detectability makes population estimates difficult. Combined with poaching, rarity is increasing. Their secretive nature has contributed to myths and misidentification. Even in protected areas, sightings are uncommon. This rarity fuels conservation urgency.

Why This Matters

Rarity highlights the fragility of specialized mammals.

It underscores the importance of research and monitoring.

Did You Know?

Global awareness campaigns rely on emphasizing rarity.

Broader Theme

Pangolins are sometimes called “ghosts of the forest.”
