Pangolins Are the Most Trafficked Mammal in the World

Traffic crisis.

Pangolins are illegally traded for scales and meat more than any other mammal. Poaching occurs across Africa and Asia. Demand is fueled by traditional medicine and exotic meat markets. Law enforcement struggles to contain trafficking. Captive rescue and rehabilitation are limited. International protection exists but illegal trade persists. Survival is threatened without strong interventions.
