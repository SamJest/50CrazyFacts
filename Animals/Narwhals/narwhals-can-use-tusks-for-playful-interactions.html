Narwhals Can Use Tusks for Playful Interactions

Social fun.

Males sometimes gently tap tusks against each other or objects, seemingly for amusement or practice in dexterity.

Why This Matters

Playful use strengthens motor skills and social bonds, while preparing them for real-world interactions. Play may also reinforce hierarchy subtly.

It shows that even serious survival tools have social and recreational functions.

Did You Know?

Calves often mimic tusk play before reaching sexual maturity.
