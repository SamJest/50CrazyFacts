Narwhals Can Influence Arctic Food Webs

Trophic engineers.

Narwhals’ feeding patterns on squid and fish shape prey populations and nutrient cycles, affecting the entire Arctic marine ecosystem.

AD 1

Why This Matters

By regulating prey abundance, narwhals indirectly influence biodiversity and ecosystem health. Their predation helps balance trophic dynamics.

It underscores the ecological importance of apex predators.

Did You Know?

Narwhal predation can redistribute nutrients across hundreds of miles of Arctic waters.
