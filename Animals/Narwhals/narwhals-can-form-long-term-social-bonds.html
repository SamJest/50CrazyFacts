Narwhals Can Form Long-Term Social Bonds

Loyal Arctic pods.

Narwhals often maintain stable social relationships for years, with individuals recognizing each other and cooperating during feeding or migration.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Long-term bonds enhance survival, reduce conflict, and improve hunting efficiency. Social structure fosters knowledge transfer across generations.

It emphasizes the complexity of marine mammal societies.

Did You Know?

Narwhals may preferentially associate with the same pod members over decades.
