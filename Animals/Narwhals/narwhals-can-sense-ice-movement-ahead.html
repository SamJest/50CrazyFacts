Narwhals Can Sense Ice Movement Ahead

Climate forecasters.

Narwhals can detect vibrations and pressure changes in ice, predicting shifts or cracks in ice floes, enhancing survival in hazardous areas.

Why This Matters

Early detection prevents entrapment under ice and guides safe navigation. Ice sensing helps avoid predators hiding beneath unstable surfaces.

It illustrates the integration of sensory and behavioral adaptation to extreme habitats.

Did You Know?

Narwhals respond to ice movement even hundreds of meters away.
