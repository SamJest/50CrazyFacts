Narwhals Can Sense Water Pressure Shifts

Pressure pros.

Narwhals detect subtle water pressure changes with tusks and inner ears, aiding navigation and prey detection during deep dives.

Why This Matters

Pressure detection helps avoid obstacles, locate food, and coordinate with pod members. This minimizes energy waste and predation risk.

Advanced sensory perception illustrates adaptation to deep, dark environments. It highlights narwhal specialization for survival under extreme pressure.

Did You Know?

Narwhals sense pressure equivalent to several hundred feet of water depth.
