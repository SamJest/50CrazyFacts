Narwhals Can Exhibit Seasonal Weight Fluctuations

Blubber cycles.

Narwhals gain significant weight in summer when prey is abundant and metabolize blubber reserves in winter.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Weight fluctuation ensures energy for reproduction, migration, and survival during scarce periods. It is a critical adaptation to Arctic cycles.

It shows how physiology is tuned to environmental rhythms.

Did You Know?

Blubber thickness can vary by up to 30% seasonally.
