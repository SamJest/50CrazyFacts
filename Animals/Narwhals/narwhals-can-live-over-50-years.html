Narwhals Can Live Over 50 Years

Arctic elders.

Narwhals have relatively long lifespans for marine mammals, often exceeding 50 years. Age can be estimated by counting layers in teeth, similar to tree rings.

Why This Matters

Long life allows for multiple breeding seasons and the passing of ecological knowledge across generations. Older individuals help maintain pod stability and teach young members survival strategies.

Extended longevity contributes to population resilience in challenging Arctic environments. It also allows for long-term social bonds.

Did You Know?

Narwhals may remain in the same migratory route for decades.
