Narwhals Can Detect Magnetic Fields

Arctic compasses.

Narwhals may sense Earth’s magnetic fields, helping them navigate thousands of miles across featureless Arctic waters.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This ability aids long-distance migration and accurate return to feeding or breeding grounds. Magnetic sensitivity likely complements other sensory adaptations.

It highlights how animals evolve navigation strategies in extreme environments.

Did You Know?

Narwhals may adjust migratory routes based on subtle magnetic shifts.
