Narwhals Can Perform Deep-Water Foraging Dives

Master divers.

Narwhals routinely dive over 1,500 meters to hunt squid and fish in the dark, oxygen-poor depths.

Why This Matters

Deep dives access prey unavailable to many other predators, reducing competition. Physiological adaptations like bradycardia conserve oxygen.

It illustrates extreme diving specialization in Arctic marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Some dives last over 25 minutes without surfacing.
