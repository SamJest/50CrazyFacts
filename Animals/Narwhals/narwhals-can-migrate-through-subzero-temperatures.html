Narwhals Can Migrate Through Subzero Temperatures

Frost-adapted travelers.

Narwhals endure water temperatures near freezing during long migrations without succumbing to hypothermia.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Physiological adaptations like thick blubber and controlled blood flow maintain core temperature. Survival in freezing waters allows access to ice-covered prey areas.

It highlights extreme cold adaptation strategies in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can remain active at temperatures below -1.8°C for hours.
