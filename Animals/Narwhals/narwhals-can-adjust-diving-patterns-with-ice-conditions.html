Narwhals Can Adjust Diving Patterns With Ice Conditions

Ice-smart divers.

Narwhals modify dive depth and duration based on ice thickness and water temperature to optimize hunting and conserve energy.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Behavioral flexibility reduces risk of entrapment and maximizes feeding efficiency. Adaptive diving shows sophisticated environmental awareness.

It highlights the impact of climate change on diving behavior.

Did You Know?

Narwhals may switch from shallow to ultra-deep dives as ice conditions shift.
