Narwhals Can Survive Without Food for Extended Periods

Fasting experts.

Narwhals can go without feeding for days during long migrations or ice entrapment, relying on fat reserves.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This ability ensures survival when prey is scarce or inaccessible under thick ice. It highlights metabolic adaptation to extreme Arctic conditions.

Fasting tolerance allows flexibility in movement and timing, reducing mortality risk during harsh seasons.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can burn up to 20% of their body fat without ill effects.
