Narwhals Can Adjust Pod Formation to Ice Conditions

Flexible groups.

Narwhals alter pod size and spacing depending on ice density and water depth, maintaining safety and feeding efficiency.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Adaptive pod formation minimizes risk from predators and environmental hazards. It improves foraging coordination.

It emphasizes behavioral flexibility in Arctic species.

Did You Know?

Pods can split or merge within hours in response to ice changes.
