Narwhals Migrate Thousands of Miles Each Year

Seasonal voyagers.

Narwhals undertake extensive migrations from summer feeding grounds to wintering areas under dense ice. These journeys can exceed 3,000 miles.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Migration ensures access to abundant prey and safer waters during harsh winters. It also enables reproduction and survival in ice-prone habitats.

Long-distance travel demonstrates incredible endurance and navigation skills. Migration patterns are finely tuned to seasonal environmental cues.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can follow the same routes for generations.
