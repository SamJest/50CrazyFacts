Narwhals Can Produce Bubble Rings

Playful divers.

Narwhals have been observed exhaling to create bubble rings underwater, possibly for play or communication.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Bubble rings may provide mental stimulation and reinforce social bonds. This shows advanced motor control and cognitive function.

It highlights intelligence and curiosity in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Narwhals sometimes manipulate bubble rings with flippers or tusks.
