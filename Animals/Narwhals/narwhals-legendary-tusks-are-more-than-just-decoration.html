Narwhals’ Legendary Tusks Are More Than Just Decoration

The Arctic unicorn.

Narwhals are famous for their long, spiraled tusks, which are actually elongated canine teeth, often growing over 8 feet long. These tusks contain millions of nerve endings and are highly sensitive to touch, temperature, and pressure.

AD 1

Why This Matters

They are believed to help males assert dominance and attract mates. Tusks may also serve as sensory organs to detect changes in the environment, providing an evolutionary edge.

Tusks’ multifunctionality underscores the unique adaptation of narwhals to harsh Arctic environments. The tusk is not just ornamental but integral to survival and social interaction.

Did You Know?

Some narwhals have two tusks, but it is extremely rare.
