Narwhals Can Track Seasonal Ice Changes

Climate navigators.

Narwhals use environmental cues, including ice cover and water temperature, to time migrations and foraging patterns.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This ensures survival in a habitat where food availability and ice movement are unpredictable. Seasonal tracking enhances reproductive and hunting success.

It demonstrates how Arctic species rely on environmental signals for survival.

Did You Know?

Narwhals often adjust migration routes within weeks based on ice formation.
