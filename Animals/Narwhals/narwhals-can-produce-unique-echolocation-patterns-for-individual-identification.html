Narwhals Can Produce Unique Echolocation Patterns for Individual Identification

Acoustic signatures.

Each narwhal has distinctive echolocation clicks that pod members can recognize, facilitating social cohesion and coordination.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This acoustic individuality aids group hunting, predator alerts, and social bonding. It may reduce confusion in dense pods.

It highlights advanced communication and individual recognition in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Researchers can identify individual narwhals by sound alone.
