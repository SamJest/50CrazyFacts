Narwhals Can Sleep While Swimming

Underwater naps.

Narwhals exhibit unihemispheric slow-wave sleep, resting one brain hemisphere at a time while continuing to swim.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This enables continuous movement and breathing at the surface, reducing predation risk. It balances rest with survival needs.

It highlights unique neurological adaptations in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Narwhals can travel while sleeping for hours at a time.
