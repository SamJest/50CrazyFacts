Narwhals Can Exhibit Cooperative Breeding

Team parenting.

In some pods, multiple adults assist in raising calves, sharing protection and foraging responsibilities.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Cooperative care increases calf survival and strengthens social bonds within pods. This social behavior enhances population resilience.

It demonstrates the importance of shared parenting in complex marine societies.

Did You Know?

Helpers may include older siblings or unrelated adults from the pod.
