Narwhals Can Hunt in Total Darkness

Deep-sea predators.

Narwhals hunt at depths where sunlight never reaches, relying entirely on echolocation and sensory perception.

AD 1

Why This Matters

They can locate prey in pitch-black conditions that would defeat most predators. Hunting in darkness reduces competition from surface-feeding species.

It shows extreme sensory specialization for survival in lightless environments.

Did You Know?

Narwhals detect movement and even the orientation of fish in darkness.
