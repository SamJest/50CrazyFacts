Narwhals Can Detect Water Pressure Shifts

Sensitive divers.

Narwhals’ tusks and inner ears can detect minute changes in water pressure, aiding navigation during deep dives.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This helps them avoid obstacles and predators while locating prey at extreme depths. Pressure detection optimizes energy expenditure during dives.

It highlights specialized sensory evolution in extreme environments.

Did You Know?

They can sense pressure changes equivalent to several hundred feet of water depth.
