Narwhals Can Vocalize Differently Between Pods

Pod dialects.

Different pods have distinct vocal patterns, which may help maintain pod identity and coordinate hunting.

Why This Matters

Pod-specific vocalizations strengthen social cohesion and prevent inter-pod conflict. Communication adapts to local environmental conditions.

It shows cultural variation and learned behavior in marine mammals.

Did You Know?

Vocal dialects may change gradually over years within a pod.
