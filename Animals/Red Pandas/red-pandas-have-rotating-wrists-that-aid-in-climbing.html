Red Pandas Have Rotating Wrists That Aid in Climbing

Tree acrobatics in action.

Red pandas possess flexible wrists that rotate to help them grip branches securely while climbing. This rotation works together with their false thumb for maximum dexterity. Juveniles practice wrist rotation to perfect tree navigation. The adaptation minimizes falls in dense forest canopies and allows for precise movements while feeding. Rotating wrists also help them manipulate bamboo and fruits with ease. This feature highlights their specialized arboreal lifestyle.
