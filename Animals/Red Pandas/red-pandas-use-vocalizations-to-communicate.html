Red Pandas Use Vocalizations to Communicate

Whistles, squeals, and chatter.

Red pandas produce a range of sounds including whistles, squeals, and chatter. These communicate alarm, mating readiness, or contact between mother and young. Juveniles quickly learn to interpret and mimic maternal calls. Vocal signals complement visual cues, tail positioning, and scent marking. Communication is subtle but effective in dense forests. It is especially important for a solitary species.
