Red Pandas Communicate with High-Pitched Whistles

Vocal signals of the forest.

Red pandas use high-pitched whistles and squeals to communicate over short distances. These sounds signal alarm, mating readiness, or contact between mother and juvenile. Juveniles learn the repertoire by imitating maternal calls. Whistles are particularly useful in dense forest where visual signals may fail. Sound complements tail and scent signals, creating a multi-modal communication system. Vocal flexibility allows adaptation to variable environmental conditions.
