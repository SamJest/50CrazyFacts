Red Pandas Are Excellent Swimmers

Aquatic abilities surprise.

Despite being forest dwellers, red pandas can swim when necessary. They use this skill to cross streams or escape predators. Juveniles practice swimming in shallow water under maternal supervision. Swimming extends their foraging territory and offers an escape route. Their water-resistant fur prevents hypothermia during brief swims. This ability demonstrates behavioral versatility beyond climbing and terrestrial movement.
