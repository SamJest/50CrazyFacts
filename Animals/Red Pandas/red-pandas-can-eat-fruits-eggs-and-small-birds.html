Red Pandas Can Eat Fruits, Eggs, and Small Birds

Flexible feeders.

While bamboo is dominant in their diet, red pandas opportunistically consume fruits, eggs, and even small birds. This supplements their nutrient intake, especially protein. Juveniles learn hunting and foraging skills under maternal supervision. Dietary flexibility helps during bamboo shortages or seasonal changes. It demonstrates adaptability beyond strict herbivory. Such behavior contributes to survival in fluctuating forest ecosystems.
