Red Pandas Can Eat Up to 2 Pounds of Bamboo Daily

Bamboo buffet.

Despite being small, red pandas consume up to 2 pounds of bamboo leaves and shoots per day. Their digestive system is adapted for high-fiber, low-nutrition bamboo. Juveniles gradually increase intake as they mature. Eating large amounts compensates for poor nutritional value. Bamboo forms the majority of their diet but is supplemented with fruits and eggs. Daily consumption shapes territorial and foraging behavior.

Why This Matters

High bamboo intake drives habitat selection and energy balance.

It underlines the importance of bamboo conservation for species survival.
