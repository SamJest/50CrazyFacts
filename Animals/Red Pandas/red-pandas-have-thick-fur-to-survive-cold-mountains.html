Red Pandas Have Thick Fur to Survive Cold Mountains

Arctic-ready coat.

Red pandas inhabit Himalayan and Tibetan forests, where temperatures can drop below freezing. Their thick fur covers their body, including the soles of their feet, providing insulation and protection from snow. The fur also camouflages them among mossy and bamboo-filled forests. Juveniles inherit dense fur for survival. Seasonal shedding adjusts insulation levels. This adaptation allows red pandas to remain active year-round in harsh climates.
