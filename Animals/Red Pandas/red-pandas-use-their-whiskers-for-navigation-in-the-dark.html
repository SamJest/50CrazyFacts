Red Pandas Use Their Whiskers for Navigation in the Dark

Night-time radar.

Red pandas have long, sensitive whiskers that detect obstacles in low-light conditions. This is essential for their nocturnal and crepuscular activity. Whiskers help measure branch thickness and sense nearby foliage. Juveniles refine whisker use while learning to climb at night. This sensory adaptation complements keen eyesight and hearing, enabling safe arboreal navigation. It ensures they can forage efficiently even in dense, shadowed forests.

Why This Matters

Whiskers enhance nocturnal navigation and safety.

They are crucial for night-time foraging and predator avoidance.
