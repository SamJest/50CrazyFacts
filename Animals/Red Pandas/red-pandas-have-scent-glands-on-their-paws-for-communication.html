Red Pandas Have Scent Glands on Their Paws for Communication

Walking messages.

Red pandas have scent glands on the soles of their feet. When they walk or scratch surfaces, they leave chemical signals. This communicates territory boundaries and presence to other pandas. Juveniles learn to detect and interpret paw scents. Chemical signaling complements visual and vocal communication. It ensures information transfer in dense forest underbrush.

Why This Matters

Paw scenting supports territorial control and social interaction.

It is essential for maintaining solitary lifestyles.

Did You Know?

Red pandas have scent glands on their paws to communicate presence and mark territory.
