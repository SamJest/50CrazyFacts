Red Pandas Can Curl Up Like a Cinnamon Bun

Sleeping strategies.

Red pandas curl their bodies and wrap their bushy tails around themselves when resting. This conserves heat during cold nights. Juveniles instinctively adopt this behavior for warmth and safety. Curling also protects vulnerable body parts from predators. Sleeping in this position maximizes insulation while minimizing energy expenditure. It is a behavior observed in wild and captive populations.
