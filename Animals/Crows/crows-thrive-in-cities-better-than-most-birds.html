Crows Thrive in Cities Better Than Most Birds

Crows excel in urban environments.

They exploit buildings, roads, and human-provided food, adapting strategies to city life. Urban populations often grow due to these advantages.

Why This Matters

Behavioral flexibility allows crows to capitalize on human environments.

It shows how intelligence supports survival in rapidly changing habitats.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Urban crows have been observed using traffic to crack nuts efficiently.
