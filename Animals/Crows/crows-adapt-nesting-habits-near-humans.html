Crows Adapt Nesting Habits Near Humans

Crows innovate with human-made structures for shelter.

They use poles, bridges, buildings, wire, and plastic for nests, reducing predator risks and demonstrating flexibility in habitat use.

Why This Matters

Nesting adaptation improves reproductive success.

It shows resourcefulness and intelligence in exploiting new environments.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crows incorporate discarded materials into nests that increase survival of chicks.
