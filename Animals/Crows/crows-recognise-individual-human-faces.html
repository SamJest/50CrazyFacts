Crows Recognise Individual Human Faces

Crows can identify humans and remember interactions for years.

They associate specific people with positive or negative experiences and communicate these associations to other crows. This ability influences social behavior and avoidance strategies.

Why This Matters

Facial recognition provides safety and resource advantages.

It demonstrates remarkable cognitive and social intelligence in an avian species.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows sometimes “warn” others about humans who have previously threatened them.

Source

Washington University (washington.edu)
