Crows Can Recognize Individual Humans Wearing Masks

Masked mystery solved.

Crows can differentiate humans even when faces are partially covered, using cues like posture, clothing, and movement. This recognition helps them identify threats or feeders in urban areas. Juveniles learn these observational skills from adults. This ability demonstrates cognitive flexibility and adaptive intelligence.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ recognition of masked humans shows high-level observational skills.

It emphasizes adaptability and memory in dynamic human environments.

Did You Know?

They react to threatening individuals regardless of masks by calling alarms.
