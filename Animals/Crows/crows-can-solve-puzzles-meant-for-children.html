Crows Can Solve Puzzles Meant for Children

Crows outperform young humans in logic and reasoning tasks.

They tackle puzzles involving multiple steps, showing memory, planning, and problem-solving skills comparable to young children and some primates.

Why This Matters

Advanced puzzle-solving demonstrates high cognitive capacity.

It highlights the remarkable intelligence and adaptability of crows.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Experiments show crows can choose tools in the correct order to solve a puzzle.

Nature (nature.com)
