Crows May Benefit From Climate Change

Crows expand their range as global temperatures rise.

Generalist diets, adaptability, and climate tolerance allow crows to exploit new territories and maintain populations in changing climates.

Why This Matters

Climate flexibility supports survival amid environmental shifts.

It suggests that intelligent generalist species may thrive where specialists struggle.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crow species are now overwintering further north than they did a century ago.
