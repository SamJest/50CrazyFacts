Crows Have Brains Proportionally Similar to Great Apes

Crows rival primates in relative brain size and processing ability.

Despite small skulls, crows have densely packed brains organized for rapid computation. This enables tool use, problem-solving, and memory tasks without a mammalian cortex.

Why This Matters

High brain-to-body ratio demonstrates cognitive efficiency.

It explains why crows achieve feats comparable to primates despite being birds.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crows outperform young primates on problem-solving tests.
