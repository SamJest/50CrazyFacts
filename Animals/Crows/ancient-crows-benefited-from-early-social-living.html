Ancient Crows Benefited from Early Social Living

Social behavior has long been part of crow survival strategies.

Early crow species formed loose groups, which helped share food information and avoid predators. Fossil evidence supports long-term social tendencies that persist in modern crow communities.

Why This Matters

Group living enhanced learning and memory.

This evolutionary social intelligence allows crows to form complex interactions and alliances today.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some fossilized crow sites show group nesting and feeding patterns.
