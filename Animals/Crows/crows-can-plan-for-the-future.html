Crows Can Plan for the Future

Think ahead, feathered edition.

Crows exhibit foresight by saving tools, storing food, or positioning themselves strategically for future events. Experiments show they will select tools in advance to solve problems later, an ability previously considered uniquely human. Planning allows them to optimize foraging, avoid risks, and increase survival rates. This behavior demonstrates reasoning, delayed gratification, and problem-solving skills.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ future-oriented behavior underscores intelligence rivaling some mammals.

It highlights the cognitive depth required to navigate complex environments.

Did You Know?

They have been observed saving a tool for use several days later.
