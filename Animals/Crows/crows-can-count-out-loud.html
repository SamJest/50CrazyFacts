Crows Can Count Out Loud

Crows exhibit numerical awareness through vocalizations.

They can represent quantities and respond to numerical challenges, demonstrating early counting ability and cognitive understanding of numbers.

Why This Matters

Numerical understanding improves decision-making in resource management.

It showcases intelligence comparable to mammals, especially in planning and foraging.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows can distinguish between small quantities and adjust their behavior accordingly.
