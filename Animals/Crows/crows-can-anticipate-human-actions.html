Crows Can Anticipate Human Actions

Reading intentions.

Crows observe humans and predict likely behaviors, such as feeding or threatening actions. This enables them to plan avoidance or approach strategies. Anticipation of human behavior enhances survival in urban settings. Juveniles learn by watching adults interact with people. Such predictive ability reflects reasoning and situational awareness.

Why This Matters

Crows’ anticipation of humans demonstrates high-level cognition.

It highlights adaptation to environments dominated by other intelligent species.

Did You Know?

They may wait near trash bins shortly before humans are scheduled to discard food.
