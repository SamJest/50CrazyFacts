Crows Can Track Human Gestures

Reading body language.

Crows understand pointing, gestures, and human cues to locate food or danger. They learn from repeated observation and adapt behavior accordingly. Gesture recognition helps them exploit resources and avoid threats. Juveniles learn from adults how to respond to human signals. This ability demonstrates cross-species communication and intelligence.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ tracking of human gestures shows observational learning.

It highlights the integration of social and environmental awareness.

Did You Know?

They may follow a pointing human to find hidden food rewards.
