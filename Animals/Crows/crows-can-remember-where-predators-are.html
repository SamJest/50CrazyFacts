Crows Can Remember Where Predators Are

Hawk-spotting experts.

Crows track predator locations and learn where threats are likely to appear. They communicate these locations to other flock members. This knowledge helps avoid danger during foraging and resting. Juveniles learn from adults where it’s safe to perch or feed. Mapping predator presence shows advanced spatial memory and risk assessment.

Why This Matters

Crows’ memory of predator locations demonstrates cognitive mapping skills.

It highlights survival strategies in dynamic environments.

Did You Know?

They may avoid specific trees where hawks frequently hunt.
