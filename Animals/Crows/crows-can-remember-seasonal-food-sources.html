Crows Can Remember Seasonal Food Sources

Seasonal planners.

Crows track locations of seasonal fruits, nuts, and insects. They remember which areas are productive at different times of the year. This memory improves foraging efficiency and survival. Juveniles learn from adults to follow seasonal patterns. Memory of resources demonstrates foresight and environmental awareness.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ seasonal tracking shows cognitive mapping and planning.

It emphasizes intelligence in resource management.

Did You Know?

They can relocate berry bushes or oak trees from the previous season.
