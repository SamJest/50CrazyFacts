Crows Can Imitate Human Speech

Crows mimic sounds from their environment, including human speech.

They replicate words, alarms, or animal sounds, often regional in tone. Vocal learning enhances survival and communication.

Why This Matters

Vocal mimicry improves social interaction and adaptability.

It showcases the flexibility and intelligence of crow communication systems.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crows can imitate the sounds of mobile phones or dogs.
