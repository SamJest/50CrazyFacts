Early Crows Thrived While Other Birds Went Extinct

Some of the earliest crow-like birds survived extinction events that wiped out many contemporaries.

Fossil records show that these birds thrived by eating varied diets, including insects, small animals, and carrion. Their dietary flexibility allowed them to persist through ice ages and other environmental upheavals, traits still seen in modern crows.

Why This Matters

This survival strategy illustrates the advantage of adaptability over specialization.

It provides insight into how crows became so widespread and resilient across continents.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Early corvids could adapt to multiple climates, from tropical to temperate, millions of years ago.
