Crows Can Recognize Themselves in Mirrors

Self-awareness at its finest.

Some studies suggest crows can pass mirror tests, indicating self-recognition. This awareness involves understanding one’s reflection as distinct from another individual. Mirror recognition reflects high cognitive functioning and consciousness. Juveniles may explore reflections playfully, learning body awareness and problem-solving skills. Self-recognition is rare in animals and often linked to intelligence.

Why This Matters
