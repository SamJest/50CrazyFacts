Crows Heal Faster Than Many Other Birds

Crows exhibit rapid recovery from injury due to strong immune responses.

Studies suggest social living enhances immunity, allowing injured birds to rejoin groups quickly. Quick healing increases individual survival and overall population stability.

Why This Matters

Enhanced immunity reduces vulnerability in harsh conditions.

It shows that social and physiological traits combine to support crow resilience.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows often help injured peers recover by sharing food.
