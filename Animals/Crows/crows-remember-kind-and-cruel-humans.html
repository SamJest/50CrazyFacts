Crows Remember Kind and Cruel Humans

Crows track individuals over time, remembering interactions.

They reward positive experiences with gifts or friendly behavior and warn other crows about harmful humans, showing long-term memory and social communication.

Why This Matters

Recognizing humans influences safety and resource management.

It reveals cognitive sophistication and cultural transmission of knowledge.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crows have been documented bringing small gifts to humans who feed them regularly.

Source

Washington University (washington.edu)
