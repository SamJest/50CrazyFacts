Crows Can Deceive Others

Master tricksters.

Crows use deception to protect food, mislead rivals, or distract predators. They may fake hiding food or pretend to look elsewhere while stealing. Juveniles observe adults using these tactics, demonstrating learning of strategy. Deception indicates theory of mind and social intelligence.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ deceptive behavior shows advanced reasoning.

It highlights strategic thinking and adaptive survival tactics.

Did You Know?

They may pretend to hide a nut while actually keeping it in a beak pocket.
