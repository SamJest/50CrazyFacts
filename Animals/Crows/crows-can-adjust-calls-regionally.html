Crows Can Adjust Calls Regionally

Regional dialects.

Crows develop local variations in calls, much like accents. These dialects help with flock cohesion, territoriality, and mate recognition. Young crows learn local calls from adults. Dialects demonstrate cultural transmission and learning flexibility.

Why This Matters

Crows’ regional call variation highlights social learning and adaptation.

It emphasizes communication complexity within populations.

Did You Know?

Calls in urban areas may differ from rural populations.
