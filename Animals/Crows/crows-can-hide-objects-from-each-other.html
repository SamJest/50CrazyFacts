Crows Can Hide Objects from Each Other

Jealousy or strategy?

Crows sometimes hide food or shiny objects when other crows are watching, demonstrating awareness of others’ intentions. This suggests theory of mind—the understanding that others have knowledge and desires separate from one’s own. Such behavior protects resources and ensures survival. Juveniles learn this tactic by observation. Object hiding indicates cognitive sophistication beyond simple instinct.

Why This Matters

Crows’ strategic hiding shows advanced reasoning.

It highlights their awareness of social dynamics and competition.

Did You Know?

They have been observed moving food to hidden spots when being observed by peers.
