Crows Can Recognize Patterns in Human Behavior

Observant and clever.

Crows notice routines, schedules, and behaviors of humans, adjusting their actions accordingly. For example, they learn garbage collection schedules or feeding times. Pattern recognition allows them to exploit resources efficiently and avoid threats. Juveniles learn these routines from adults. This behavior demonstrates adaptability and intelligence in dynamic environments.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ recognition of human patterns underscores environmental learning.

It highlights cognitive skills used to thrive in urban landscapes.

Did You Know?

They may retrieve food from trash only after observing collection patterns.
