Crows Can Recognize Individual Predators

Know your enemy.

Crows can identify specific hawks, humans, or other predators and remember past encounters. This allows targeted avoidance or mobbing behavior. Juveniles learn predator recognition from adults. Recognizing individuals demonstrates memory, pattern recognition, and risk assessment.

Why This Matters

Crows’ predator recognition highlights survival intelligence.

It emphasizes learning from experience to navigate threats effectively.

Did You Know?

They may dive-bomb a particular hawk known to hunt in their area.
