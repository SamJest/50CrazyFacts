Crows Can Use Water to Retrieve Food

Feathered engineers.

Crows drop stones into water to raise the level and access floating food. This behavior shows an understanding of physics and cause-effect relationships. Juveniles learn the technique by watching adults. This tool-assisted foraging improves survival and demonstrates problem-solving.

Why This Matters

Crows’ water manipulation illustrates reasoning and planning.

It highlights intelligence and adaptability in resource acquisition.

Did You Know?

They have successfully raised water levels in tubes to reach floating mealworms.
