Crows Can Solve Multi-Step Mechanical Problems

Problem-solving masters.

Crows complete tasks that require multiple sequential steps, like using one tool to get another to reach food. They can plan actions in advance, showing reasoning and memory. Juveniles learn by observing adults performing these tasks. Multi-step problem-solving enhances foraging efficiency and adaptability.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ multi-step reasoning demonstrates cognitive sophistication.

It highlights intelligence comparable to primates in problem-solving tasks.

Did You Know?

They can pull strings, use sticks, and manipulate objects in sequence to access food.
