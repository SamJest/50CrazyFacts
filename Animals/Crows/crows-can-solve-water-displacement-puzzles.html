Crows Can Solve Water Displacement Puzzles

Physics in flight.

Crows can raise water levels in tubes by dropping stones to reach floating food. This shows understanding of cause and effect, volume, and problem-solving. Such experiments reveal innovative thinking and tool use. Juveniles observe and learn the technique from adults. This behavior demonstrates both intelligence and adaptability.

Why This Matters

Crows’ success with water displacement illustrates abstract reasoning.

It emphasizes problem-solving skills comparable to primates.

Did You Know?

They can drop multiple stones into water to retrieve floating treats.
