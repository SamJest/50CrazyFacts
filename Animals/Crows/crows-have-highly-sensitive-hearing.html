Crows Have Highly Sensitive Hearing

Crows can detect subtle sounds crucial for survival.

Advanced auditory systems allow crows to locate predators, track food, and respond rapidly to alarm calls, supporting social and environmental awareness.

Why This Matters

Acute hearing strengthens communication and group coordination.

It enables crows to navigate complex social dynamics and environmental hazards.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows can distinguish individual human voices and remember them for years.
