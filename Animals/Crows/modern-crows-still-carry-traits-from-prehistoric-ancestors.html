Modern Crows Still Carry Traits from Prehistoric Ancestors

Crows exhibit behaviors inherited from millions of years ago.

Today’s crows retain caution, curiosity, and social awareness, reflecting ancestral survival strategies. Fossil and behavioral studies confirm continuity over time.

Why This Matters

Ancient traits provide insights into how intelligence evolves across generations.

It shows that evolution refines rather than replaces successful survival strategies.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Even modern urban crows display food caching behaviors similar to prehistoric corvids.

Source

American Museum of Natural History (americanmuseum.org)
