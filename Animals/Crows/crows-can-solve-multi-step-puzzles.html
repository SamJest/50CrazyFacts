Crows Can Solve Multi-Step Puzzles

Crows are capable of performing tasks that require several sequential actions.

They can complete puzzles with multiple steps, adapting strategies as rules change. This demonstrates advanced problem-solving abilities.

Why This Matters

Multi-step problem-solving improves foraging efficiency.

It shows flexibility and planning ability that rival mammals.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows have solved puzzles designed for young children, outperforming them in some cases.
