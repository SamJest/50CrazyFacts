Crows Can Recognize Themselves in Photos

Self-aware birds.

Crows show reactions to their own image in photos, sometimes treating them as real crows or assessing behavior. This indicates self-recognition, memory, and visual discrimination. Adults demonstrate awareness while juveniles explore responses through mimicry. Such behavior is rare in animals and linked to high intelligence.

Why This Matters

Crows’ photo recognition suggests self-awareness.

It highlights advanced visual processing and cognition.

Did You Know?

They may react differently to photos of themselves versus unknown crows.
