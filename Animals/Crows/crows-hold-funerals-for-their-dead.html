Crows Hold Funerals for Their Dead

Crows gather to inspect deceased group members, often silently and attentively.

These gatherings allow the assessment of threats and social learning about predators or dangers, resembling primitive funerals.

Why This Matters

Social awareness improves group safety.

It indicates emotional intelligence and complex social behavior in birds.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Funerals may last for several hours as crows observe the deceased carefully.
