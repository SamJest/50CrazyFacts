Crows Benefit From Farming and Agriculture

Crows exploit agricultural landscapes for easy food access.

They follow ploughs for insects and feed on crops, benefiting from predictable human activity. This interaction affects their population and distribution.

Why This Matters

Adaptation to human-altered landscapes supports species growth.

It shows how intelligent generalists thrive alongside human development.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crow populations often increase significantly near large farms due to abundant food.

Source

RSPB (rspb.org.uk)
