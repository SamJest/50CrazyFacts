Crows Learn Traffic Patterns to Find Food

Crows use roads and vehicles as tools for feeding.

They drop hard nuts or shells onto pedestrian crossings and wait for cars to crack them open, demonstrating remarkable understanding of timing and cause-effect.

Why This Matters

Adapting to traffic patterns improves resource acquisition.

It highlights innovative problem-solving in human-dominated environments.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Some crows have learned to avoid specific cars while timing others to crack nuts.
