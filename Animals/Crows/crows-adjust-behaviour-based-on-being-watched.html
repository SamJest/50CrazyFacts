Crows Adjust Behaviour Based on Being Watched

Crows change their actions when under observation.

They may hide food more carefully or alter strategies if a rival or human is watching, reflecting self-awareness and social intelligence.

Why This Matters

Self-monitoring protects resources and enhances survival.

It demonstrates advanced cognition and understanding of others’ perspectives.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows have been seen re-hiding food if they know a human is watching them.
