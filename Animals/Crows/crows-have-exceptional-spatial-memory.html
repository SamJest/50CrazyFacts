Crows Have Exceptional Spatial Memory

Crows remember the location of hundreds of food caches.

Their hippocampus supports advanced spatial memory, enabling long-term storage and retrieval of resources, crucial for survival in variable environments.

Why This Matters

Spatial memory allows for efficient foraging strategies.

It explains their ability to plan and navigate complex landscapes over time.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows can recall exact hiding spots months later, even in urban areas.
