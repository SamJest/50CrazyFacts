Crows Can Use Social Learning to Avoid Traps

Street-smart survival.

Crows learn from observing peers which human traps or dangers to avoid. If one crow is caught in a trap, others remember the location and method, adapting behavior accordingly. This learning reduces risk for the group. Juveniles learn survival strategies through observation.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ social learning demonstrates adaptive intelligence and collective knowledge.

It highlights learning from experience and social networks.

Did You Know?

Flocks avoid areas where one member was trapped previously.
