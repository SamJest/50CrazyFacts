Crows Can Use Disguise Tactics

Feathers meet stealth.

Crows sometimes hide their presence using trees, shadows, or environmental objects when stealing food or avoiding predators. This shows awareness of how others perceive them and strategic thinking. Juveniles learn hiding tactics from adults. Disguise tactics increase success in foraging and safety.

Why This Matters

Crows’ use of stealth demonstrates planning and theory of mind.

It highlights strategic thinking in social and survival behaviors.

Did You Know?
