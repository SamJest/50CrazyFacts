Crows Use Their Feet Like Hands

Crows’ feet function almost like human hands in holding objects.

They often stabilize food with one foot while pecking, enabling tool use and manipulation. This dexterity complements their beak and cognitive abilities.

Why This Matters

Foot coordination enhances survival through precise handling.

It highlights a remarkable combination of anatomy and intelligence in crows.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Crows can carry sticks with one foot while using the beak to probe.
