Crows Adapted to Both Forests and Open Landscapes

Crows thrive in a wide variety of habitats.

Unlike birds specialized for single environments, crows evolved to inhabit forests, plains, and coastal areas. This ecological versatility reduced extinction risk and allowed them to colonize diverse regions.

Why This Matters

Habitat flexibility illustrates the survival advantage of being a generalist.

It explains why crows remain common in both wild and urban landscapes worldwide.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Urban crows often mimic natural behaviors adapted for forest and field environments.
