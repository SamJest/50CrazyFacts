Crows Can Engage in Cultural Transmission of Skills

Passing down knowledge.

Crows teach and learn behaviors within their communities, creating traditions of tool use, food preferences, and social rituals. Knowledge spreads across generations, ensuring survival and adaptation. Juveniles acquire behaviors faster by observing adults. Cultural transmission is rare in animals and indicates high cognitive abilities.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Crows’ cultural learning shows advanced intelligence and community knowledge.

It highlights the social spread of skills beyond instinctual behavior.

Did You Know?

They may adopt new foraging techniques introduced by innovative group members.
