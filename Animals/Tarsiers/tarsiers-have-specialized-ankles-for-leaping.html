Tarsiers Have Specialized Ankles for Leaping

Ankle engineering.

Tarsiers’ ankles are highly flexible and strong, absorbing impact during jumps. This adaptation allows precise landings on narrow branches. Long hind limbs work with ankles for extreme leaping distance. Ankles prevent injuries during high-velocity leaps. Flexibility contributes to stealth and stability in dense forest canopies. Specialized joints support arboreal agility and predator evasion. Evolution optimized tarsier ankle morphology for arboreal leaping. Precise landing reduces energy loss and maximizes hunting efficiency.

Why This Matters

Ankle adaptations prevent injury and aid mobility.

They enhance leaping efficiency and arboreal survival.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers have highly flexible ankles that enable precise and safe leaping.
