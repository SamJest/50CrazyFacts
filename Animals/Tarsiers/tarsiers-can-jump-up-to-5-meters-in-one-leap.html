Tarsiers Can Jump Up to 5 Meters in One Leap

Arboreal acrobatics.

Tarsiers can leap up to 5 meters between trees using strong hind legs. This ability allows them to navigate dense canopies efficiently and escape predators. Powerful leg muscles store energy for explosive jumps. Long tail assists with balance and trajectory control. Precise landings reduce risk of injury and maintain stealth. Leaping skill is vital for hunting moving prey at night. It exemplifies extreme adaptation to arboreal nocturnal life. Tarsiers’ jumping ability amazes researchers and is a survival cornerstone.
