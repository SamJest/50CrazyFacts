Tarsiers: The Tiny Primates With Giant Eyes

Meet the primates whose eyes are bigger than their brains! Tarsiers are nocturnal wonders with eyes perfectly adapted for seeing in the dark. Their oversized orbs dominate their small faces and give them an almost alien appearance.

Each tarsier eye is roughly the same size as its entire brain, allowing exceptional night vision. This adaptation helps them detect prey like insects and small vertebrates in pitch-dark forests. Their eyes are fixed in their sockets, so tarsiers rotate their heads nearly 180 degrees to look around, similar to owls. This gives them a predator and prey advantage simultaneously. Evolution favored eye size over brain size for survival in dense, nocturnal habitats. Their striking appearance has fascinated scientists and wildlife enthusiasts alike.
