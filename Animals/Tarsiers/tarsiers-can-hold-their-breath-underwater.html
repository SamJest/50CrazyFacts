Tarsiers Can Hold Their Breath Underwater

Surprising swimmers.

Though primarily arboreal, tarsiers can hold their breath for several minutes to cross small streams or evade predators. They fold their limbs close to their body and remain completely still underwater. This behavior is rare among primates and shows remarkable adaptability. Water-holding ability adds an extra survival strategy in fragmented forest habitats. It allows them to exploit areas other predators might avoid. This skill is likely learned and refined through experience. Tarsiers’ ability to use water as a hiding place enhances overall fitness.

Why This Matters

Holding breath improves predator evasion.

It expands the types of habitats they can safely navigate.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers can stay submerged for several minutes when crossing water.
