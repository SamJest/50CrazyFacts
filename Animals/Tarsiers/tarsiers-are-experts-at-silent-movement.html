Tarsiers Are Experts at Silent Movement

Stealth masters.

Tarsiers move with extreme quietness to avoid alerting prey or predators. Soft footpads, careful limb placement, and slow branch navigation reduce sound. Silent movement complements nocturnal hunting and ambush techniques. It allows tarsiers to approach prey closely without startling them. Stealth improves hunting efficiency and reduces exposure to nocturnal predators. Energy is conserved by minimizing unnecessary movement and noise. Tarsiers’ silent locomotion is a key adaptation for nocturnal arboreal life. The behavior enhances both survival and predatory success.

Why This Matters

Silent movement increases hunting success.

It reduces detection by predators and prey alike.

Did You Know?

Tarsiers move silently through trees to avoid alerting prey or predators.
