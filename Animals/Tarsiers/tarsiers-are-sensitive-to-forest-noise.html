Tarsiers Are Sensitive to Forest Noise

Acoustic specialists.

Tarsiers rely on subtle sounds to detect prey or predators. Excessive forest noise from human activity can disrupt their hunting and communication. Sensory sensitivity allows pinpointing insect movements in complete darkness. Noise pollution can increase stress and reduce feeding efficiency. Conservation efforts must consider acoustic habitat quality. Understanding sensitivity helps protect critical forest corridors. It highlights the importance of natural soundscapes for nocturnal species.
