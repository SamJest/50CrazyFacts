Tarsiers Can Leap Vertically From the Ground

Vertical acrobats.

Tarsiers can jump straight up from a standing position to reach low branches or escape predators. Powerful hind limbs store elastic energy for explosive upward propulsion. Vertical leaping complements horizontal jumps and allows access to multiple forest layers. This agility is uncommon among small primates. It demonstrates muscle specialization and neuromuscular coordination. Vertical leaps conserve energy compared to climbing. Such versatility improves foraging and predator evasion.
