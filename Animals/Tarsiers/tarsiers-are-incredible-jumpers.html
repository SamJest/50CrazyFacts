Tarsiers Are Incredible Jumpers

Jump like superheroes.

Tarsiers can leap distances up to 40 times their body length. Powerful hind limbs and elongated ankle bones give them extraordinary propulsion. They can jump between branches with precision, avoiding predators and catching prey mid-air. Jumping ability is complemented by strong claws for gripping bark. This makes them effective hunters and highly agile arboreal acrobats. Evolution has fine-tuned muscles and tendons for maximum jumping efficiency. It’s a defining characteristic of their survival strategy.
