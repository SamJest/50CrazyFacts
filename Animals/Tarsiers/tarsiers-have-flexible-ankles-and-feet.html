Tarsiers Have Flexible Ankles and Feet

Acrobatic feet.

Tarsiers’ ankle and foot joints are highly flexible, allowing precise grasping and landing during leaps. Their hind feet have elongated tarsal bones for powerful jumps. Toes spread widely for stability on branches. This adaptation supports vertical clinging and leaping in dense forests. Combined with long fingers, it provides exceptional arboreal agility. Joint flexibility reduces risk of injury during rapid movements. These traits are crucial for hunting, predator avoidance, and energy-efficient locomotion.
