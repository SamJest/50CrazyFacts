Sloths Can Swim Efficiently

Unexpected aquatic skill.

Sloths are surprisingly good swimmers, moving three times faster in water than on land. Their long limbs and slow metabolism allow them to float and paddle for distances. Swimming helps them cross rivers to reach new feeding areas. It is also an escape mechanism from predators. They can hold their breath and adjust buoyancy for efficient movement. Some species actively seek water, showing comfort in aquatic environments. This skill complements their slow arboreal lifestyle.

Why This Matters

Swimming increases habitat access and predator avoidance.

It shows versatility beyond their tree-bound life.

Did You Know?

Sloths are surprisingly agile swimmers despite their slow land movements.
