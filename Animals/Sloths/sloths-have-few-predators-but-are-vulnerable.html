Sloths Have Few Predators but Are Vulnerable

Selective threats.

Sloths’ main predators include harpy eagles, jaguars, and humans. Slow movement makes them easy targets on the ground, but camouflage and canopy life reduce encounters. Predation pressure is low but significant, shaping behavior. Threats include habitat destruction and poaching. Predator avoidance influences sleep location, activity patterns, and vigilance. Despite few natural enemies, sloths must remain strategically cautious. Their survival depends on blending in and choosing safe canopy routes.
