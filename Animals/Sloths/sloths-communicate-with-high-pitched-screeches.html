Sloths Communicate With High-Pitched Screeches

Vocal surprises.

Despite being slow and seemingly quiet, sloths can produce high-pitched sounds to communicate. These vocalizations are mostly used by mothers to locate infants or during mating. Screeches can carry through dense foliage. Communication is rare and energy-efficient, conserving stamina. The vocal patterns vary between species and individuals. Sound signals complement their subtle scent-marking behaviors. This method ensures social and reproductive interactions without unnecessary energy use.
