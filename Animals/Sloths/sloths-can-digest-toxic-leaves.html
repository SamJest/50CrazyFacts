Sloths Can Digest Toxic Leaves

Folate and tannin tolerance.

Some sloth species can eat leaves toxic to other animals, including tannin-rich foliage. Their stomach microbes neutralize toxins over slow fermentation. This adaptation expands food availability and reduces competition. Detoxification requires energy-efficient metabolism and patience. By tolerating toxins, sloths exploit niches inaccessible to faster mammals. Slow feeding allows for gradual ingestion, avoiding poisoning. This makes them versatile feeders despite limited mobility.
