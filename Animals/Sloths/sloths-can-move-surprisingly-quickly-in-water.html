Sloths Can Move Surprisingly Quickly in Water

Aquatic speedster.

Despite slow land movement, sloths are agile swimmers. They use all four limbs to paddle efficiently, often traveling three times faster in water than on land. Swimming allows them to cross rivers, escape predators, or access new feeding areas. Water buoyancy helps reduce energy expenditure. Even with a slow metabolism, sloths can cover distances effectively when submerged. This skill increases habitat flexibility and survival. It’s a remarkable contrast to their reputation as “slow” animals.

Why This Matters

Swimming expands habitat range and safety.

It highlights an unexpected versatility in their abilities.

Did You Know?

Sloths can swim faster than they move on land using a four-limbed doggy-paddle style.
