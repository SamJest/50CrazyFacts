Sloths Can Hang by One Limb

Arboreal balancing act.

Sloths’ powerful limbs allow them to hang from a single arm or leg for short periods. Their tendons lock around branches, making this effortless. This ability allows access to food or repositioning in tight spots. It also serves defensive purposes by letting them evade threats while staying concealed. Limb strength and claw curvature make this possible. Hanging by one limb reduces energy use compared to constant climbing. It’s a testament to the specialized evolution of sloth anatomy.

Why This Matters

Single-limb hanging expands mobility in trees.

It’s a remarkable energy-saving adaptation.

Did You Know?

Sloths can hang from a single limb thanks to specialized tendons and claws.
