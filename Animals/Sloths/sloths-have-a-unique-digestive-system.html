Sloths Have a Unique Digestive System

Specialized leaf processing.

Sloths have a multi-chambered stomach with symbiotic bacteria to ferment tough leaves. Digestion can take up to a month to extract minimal nutrients. This slow process explains their low-energy lifestyle and extended sleep. They occasionally regurgitate cud-like material to aid digestion. Different bacterial communities specialize in breaking down various plant compounds. This system allows survival on leaves that many other animals cannot digest. Evolution has optimized their digestive tract for efficiency, not speed.

Why This Matters

Their unique digestion supports survival on a leaf-based diet.

It enables energy conservation and niche specialization.
