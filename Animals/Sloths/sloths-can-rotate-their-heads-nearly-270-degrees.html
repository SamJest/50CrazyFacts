Sloths Can Rotate Their Heads Nearly 270 Degrees

360-degree awareness.

Three-toed sloths can turn their heads almost 270 degrees thanks to extra neck vertebrae. This allows constant scanning for predators without moving their bodies. Combined with camouflage, this reduces energy expenditure while maintaining vigilance. Two-toed sloths are slightly less flexible but still remarkable. The ability compensates for slow movement, making stealthy monitoring possible. It’s a critical arboreal survival adaptation.
