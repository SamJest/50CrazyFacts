Why Sloths Move at a Glacial Pace

Slow and steady, but not lazy.

Sloths are famous for their slow movements, averaging just 0.24 kilometers per hour. This extreme slowness is due to a low metabolism and specialized muscles that conserve energy. Their slow pace helps them avoid detection by predators like jaguars and harpy eagles. Sloths spend almost all their time hanging upside down in trees. Their diet of leaves provides little energy, reinforcing this sluggish lifestyle. Despite moving slowly on land, sloths can swim three times faster than they walk. This unique lifestyle is a brilliant evolutionary adaptation to rainforest life.
