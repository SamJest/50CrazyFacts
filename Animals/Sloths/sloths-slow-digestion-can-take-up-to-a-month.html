Sloths’ Slow Digestion Can Take Up to a Month

Leaf processing marathon.

Sloths have a multi-chambered stomach with slow fermentation to break down tough leaves. Digestion can take up to a month to fully process a meal. This extreme slowness conserves energy and extracts maximum nutrients from low-calorie foliage. Specialized gut bacteria help ferment cellulose efficiently. Low-speed digestion limits mobility and requires long rest periods. Despite slow digestion, it ensures survival on a diet most mammals could not tolerate. This adaptation is central to their low-energy lifestyle.
