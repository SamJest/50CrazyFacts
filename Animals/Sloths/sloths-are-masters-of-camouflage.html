Sloths Are Masters of Camouflage

Cloaked in green.

Sloths’ fur hosts algae, giving a greenish tint that blends perfectly with rainforest foliage. This camouflage reduces predation risk by making them nearly invisible from above. Combined with slow, deliberate movements, it enhances survival. Algae growth is encouraged by the micro-ecosystem in their fur, including moths. Their coloring adapts to local environments, enhancing concealment. Camouflage also reduces stress by limiting predator encounters. It’s a remarkable example of symbiosis and evolutionary adaptation.
