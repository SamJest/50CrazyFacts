Sloths Have Specialized Mitochondria to Conserve Energy

Cellular energy hacks.

Sloths’ cells have unique mitochondria that produce energy slowly but efficiently. This supports their low metabolic rate and long lifespan. Reduced oxidative stress limits tissue damage. Mitochondrial adaptations help digest tough leaves slowly. Energy efficiency extends to muscles, heart, and brain. This cellular-level adaptation is rare among mammals. It aligns perfectly with their slow, low-energy lifestyle.
