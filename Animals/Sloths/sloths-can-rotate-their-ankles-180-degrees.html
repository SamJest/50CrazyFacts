Sloths Can Rotate Their Ankles 180 Degrees

Foot flexibility.

Sloths can rotate their ankles nearly 180 degrees, aiding movement along branches. This allows them to descend headfirst or grasp multiple directions efficiently. Flexible joints reduce energy needed for climbing and repositioning. The adaptation also improves balance while hanging in the canopy. It’s critical for accessing leaves and evading predators. Evolution has fine-tuned limb articulation for arboreal life. The range of motion exceeds that of many mammals.
