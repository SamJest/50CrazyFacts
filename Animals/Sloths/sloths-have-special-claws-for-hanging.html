Sloths Have Special Claws for Hanging

Perfect grip.

Sloths have long, curved claws that allow them to hang effortlessly from branches. These claws lock around tree limbs, enabling hours of suspended rest without muscle strain. The curvature also helps sloths climb slowly but safely. Combined with strong limb muscles, claws make up their primary survival tool in the canopy. They prevent falls during storms or when reaching for distant leaves. Claws also aid in defense, though primarily they are for arboreal life. Evolution has optimized their claws for energy-efficient hanging.
