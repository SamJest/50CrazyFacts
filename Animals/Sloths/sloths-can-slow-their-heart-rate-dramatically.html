Sloths Can Slow Their Heart Rate Dramatically

Cardiac slow-down.

Sloths can reduce their heart rate to as low as 30 beats per minute while resting. This extreme bradycardia conserves energy and supports their low-metabolism lifestyle. Their slow circulation also reduces the need for frequent feeding. Even while upside down in trees, heart function remains steady. This adaptation allows survival on a diet that provides minimal calories. The ability to control heart rate is rare among mammals. Sloths’ cardiovascular system is highly tuned to a slow-paced life.

Why This Matters

Slow heart rate conserves energy.

It’s a critical survival adaptation for low-calorie diets.

Did You Know?

Sloths can lower their heart rate drastically to conserve energy and match their slow lifestyle.
