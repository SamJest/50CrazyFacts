Sloths Have a Low Body Temperature

Chilly and slow.

Sloths maintain a body temperature of about 30–34°C (86–93°F), lower than most mammals. This low temperature conserves energy and suits their low-calorie diet. It also slows digestion, allowing them to process tough leaves efficiently. Temperature regulation is assisted by slow movements and dense fur. Some sloths are ectothermic-like in behavior, warming under the sun when needed. Low temperature also limits metabolic rate, supporting long-term survival. Their unusual physiology is an adaptation to rainforest life.
