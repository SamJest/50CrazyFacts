Sloths Are Surprisingly Strong for Their Size

Hanging powerhouses.

Sloths’ slow movements hide remarkable strength. They can support their entire body weight with just a few fingers. Muscles are optimized for endurance rather than speed. This strength ensures they remain securely attached to branches, even during storms. Juveniles gradually develop sufficient strength under maternal guidance. Despite weak appearances, their musculature is highly adapted for life in trees. Strength also allows sloths to climb and occasionally swim effectively.
