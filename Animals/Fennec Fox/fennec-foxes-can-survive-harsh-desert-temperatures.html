Fennec Foxes Can Survive Harsh Desert Temperatures

Heat-tested foxes.

Fennec foxes endure daytime temperatures over 120°F by staying in burrows and hunting nocturnally. Their fur, ears, and nocturnal habits all work together to prevent overheating. Physiological adaptations reduce water loss and maintain body function. Juveniles gradually learn activity timing to avoid heat stress.

Why This Matters

Temperature tolerance ensures survival in extreme climates.

It emphasizes the integration of behavior, anatomy, and physiology.

Did You Know?

They are active during cool desert nights and avoid daytime heat.
