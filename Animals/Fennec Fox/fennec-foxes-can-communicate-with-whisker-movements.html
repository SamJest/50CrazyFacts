Fennec Foxes Can Communicate With Whisker Movements

Tiny sensory signals.

Fennec foxes use subtle whisker twitches to signal curiosity, alertness, or submission to others. Whisker communication complements vocal and tail signals. Juveniles learn whisker signaling by observing adults.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Whisker communication strengthens social understanding.

It highlights nuanced communication in social mammals.

Did You Know?

Whiskers may twitch when alerting siblings to food or danger.
