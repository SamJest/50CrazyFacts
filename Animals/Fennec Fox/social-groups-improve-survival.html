Social Groups Improve Survival

Fennec foxes live in small, cooperative family groups.

Family units share burrows, resources, and duties such as raising young. Social cohesion provides protection, efficient resource use, and coordinated defense.

Why This Matters

Cooperative living enhances survival in harsh conditions.

It demonstrates how social structures can increase the resilience of small mammals.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Fennec fox groups coordinate alarm calls to warn of danger.
