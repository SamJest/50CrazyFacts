Fennec Foxes Can Hear Prey Underground

Acoustic hunters.

Fennec foxes detect small mammals and insects moving beneath the sand using acute hearing. They pinpoint prey location and pounce with precision. Juveniles refine auditory hunting skills by observing adults.

Why This Matters

Subsurface hearing ensures efficient food acquisition.

It highlights sensory specialization for survival in harsh environments.

Did You Know?

They can detect rodents tunneling several inches below the surface.
