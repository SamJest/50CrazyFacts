Fennec Foxes Can Swim Using Sand Like Snow

Desert ingenuity.

Fennec foxes “swim” through soft desert sand while chasing prey or escaping predators, using all four limbs to propel themselves like snow skiing. This adaptation allows efficient movement in loose terrain. Juveniles learn technique by imitation.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Sand-swimming improves mobility and hunting efficiency.

It highlights evolutionary adaptation to extreme terrain.

Did You Know?

They can move through deep sand faster than predators can follow.
