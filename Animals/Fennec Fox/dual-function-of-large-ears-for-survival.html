Dual Function of Large Ears for Survival

Fennec fox ears are remarkable tools for hearing and temperature control.

These oversized ears detect underground prey like insects and lizards while dispersing excess body heat. Ear movement also helps pinpoint prey location precisely.

Why This Matters

Acute hearing enhances hunting, while thermoregulation prevents overheating.

It illustrates how a single adaptation can serve multiple survival purposes.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Fennec foxes can rotate their ears independently to locate prey underground.
