Large Eyes Support Night Hunting

Fennec foxes rely on oversized eyes to see clearly at night.

Their eyes include a reflective layer that enhances low-light vision, helping them spot prey and navigate the desert in darkness.

Why This Matters

Large eyes improve hunting efficiency during nocturnal activity.

It highlights how sensory adaptations are critical for nocturnal survival.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: The size of their eyes is proportionally larger than in most other fox species.
