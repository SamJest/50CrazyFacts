Small-Scale Migrations Access Resources

Fennec foxes move to find food and water.

They conduct short seasonal migrations within their home ranges, adjusting to changing resource availability. This mobility improves survival in arid environments.

Why This Matters

Flexible movement strategies reduce competition and allow consistent access to essential resources.

Migrations under cover of darkness minimize predation risk and optimize hunting success.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: These movements rarely exceed a few kilometers but are critical for finding scarce water and prey.
