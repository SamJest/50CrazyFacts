Exotic Pet Trade Requires Special Care

Fennec foxes are occasionally kept as pets.

They require large enclosures, appropriate diets, and nocturnal schedules to maintain health and behaviour. Improper care can lead to stress and illness.

Why This Matters

Caring for these wild animals demands knowledge and commitment, highlighting the challenges of domestication.

Responsible ownership ensures welfare and prevents disruption of natural behaviours.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Fennec foxes’ natural instincts can make them challenging pets if enrichment and social needs aren’t met.
