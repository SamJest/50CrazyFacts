Fennec Foxes Can Swim Through Loose Sand

Sand skiing specialists.

Fennec foxes move efficiently through loose desert sand by spreading their limbs and using a bounding gait, almost “swimming” through the surface. This aids in hunting and predator evasion. Juveniles practice this through playful chasing.

Why This Matters

Sand-swimming increases mobility and foraging efficiency.

It emphasizes anatomical and behavioral adaptation to extreme environments.

Did You Know?

They can travel over deep sand faster than many desert predators.
