Adaptation Enables Survival Near Humans

Fennec foxes show remarkable flexibility.

Some individuals forage in urban or agricultural areas without losing nocturnal habits, demonstrating adaptability to human presence.

Why This Matters

This ability highlights their cognitive and behavioural plasticity.

Flexible foraging reduces competition and increases access to resources, helping populations persist in changing landscapes.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Even when living near humans, fennec foxes remain primarily nocturnal to avoid conflict.
