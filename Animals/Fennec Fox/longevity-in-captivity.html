Longevity in Captivity

With proper care, fennec foxes can live over a decade.

Captive individuals benefit from controlled diets, veterinary care, and protection from predators. Lifespan in the wild is shorter due to natural hazards.

Why This Matters

Long life allows for extended observation of behaviour and social dynamics.

Captive longevity aids research on physiology, cognition, and social interactions.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Mental stimulation and social enrichment are essential to ensure well-being in captivity.
