Fennec Foxes Can Live Up to 10–14 Years in Captivity

Long-lived desert foxes.

While in the wild their lifespan is shorter due to predators and harsh conditions, fennec foxes can live up to 14 years in captivity with proper care. This longevity allows extensive social learning, complex behaviors, and multiple breeding cycles. Their health depends on diet, exercise, and enrichment. Juveniles in captivity develop skills through interaction with adults and human caretakers.
