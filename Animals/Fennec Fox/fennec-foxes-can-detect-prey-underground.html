Fennec Foxes Can Detect Prey Underground

Seismic hunters.

Fennec foxes sense movement of insects and rodents beneath the sand using acute hearing and paw sensitivity. They then pounce or dig to capture hidden prey. This ability is crucial for surviving in barren desert environments. Juveniles refine detection skills by observing adults.

Why This Matters

Underground prey detection ensures efficient foraging.

Did You Know?

They can pinpoint rodents burrowing several inches under sand.
