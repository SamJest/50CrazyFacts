Fennec Foxes Can Store Water From Food

Metabolic hydration.

Fennec foxes obtain water from fruits, plants, and prey, allowing them to survive long periods without drinking. Metabolic water derived from food supports physiological functions in arid conditions. Juveniles learn to select hydrating foods from adults.

Why This Matters

Water extraction from food enables survival without direct water sources.

It highlights dietary adaptation to desert scarcity.

Did You Know?

They can survive primarily on moisture-rich prey during dry spells.
