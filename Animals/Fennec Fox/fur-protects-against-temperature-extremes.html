Fur Protects Against Temperature Extremes

Fennec foxes rely on thick coats to survive scorching days and freezing nights.

Dense fur retains warmth during desert nights and shields them from solar heat. Their bushy tails can be used as blankets for added insulation.

Why This Matters

Adapted fur allows survival in extreme daily temperature swings.

It highlights the intricate ways mammals manage thermal stress in deserts.

Did You Know?

DID YOU KNOW: Their fur also helps camouflage them under moonlight and desert sand.
