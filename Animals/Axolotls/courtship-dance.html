Courtship Dance

During mating, axolotls perform a subtle tail dance to signal readiness.

This behavior can last hours and is critical for successful reproduction.

Why This Matters

Observing these dances helps researchers understand breeding behaviors.

Courtship rituals reflect complex social interactions and reproductive strategies.

Did You Know?

Did you know the tail dance involves precise movements to guide sperm packets to the female?

Source

Animal Behaviour Journal (sciencedirect.com)
