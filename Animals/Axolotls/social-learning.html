Social Learning

Axolotls can learn by observing the behavior of other axolotls.

They mimic feeding behaviors and problem-solving strategies, reducing trial-and-error learning.

Why This Matters

This type of social learning is rare among amphibians and highlights their adaptability.

It provides insight into how learning in groups can influence survival and efficiency.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls can watch others feed and then adopt the same techniques without direct trial and error?

Source

Animal Cognition Journal (springer.com)
