Axolotls Can Detect Subtle Changes in Water Currents

They are aquatic environmental experts, sensing tiny shifts in flow.

Axolotls detect minute variations in water currents using their lateral line system. This helps locate prey, avoid predators, and orient themselves. Sensory hairs along the body pick up even slight movement differences, allowing rapid behavioral responses. In murky lakes, this system is critical for survival. Researchers study lateral line function to understand mechanosensory biology. This skill makes axolotls highly aware of their surroundings despite limited vision.
