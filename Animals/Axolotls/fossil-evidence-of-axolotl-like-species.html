Fossil Evidence of Axolotl-like Species

Fossils from ancient lakebeds reveal salamander species similar to modern axolotls.

These lakes provided stable environments that reduced pressures for evolutionary change, allowing these species to maintain consistent body forms.

Why This Matters

Studying these fossils helps scientists understand how habitat stability can influence long-term species survival.

It also highlights the link between environmental consistency and evolutionary conservatism.

Did You Know?

Did you know ancient lakes acted as safe havens for species like axolotls?
