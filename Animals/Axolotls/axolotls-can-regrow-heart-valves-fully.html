Axolotls Can Regrow Heart Valves Fully

Even intricate heart structures like valves can regenerate in axolotls.

If heart valves are damaged, axolotls can replace them with perfectly functional tissue. This process uses cardiomyocyte proliferation and precise tissue remodeling. Function is fully restored without scarring. This level of regeneration is unmatched in mammals. Studying valve regrowth provides insights into cardiovascular repair and potential therapies for humans. Their ability ensures survival after traumatic cardiac injuries.
