External Gills for Underwater Life

Axolotls breathe through feathery external gills that extend from their heads.

These gills allow oxygen extraction directly from water, and their skin also absorbs oxygen. This adaptation enables survival in low-oxygen habitats and supports their fully aquatic lifestyle.

Why This Matters

Their gills demonstrate how anatomy adapts to environmental demands.

It allows axolotls to thrive in habitats that might challenge other amphibians.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls can breathe entirely through their gills and skin?
