Captive Breeding for Research

Humans breed axolotls extensively in labs for scientific studies.

Their unique regenerative abilities make them key model organisms in medicine and genetics.

Why This Matters

This ensures controlled populations while supporting groundbreaking research.

Captive breeding links science and conservation by maintaining viable populations outside their natural habitat.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls are used to study tissue regeneration, including heart and spinal cord repair?
