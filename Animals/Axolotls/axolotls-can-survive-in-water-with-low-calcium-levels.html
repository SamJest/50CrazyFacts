Axolotls Can Survive in Water With Low Calcium Levels

Calcium is essential for bones and cellular function, but axolotls can endure shortages temporarily.

They can regulate calcium uptake efficiently, slowing growth and metabolism if environmental calcium is low. This adaptation allows survival in lakes with variable mineral content. Over long periods, deficiencies can affect regeneration and skeletal health, but short-term tolerance is high. This ability ensures they remain viable in habitats other amphibians might avoid. It’s a testament to their resilience and physiological flexibility.
