Wide-Eyed Appearance

Axolotls lack eyelids, giving them a permanently wide-eyed look.

Their eyes are adapted to detect movement in low-light underwater conditions. This distinctive feature contributes to their unusual and memorable appearance.

Why This Matters

It attracts interest from both scientists and enthusiasts.

It also demonstrates the interplay between form and function in evolution.

Did You Know?

Did you know their wide-eyed expression is functional, not just aesthetic?
