Axolotls Can Survive With One Lung Damaged

Even if part of a lung is compromised, axolotls continue to thrive.

If one lung is injured or removed, the other lung compensates along with gill and skin respiration. Regeneration eventually restores the damaged lung fully. This redundancy ensures survival even after significant trauma. Scientists study this adaptability to understand organ compensation and resilience. It illustrates how axolotls’ physiology allows survival under stress that would incapacitate other vertebrates. This adaptation contributes to their robustness in variable environments.
