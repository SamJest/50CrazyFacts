Ancient Lineage of Axolotls

Axolotls trace their ancestry back more than 150 million years, placing them in the age of dinosaurs.

Fossils show that early salamander-like amphibians adapted to freshwater environments long ago. Axolotls inherited traits that favored aquatic life and survived while many other species went extinct. Understanding this lineage helps scientists explore how species endure massive environmental changes.

Why This Matters

Their ancient heritage makes them critical for studying survival strategies over geological time scales.

It emphasizes the resilience of certain species and their ability to persist through dramatic shifts in climate and habitat.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls’ ancestors coexisted with dinosaurs?
