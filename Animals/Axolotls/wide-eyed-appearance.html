Wide-Eyed Appearance

Axolotls have no eyelids, giving them a perpetually wide-eyed look.

This feature allows them to see well in dim underwater conditions and contributes to their distinctive, almost otherworldly appearance.

Why This Matters

It also makes them easily recognizable and beloved by enthusiasts worldwide.

The lidless eyes enhance their underwater vision, crucial for spotting prey and avoiding danger in low-light environments.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls rely more on smell and detecting movement than sharp eyesight to navigate their world?
