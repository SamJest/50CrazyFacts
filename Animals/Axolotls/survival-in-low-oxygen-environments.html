Survival in Low Oxygen Environments

Axolotls can tolerate waters with low oxygen levels better than most amphibians.

Their slow metabolism and ability to extract oxygen through skin and gills support survival in murky or stagnant habitats.

Why This Matters

This adaptation demonstrates their resilience and ecological flexibility.

It also underscores why axolotls can persist in challenging conditions where others may perish.

Did You Know?

Did you know they can switch breathing strategies based on oxygen availability?
