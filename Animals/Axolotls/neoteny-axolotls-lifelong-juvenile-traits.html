Neoteny: Axolotls’ Lifelong Juvenile Traits

Axolotls maintain juvenile characteristics into adulthood, a phenomenon known as neoteny.

They retain gills, tail fins, and aquatic habits instead of undergoing full metamorphosis. This trait also supports their remarkable regenerative abilities, making them important subjects in scientific research.

Why This Matters

Neoteny reveals unique evolutionary strategies in amphibians.

It allows axolotls to thrive in aquatic environments while maintaining regenerative powers that other species lack.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls remain juvenile their entire lives while still reproducing?
