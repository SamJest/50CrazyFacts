Early Scientific Discovery of Axolotls

Axolotls were first formally studied in Europe in the early 1800s.

Scientists were fascinated by their feathery gills and fully aquatic lifestyle. These early observations laid the foundation for centuries of research, including studies in regeneration and development.

Why This Matters

Their introduction to scientific study sparked curiosity that continues to this day.

It also helped establish axolotls as important model organisms in biology and medicine.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls quickly became a favorite in early laboratories?
