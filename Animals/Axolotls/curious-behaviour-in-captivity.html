Curious Behaviour in Captivity

Axolotls show playful and inquisitive behavior when kept in aquariums.

They explore objects, investigate movement, and sometimes interact with humans. This curiosity demonstrates learning ability and environmental awareness.

Why This Matters

Monitoring their behavior improves captive care and enrichment.

It also reveals surprising levels of cognitive engagement in amphibians.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls will follow movement and investigate objects in their tank?
