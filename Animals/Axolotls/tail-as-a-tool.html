Tail as a Tool

Axolotls can manipulate objects with their tails.

They push obstacles, adjust position, and interact with their environment, showing surprising dexterity.

Why This Matters

This capability enhances hunting efficiency and environmental exploration.

It highlights the multifunctionality of their anatomy beyond swimming.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls can use their tail to nudge objects and probe their surroundings?
