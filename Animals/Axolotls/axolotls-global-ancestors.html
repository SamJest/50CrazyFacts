Axolotls’ Global Ancestors

Fossil evidence reveals that axolotl relatives once lived across multiple continents.

As continents shifted and climates changed, many related populations disappeared. Modern axolotls survived only in select freshwater habitats, making them unique examples of evolutionary isolation.

Why This Matters

Studying their limited modern distribution helps scientists understand how geography influences species survival.

It underscores the importance of protecting their remaining habitats to conserve a lineage that was once widespread.

Did You Know?

Did you know their ancestors were found on nearly every continent?

Source

US Geological Survey (usgs.gov)
