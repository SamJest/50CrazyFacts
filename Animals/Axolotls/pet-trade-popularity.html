Pet Trade Popularity

Axolotls are popular in the aquarium trade but require specialized care.

They need controlled temperature, clean water, and a protein-rich diet for health.

Why This Matters

This awareness prevents accidental neglect and ensures responsible ownership.

Their care requirements highlight how exotic pets can thrive only with informed attention.

Did You Know?

Did you know improper tank conditions are the leading cause of pet axolotl deaths?
