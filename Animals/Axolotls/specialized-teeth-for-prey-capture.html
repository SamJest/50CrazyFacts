Specialized Teeth for Prey Capture

Axolotls have tiny teeth designed to grasp rather than chew prey.

They swallow food whole, feeding mainly on worms, insects, and small aquatic animals. This design ensures successful feeding without complex processing.

Why This Matters

Studying feeding adaptations helps understand ecological roles.

It illustrates the efficiency of evolutionary design in predation.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls’ teeth prevent even the slipperiest prey from escaping?
