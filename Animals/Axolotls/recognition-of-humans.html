Recognition of Humans

Axolotls can learn to recognize individual humans over time.

They react differently to familiar caretakers, showing signs of memory and preference.

Why This Matters

This ability challenges assumptions that amphibians have limited learning capacities.

It also allows owners and researchers to interact with them more effectively, improving care and feeding routines.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls can remember who feeds them and will often respond more eagerly to those people?
