Urban Water Adaptation

Axolotls can survive in polluted waters to some degree.

While resilient, poor water quality affects reproduction and long-term health.

Why This Matters

This demonstrates partial adaptability but also underscores the need for clean habitats.

Understanding their tolerance helps conservationists develop strategies for urban populations.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls have survived short-term in city waterways despite significant pollution?
