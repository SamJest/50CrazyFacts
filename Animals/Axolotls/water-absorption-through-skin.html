Water Absorption Through Skin

Axolotls can hydrate by absorbing water directly through their skin.

This highly permeable skin allows them to take in water without drinking, which is essential for maintaining proper hydration in their aquatic habitats.

Why This Matters

Healthy water conditions are crucial because their skin is sensitive to pollutants, which can lead to illness if ignored.

This adaptation demonstrates the specialized biology that supports their fully aquatic lifestyle, showing how evolution fine-tunes survival traits.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls’ skin acts almost like a living sponge, soaking up water and oxygen simultaneously?
