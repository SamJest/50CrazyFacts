Cold Tolerance

Axolotls can survive cooler temperatures better than many tropical amphibians.

Lower temperatures slow their metabolism and reduce stress, making cooler environments preferable for health.

Why This Matters

This adaptation allows them to occupy habitats that might be challenging for other amphibians.

Temperature management is key for captive care and scientific studies.

Did You Know?

Did you know warm water can actually stress axolotls more than cooler conditions?

Source

University of Exeter (exeter.ac.uk)
