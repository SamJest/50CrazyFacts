Limited Natural Range

Axolotls are native to only a few lakes near Mexico City.

Their extremely restricted habitat makes them highly vulnerable to environmental changes and human activity.

Why This Matters

This rarity emphasizes the importance of conservation efforts.

It also makes axolotls an emblematic species for habitat preservation.

Did You Know?

Did you know wild axolotls are now almost exclusively found in Lake Xochimilco?

Source

IUCN (iucnredlist.org)
