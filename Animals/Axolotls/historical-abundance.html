Historical Abundance

Axolotls once thrived in the Xochimilco canal system.

Urbanization and water pollution drastically reduced their populations.

Why This Matters

This history illustrates the impact of humans on freshwater ecosystems.

Restoration projects in Xochimilco aim to reestablish sustainable populations.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls were once so common in these canals that they were a staple in local diets?
