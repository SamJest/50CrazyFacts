Shared Ancestry With Salamanders

Axolotls share a common ancestor with land-dwelling salamanders but evolved along a different path.

While many relatives transitioned to life on land, axolotls remained fully aquatic, keeping gills and tail fins throughout life. Comparing these species helps scientists understand environmental influences on evolution.

Why This Matters

This divergence illustrates how habitat shapes anatomy and survival strategies.

It highlights axolotls’ unique adaptation among amphibians.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls never fully leave the water like their terrestrial relatives?
