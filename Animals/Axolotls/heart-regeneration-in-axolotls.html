Heart Regeneration in Axolotls

Axolotls can regrow damaged parts of their heart without scarring.

This extraordinary ability is rare among vertebrates and makes them valuable for medical research into tissue regeneration.

Why This Matters

Studying heart regeneration may lead to breakthroughs in human medicine.

It highlights the potential of axolotls as living models for healing and recovery.

Did You Know?

Did you know axolotls can regenerate heart tissue completely?
