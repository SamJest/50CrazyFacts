Urban Baboon Adaptation

Baboons navigate towns, stealing food and using human structures.

They demonstrate problem-solving and opportunistic behaviour in urban settings.

Why This Matters

Adaptation to human environments showcases behavioural flexibility.

It illustrates how wildlife can adjust to anthropogenic pressures.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Urban baboons may also mimic human actions to access resources.
