Sweat Helps Cool Baboons

Baboons use sweat to regulate body temperature in hot climates.

Although less efficient than humans, sweat glands allow activity during tropical heat.

Why This Matters

Physiological adaptations enable survival in extreme environments.

It shows how even subtle traits can influence behaviour and energy management.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboon sweat is often concentrated on the palms and soles.
