Baboons Can Display Food Sharing Under Certain Conditions

Sharing isn’t just human behavior.

Baboons sometimes share food with close allies or kin. Sharing strengthens social bonds and can be strategic for alliance-building. Females often share with offspring or relatives to support survival. Food sharing may also occur to reduce tension after conflicts or reinforce grooming alliances. This behavior demonstrates that even competitive species exhibit cooperative tendencies. Understanding sharing behavior helps explain social cohesion in complex societies.

Why This Matters

Highlights prosocial behavior in primates.

It illustrates how cooperation can coexist with competition.

Did You Know?

They have been observed handing food to an ally to strengthen social ties.
