Baboons Evolved to Roam the Ground

Baboons are more terrestrial than many primates, an adaptation for savannah life.

Strong limbs and a robust body allowed early baboons to travel long distances while foraging and evading predators.

Why This Matters

These adaptations demonstrate how anatomy and behaviour evolve in tandem.

It also explains why modern baboons are excellent walkers and capable climbers.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Their walking endurance helps them cover several kilometers each day in search of food.
