Baboons Can Use Their Teeth as Weapons

Beyond a bite—they are tactical fighters.

Baboons have long canine teeth that can inflict serious injuries. These teeth are used in aggressive displays, fights with rivals, and defense against predators. Teeth size correlates with dominance in males, influencing mating opportunities. Threat displays often include showing canines to intimidate opponents without actual combat. These adaptations reduce physical risk while maintaining social hierarchy. Canines are also used to tear tough vegetation or prey when necessary.

Why This Matters

Shows how physical traits serve both offense and social signaling.

It highlights the dual role of anatomy in survival and reproduction.

Did You Know?

Male baboons can intimidate rivals merely by showing their canines.
