Impact of Habitat Loss

Deforestation and land conversion threaten baboon populations.

Loss of habitat affects troop size, food availability, and behaviour.

Why This Matters

Understanding these effects informs conservation strategies.

It highlights the importance of habitat protection for primates.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons may travel further distances when their home range shrinks.
