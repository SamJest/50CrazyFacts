Dexterous Hands for Foraging and Grooming

Baboon hands combine strength and precision.

Opposable thumbs allow them to manipulate objects, groom each other, and extract food efficiently.

Why This Matters

Manual dexterity is key for survival and social bonding.

It demonstrates the evolutionary advantage of versatile limbs.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Grooming is often performed with delicate finger movements that remove debris and parasites.
