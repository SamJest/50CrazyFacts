Baboons Can Survive in Extreme Temperatures

They are resilient savannah survivors.

Baboons live in habitats ranging from hot, arid savannahs to cool montane forests. They thermoregulate through shade-seeking, panting, and altering activity patterns. Troops may rest during midday heat and forage in cooler periods. This adaptability allows them to exploit environments with large temperature fluctuations. It demonstrates behavioral and physiological resilience. Extreme weather tests survival skills, shaping troop strategies.
