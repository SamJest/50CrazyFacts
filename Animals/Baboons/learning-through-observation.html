Learning Through Observation

Baboons observe and imitate others to acquire skills.

Social learning helps juveniles master foraging, predator avoidance, and social navigation.

Why This Matters

Observation enhances survival and troop efficiency.

It demonstrates the role of culture-like behaviour in primates.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some skills, once learned, can spread across the entire troop.
