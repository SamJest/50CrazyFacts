Ecological Contributions

Baboons disperse seeds through their diet, aiding plant regeneration.

They play an essential role in maintaining ecosystem health.

Why This Matters

Seed dispersal supports biodiversity and forest regeneration.

It shows how even medium-sized mammals influence ecosystems.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some plants rely heavily on baboons for propagation.
