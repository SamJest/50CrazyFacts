Evolution Shaped Baboon Societies

Baboon social structures grew more complex over time.

Early baboons lived in small groups that eventually formed the larger hierarchical troops seen today.

Why This Matters

This evolution enhanced predator protection and efficient foraging.

Understanding this development gives insight into the social strategies of modern baboons.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Early group sizes influenced survival and mating success.
