Baboon Parenting Includes Teaching

Adult baboons actively instruct juveniles in survival skills.

They guide young ones on foraging, predator avoidance, and social navigation.

Why This Matters

This behaviour ensures troop survival and knowledge transfer.

It illustrates natural methods of education in the wild.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Juveniles learn the most from observing mothers and high-ranking females.
