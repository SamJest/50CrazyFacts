Speedy Ground Runners

Despite their build, baboons can sprint at impressive speeds.

They can reach up to 35 km/h to evade predators.

Why This Matters

High-speed bursts are crucial for survival in open habitats.

It underscores their physical agility and predator awareness.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons can maintain high speed over short distances to escape threats.
