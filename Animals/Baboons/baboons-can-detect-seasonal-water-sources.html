Baboons Can Detect Seasonal Water Sources

They are savvy survivalists in dry habitats.

Baboons locate seasonal rivers, streams, or watering holes based on memory and environmental cues. Access to water is critical, especially during dry seasons. Troop movements are often coordinated to reach these sources efficiently. Knowledge of water location is passed through generations. This ecological intelligence ensures survival in arid and semi-arid landscapes.
