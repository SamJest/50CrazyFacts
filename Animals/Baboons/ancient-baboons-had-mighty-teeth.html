Ancient Baboons Had Mighty Teeth

Some early baboons possessed larger teeth for processing tough foods.

Fossil records suggest strong canines and molars enabled them to crack nuts and roots, reflecting their dietary needs.

Why This Matters

Studying tooth evolution reveals how diet directly influences physical traits.

It highlights the connection between feeding strategies and survival adaptations.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Tooth size and wear patterns can indicate what ancient baboons ate.
