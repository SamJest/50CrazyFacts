Planning for the Future

Baboons anticipate upcoming needs, such as storing food or preparing travel paths.

Research shows they can think ahead and modify behaviour accordingly.

Why This Matters

Foresight helps in resource management and troop coordination.

It highlights advanced cognitive capabilities rare in non-human species.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons sometimes move to water sources before dry seasons begin.
