Vocal Communication in Troops

Baboons use diverse calls for coordination and alerting threats.

Grunts, barks, and alarm calls convey messages about food, danger, and status.

Why This Matters

Complex vocalisations maintain troop cohesion.

It demonstrates the role of communication in social survival.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Alarm calls can prompt synchronized troop movements.
