Living Near Humans

Baboons often inhabit areas close to villages and farms.

They raid crops, showcasing adaptability but also creating human-wildlife conflict.

Why This Matters

Interactions highlight behavioural flexibility and resourcefulness.

It underscores the challenges of coexisting with humans.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some troops learn to open gates or access food stores.
