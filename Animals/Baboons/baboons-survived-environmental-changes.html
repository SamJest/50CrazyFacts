Baboons Survived Environmental Changes

Baboons adapted to shifting climates for millions of years.

They transitioned from dense forests to open savannahs, demonstrating remarkable resilience.

Why This Matters

This adaptability is a key reason baboons remain successful today.

It offers lessons in how animals can adjust to environmental stressors over long periods.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons’ social behaviour helped them cope with changing habitats.
