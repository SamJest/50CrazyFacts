Keen Eyes for Motion Detection

Baboon vision excels at detecting movement.

Forward-facing eyes provide depth perception, helping spot predators and forage efficiently.

Why This Matters

Visual adaptations support survival and vigilance.

It illustrates the importance of sensory evolution in primates.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons can notice slight movements in grass from several meters away.
