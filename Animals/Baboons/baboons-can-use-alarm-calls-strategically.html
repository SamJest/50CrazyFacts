Baboons Can Use Alarm Calls Strategically

They’re vocal tacticians of the wild.

Baboons sometimes emit false alarm calls to distract rivals or gain access to food. These deceptive calls exploit the attention of others, providing temporary advantages. Strategic alarm calling requires memory and understanding of troop behavior. Overuse can reduce trust, so baboons balance risk and reward. Such vocal tactics demonstrate advanced social intelligence. Observing deception helps researchers understand cognition and communication evolution in primates.
