Baboons’ Place Among Primates

Baboons share a lineage with other Old World monkeys, revealing their evolutionary history.

Genetic studies indicate baboons diverged from other primates about 7-8 million years ago, giving rise to their unique intelligence and complex social behaviour.

Why This Matters

This evolutionary relationship provides insight into primate cognition and social structures.

It underscores how baboons’ adaptations are connected to millions of years of primate evolution.

Did You Know?

Did you know? This divergence helped baboons evolve traits that make them excellent ground dwellers.
