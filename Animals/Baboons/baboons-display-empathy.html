Baboons Display Empathy

These primates can show concern for distressed troop members.

Baboons console and protect vulnerable individuals, demonstrating social intelligence.

Why This Matters

Empathy strengthens social cohesion and trust within troops.

It highlights the emotional depth of non-human primates.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Baboons have been observed comforting each other after fights.
