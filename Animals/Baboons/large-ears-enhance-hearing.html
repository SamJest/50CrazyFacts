Large Ears Enhance Hearing

Baboons rely on acute hearing to detect predators and communicate.

Their large ears pick up subtle sounds, aiding survival and social coordination.

Why This Matters

Enhanced auditory perception improves group awareness.

It highlights the importance of senses in complex social animals.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Ear movement can indicate alertness to nearby threats.
