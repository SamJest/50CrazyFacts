Baboons Can Exhibit Conflict Resolution Behavior

They are peacemakers as well as fighters.

After aggressive encounters, baboons often engage in reconciliation behaviors such as grooming, sitting in close proximity, or soft vocalizations. Conflict resolution reduces stress and maintains troop stability. Both aggressors and victims participate to restore harmony. Such behavior improves long-term cooperation and alliance maintenance. Reconciliation is critical in dense social networks where repeated interactions are inevitable.
