Long Limbs for Survival

Baboons developed elongated arms and legs for mobility.

These limbs support running, climbing, and navigating wide territories while avoiding predators.

Why This Matters

Evolutionary limb design shows how anatomy supports both survival and versatility.

It explains how baboons balance terrestrial life with occasional tree climbing.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Their limb length allows them to leap across rocky terrain with ease.
