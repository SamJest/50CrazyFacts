Grooming Strengthens Social Bonds

Grooming is crucial for social harmony.

Baboons spend hours cleaning each other’s fur to reduce stress and build alliances.

Why This Matters

Social grooming reinforces trust and group stability.

It highlights how hygiene and social strategy are intertwined.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Grooming can also lower heart rates and stress levels.
