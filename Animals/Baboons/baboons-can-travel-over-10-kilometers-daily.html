Baboons Can Travel Over 10 Kilometers Daily

They are long-distance foragers of the savannah.

Baboons walk several kilometers each day searching for food, water, and safe sleeping sites. Troop coordination ensures they move together efficiently while avoiding predators. Daily travel allows exploitation of diverse feeding areas and reduces competition. Long-range memory supports navigation across large home ranges. Distance traveled varies with season, troop size, and habitat. Efficient movement is essential for survival in expansive landscapes.

Why This Matters

Shows the link between mobility and ecological success.

It emphasizes spatial cognition and endurance in primates.

Did You Know?

They can cover more than 10 kilometers in a single day while foraging.
