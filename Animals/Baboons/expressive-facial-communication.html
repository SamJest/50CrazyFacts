Expressive Facial Communication

Baboons use faces to convey emotion and intent.

Expressions communicate anger, submission, curiosity, and other social signals.

Why This Matters

Facial expressions are essential for maintaining troop harmony.

It highlights non-verbal communication complexity in primates.

Did You Know?

Did you know? A baboon’s yawn can signal dominance or threat.
