Facing Hunting Pressures

In some areas, baboons are hunted for bushmeat or sport.

Hunting alters troop dynamics and prompts behavioural changes.

Why This Matters

Awareness of threats affects movement and social structures.

It highlights the human impact on wildlife survival.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some baboons learn to avoid areas frequented by hunters.
