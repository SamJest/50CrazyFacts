Baboons Can Form Multi-Male Coalitions

Male baboons aren’t lone wolves—they team up.

Male baboons often form coalitions to challenge dominant males or defend territory. Alliances increase their chances of mating and maintaining social rank. Coalition partners monitor each other, share support, and sometimes exchange grooming for favors. These temporary alliances are strategic and can dissolve or change as circumstances evolve. Such social flexibility demonstrates advanced cognition and risk assessment. Coalitions also help stabilize troop dynamics by balancing power distribution.
