Water Play Enhances Development

Young baboons play in rivers and waterholes.

Splashing, swimming, and playful interactions develop social and physical skills.

Why This Matters

Play behaviour fosters learning and coordination.

It illustrates the importance of recreational activity in animal development.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Play can also strengthen social bonds within the troop.
