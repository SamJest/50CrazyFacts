Adaptability Across Habitats

Baboons thrive in savannas, woodlands, and rocky terrains.

Their flexible behaviour allows them to exploit diverse environments successfully.

Why This Matters

Habitat versatility is crucial for survival and population resilience.

It shows ecological adaptability in primates.
