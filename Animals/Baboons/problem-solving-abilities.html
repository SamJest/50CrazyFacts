Problem-Solving Abilities

Baboons display clever solutions for accessing food and navigating challenges.

They use trial-and-error and observational learning to overcome obstacles.

Why This Matters

Cognitive abilities enhance survival and adaptability.

It shows that intelligence is critical for ecological success.

Did You Know?

Did you know? Some baboons use simple tools or strategies to reach difficult foods.
