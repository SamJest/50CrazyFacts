Black-and-White Ruffed Lemurs Produce Loud Alarm Calls

Sonic sentinels.

These lemurs emit high-pitched calls to alert troop members of predators, ensuring safety in dense forests. Alarm calls are species-specific and understood by all members.

Why This Matters

Alerting behavior increases survival for the group.

It emphasizes auditory communication in predator-rich habitats.

Did You Know?

Alarm calls can travel over 500 meters through thick forest.
