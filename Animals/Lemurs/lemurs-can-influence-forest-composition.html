Lemurs Can Influence Forest Composition

Vegetation gardeners.

Through selective feeding and seed dispersal, lemurs affect which plant species thrive, shaping forest structure over time.

Why This Matters

Juveniles follow adults to learn which plants to feed on and disperse.

Ecological impact ensures biodiversity and ecosystem resilience.

Did You Know?

It emphasizes lemurs’ keystone role in Madagascar’s forests.

Broader Theme

Some seeds germinate only after passing through a lemur’s digestive tract.
