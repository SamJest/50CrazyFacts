Lemurs Can Exhibit Seasonal Breeding

Clocked for survival.

Most lemur species breed seasonally, timing births to coincide with food abundance for higher infant survival.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn reproductive timing through observation of adult cycles.

Seasonal breeding aligns offspring growth with resource availability.

Did You Know?

It highlights adaptation to environmental constraints.

Broader Theme

Mating periods often trigger changes in scent marking and vocalization intensity.
