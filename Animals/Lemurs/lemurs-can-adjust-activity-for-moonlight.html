Lemurs Can Adjust Activity for Moonlight

Lunar trackers.

Cathemeral lemurs modify foraging activity according to lunar brightness, increasing night activity during full moons and reducing predation risk during darker nights.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn to gauge activity timing from adults.

Activity adjustment improves foraging success and predator avoidance.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates environmental cue-based behavioral flexibility.

Broader Theme

Moonlight can shift lemurs’ feeding times by several hours.
