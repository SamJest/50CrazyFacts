Lemurs Can Use Sunbathing to Warm Themselves

Thermal tuning.

Some lemur species, like ring-tailed lemurs, spread limbs and sit in sun patches to raise body temperature during cool mornings.

Why This Matters

Juveniles observe and imitate this behavior for thermoregulation.

Sunbathing reduces energy expenditure during cold periods.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates behavioral adaptation to climate in small mammals.

Broader Theme

Lemurs can absorb sunlight for up to an hour per session.
