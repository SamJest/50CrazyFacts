Coquerel’s Sifaka Lemurs Use Unique “Dancing” Locomotion

Bipedal elegance.

On the ground, sifakas move with upright hopping, swinging arms for balance. This unusual locomotion helps them cross open spaces safely while avoiding predators.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn proper hopping and balance by practicing with adults.

Bipedal hopping reduces risk during terrestrial travel.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates evolution’s innovative locomotor solutions.

Broader Theme

They appear to “dance” across sandy forest clearings.
