Red-Bellied Lemurs Practice Cooperative Parenting

Shared care.

Both male and female red-bellied lemurs participate in carrying and protecting infants, teaching juveniles survival skills.

Why This Matters

This behavior enhances offspring survival and social bonding.

It highlights cooperative strategies in primates.

Did You Know?

Males often carry infants for hours while foraging.
