Lemurs Can Cling and Leap Across Canopy

Gymnastic primates.

Lemurs, like sifakas, cling vertically to tree trunks and propel themselves to adjacent trees using powerful leg muscles.

Why This Matters

Juveniles practice leaps to develop coordination.

Clinging and leaping allow access to dispersed food and reduce predation risk.

Did You Know?

It highlights anatomical specialization for arboreal life.

Broader Theme

Sifakas can leap up to 10 meters between trees.
