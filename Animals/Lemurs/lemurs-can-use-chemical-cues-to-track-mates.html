Lemurs Can Use Chemical Cues to Track Mates

Scents of attraction.

Male and female lemurs detect pheromones to locate receptive mates, sometimes over long distances.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn to interpret these chemical cues by observing adult behaviors.

Chemical tracking enhances reproductive success.

Did You Know?

It highlights the importance of olfactory communication in primates.

Broader Theme

Males follow scent trails to find estrous females.
