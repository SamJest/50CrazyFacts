Lemurs Can Be Nocturnal Hunters

Stealthy stalkers.

Nocturnal lemurs, like mouse lemurs and aye-ayes, hunt insects and small prey under cover of darkness using enhanced vision and hearing.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn foraging techniques by shadowing parents.

Nocturnal activity reduces competition with diurnal species.

Did You Know?

It emphasizes behavioral adaptation for predator avoidance and resource access.

Broader Theme

Some species locate prey entirely by tapping and listening.
