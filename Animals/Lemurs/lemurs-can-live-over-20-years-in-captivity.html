Lemurs Can Live Over 20 Years in Captivity

Primate longevity.

While smaller lemurs typically live 12–15 years in the wild, many species reach 20+ years in zoos due to protection from predators and consistent nutrition.

Why This Matters

Long lifespan enables extended social learning and reproductive opportunities.

It highlights the importance of conservation and captive breeding programs.

Did You Know?

Larger lemurs like the indri and sifaka have shorter wild lifespans than in captivity.
