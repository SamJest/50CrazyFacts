Lemurs Can Exhibit Allogrooming

Peacekeepers.

Allogrooming, or grooming others, strengthens social bonds, reduces tension, and reinforces troop hierarchies.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles observe and participate to integrate socially.

Social grooming supports group stability and conflict resolution.

Did You Know?

It emphasizes the importance of cooperative behavior in primates.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Some lemurs spend over 20% of their day grooming others.
