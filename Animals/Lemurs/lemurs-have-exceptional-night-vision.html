Lemurs Have Exceptional Night Vision

Night hunters.

Nocturnal lemurs such as the aye-aye and mouse lemur have large eyes and a reflective layer behind the retina called the tapetum lucidum, improving light sensitivity.

Why This Matters

Juveniles develop night foraging skills by following parents.

It highlights adaptation to nocturnal ecological niches.

Did You Know?

They can navigate dense forest undergrowth in near-complete darkness.
