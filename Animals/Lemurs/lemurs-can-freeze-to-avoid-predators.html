Lemurs Can Freeze to Avoid Predators

Stealth survival.

When threatened, some lemurs remain motionless, blending into foliage to evade predators.

Why This Matters

This “freeze” behavior is taught to juveniles through observation.

Remaining motionless increases survival during predator encounters.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates anti-predator adaptation through behavior rather than speed.

Broader Theme

Some lemurs can remain still for several minutes without moving.
