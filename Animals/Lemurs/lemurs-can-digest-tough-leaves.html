Lemurs Can Digest Tough Leaves

Fiber specialists.

Some lemur species, like bamboo lemurs, can process fibrous or toxic leaves using specialized gut bacteria and long digestion periods.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This adaptation allows them to exploit niches with less competition.

It highlights coevolution with gut microbiota for survival.

Did You Know?

They can consume bamboo containing cyanide without harm.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Dietary specialization enables access to unique food resources.
