Indri Lemurs Are Incredible Leapers

Tree acrobats.

Indri lemurs can leap up to 10 meters between trees using strong hind legs and precise coordination. Their flexible spines and gripping hands help maintain balance during jumps.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Leaping ability allows access to widely spaced trees and food sources.

It emphasizes specialized anatomical adaptations in arboreal primates.

Did You Know?

They rarely descend to the ground due to their powerful jumping skill.
