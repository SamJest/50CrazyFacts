Sportive Lemurs Are Master Leapers

Jungle gymnasts.

Sportive lemurs use powerful hind limbs to leap between trees, covering up to 6 meters in a single bound. Their tails act as stabilizers during jumps.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Jumping efficiency reduces predation risk and aids foraging.

It highlights the importance of limb specialization in arboreal primates.

Did You Know?

Juveniles practice leaping to perfect landing accuracy.
