Sifaka Lemurs Communicate with Dance-Like Movements

Footwork in the forest.

Sifaka lemurs move across open ground using bipedal hopping while swinging arms for balance. This movement doubles as a visual signal to other lemurs, showing alertness or readiness to move.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Locomotion serves both mobility and social signaling functions.

It emphasizes evolution’s integration of movement and communication.

Did You Know?

Sifakas can leap sideways between trees with remarkable precision.
