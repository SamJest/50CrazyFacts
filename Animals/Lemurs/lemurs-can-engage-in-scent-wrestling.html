Lemurs Can Engage in Scent “Wrestling”

Fragrant fights.

Male lemurs sometimes engage in “stink fights” by rubbing scent glands on their tails and wafting it toward rivals to establish dominance without physical combat.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn social boundaries by observing these contests.

Scent-based conflicts reduce injury risk while maintaining hierarchy.

Did You Know?

It highlights innovative chemical communication strategies in primates.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Males may circle each other for hours while waving scented tails.
