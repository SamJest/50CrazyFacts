Lemurs Can Utilize Tree Hollows for Nesting

Safe havens.

Many lemurs, including sportive and dwarf lemurs, use tree hollows to shelter infants and hide from predators.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn nesting locations from parents.

Tree hollow nesting improves offspring survival and reduces exposure to predators.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates behavioral innovation in small mammals.

Broader Theme

Nests are often lined with leaves for insulation.
