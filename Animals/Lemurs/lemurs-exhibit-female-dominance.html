Lemurs Exhibit Female Dominance

Matrilineal power.

In many lemur species, females are dominant over males, controlling access to food and decision-making within the troop. This is unusual among primates and influences social behavior.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn social rules by observing female interactions.

Female dominance reduces conflict and ensures group stability.

Did You Know?

It highlights diversity in primate social systems.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Females often lead movement and feeding priorities in ring-tailed lemur troops.
