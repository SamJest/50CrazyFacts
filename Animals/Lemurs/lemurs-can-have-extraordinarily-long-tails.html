Lemurs Can Have Extraordinarily Long Tails

Balance and signaling.

Many lemur species, like ring-tailed lemurs, have long tails that assist in balance during arboreal movement and serve as visual signals for group coordination. Juveniles practice tail use while climbing and leaping.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Long tails enhance mobility and troop cohesion.

It highlights evolutionary adaptation for both locomotion and social communication.

Did You Know?

Tails can be as long as the lemur’s body itself.
