Lemurs Are Found Only in Madagascar

Island exclusives.

All wild lemur species are native exclusively to Madagascar, having evolved in isolation for millions of years. This unique geographic restriction has led to incredible diversity and endemism.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Isolation promotes biodiversity and adaptive radiation.

It emphasizes the importance of preserving Madagascar’s ecosystems.

Did You Know?

Lemurs represent about 20% of Madagascar’s mammal species.
