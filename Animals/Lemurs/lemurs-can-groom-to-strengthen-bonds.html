Lemurs Can Groom to Strengthen Bonds

Furry therapists.

Grooming is a vital social activity for lemurs, reducing tension and reinforcing alliances within troops. They use teeth and hands to remove dirt and parasites from fur.

Why This Matters

Grooming enhances troop cohesion and individual health.

It demonstrates the link between hygiene and social structure.

Did You Know?

They spend hours each day in mutual grooming sessions.
