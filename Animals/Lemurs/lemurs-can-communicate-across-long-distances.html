Lemurs Can Communicate Across Long Distances

Vocal bridges.

Loud calls allow lemurs to stay in contact across dense forests, ensuring troop cohesion and coordinated movement.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn to respond to distant calls by following adults.

Long-range communication reduces separation and predation risk.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates evolution of acoustic signaling in forest-dwelling primates.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Calls can carry over 500 meters in dense foliage.
