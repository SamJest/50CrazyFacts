Red Ruffed Lemurs Have Explosive Energy

Fruit frenzy.

Red ruffed lemurs are highly active frugivores, consuming large amounts of fruit and moving quickly through the canopy to access seasonal resources. Their energy levels support long-distance travel between feeding trees.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn efficient foraging by observing adults.

High-energy foraging promotes seed dispersal and forest regeneration.

Did You Know?

It highlights the role of lemurs in ecosystem health.

AD 2

Broader Theme

They can eat up to 1.5 kilograms of fruit per day.
