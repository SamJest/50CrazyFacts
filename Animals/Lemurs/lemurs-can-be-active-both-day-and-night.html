Lemurs Can Be Active Both Day and Night

Flexible schedules.

While many lemurs are nocturnal, some species are cathemeral, being active during both day and night depending on food availability and predation risk.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles adapt to variable activity periods by observing adults.

Flexible activity timing maximizes survival and resource acquisition.

Did You Know?

It highlights behavioral plasticity in primates.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Cathemeral species adjust foraging according to moonlight and predator presence.
