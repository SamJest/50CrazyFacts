Lemurs Are Critical Seed Dispersers

Forest gardeners.

By consuming fruit and excreting seeds elsewhere, lemurs play a vital role in regenerating Madagascar’s forests. Different species target different fruiting plants, aiding biodiversity.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Seed dispersal promotes forest health and species survival.

It emphasizes the ecological importance of lemurs in Madagascar.

Did You Know?

Some seeds require passage through a lemur’s digestive tract to germinate effectively.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Lemurs sustain the ecosystem by supporting plant reproduction.
