Lemurs Exhibit Species-Specific Leaf Preferences

Food connoisseurs.

Leaf-eating lemurs selectively feed on certain plant species based on nutritional value and secondary compounds.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Selective feeding improves health and minimizes toxin ingestion.

It highlights ecological specialization in lemurs.

Did You Know?

Juveniles learn preferences by observing adults.
