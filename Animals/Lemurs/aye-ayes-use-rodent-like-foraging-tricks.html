Aye-Ayes Use Rodent-Like Foraging Tricks

Nighttime engineers.

Aye-ayes tap on wood with their elongated middle finger to detect hollow spaces containing insects. Once located, they gnaw a hole and extract larvae with the same finger.

Why This Matters

Unique foraging behavior reduces competition and exploits hidden resources.

It highlights the combination of intelligence and physical adaptation.

Did You Know?

Aye-ayes’ middle finger can be twice as long as the other fingers relative to body size.
