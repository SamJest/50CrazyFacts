Crowned Lemurs Use Nose-to-Nose Greetings

Fragrant diplomacy.

Crowned lemurs greet each other by touching noses, exchanging scents to reinforce social bonds and confirm troop identity.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn this greeting behavior by observing adults.

Nose-to-nose interactions reduce aggression and strengthen alliances.

Did You Know?

It highlights sophisticated social signaling in primates.

Broader Theme

These greetings are especially common after territorial disputes.
