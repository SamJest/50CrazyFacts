Mouse Lemurs Are the World’s Smallest Primates

Tiny titans.

Microcebus species weigh only 30–70 grams and fit in the palm of a hand. Despite their size, they have complex behaviors including nocturnal hunting and social interactions.

Why This Matters

Small body size allows them to exploit niches inaccessible to larger primates.

It highlights the diversity and adaptability of primates in Madagascar.

Did You Know?

Mouse lemurs use torpor to conserve energy during food shortages.
