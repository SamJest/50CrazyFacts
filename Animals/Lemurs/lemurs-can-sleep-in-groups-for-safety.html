Lemurs Can Sleep in Groups for Safety

Sleeping huddles.

Many lemurs sleep in communal nests or tree branches to reduce predation risk and conserve warmth.

Why This Matters

Juveniles gain protection and social learning through group sleeping.

Group rest enhances survival during vulnerable hours.

Did You Know?

It emphasizes the social and ecological importance of sleeping arrangements.

Broader Theme

Troop members often huddle tightly, even wrapping tails around each other.
