Lemurs Can Swim

Unexpected paddlers.

While primarily arboreal, some lemur species, such as ring-tailed lemurs, can swim across small rivers or streams to reach feeding areas or escape predators.

Why This Matters

Swimming expands habitat accessibility and reduces isolation.

It demonstrates behavioral flexibility in challenging environments.

Did You Know?

They paddle with hands and feet while keeping heads above water.
