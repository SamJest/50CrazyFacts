Lemurs Can Recognize Individuals by Scent

Social olfaction.

Lemurs use scent to distinguish between troop members, rivals, and potential mates. They remember individual chemical signatures over time.

Why This Matters

Juveniles learn to identify kin and allies through scent recognition.

Individual recognition reduces conflict and improves mating success.

Did You Know?

It demonstrates advanced olfactory cognition in mammals.

Broader Theme

Some lemurs can remember the scent of other individuals for years.
