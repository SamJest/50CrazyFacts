Aye-Aye Lemurs Tap on Wood for Insects

Nocturnal percussionists.

Aye-ayes tap on tree bark with elongated fingers to detect cavities harboring larvae. They then gnaw holes and extract insects.

AD 1

Why This Matters

Juveniles practice tapping with guidance from adults.

Tapping behavior allows exploitation of hidden food resources.

Did You Know?

It highlights the combination of intelligence and physical adaptation.

AD 2

Broader Theme

Middle finger can move independently like a tiny probing tool.
