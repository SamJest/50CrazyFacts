Black Lemurs Can Change Nose Color

Sexual signaling.

Male black lemurs’ noses turn bright red during the mating season, signaling reproductive readiness to females.

Why This Matters

Nose color change is hormonally controlled and temporary.

Visual signaling reduces the need for aggressive mating competition.

Did You Know?

It highlights the role of coloration in primate communication.

Broader Theme

Noses revert to dark outside mating season.
