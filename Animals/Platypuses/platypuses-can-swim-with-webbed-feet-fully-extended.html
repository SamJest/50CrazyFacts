Platypuses Can Swim with Webbed Feet Fully Extended

Optimized propulsion.

Platypuses extend their webbed feet to maximize swimming efficiency. Webs fold back on land but fan out underwater. This increases thrust for diving and chasing prey. Combined with tail movements, it allows smooth navigation through currents. Juveniles practice proper foot extension to optimize speed. Such mechanics demonstrate specialized adaptation for semi-aquatic life. It is key for hunting and long-distance swimming.

Why This Matters

Webbed feet provide powerful propulsion and maneuverability.

It allows platypuses to capture prey and evade threats efficiently.

Did You Know?

Platypuses use fully extended webbed feet underwater for maximum swimming power.
