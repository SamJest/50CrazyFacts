Okapids Avoid Other Large Herbivores

Territory preference.

Okapids generally avoid sharing space with other large herbivores. Competition for food is minimized. Solitary feeding reduces conflict. This strategy spreads grazing pressure. It helps preserve plant resources. Avoidance supports ecosystem balance.

Why This Matters

This reduces stress and injury risk. Peaceful spacing improves survival.

It shows how behavior shapes ecosystems. Distribution matters.

Did You Know?

Okapi ranges rarely overlap heavily with similar-sized herbivores.
