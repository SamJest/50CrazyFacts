Okapids Have Specialized Gut Bacteria

Microbial partners.

Their digestive system hosts unique gut microbes. These bacteria help break down tough plant matter. Without them, digestion would fail. The relationship is symbiotic. Gut health is critical. Microbes drive nutrition.

Why This Matters

This highlights unseen dependencies. Survival relies on microscopic life.

It shows conservation must consider internal ecosystems. Losing diet plants affects gut health.

Did You Know?

Antibiotics can seriously disrupt okapi digestion.
