Okapids Communicate Stress Through Body Posture

Quiet signals.

Okapids use subtle body language to signal discomfort or stress. Ear position, tail movement, and stance convey information. Flattened ears can indicate agitation or alertness. These signals are used instead of vocal warnings. Silent communication avoids drawing attention. It is especially effective in dense forest.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This behavior shows that communication does not require sound. Visual cues can be safer.

It highlights the importance of observation in animal behavior studies. Small movements carry big meaning.

Did You Know?

Researchers rely on ear position to assess okapi stress levels.
