Okapids Are Extremely Stress-Sensitive Animals

Stress fragile.

Okapids respond strongly to stress. Loud noise and disruption affect health. Chronic stress weakens immunity. This sensitivity complicates captivity. Calm environments are essential. Stress management saves lives.

Why This Matters

This makes habitat protection critical. Human disturbance has serious impact.
