Okapids Are Native Only to One Small Region on Earth

Extreme endemism.

Okapids are found exclusively in the Ituri Forest of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. They do not naturally occur anywhere else on the planet. This narrow range makes them highly vulnerable to habitat loss. Their survival is tied directly to this specific ecosystem. Environmental changes here have outsized impacts. Few large mammals have such limited geographic distribution.

AD 1

Why This Matters

This fact underscores the fragility of specialized species. Losing one habitat could mean global extinction.

It highlights the importance of regional conservation efforts. Protecting one forest protects an entire species.

Did You Know?

The okapi is a national symbol of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
