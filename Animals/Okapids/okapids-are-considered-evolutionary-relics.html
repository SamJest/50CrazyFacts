Okapids Are Considered Evolutionary Relics

Living history.

Okapids represent an ancient evolutionary line. They retain traits from early giraffids. Their isolation preserved these features. Modern giraffes evolved differently. Okapids offer insight into past ecosystems. They are living fossils.

Why This Matters

This makes them scientifically invaluable. Losing them would erase history.

It emphasizes conservation beyond numbers. Some species hold irreplaceable data.

Did You Know?

Okapids were unknown to Western science until 1901.
