Okapids Change Feeding Height Throughout the Day

Dynamic browsing.

Okapids adjust feeding height depending on time. Lower vegetation is eaten in mornings. Higher leaves are browsed later. This reduces strain on plants. It also follows nutrient cycles. Feeding strategy is time-based.

Why This Matters

This supports forest regeneration. Smart feeding protects resources.

It highlights animals as ecosystem managers. Behavior affects plant health.

Did You Know?

Their feeding patterns help prevent overgrazing.
