Obscure Behavioral Traits

Axolotls display unusual behaviors like limb waving, territorial displays, and substrate manipulation. Humans care because behavior reflects adaptation to aquatic habitats. Observations reveal complex interactions and communication signals. Behavioral studies inform welfare in captivity. These traits may influence mating and feeding success. Studying behavior aids understanding of neural and environmental factors. Some behaviors appear only under stress or in captivity. Axolotls are mostly nocturnal but occasionally show diurnal activity. Behavioral plasticity indicates adaptability. Social interactions are subtle and context-dependent.
