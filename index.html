Why Aardvarks Appear in African Boundary Myths

Aardvarks appear in traditional stories marking territorial boundaries in parts of Africa. Ethnographic research on aardvarks in African boundary folklore found symbolic links to digging and land. A secondary semantic variant involves animals as territorial markers in oral cultures. Burrows signified ownership and limits. The animal became a living signpost. Humans care because culture encodes ecology. Animals shape meaning.
