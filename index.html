Why Phishing Often Exploits Payment Scares

Attackers impersonate banks or subscription services claiming urgent payment issues. Fear of suspension drives immediate action. Emails often include realistic links to fake login pages. Users act without verification. Scammers harvest credentials or financial information. This tactic leverages anxiety and urgency.
