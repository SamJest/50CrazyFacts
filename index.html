How Spear Phishing Targets You Personally

Unlike generic phishing, spear phishing uses personalized information such as your name, workplace, and social media activity. Attackers craft messages that seem directly relevant to you, increasing the likelihood of engagement. Emails may reference colleagues, recent purchases, or events. Because they feel highly specific, they bypass usual skepticism. This method is responsible for many high-profile breaches.
